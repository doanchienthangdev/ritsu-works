# The Wedge and ICP

The Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) is the customer who experiences the most value the fastest, refers others most often, and churns least. It is narrower than "target market" — a single coherent persona, not a demographic bucket.

The wedge is a NARROWER cut of the ICP through which the startup enters. Facebook's wedge was Harvard students. Airbnb's wedge was conference attendees in San Francisco. A correctly chosen wedge has high willingness to pay AND high referral velocity within the segment.

Product-led growth (often abbreviated PLG) is one go-to-market strategy that uses wedge-driven word-of-mouth to scale.

Observation: companies that narrow their wedge from "small businesses" to "boutique fitness studios with 2-5 locations" typically see CAC drop 40-60% and LTV grow 2-3x within 6 months.
