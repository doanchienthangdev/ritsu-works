# PLG Fundamentals

Product-led growth is the strategy in which the product itself drives acquisition. Defining property: a clear "wow moment" within minutes of first use that pulls more users in via word-of-mouth.

Activation is the milestone where a new user reaches the wow moment. Activation rate (the % of signups reaching it within N days) is the single strongest leading indicator of long-term retention.

The wedge is the narrowest viable segment of the ICP through which a startup enters the market. A correctly chosen wedge maximizes both willingness to pay and word-of-mouth velocity inside that segment.
