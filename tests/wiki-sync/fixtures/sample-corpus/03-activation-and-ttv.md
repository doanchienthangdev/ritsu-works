# Activation and Time-to-Value (TTV)

Activation is the moment a new user reaches the product's "wow moment" — the experience that demonstrates the core value claim. Activation rate is the single best leading indicator of retention.

Time-to-value (TTV) is the elapsed time from signup to activation. Shorter TTV correlates with higher activation. Sub-5-minute TTV is the loose threshold for a product-led-growth (PLG) product that "sells itself."

Observation: companies activating users within their first session retain 4-5x better at 30 days than those whose median TTV exceeds 24 hours. The wedge ICP segment is where TTV is shortest by design — that's how you identify the wedge in the first place.

Decision: prefer free trial over freemium for sub-$100 ACV B2C SaaS. Freemium leaves a pool of users who never convert; free trial forces a decision moment that converts 2-3x better.
