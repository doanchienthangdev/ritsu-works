# Founders who personally onboard first 30 customers discover ICP 3-6 months faster

Founders who personally onboard their first 30 paying customers via 1:1 video calls discover their ICP 3-6 months faster than founders who scale onboarding through documentation alone. Documented in Paul Graham's "Do Things That Don't Scale" (2013) and validated by Airbnb, Stripe, and DoorDash retrospectives.
