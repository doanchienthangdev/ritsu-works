# First-session activation drives 4-5x better 30-day retention vs >24h TTV

Companies whose users activate within their first session retain 4-5x better at 30 days than companies whose median TTV exceeds 24 hours. Observed across thousands of B2B + consumer SaaS products in Amplitude, Mixpanel, and Heap datasets.
