# TTV vs willingness-to-pay tradeoff is under-explored

Empirical observation: products with <5-min TTV often sell at lower ACVs because the "magic" is too easy to dismiss; products with 30-60 min TTV (requiring setup investment) often sell at higher ACVs because the user has psychological skin in the game. Worth a controlled experiment: same product, two onboarding flows, measure ACV willingness 30 days post-activation.
