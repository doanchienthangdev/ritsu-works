# Corporate Strategy Alignment Mapping

*Category: strategy · Toolkit: Data Analytics & AI Strategy*

## What it is
A linkage document that explicitly connects data analytics and AI investments to the company's top 3–5 corporate strategic priorities, ensuring every data initiative has a clear line-of-sight to a business objective the board has already committed to.

**Origin:** Derived from the Balanced Scorecard (Kaplan and Norton, 1992) strategy cascade and the Logical Framework Approach (LFA). Applied to technology and data strategy by McKinsey Digital (2019) as 'anchoring the data strategy to the business strategy'.

## Why it works
The most common reason data and AI programmes lose funding is that they are perceived as 'IT projects' running parallel to the business strategy rather than serving it. Alignment mapping makes the connection explicit and changes the CDO's conversation with the CFO from a cost conversation to a return conversation — every line item in the budget traces to a corporate objective the board has already approved.

## When to use
At Phase 1 Step 1 (summarise the corporate strategy) and as the opening section of every investment proposal for a data or AI initiative.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Interview CEO, CFO, COO, CMO and Head of Strategy to identify the top 3–5 corporate priorities for the next 3 years. Document each as: 'Increase [metric] from [baseline] to [target] by [date]'.
2. For each corporate priority, ask: 'Which data and analytics limitations most constrain our ability to achieve this?' Document the answer — this is the natural alignment opportunity.
3. Map every proposed data or AI initiative to the corporate priority it enables. Initiatives that cannot be mapped are candidates for deprioritisation.
4. Estimate the contribution: 'Initiative A directly enables Corporate Priority 2 by providing the personalisation engine for the e-commerce growth target, contributing ~$5M of the $20M revenue uplift target.'
5. Build the alignment tree: Corporate Priority → Strategic Data Objective → Data/AI Initiative → KPI. This is the CDO's budget narrative.
6. Validate the alignment mapping with the CEO and CFO before finalising the strategy. If the mapping does not resonate, the strategy is not aligned — revise it.

## Real-life example — Walmart
Walmart's data strategy was explicitly anchored to two corporate priorities: (1) compete with Amazon on personalisation (data initiative: Customer 360 data product powering personalised promotions) and (2) reduce operating costs by $1B through supply-chain efficiency (data initiative: demand forecasting and inventory optimisation at SKU-store level). Every data investment was justified by one of these two mappings, giving the CDO a defensible budget narrative.

**So what:** Explicit strategy alignment transforms the data function's budget conversation from 'trust us, data is important' to 'here is the line-of-sight from this specific investment to the revenue and cost targets the board has already committed to'.

## Template
Map every data or AI initiative to at least one corporate priority. Initiatives with no mapping should not be funded. Show the contribution estimate in dollar terms.

- [ ] Corporate priority: metric + target + date (from CEO/CFO strategic plan)
- [ ] Key data/analytics limitation constraining this priority
- [ ] Data or AI initiative that addresses the limitation
- [ ] Estimated contribution to the corporate priority ($ or % improvement)
- [ ] Dependencies: data, team, or platform capability required first
- [ ] Wave assignment (Wave 1 / 2 / 3)
- [ ] Business sponsor accountable for the KPI this enables

## Pitfalls
- Bottom-up alignment (retrofitting initiatives to strategy): counter: always start from corporate priorities and work down. Bottom-up produces rationalisation, not strategy.
- Overly broad alignment ('this initiative supports all five priorities'): counter: force a primary alignment to one priority. Multiple attributions obscure accountability and make ROI measurement impossible.
- Failing to update when corporate strategy changes: counter: rerun the alignment mapping at every annual strategy review.
