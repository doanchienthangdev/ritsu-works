# Change Impact Assessment

*Category: change-management · Toolkit: Digital Transformation & IT Strategy*

## What it is
A structured analysis tool that identifies which roles, processes, and systems are affected by a digital change initiative, quantifies the magnitude of change per group, and surfaces 'change saturation hotspots' where multiple simultaneous changes risk overwhelming teams.

**Origin:** Standard tool in change management practice; formalised in the Prosci methodology and OGC's Managing Successful Programmes. Widely used by Accenture, KPMG, and PwC change management practices.

## Why it works
Different teams experience digital transformation differently: a new ERP system may be a minor system change for finance leadership but a complete workflow redesign for accounts payable clerks. The Change Impact Assessment makes this explicit, enabling the change management team to allocate support resources proportionally.

## When to use
Use in Phase IV Step 3 before planning change management interventions; use to sequence project go-lives to avoid change saturation.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. 1. List all roles and teams affected by each digital project.
2. 2. For each affected group, assess impact magnitude on 4 dimensions: Role change (how different will their job be?), Process change (how different are the ways of working?), System change (new or replaced systems?), Culture change (new behaviours required?).
3. 3. Score magnitude 1–5 per dimension (5 = complete redesign of role/process).
4. 4. Sum to an overall impact score per group.
5. 5. Plot a department-level heat map showing change burden by department by quarter.
6. 6. Identify change saturation hotspots (departments with multiple high-magnitude changes in the same quarter).
7. 7. Sequence or resource-augment to address hotspots.
8. 8. Use the assessment to right-size the change management intervention for each group.

## Real-life example — Rolls-Royce
Rolls-Royce's digital transformation of its MRO (Maintenance, Repair, Overhaul) operations identified aircraft engineers as a Red change saturation group: simultaneous changes to their tooling systems, diagnostic processes, and work scheduling in the same quarter. The Change Impact Assessment triggered a 3-month stagger: tooling system change moved to Q3, diagnostic process change remained Q2, resolving the saturation.

**So what:** Change saturation detection is the CIA's most valuable output — preventing simultaneous high-magnitude changes that overwhelm teams and drive resistance.

## Template
For each affected role/team, score each impact dimension 1–5. Aggregate to a heat map by department and quarter.

- [ ] [Repeat for all affected groups]
- [ ] Saturation hotspots (department × quarter with score >15): [List]
- [ ] Sequencing changes recommended: [Fill in]

## Pitfalls
- Assessment done without interviewing affected teams — impact scores set by project teams are systematically underestimated.
- Assessment done once — re-run when project scope changes.
