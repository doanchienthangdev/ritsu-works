# Manager Integration Toolkit

*Category: change-management · Toolkit: Post Merger Integration*

## What it is
A packaged set of tools, scripts, and guidance enabling every people manager to lead their team through the integration — covering: new team charter facilitation, role-change communication scripts, Day-1 team session agendas, and performance management during transition.

**Origin:** Standard change management practice from Prosci, McKinsey, and Deloitte integration toolkits; codified as a manager-enablement deliverable in all major PMI change management frameworks.

## Why it works
Research consistently shows employees look to their direct manager — not the CEO — for trusted information about how integration will affect them personally. Unprepared managers give inconsistent, anxiety-inducing messages. The manager toolkit converts managers from a risk factor into the integration's most effective change agents.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Design the toolkit around the four highest-anxiety manager scenarios: introducing a new team (combined from both organizations), communicating a role change, running the first team meeting, and managing performance when roles are unclear.
2. For each scenario, provide a step-by-step guide (not just principles), a script or agenda (so managers have words, not just intentions), and a 'common questions' Q&A guide.
3. Deliver the toolkit in a 90-minute live training session (not a PDF drop) so managers can ask questions and practice.
4. Provide managers with a dedicated HR Business Partner contact for situations not covered by the toolkit.
5. Track manager toolkit usage: which modules are being accessed, which scenarios are generating the most HR escalations (this reveals gaps in the toolkit or in the integration itself).
6. Update the toolkit at 30, 60, and 90 days as new integration scenarios emerge.

## Real-life example — Microsoft acquisition of Nokia Mobile (2014, $7.2B)
Microsoft acquired Nokia's mobile business and faced the challenge of managing 25,000 Nokia employees through a cultural transition. Microsoft's manager toolkit included specific guidance for Nokia-origin managers on how to work within Microsoft's 'growth mindset' culture — not just the 'what changed' but the 'how we work differently now'. The toolkit used Nokia managers as change agents rather than passive recipients of the transition.

**So what:** Manager toolkits that are co-designed with managers from the target organization are significantly more credible and used than toolkits designed solely by the acquirer's HR team.

## Template
Deliver as a packaged folder with one document per module. Train all people managers before Day 30.

- [ ] Module 1: Team Charter Introduction — agenda + talking points + Q&A guide
- [ ] Module 2: Role Change Communication — scripts + reactions guidance + HR contacts
- [ ] Module 3: Day-1 / Week-1 Team Session — agenda + icebreaker + psychological safety discussion
- [ ] Module 4: Performance Management During Integration — goal-setting guidance + performance issue handling
- [ ] Module 5: Escalation Guide — when and how to escalate to HR or IMO Director
- [ ] Resources: intranet link, HR Business Partner contacts, integration FAQ

## Pitfalls
- Delivering the toolkit as a document dump — managers need training and practice, not just PDFs.
- Toolkit that is too generic — 'communicate transparently' is not a script; managers need specific words for specific situations.
- Not updating the toolkit as integration evolves — a toolkit built for Day 1 is largely irrelevant by Month 6; update it for the current integration phase.
