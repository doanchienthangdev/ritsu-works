# 7 Degrees of Freedom for Growth

*Category: growth · Toolkit: Business Strategy & Strategic Planning*

## What it is
A McKinsey framework that expands the strategic option set by identifying seven distinct levers through which a company can pursue growth, from winning with existing customers to changing the industry model.

**Origin:** McKinsey & Company, popularized in the 2000s through client work; formalized in Patrick Viguerie, Sven Smit, and Mehrdad Baghai's book 'The Granularity of Growth' (2008).

## Why it works
Most companies instinctively pursue only 1–2 growth levers. By systematically exploring all seven dimensions, leadership avoids leaving high-value growth options unexplored. The framework is generative — it prevents the premature narrowing of options that kills strategic creativity.

## When to use
When growth has plateaued on the current trajectory and leadership needs to expand the option set; when setting a new growth ambition; when responding to a competitor's aggressive growth move.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. For each of the seven degrees, run a structured brainstorm: generate all plausible growth options within that lever for your specific company.
2. Score each option on strategic fit, market size, competitive advantage potential, and execution feasibility.
3. Map each option to the Ansoff quadrant it represents — this ensures the 7DoF analysis is connected to the portfolio risk profile.
4. Consolidate the long-list into a short-list of 5–8 options for detailed evaluation in Phase 3.
5. Ensure at least one high-potential option from each of the seven levers has been genuinely considered before closing the option set — the discipline of the framework is in the exhaustiveness of the search.

## Real-life example — Amazon
Amazon has systematically activated all 7 degrees: (1) more purchases per Prime member through subscriptions and personalization; (2) marketplace share gain through third-party seller growth; (3) adjacencies: groceries (Whole Foods), pharmacy (PillPack); (4) new products: Echo/Alexa hardware; (5) international geographies: India, MENA; (6) new business models: AWS cloud services; (7) acquisitions: MGM Studios, Zappos, Twitch.

**So what:** Amazon's exceptional growth story is a masterclass in simultaneously activating all 7 degrees — each business line created new capabilities that made adjacent levers easier to pull.

## Pitfalls
- Generating options only within the first 2–3 degrees — the framework's value is in forcing exploration of less obvious levers.
- Not connecting 7DoF output to the Ansoff matrix: the options generated need to be assessed on risk profile, not just opportunity size.
- Treating inorganic growth (Degree 7) as the default answer for ambitious growth targets — organic options in Degrees 1–5 often have better risk-adjusted returns.
