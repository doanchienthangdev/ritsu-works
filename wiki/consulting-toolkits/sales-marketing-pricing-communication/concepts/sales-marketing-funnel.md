# Sales & Marketing Funnel

*Category: analysis · Toolkit: Sales, Marketing, Pricing & Communication*

## What it is
A staged model of the customer acquisition and retention journey — from initial Awareness through Interest, Consideration, Intent, Purchase, and Retention/Advocacy — used to diagnose conversion gaps and allocate marketing investment.

**Origin:** Elias St. Elmo Lewis, 1898 (AIDA model: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action); evolved into the modern multi-stage sales funnel through 20th-century direct marketing practice

## Why it works
Revenue is a product of the number of prospects entering the funnel multiplied by the conversion rate at each stage. Improving a conversion rate at a stage with the largest absolute drop-off delivers the highest incremental revenue with the least additional spend.

## When to use
When diagnosing underperforming marketing or sales efficiency, allocating budget across channels, or designing the customer acquisition system from scratch.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Define the specific metric (volume and conversion rate) for each funnel stage using your CRM and analytics data.
2. Calculate stage-by-stage conversion rates: Awareness → Interest, Interest → Consideration, etc.
3. Identify the largest absolute drop-off stage (not just the lowest conversion rate — a large volume at the top with a small rate drop can lose more prospects than a small rate drop at the bottom).
4. Conduct root-cause analysis for the biggest drop-off: interview 5–10 customers who did not convert at that stage; analyse funnel data for patterns (device, channel, segment).
5. Design 2–3 experiments to improve conversion at the diagnosed stage; measure results over 4–6 weeks.
6. Extend the funnel analysis downstream: calculate Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and LTV:CAC ratio by acquisition channel to determine which channels are profitable.

## Real-life example — HubSpot (2011 freemium launch)
HubSpot's funnel analysis in 2011 identified that the largest absolute drop-off was between Awareness (high traffic from content marketing) and Consideration (free trial sign-up). Investigation revealed that the trial sign-up form had 11 fields — reducing it to 4 fields increased trial sign-up conversion from 1.2% to 2.4%, effectively doubling the number of leads entering the sales pipeline with zero additional spend. This single funnel optimisation was worth approximately $15m in incremental pipeline annually at HubSpot's 2011 scale.

**So what:** Funnel analysis is the ROI-maximising lens: find the biggest drop-off, diagnose root cause, run a targeted experiment.

## Pitfalls
- Optimising top-of-funnel (awareness) when the real problem is mid-funnel conversion — more leads into a leaky funnel wastes budget.
- Not segmenting the funnel by channel: a 2% overall conversion rate may be 6% from organic and 0.5% from display — these need different interventions.
- Ignoring the retention stages: acquiring a new customer costs 5–7× retaining an existing one; the funnel must extend to advocacy.
