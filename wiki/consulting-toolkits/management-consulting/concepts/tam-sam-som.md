# TAM / SAM / SOM Market Sizing

*Category: analysis · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
A nested estimate of Total Addressable, Serviceable Addressable, and Serviceable Obtainable Market.

**Origin:** Standard venture-finance and strategy practice for quantifying opportunity size.

## Why it works
Decisions need a sized prize. TAM bounds the universe, SAM the slice your model can serve, SOM the realistic short-term capture — and computing top-down and bottom-up independently triangulates a defensible number.

## When to use
When sizing any market — for the practice, for market entry, or for a launch.

## Visual
`funnel`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Define the unit of demand and the customer you serve.
2. Estimate TAM top-down from industry data (total spend on the category).
3. Filter to SAM by geography, segment and channel you can actually reach.
4. Estimate SOM bottom-up: (reachable accounts × average value × expected win rate).
5. Reconcile top-down vs bottom-up; investigate any gap larger than ~2x.

## Real-life example — Airbnb
Airbnb's early model famously sized its opportunity from total trips/nights booked (TAM) down to budget/online-bookable travel (SAM) to a realistic near-term share (SOM), grounding its growth case.

**So what:** A triangulated market size turns 'huge opportunity' into a number you can plan against.

## Pitfalls
- Top-down-only sizing — counter: always build the bottom-up too.
- Confusing SAM with SOM — counter: SOM applies a realistic win rate.
