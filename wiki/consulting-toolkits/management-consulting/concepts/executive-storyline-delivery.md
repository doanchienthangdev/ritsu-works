# Executive Storyline Delivery

*Category: communication · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
Rehearsing and delivering the recommendation to drive a decision — leading with the answer, pre-empting objections, and closing on owned next steps.

**Origin:** Consulting practice for executive/board communication.

## Why it works
A great analysis dies in a bad meeting. Leading with the recommendation, anticipating the 'killer questions', and explicitly securing decisions and owners converts the work into action rather than a filed PDF.

## When to use
When delivering the final recommendation to the decision-maker.

## Visual
`process-flow`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Dry-run the deck end-to-end, timing to the governing thought.
2. List the toughest likely questions and prepare evidence-backed answers.
3. Open the meeting with the recommendation, not the journey.
4. Handle objections by returning to the data, not opinion.
5. Close by securing the decision and writing down next-step owners and dates.

## Real-life example — Board recommendation (method)
Effective consultants open a board session with the one-line recommendation and the value at stake, then walk supporting arguments only as needed — and leave with named owners and dates, not 'we'll circulate the deck'.

**So what:** The job isn't to present; it's to get a decision and commitment.

## Template
Prep the storyline, objections and the close.

- [ ] Governing thought (1 line)
- [ ] Top 3 killer questions + answers
- [ ] Opening line
- [ ] Decision sought
- [ ] Next-step owners + dates

## Pitfalls
- Narrating the analysis chronologically — counter: lead with the answer.
- Ending without a decision — counter: always close on owners/dates.
