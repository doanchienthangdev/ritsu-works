# PESTLE Analysis

*Category: analysis · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
A scan of the macro-environment across Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental factors.

**Origin:** Evolved from Francis Aguilar's ETPS scan (1967); expanded to PEST and then PESTLE.

## Why it works
Firm performance is shaped by forces outside its control. A structured external scan surfaces tailwinds and threats early so strategy is built for the world as it will be, not as it was.

## When to use
When assessing whether a problem/opportunity is structural, before market entry, and in environmental analysis.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. For each of the six factors, list the 2-3 trends most relevant to the business.
2. Rate each trend's impact and likelihood.
3. Translate each high-impact trend into a 'so what' implication for strategy.
4. Flag the top 3 forces to monitor and feed them into SWOT (O/T).
5. Refresh periodically — the macro picture moves.

## Real-life example — Netflix
Netflix read Technological (broadband ubiquity) and Social (on-demand viewing) trends ahead of rivals, pivoting from DVD-by-mail to streaming and then original content.

**So what:** Acting on macro signals early can reposition a whole business.

## Template
Two trends per factor minimum; convert each into an implication.

- [ ] Political trends
- [ ] Economic trends
- [ ] Social trends
- [ ] Technological trends
- [ ] Legal trends
- [ ] Environmental trends
- [ ] Top-3 to monitor

## Pitfalls
- Listing facts with no 'so what' — counter: every entry needs an implication.
- Boiling the ocean — counter: keep only decision-relevant trends.
