# Competitor Profiling

*Category: analysis · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
A deep profile of each key rival — strategy, cost position, capabilities, recent moves and likely intent — to anticipate competitive response.

**Origin:** Standard competitive-intelligence practice within Porter-style competitor analysis (1980).

## Why it works
Strategy is interactive; your move triggers theirs. Understanding each rival's objectives, assumptions and capabilities lets you predict and pre-empt their reactions.

## When to use
In competitor analysis and before any move likely to provoke a response.

## Visual
`table`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Shortlist the 2-3 most threatening competitors.
2. Profile each across strategy, financials, cost position and capabilities.
3. Infer their assumptions about the market and their objectives.
4. List recent moves and predict their likely response to your strategy.
5. Build counters into your own plan.

## Real-life example — Coca-Cola vs PepsiCo
Each profiles the other obsessively — pricing, distribution, marketing spend, new launches — to anticipate and counter moves in the 'cola wars', shaping promotion calendars and shelf strategy.

**So what:** Anticipating a rival's response is part of choosing your own move.

## Pitfalls
- Mirror-imaging (assuming they think like you) — counter: model their incentives explicitly.
- Stale intel — counter: refresh from recent signals.
