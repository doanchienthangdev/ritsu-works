# Financial Analysis (ratio & trend)

*Category: financial · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
Analysis of a company's financial statements via ratios and trends to assess profitability, liquidity, efficiency and leverage.

**Origin:** Modern financial-statement analysis; DuPont ratio decomposition (DuPont, 1920s).

## Why it works
The financials are the scoreboard. Ratios and trends translate raw statements into comparable signals (margin trend, ROIC, leverage) that locate where economic performance is strong or deteriorating.

## When to use
In the internal analysis and profit-diagnosis work of Phase III/IV.

## Visual
`table`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Gather 3-5 years of income statement, balance sheet and cash flow.
2. Compute profitability, liquidity, efficiency and leverage ratios.
3. Trend each over time and benchmark against peers.
4. Use DuPont to decompose ROE/ROIC into margin × turnover × leverage.
5. Flag the deteriorating drivers and feed them into the issue tree.

## Real-life example — General analysis (DuPont)
A DuPont decomposition can show two firms with the same ROE achieving it very differently — one via high margin, the other via high asset turnover or leverage — pointing diagnosis at the right lever.

**So what:** Ratios reveal not just how a firm performs, but why.
