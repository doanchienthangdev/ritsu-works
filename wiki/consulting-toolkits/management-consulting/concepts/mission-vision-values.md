# Mission, Vision & Values

*Category: planning · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
Three short statements that define why the firm exists (mission), where it is going (vision), and the principles that govern its choices (values).

**Origin:** Long-standing strategic-management practice; codified in works such as Collins & Porras, 'Built to Last' (1994).

## Why it works
A clear purpose and decision-rules let a small team make consistent choices without constant top-down direction, and signal credibility to clients and hires.

## When to use
When founding or repositioning the practice, and when onboarding people.

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Write the mission in one sentence: who you serve and the change you make today.
2. Write the vision: the future state you are moving the firm/clients toward.
3. List 3-5 values, each phrased as a behaviour you would enforce.
4. Test each value against a real trade-off it should resolve.
5. Publish and use them in hiring and client-selection decisions.

## Real-life example — Patagonia
Patagonia's mission ('We're in business to save our home planet') visibly drives concrete decisions — materials, the '1% for the Planet' pledge, and even discouraging over-consumption.

**So what:** Decision-useful purpose statements shape real choices, not just wall art.

## Pitfalls
- Generic platitudes — counter: keep only values that would change a hiring or client decision.
