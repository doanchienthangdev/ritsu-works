# Operating & Legal Structure Design

*Category: planning · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
Choosing the legal entity, delivery model and organisational structure for the consulting practice.

**Origin:** Standard business-formation and operating-model practice.

## Why it works
Structure affects liability, tax, scalability and credibility. Deciding entity type, how associates are engaged, and the delivery model deliberately avoids costly retrofits as the practice grows.

## When to use
When formalising the consulting practice.

## Visual
`table`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Choose a legal entity (sole prop / LLC / Ltd) weighing liability and tax.
2. Decide the talent model (solo, employees, subcontractor bench).
3. Define the delivery model (solo delivery vs networked partners).
4. Select the tooling stack (CRM, project, analysis, deck).
5. Draw the org chart, even if minimal, to make scaling intentional.

## Real-life example — Boutique consultancies (practice)
Many boutiques start as an LLC with a flexible subcontractor bench and partner network, scaling to employees only once utilisation justifies fixed cost — an intentional, staged operating model.

**So what:** Designing structure up front avoids expensive restructuring later.

## Pitfalls
- Defaulting to sole prop and inheriting unlimited liability — counter: weigh entity types.
- No bench plan — counter: design how you scale capacity.
