# Marketing & BD Funnel

*Category: growth · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
A staged model of the buyer's journey (awareness → interest → consideration → intent → purchase → retention) used to design and measure demand generation.

**Origin:** Rooted in the AIDA model (E. St. Elmo Lewis, 1898); modern marketing-funnel practice.

## Why it works
Demand is a leaky pipe. Modelling conversion stage-by-stage shows where prospects drop off and where to focus channels and content to improve the end-to-end conversion rate.

## When to use
When designing the practice's BD/marketing engine.

## Visual
`funnel`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Map the buyer's journey stages for your service.
2. Assign channels and content to each stage.
3. Measure conversion between stages and the cost per stage.
4. Find the biggest leak and fix that stage first.
5. Set a target CAC and qualified-conversation volume per month.

## Real-life example — HubSpot
HubSpot built its growth on a content-led inbound funnel — free tools/blog (awareness) → gated content (interest) → demos (consideration) → trial (intent) → paid (purchase) — measuring conversion at each step.

**So what:** Designing and measuring the funnel turns marketing from art into a managed conversion system.

## Pitfalls
- Top-of-funnel obsession — counter: fix the worst-converting stage.
- No measurement — counter: instrument every stage.
