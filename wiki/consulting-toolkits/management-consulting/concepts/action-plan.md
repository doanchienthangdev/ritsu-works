# Action Plan (Next Steps)

*Category: planning · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
A sequenced list of actions with owners, dates and dependencies that converts a plan into execution.

**Origin:** Standard project-management / execution practice.

## Why it works
Plans without owned, dated actions don't happen. An action plan assigns accountability and sequence so the strategy becomes motion.

## When to use
To close any plan or engagement phase and drive execution.

## Visual
`table`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. List the concrete actions required to execute the plan.
2. Assign a single accountable owner to each.
3. Set start and due dates and note dependencies.
4. Sequence into a 30/60/90-day view.
5. Review progress on a regular cadence and update status.

## Real-life example — Practice launch (method)
A consulting launch action plan sequences legal setup → brand/site → 10 BD conversations → first proposal, each owned and dated across 90 days, turning the business plan into weekly motion.

**So what:** Owned, dated actions are what turn strategy into results.

## Template
One row per action with owner and date.

- [ ] Action
- [ ] Owner
- [ ] Start
- [ ] Due
- [ ] Dependency
- [ ] Status

## Pitfalls
- Actions with no single owner — counter: one accountable name each.
- No cadence — counter: schedule recurring reviews.
