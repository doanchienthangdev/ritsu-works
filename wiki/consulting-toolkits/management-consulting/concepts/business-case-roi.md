# Business Case & ROI Model

*Category: financial · Toolkit: Management Consulting*

## What it is
A quantified case showing the value (incremental profit, cost saved, risk avoided) of the engagement against its fee, with ROI and payback.

**Origin:** Standard investment-appraisal / business-case practice.

## Why it works
Decision-makers fund value, not effort. Quantifying the prize against the fee makes the fee a rounding error and is the strongest driver of proposal win rate.

## When to use
In the proposal and to justify the recommendation's value.

## Visual
`table`

## Step-by-step tutorial
1. Quantify the value the recommendation will create (with ranges).
2. State the total fee/investment.
3. Compute net benefit, ROI and payback period.
4. Show base/upside/downside scenarios.
5. Lead the proposal's commercial section with the ROI.

## Real-life example — Consulting (method)
A procurement-savings engagement quantifies $5M annual savings against a $400K fee — a >10x first-year ROI with a sub-two-month payback — making the buy decision obvious.

**So what:** A strong ROI makes the fee easy to approve.

## Template
Quantify value vs fee; show ROI and payback.

- [ ] Value created
- [ ] Fee

- [ ] ROI
- [ ] Payback

## Pitfalls
- Over-claiming value — counter: use conservative ranges and cite basis.
- Ignoring cost-to-achieve — counter: net the client's implementation cost.
