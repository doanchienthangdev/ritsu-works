# Spaced Repetition

Spaced repetition is a learning technique where review intervals
expand over time. Reviewing a piece of information just before you
forget it strengthens long-term retention more efficiently than
massed practice.

## Origins

The technique grew out of psychology research in the 1960s-70s,
notably the work that informed [[book/make-it-stick]] and the
algorithms behind tools like Anki.

## Mechanism

When you successfully recall an item, the next interval grows
(e.g., 1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 1 month). Failure resets the
interval to a short window.
