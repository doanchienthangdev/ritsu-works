# Ritsu's North Star as a Mastery-Rate Metric

**Type:** idea · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

The book defines a North Star as a metric expressing the value users get—for Ritsu, user value is mastery/true understanding. Could Ritsu's North Star Metric be operationalized as something like 'users who demonstrate measurable mastery within N days of upload'—a rate that directly expresses the core value promise and cannot be moved by any single feature team alone?

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's current north star is expressed as a business outcome ('100 paying who love'), but the framework calls for the metric to express customer value first. A mastery-rate metric would be more product-led and serve as a true leading indicator of retention and revenue.
