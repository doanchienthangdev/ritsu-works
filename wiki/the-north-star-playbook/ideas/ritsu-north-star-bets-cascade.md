# Map Ritsu's Sprint Work to a Levels-of-Bets Cascade

**Type:** idea · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Ritsu currently has a north-star metric ('100 paying who love') and input metrics (activation rate, day-30 retention, free-to-paid conversion) but the connection between sprint-level work and those inputs is implicit. Explicitly tagging every sprint item with its Level (3 = sprint intervention, 2 = opportunity, 1 = input assumption) would surface misaligned work early and make the product roadmap more transparent to the whole AI workforce.
