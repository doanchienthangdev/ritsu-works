# North Star Onboarding for New AI Agents

**Type:** idea · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

The chapter recommends an onboarding process to educate new team members on the North Star Framework. For Ritsu's AI-native workforce, this maps to ensuring every new agent role definition (in governance/ROLES.md and .claude/agents/) includes explicit linkage from the agent's KPIs back to the company north star ('100 paying who love'), making the connection as automatic for agents as it should be for human employees.
