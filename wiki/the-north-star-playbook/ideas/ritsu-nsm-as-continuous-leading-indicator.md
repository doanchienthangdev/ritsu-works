## Summary

Ritsu's current north star ('100 paying who love') is a milestone count rather than a continuously tracked leading indicator of revenue direction. The chapter's directional-alignment test suggests Ritsu should additionally define a weekly-cadence NSM — such as 'weekly active learners completing at least one active-learning activity' — that can be checked against MRR trajectory to validate the metric's predictive power and trigger revision when they diverge.
