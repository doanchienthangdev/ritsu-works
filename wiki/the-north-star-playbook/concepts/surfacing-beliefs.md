# Surfacing Beliefs

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

A structured practice of making explicit the hidden assumptions, biases, and theories team members hold about customers, causation, market trends, technology, and competitors. The chapter treats unexamined beliefs as a root cause of teams failing to converge on a North Star. Using fill-in-the-blank templates, teams cast a wide net to expose these biases before aligning on a metric.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps](../chapters/chapter-04-troubleshooting-fixing-issues-and-avoiding-tr.md)** (PDF pp. 34–42, confidence 0.95):

  > Every person approaches a business problem or product opportunity with beliefs and biases.

  > If you fail to uncover your colleagues' beliefs, you may leave unresolved tensions within a team, miss opportunities to align, and overlook unique perspectives about your product.
