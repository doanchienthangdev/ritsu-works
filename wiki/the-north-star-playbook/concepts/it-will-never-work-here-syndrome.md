# 'It'll Never Work Here' Syndrome

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

A predictable moment in workshops where teams become so overwhelmed by existing organisational dysfunction — vanity metrics, feature factories, tech-stack silos, KPI abuse — that they abandon progress. The chapter names this as a trap and prescribes two antidotes: 'start where you are' (acknowledge imperfections without letting them block movement) and 'start small' (pick a bounded product scope you can actually impact).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps](../chapters/chapter-04-troubleshooting-fixing-issues-and-avoiding-tr.md)** (PDF pp. 34–42, confidence 0.95):

  > They start with enthusiasm, but then realize their company has so many problems and fear they can't ever change. It's the classic "it'll never work here" syndrome, exacerbated by the lack of a common framework.

  > Acknowledge current imperfections but don't let them stop you from making progress.
