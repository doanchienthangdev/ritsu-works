# Company as a Value-Creation System

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

The chapter frames a product-led company as a whole system designed for sustainable value creation, where both product-facing and operational/support systems must function well. When support systems fail—analogous to the circulatory, nervous, and digestive systems—they create drag that slows the core value-creation engine. This framing reframes technical debt as 'drag on value' rather than an engineering concern.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.95):

  > One way to think of a product-led company is to imagine the whole company as a value creation system that meets the needs of everyone involved—customers, the team, the broader community, etc.

  > Reframe technical debt as drag on value to emphasize how it limits impact and affects business results.
