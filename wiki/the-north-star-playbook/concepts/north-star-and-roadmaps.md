# North Star Framework and Theme-Based Roadmaps

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

The North Star works best integrated with theme-based roadmaps that account for uncertainty over time, rather than prescriptive feature-and-date declarations. Every roadmap item should link to inputs and the NSM. The chapter recommends replacing requirement documents with succinct one-pagers that make bets explicit and transparent—'a roadmap of one-pagers is far more powerful than a roadmap of one-word project/feature names.'
