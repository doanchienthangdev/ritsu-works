# North Star-Driven Prioritization

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Prioritization under the North Star considers both the input's influence on the NSM and the opportunity's likelihood of driving that input. The framework favors many small bets over one large bet, optimizes for expected value rather than work throughput, and warns against large batches with infrequent learning loops. 'Experimentation friendliness' is more valuable than duration estimates in product-led contexts.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.95):

  > When prioritizing, consider both the influence of the input on the North Star and the likelihood of the opportunity to drive the input.

  > A series of small bets or experiments is preferable to one big bet or experiment.
