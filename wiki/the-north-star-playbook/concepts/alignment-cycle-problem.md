# Alignment Cycle Problem

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 0 — Introduction: Why should you read this playbook?

## Summary

Many product organizations fall into a recurring pattern of misalignment—cycling through shiny objects, success theater, and false starts—even when team members are experienced and data-literate. Discussions produce momentary consensus but fail to stick, reverting to 'business as usual.' The root cause is the absence of a shared connective metric that bridges customer, product, and business perspectives.
