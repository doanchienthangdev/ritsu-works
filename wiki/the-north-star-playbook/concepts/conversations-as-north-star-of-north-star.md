# Conversations as the North Star of the North Star

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

The chapter argues that the most valuable output of the North Star Framework is not the metric itself but the quality of conversations it generates. Teams discussing beliefs, assumptions, strategy, value exchanges, and users surface what matters to teammates, customers, and the business. These conversations lead to better products and happier teams.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework](../chapters/chapter-07-reap-the-benefits-of-the-north-star-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 59–63, confidence 0.95):

  > The quality of your conversations is the real secret to the North Star— the North Star of the North Star.

  > But through this work, you'll have meaningful conversations about beliefs and assumptions, strategy and value exchanges, users and customers, inputs and outputs, leading and lagging, your game and your bets.
