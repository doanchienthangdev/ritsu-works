# North Star Adoption Signals

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

A set of observable behavioral and linguistic signals that indicate the North Star Framework is working within an organization. These include team members being able to explain how their daily work connects to the NSM, improved morale and cohesion, the ability to say 'no' with evidence, and non-product staff using language like 'inputs' or 'our North Star'. The signals collectively indicate cultural adoption has occurred.
