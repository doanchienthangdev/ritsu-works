# Feature Factory Risk

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

The failure mode where PLG teams without a North Star Metric default to shipping features without a coherent strategy, unable to sustainably improve user experience. The term 'feature factory' describes a team that builds requirements and delivers specifications rather than solving customer needs through missions, experiments, and bets.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework](../chapters/chapter-01-about-the-north-star-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 6–13, confidence 0.9):

  > Without a North Star, PLG teams run the risk of becoming feature factories—and will struggle to sustainably improve the user experience.
