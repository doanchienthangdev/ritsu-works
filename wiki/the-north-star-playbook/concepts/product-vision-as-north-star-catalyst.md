# Product Vision as North Star Catalyst

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

An existing product vision statement can serve as a rich source of clues for defining a North Star Metric. The chapter shows how to extract insights about target customer, key benefit, and differentiation from vision statements, and also flags when a vision is too broad or absent—requiring teams to first collaboratively write one using Geoffrey Moore's Crossing the Chasm template.
