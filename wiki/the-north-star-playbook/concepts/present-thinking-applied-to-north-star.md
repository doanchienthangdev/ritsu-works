# Present Thinking Applied to North Star

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

An adaptation of Jabe Bloom's present thinking questions specifically for the North Star Framework. Teams regularly ask: Can we align current efforts with our inputs? Is the framework supporting today's decisions? Can every team member trace their work to the NSM? Has confidence in our inputs changed, and why? What have we learned that requires us to revisit our beliefs and value exchanges? The goal is continuous refinement rather than periodic overhaul.
