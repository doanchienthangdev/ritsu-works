# North Star Sponsor and Leadership Buy-In

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

Two of the five structural requirements for sustaining the North Star Framework: a sponsor who has both influence and authority, and genuine buy-in from leadership. The chapter frames these as non-negotiable prerequisites for making the framework stick beyond workshops and announcements.
