# Bet Language Over Experiments

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

The chapter advocates replacing 'experiment' with 'bet' to reduce executive resistance and make outcome-oriented thinking palatable across hierarchies. A bet is any initiative associated with a potential outcome—a hypothesis you're trying to prove. Using 'bet' acknowledges risk, reward, and the legitimacy of uncertainty without triggering anxiety about teams 'not getting to work.'

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.95):

  > A bet is an initiative of any size that's associated with a potential outcome. Think of a bet akin to a hypothesis that you're trying to prove.

  > Bets capture that perfect interplay between assumptions, desired outcomes, experimentation, risk, and reward.
