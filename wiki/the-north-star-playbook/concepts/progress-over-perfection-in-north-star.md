# Progress Over Perfection in North Star Definition

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

The chapter explicitly rejects the belief that only a statistically rigorous model is acceptable for a North Star Metric. Thresholds embedded in the metric (e.g., 'two or more people') are not mathematically proven — they are aspirational and achievable guesses. The goal is a combination of customer behaviors consistent with strategy, held openly and with acknowledged uncertainty.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > The important thing is to identify thresholds that are both aspirational and achievable. Once you've done that, don't get too worried about whether you've selected the perfect threshold.
