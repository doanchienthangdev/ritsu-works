# Measuring-Before-Perfecting Trap

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

One of the four named common traps: teams — especially those with histories of failing to predict customer success — discard conceptually valid North Star candidates because they cannot immediately specify measurement. The trap conflates 'currently unmeasured' with 'unmeasurable', causing teams to retreat to metrics that are easy to capture but strategically hollow.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps](../chapters/chapter-04-troubleshooting-fixing-issues-and-avoiding-tr.md)** (PDF pp. 34–42, confidence 0.95):

  > Though "measurable" is a characteristic of a good North Star, avoid getting too concerned too soon with how you'll calculate the metric or its inputs.

  > They disregard promising concepts because they aren't yet sure how to measure them.
