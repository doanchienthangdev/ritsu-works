# North Star Inputs–Opportunities–Interventions Hierarchy

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

The statement exercise structures thinking into four levels: North Star (stable, customer-centric, mid/long-term), Inputs (addressable variables that drive the North Star, lasting quarters to years), Opportunities (leverage points that influence inputs, directional but solution-agnostic), and Interventions (specific experiments and work items, time-bound and changeable). Each level has distinct characteristics to prevent conflation.
