# North Star Metric Name and Definition

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

Every North Star Metric requires both a name and a definition. The name should be engaging, pithy, and inspirational; the definition must be precise and clear, specifying exactly how the metric will be measured. The chapter contrasts a vague metric name with a strong alternative (Frequent Content Sharers with a 12-week rolling window) to illustrate the difference.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > Your North Star Metric's name should be engaging and descriptive—something pithy and punchy that inspires your organization—and your definition should be precise and clear, explaining exactly how you'll measure it.

  > Our North Star Metric: Frequent Content Sharers (FCS) Definition: The number of unique subscribers who share an average of two or more articles per week during the previous 12-week period

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's North Star ('100 paying who love') benefits from this discipline: a crisp name + an exact operationalization (paying = active subscription, love = NPS/retention threshold) prevents internal drift in how teams track the metric.
