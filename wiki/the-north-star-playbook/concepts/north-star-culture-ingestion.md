# North Star Culture Ingestion

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

The process of deeply embedding the North Star Framework into an organization's culture so it becomes an ongoing practice rather than a one-time initiative. The chapter identifies five structural enablers: a sponsor with influence and authority, leadership buy-in, communication and change management processes, an onboarding process, and approval processes. Without these systems, the North Star risks being treated as a 'flavor of the week' proclamation.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > Your North Star is not a proclamation you nail to the wall and magically everyone starts to follow. Instead, you need to have systems in place to drive it forward.

  > By deeply ingraining it into your culture.
