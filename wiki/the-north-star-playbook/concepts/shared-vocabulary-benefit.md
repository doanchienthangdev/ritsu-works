# Shared Vocabulary as a North Star Benefit

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

One concrete benefit the North Star Framework delivers is a shared vocabulary across teams. When engineers, product managers, designers, and data scientists work through North Star inputs together, they develop common language for discussing strategy, value exchanges, and bets — reducing miscommunication in ongoing decision-making.
