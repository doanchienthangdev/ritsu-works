# Psychological Safety for Productive Disagreement

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

The chapter's diagram positions psychological safety as a foundational enabler of valuable conversations, which in turn yield insights, feedback loops, shared vocabulary, better products, and happier teams. The framework deliberately creates space for nagging disagreements to surface, treating them as productive rather than threatening.
