# The Work

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

The chapter's term for the day-to-day tasks teams perform—research, design, software development, refactoring, prototyping, testing. The North Star Metric and inputs should be directly connected to this work, ensuring that what teams do daily aligns with the product strategy the North Star guides.
