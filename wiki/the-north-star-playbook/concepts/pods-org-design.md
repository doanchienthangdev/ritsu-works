# Pods: Input-Focused Team Organization

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Rather than organizing teams around tech stacks, features, or workflows, the chapter recommends 'pods'—teams or groups of teams dedicated to a single North Star input for extended periods. This structure minimizes handoffs, keeps teams outcome-focused, and resists the pull of organizing around organizational convenience. Ben Horowitz's dictum that 'all organizational designs are bad' is cited to lower the bar for experimentation.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.9):

  > Consider letting a team or group of teams focus on a single input for an extended period of time. Amplitude refers to this product team structure as pods.

  > Organize to minimize handoffs and start together whenever possible.
