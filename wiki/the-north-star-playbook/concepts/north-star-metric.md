# North Star Metric

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 0 — Introduction: Why should you read this playbook?

## Summary

A single critical rate, count, or ratio that represents a product's strategy and captures the value users derive from it. It must represent user value, be within product and marketing's sphere of influence, and serve as a leading indicator of revenue. It is a dependent variable—an outcome—that should not be influenced directly; instead teams influence it by moving its input metrics.
