# North Star Strategy Shift and Metric Change

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

When an organization's strategy changes significantly, its North Star Metric should change too. A revised NSM then cascades: it changes the bets teams prioritize and the metrics by which they measure success, creating clarity throughout the product organization. The chapter frames this as a healthy, expected evolution rather than a failure.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > Amplitude changes its North Star whenever the company makes a significant shift in strategy.

  > These shifts should also change what bets you prioritize and create clarity for the team.
