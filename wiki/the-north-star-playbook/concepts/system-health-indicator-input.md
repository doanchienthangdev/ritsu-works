# System Health Indicator Input

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Alongside product-facing inputs, teams are advised to include a 'system health indicator' as a dedicated North Star input. This composite metric covers non-feature factors—uptime, cycle times, deployment processes, tooling, ramp-up time—that users don't directly experience but that create drag on value creation if neglected. Troy Magennis's six health areas (value, consistency, quality, speed, quantity, sustainability) provide a template.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.95):

  > This input can cover critical, non-feature factors that users might not directly experience but that indirectly affect your product's overall quality and your engineering and design teams' ability to work effectively.

  > Most important is that health is a balance. It is not just one thing, and you can't just focus on one thing.
