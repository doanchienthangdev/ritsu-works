# Three Organizational Languages

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 0 — Introduction: Why should you read this playbook?

## Summary

Product teams typically speak three distinct languages simultaneously: the language of the customer (needs, goals, experiences, delight), the language of the product (features, workflows, releases), and the language of the business (vision, differentiation, revenue, growth). Misalignment arises when these languages are not bridged by a shared metric. The North Star Framework is positioned as the connective tissue between them.
