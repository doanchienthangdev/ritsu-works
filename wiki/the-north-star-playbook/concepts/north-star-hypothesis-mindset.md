# North Star as a Hypothesis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

The chapter frames the first version of a North Star Metric not as a final answer but as a hypothesis about the levers of growth. Uncertainty is explicitly normal; teams should expect to iterate, pressure-test, and refine the metric over time rather than demanding perfection upfront.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > "You're trying to elicit a hypothesis. A lot of people don't realize that their first pass at the North Star is literally a hypothesis about the levers of growth for their company. So uncertainty is normal."

  > The key is to build a North Star that's directionally accurate and then adapt as you learn.
