# North Star Metric Inputs Primacy

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

The chapter emphasises that the North Star Metric itself is not directly actionable — the input metrics are. Teams that fixate on the headline metric neglect the levers they can actually pull. The recommended fix is brainstorming and mind-mapping the formula of inputs that produce the metric, using the depth/breadth/frequency/efficiency heuristic to translate abstract inputs into concrete product decisions.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps](../chapters/chapter-04-troubleshooting-fixing-issues-and-avoiding-tr.md)** (PDF pp. 34–42, confidence 0.95):

  > By design, the North Star Metric is not immediately actionable—the inputs are.

  > If you find yourself thinking only about your metric, revisit your inputs. Do some brainstorming or group mind-mapping about the formula of inputs that produces the metric and vice versa.

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's '100 paying who love' North Star requires specific input metrics (activation rate, day-7 retention, very-disappointed %) in 10-metrics; this concept explains why those inputs — not the composite — are what product and GTM teams act on week to week.
