# North Star Framework Scope Exclusions

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

The North Star Framework is explicitly not a roadmap, software development process, prioritization framework, goal-setting framework (like OKRs), management by objectives (MBOs), or a one-time quick fix. Conflating it with these adjacent tools dilutes its value as a sustained strategic alignment mechanism.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > your North Star Framework is not: • Your roadmap • Your software development process • A prioritization framework • A goal-setting framework, like objectives and key results (OKRs)

  > The North Star Framework is powerful—but it's not a silver bullet.
