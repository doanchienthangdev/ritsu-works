# North Star Revision Trigger

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

The chapter identifies three conditions that signal a North Star Metric should be revised: the market has changed, the business is at a different stage, or the metric is not controllable. The key diagnostic test is directional alignment with revenue — if the NSM is flat but revenue is not, or vice versa, the metric is no longer serving as a valid leading indicator and must change.
