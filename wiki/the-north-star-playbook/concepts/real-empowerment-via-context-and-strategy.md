# Real Empowerment via Context and Strategy

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

The chapter distinguishes pseudo-empowerment (letting teams do whatever they want, then penalizing divergence) from real empowerment. Real empowerment, as articulated through the Jane case study, requires providing context, a well-thought-out strategy, and no sugar-coating so that teams can make high-impact decisions rather than spin their wheels.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework](../chapters/chapter-07-reap-the-benefits-of-the-north-star-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 59–63, confidence 0.95):

  > "It feels like they equate empowerment to just letting us do what we want, and then getting nervous—or angry even—when what we do isn't what they would have done. That's not how I view real empowerment. We're just spinning our wheels at the moment."

  > Jane went on to describe her view of real empowerment as containing context, a well thought-out strategy, and no sugar coating. She didn't want work to be easier; rather, she wanted her work to have more impact.
