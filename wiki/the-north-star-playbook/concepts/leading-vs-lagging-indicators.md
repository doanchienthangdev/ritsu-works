# Leading vs. Lagging Indicators

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

The North Star Metric is explicitly a leading indicator—it predicts future business results (revenue, retention) rather than measuring them after the fact. The framework distinguishes between leading indicators (the North Star and its inputs, which teams can act on) and lagging indicators (revenue, business results), with the North Star sitting between day-to-day work and long-term business outcomes.
