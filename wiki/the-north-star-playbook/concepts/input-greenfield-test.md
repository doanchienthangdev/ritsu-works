# Input Greenfield Test

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

A two-minute brainstorm exercise to test whether an input is pitched at the right level of specificity. The team ignores current roadmaps and tries to generate as many opportunities as possible to influence the input. Too few ideas suggests the input is too specific; too many unfocused ideas suggests it is too broad.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > If your team quickly runs out of ideas, then you might need higher level inputs. If they're swimming in ideas—many of which are overly broad—then you should get more specific.
