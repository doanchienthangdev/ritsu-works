# Gap Thinking vs. Present Thinking

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

A contrast coined by product development expert Jabe Bloom. Gap thinking fixes teams on a future end state and attempts to close the gap; it produces long feedback cycles and loss of momentum when the future state is not achieved. Present thinking instead asks 'Where are we? What do I need now? How do I improve the current way of working?' — treating improvement as continuous iteration on current conditions rather than pursuit of a fixed endpoint.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > In a gap-thinking model, teams examine the current condition, envision a future state, and attempt to define and close the gap between the two.

  > In present thinking, you recognize the reality of the present and work to change it. Present thinking asks, 'Where are we? What do I need now? How do I improve the current way of working?'
