# Neutral Third Party in North Star Sessions

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

The chapter recommends including a neutral third party — someone from outside the immediate team (e.g., a data scientist from a different product team) — in North Star workshops. This person helps prevent groupthink, challenges assumptions, and keeps the conversation grounded rather than overly theoretical.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework](../chapters/chapter-07-reap-the-benefits-of-the-north-star-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 59–63, confidence 0.9):

  > a neutral third party can be a welcome addition

  > The data scientist talked them out of getting too theoretical ("As much as I want to dig into this…").

  > The designer challenged some assumptions about their chosen value exchanges ("We don't know that, and need to do more research").
