# Customer Value in North Star Metric

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

A North Star Metric must represent what customers genuinely value about the product, not simply usage counts. Metrics like 'Daily Active Users' or 'Registered Users' are explicitly rejected because they say nothing about customer value. The metric should reflect the outcome customers seek, not just their activity.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):
