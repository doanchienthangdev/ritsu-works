# Product-Led Growth (PLG)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

A growth motion that leverages the product itself—rather than marketing or sales—to drive acquisition, retention, and monetization. Product-led organizations optimize team structures, funding cycles, and communication channels around product success. The North Star Framework is described as especially well-suited for PLG companies. Without a North Star, PLG teams risk becoming feature factories that struggle to sustainably improve user experience.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework](../chapters/chapter-01-about-the-north-star-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 6–13, confidence 0.95):

  > PLG is a growth motion that leverages the product, rather than marketing or sales, to drive acquisition, retention, and monetization.

  > Without a North Star, PLG teams run the risk of becoming feature factories—and will struggle to sustainably improve the user experience.

  > The backbone of product-led growth is about your user experience. Is the user getting something out of the product that's satisfying their reason for engaging, and is it easy to get to that point?
