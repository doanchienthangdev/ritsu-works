# Mind Mapping for Input Discovery

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

The chapter recommends collaborative mind mapping on a whiteboard to discover and cluster the inputs that drive a North Star Metric. The process starts messy, combining related concepts and eliminating extraneous information across multiple passes until each contributing factor is cleanly named and defined at a high level before being operationalized into exact metrics.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > Mind mapping is a helpful technique for identifying inputs and metrics. Start by noting your North Star Metric—or even just a candidate for it—on a whiteboard. Then collaboratively note relationships and concepts in clusters.

  > Spend time editing and clarifying the concepts in your mind map so that your metric is at its heart and the contributing factors are all neatly named and defined at a high level
