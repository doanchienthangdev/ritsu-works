# Levels of Bets

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

A four-level hierarchy (0–3) for connecting daily work to the North Star. Level 0 ties the North Star Metric to company success over years; Level 1 links inputs to the NSM over quarters; Level 2 identifies opportunities to influence inputs over months; Level 3 covers sprint-scale interventions. Each level is related—smaller bets cascade up to accomplish larger bets, with risk and uncertainty increasing from Level 3 to Level 0.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.95):

  > Teams can categorize bets into levels, and associate a specific time frame for feedback, execution, or review. Each level is related—you knock out the smaller bets to get to the larger bets.
