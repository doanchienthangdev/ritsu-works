# Leading vs. Lagging Indicator

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

Lagging indicators describe what has already happened (e.g., Monthly Recurring Revenue, ARPU), while leading indicators predict future business outcomes. A good North Star Metric is a leading indicator that forecasts medium- to long-term sustainable growth, not a backward-looking revenue figure.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > Some metrics tell you what has already happened to your business; these are lagging indicators. Other metrics predict what will happen to your business; these are leading indicators.

  > Ideally, your North Star Metric predicts medium- to long-term sustainable growth.
