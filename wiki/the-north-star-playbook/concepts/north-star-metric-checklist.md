# North Star Metric Checklist

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

A seven-criterion checklist for evaluating whether a North Star Metric is effective. The criteria are: expresses customer value, represents vision and strategy, is a leading indicator, is actionable, is understandable to non-technical partners, is measurable, and is not a vanity metric. Teams use it to avoid common pitfalls during planning.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > Does it express customer value?

  > Is it a leading indicator of success?

  > Is it actionable?

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's north star ('100 paying who love') can be stress-tested against these seven criteria; in particular the 'leading indicator' criterion maps directly to the activation and retention signals tracked in 10-metrics.
