# North Star Statement Exercise

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

A structured top-down worksheet that guides teams to articulate their North Star qualitatively before quantifying it. The hierarchy flows from North Star statement → Inputs → Opportunities → Interventions, each with measurement options. The exercise prevents premature fixation on numbers and ensures the team understands the purpose behind the metric.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > The North Star Framework statement exercise helps focus your teams on ideas before metrics.

  > Completing this worksheet is a top-down exercise because you go from the statement and functions to more granular roadmap stuff like measurement options.

  > One thing we've observed is that if someone can't explain the North Star Framework qualitatively, then they won't be able to explain it quantitatively.
