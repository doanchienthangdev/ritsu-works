# North Star Framework and OKRs Integration

**Type:** concept · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

OKRs are point-in-time goals while the North Star Framework is less prescriptive about time—teams must be mindful of this mismatch. The recommended approach is to frame OKRs as the expected impact of Level 2 bets on one or more inputs, rather than delivery milestones. Some teams find inputs sufficient and safely retire OKRs entirely.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action](../chapters/chapter-06-putting-your-north-star-into-action.md)** (PDF pp. 48–58, confidence 0.95):

  > OKRs are usually point-in-time goals. Teams want to produce a result in a timeframe. The North Star Framework is less prescriptive about time.

  > Some teams find that linking their work to inputs—and setting goals based on the expected impact of their work on inputs—means that they can safely retire OKRs.
