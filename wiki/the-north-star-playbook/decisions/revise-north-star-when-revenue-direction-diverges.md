# Revise North Star When Revenue Direction Diverges

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

If the North Star Metric is flat while revenue is not (or vice versa), it is no longer acting as a valid leading indicator and must be revised. This is the primary operational test the chapter recommends for deciding when to change an NSM. Attachment to the existing metric should not override this signal.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > If your North Star is flat, then expect your revenue to be flat. But if at any point your North Star isn't directionally indicating where your revenue is going, then it's time to revise it.

  > Changing their North Star is often one of the hardest things for companies: 'They get too attached to it.'
