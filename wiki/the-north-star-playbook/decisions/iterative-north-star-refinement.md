# Iteratively Refine the North Star Rather Than Seeking Linear Perfection

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

Defining the North Star and its inputs is explicitly non-linear: teams should expect to cycle between the metric and inputs, adapting each as the other becomes clearer. The recommended approach is to 'try on' a candidate metric, work on inputs, and then revisit the metric — treating iteration as healthy rather than as failure.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > "Be willing to just try on a North Star for size," John recommends. "When you get one that's halfway decent, throw it in there. Pick one, work on inputs, and then you can go back and iterate."

  > Defining your metric and its inputs isn't a linear process, so you might experience a lot of back and forth in these steps.
