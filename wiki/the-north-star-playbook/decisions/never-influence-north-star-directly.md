# Never Influence the North Star Directly

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

A North Star Metric should be designed so that it cannot be moved by any single team action in isolation. If the metric can be moved directly, it is not a good North Star—it should be 'one level out of reach,' acting as a composite of the parts of the company working in tandem. Teams should influence the North Star only by moving its input metrics.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework](../chapters/chapter-01-about-the-north-star-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 6–13, confidence 0.95):

  > Never try to influence the North Star directly.

  > If you can move your North Star directly, it's probably not a good North Star. The goal of the North Star is to be one level out of reach. It's to get people thinking about why is that going up, or why is that going down. It's meant to be a composite of the parts of the company working in tandem together.
