# Start Small When Overwhelmed by Dysfunction

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

When an organisation's dysfunction makes full North Star adoption feel impossible, the chapter advises picking a bounded product scope — even a small percentage of the company's portfolio — where a team has real ability to influence outcomes. Building and testing the North Star framework within that slice generates evidence and momentum without requiring company-wide change first.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps](../chapters/chapter-04-troubleshooting-fixing-issues-and-avoiding-tr.md)** (PDF pp. 34–42, confidence 0.95):

  > Pick a product scope you have an ability to impact, even if it just represents a small percentage of your company's business.

  > Build and test a North Star—the metric, the inputs, the results they impact—for just that portion of your company or product portfolio.
