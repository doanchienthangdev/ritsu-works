# Socialize the North Star Repeatedly Across Forums

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

Once defined, the North Star should be shared not just at a single all-company presentation but continuously — formally and informally across all sorts of forums. Encouraging colleagues to share it themselves amplifies organizational alignment and prepares the team for the next challenge of putting the framework into action.
