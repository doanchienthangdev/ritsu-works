# Define the Opportunity Before Connecting Work to Inputs

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Teams commonly skip the opportunity-definition step and jump straight from a feature idea to claiming it serves a North Star input. The chapter calls this out as a key implementation failure. Connecting features to inputs without first defining the opportunity leads to work that feels aligned but isn't.
