# Shift 'Done' Language to 'Review' Language

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Many companies label work 'done' and move on, missing the learning loop. The chapter recommends shifting vocabulary from 'to do / doing / done' to 'focus on next / focusing / review' or 'to try / trying / review.' This linguistic shift embeds continuous learning into the workflow rather than treating completion as the endpoint.
