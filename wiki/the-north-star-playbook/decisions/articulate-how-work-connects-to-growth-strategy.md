# Articulate How Every Role Connects to Growth Strategy

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 0 — Introduction: Why should you read this playbook?

## Summary

The playbook argues that if any person in an organization cannot articulate how their daily work connects to a sustainable growth strategy, the organization has a strategic or communication failure. Leaders must ensure this connection is transparent and explicit for everyone, not just executives. The North Star Framework is the mechanism to achieve this.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 0 — Introduction: Why should you read this playbook?](../chapters/chapter-00-introduction-why-should-you-read-this-playboo.md)** (PDF pp. 4–5, confidence 0.95):

  > If anyone in your organization can't come into work and articulate how the work they're doing is connected to a sustainable growth strategy, then either you don't have a strategy, there's a messy middle problem where we're not explaining it right, or you're working on the wrong thing.
