# Include Cross-Functional and Neutral Voices in North Star Workshops

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

The chapter recommends that North Star workshops include participants beyond the immediate product team — specifically a neutral third party from a different team. This prevents insular thinking, surfaces unspoken assumptions, and prevents the conversation from becoming too theoretical or too deferential to hierarchy.
