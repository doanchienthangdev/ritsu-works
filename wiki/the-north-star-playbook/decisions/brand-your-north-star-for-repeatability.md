# Brand Your North Star for Repeatability

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

The chapter recommends naming the North Star Metric in a way that is simple enough to explain and repeat across the organisation. The name and framing should focus the entire growth product roadmap on moving the metric, so simplicity and memorability are as important as precision.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > "You have to be able to explain it to other people," writes Chen. "So make it dead simple to talk about, repeat it over and over, and generally simplify it to the point where a lot of your growth product roadmap is focused on moving the metric up."
