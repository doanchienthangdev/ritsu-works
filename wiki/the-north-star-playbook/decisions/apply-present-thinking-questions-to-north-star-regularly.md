# Apply Present Thinking Questions to North Star Regularly

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

To maintain momentum and avoid gap-thinking stagnation, teams should regularly apply a set of present-thinking diagnostic questions to their North Star: Can we align current efforts with our inputs? Is the framework supporting near-term decisions? Can every team member trace their work to the NSM? Has confidence in inputs changed? What have we learned that requires revisiting beliefs? These questions should be part of regular review cadences.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > What decisions do we need to make today and in the near future? Is the framework supporting those decisions? If not, how can we adapt it?

  > What have we learned recently? Let's revisit our beliefs, value exchanges, etc. to see what needs tweaking.
