# Run Iterative North Star Workshops, Not a Single Session

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

The first North Star workshop is only round one. Most teams need subsequent sessions to gather additional stakeholder perspectives, pressure-test ideas with data, precisely define the metric and inputs, strengthen the connection to strategy, and align the North Star to development processes. A workshop can be completed in under two hours but should not be treated as producing a final answer.
