# Choose an Actionable Metric, Not One Externally Determined

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

The North Star Metric must be something the team can directly influence. Metrics driven primarily by macro trends, market forces, or external realities outside the product's control are disqualified. The chapter gives the example of 'Customers' Lifelong Employees' being invalid for an HR app because labor market trends dominate it.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > Your North Star should be something you believe you can influence or change. This means it shouldn't measure a broader market trend or reflect real-world realities that would be true whether your product existed or not.
