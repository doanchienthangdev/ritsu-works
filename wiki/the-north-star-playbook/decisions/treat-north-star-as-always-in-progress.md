# Treat North Star as Always In Progress

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

Teams should resist the temptation to declare their North Star 'done.' The chapter states that successful North Star teams are never done — they are always learning, grappling with uncertainty, and continuously checking whether the NSM and its inputs represent current beliefs, product vision, and product strategy, then refining accordingly.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > Teams that successfully implement frameworks like the North Star Framework are never 'done.' They're always learning and grappling with uncertainty.

  > The real muscle to develop is continuously checking to see whether your North Star Metric and inputs represent your current beliefs, product vision, and product strategy—then refine accordingly.

## Ritsu relevance

Directly applicable to Ritsu's north-star.md governance — the metric and its input metrics should be reviewed at each weekly review cycle, not treated as a fixed declaration.
