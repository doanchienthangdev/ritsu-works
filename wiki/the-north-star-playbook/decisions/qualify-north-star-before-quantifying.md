# Qualify the North Star Before Quantifying It

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

Teams should articulate the North Star qualitatively — in plain language, as a statement of purpose — before attempting to define the quantitative metric. The chapter warns against rushing to numbers and recommends the statement exercise as a prerequisite step.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > we recommend spending a bit more time thinking about your North Star qualitatively—rather than jumping to the quantitative—to ensure your team doesn't lose sight of your overall purpose.

  > Don't treat the statement exercise as a bunch of boxes your team just fills out. Try not to rush through it. Encourage introspection and fine-tune the statement as a team.
