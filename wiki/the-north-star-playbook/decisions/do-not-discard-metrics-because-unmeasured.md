# Do Not Discard Metrics Because They Are Currently Unmeasured

**Type:** decision · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps

## Summary

Teams should not reject a promising North Star Metric candidate simply because no current instrumentation captures it. The chapter instructs: identify how you might measure it, even imperfectly, and start there. The value of reducing uncertainty around a genuine strategic signal outweighs the cost of an imperfect proxy.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Troubleshooting: fixing issues and avoiding traps](../chapters/chapter-04-troubleshooting-fixing-issues-and-avoiding-tr.md)** (PDF pp. 34–42, confidence 0.95):

  > Don't disregard the metric. Instead, identify how you might measure it, even if it's imperfect. After all, you need to start somewhere.
