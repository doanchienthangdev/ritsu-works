# Burger King Digital Transactions Per User North Star

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

Burger King's digital team implemented the North Star Framework with 'digital transactions per user' as their North Star Metric, with three inputs: new user activation, registration, and frequency. They organized cross-functional 'squads' around these inputs, with each squad tracing its feature development back to a specific input (e.g., a 'mobile order coupons' initiative to drive the frequency input).
