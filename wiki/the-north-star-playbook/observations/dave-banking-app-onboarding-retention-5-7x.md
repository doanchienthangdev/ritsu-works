# Dave Banking App: Onboarding Change Drives 5.7x Retention Lift

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 6 — Putting your North Star into action

## Summary

Dave, a banking app targeting Americans living paycheck-to-paycheck, discovered in a North Star workshop that retention was higher for users who added more recurring expenses to their account. By reworking the onboarding flow to increase average expenses added, users who added expenses during onboarding became 5.7x more likely to still be using Dave three months later—and revenue increased.
