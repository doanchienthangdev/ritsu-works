# Netflix DVD North Star Metric (2005)

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 1 — About the North Star Framework

## Summary

In 2005, when Netflix was still a DVD-by-mail service, the product team established a North Star Metric of 'Percentage of customers placing three or more DVDs in their queue during their first session.' This metric was chosen because it encapsulated Netflix's differentiation strategy and was expected to improve both customer retention and subscription revenue.
