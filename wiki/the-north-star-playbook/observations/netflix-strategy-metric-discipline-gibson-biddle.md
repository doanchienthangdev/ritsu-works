# Netflix Strategy-Metric Discipline (Gibson Biddle)

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise

## Summary

Gibson Biddle, former VP of Product at Netflix, describes a discipline where every product strategy had a specific metric to validate it. If the metric moved, the strategy was valid; if not, the team moved on. This removed politics and ambiguity from strategy evaluation, illustrating how a North Star–aligned metric culture operates in practice.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — The North Star checklist and statement exercise](../chapters/chapter-02-the-north-star-checklist-and-statement-exerci.md)** (PDF pp. 14–22, confidence 0.95):

  > Each strategy we had at Netflix—from our personalization strategy to our theory that a simpler experience would improve retention—had a very specific metric that helped us to evaluate if the strategy was valid or not. If the strategy moved the metric, we knew we were on the right path. If we failed to move the metric, we moved on to the next idea.
