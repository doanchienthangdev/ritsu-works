# Amplitude NSM Changed to Measure Collaboration

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

Amplitude revised its North Star Metric when it shifted strategy from growing usage among individual analysts to growing usage within teams. The new NSM measured collaboration in the product rather than individual analyst activity. This is cited as an example of how a significant strategic shift should drive a corresponding NSM change, which in turn changes the bets teams prioritize.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star](../chapters/chapter-05-making-the-north-star-framework-stick-and-cha.md)** (PDF pp. 43–47, confidence 0.95):

  > When Amplitude changed our strategy to focus more on growing usage within teams versus building features for individual analysts, we shifted our North Star to measure collaboration in our product.

  > We went back to the drawing board on our North Star when we realized that how people were using the product was changing, when we were going upmarket into the enterprise, and wanted to invest more into a team-focused strategy.

## Ritsu relevance

If Ritsu adds collaborative study features (group quiz, shared knowledge maps) and shifts focus from individual learners to teams or classrooms, this example provides a direct precedent for revising the NSM.
