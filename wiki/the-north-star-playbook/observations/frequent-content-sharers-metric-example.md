**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

The chapter contrasts a vague North Star ('tracks subscribers who share our content') with a well-formed alternative: 'Frequent Content Sharers (FCS) — the number of unique subscribers who share an average of two or more articles per week during the previous 12-week period.' This example demonstrates precision, time-bounding, and behavioral specificity in metric definition.
