# Amplitude's Weekly Learning Users North Star

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star

## Summary

Amplitude's own historical North Star Metric was Weekly Learning Users (WLUs): the count of active Amplitude users who shared a learning consumed by at least two other people in the previous seven days. The threshold of 'two or more' was not derived from statistical proof but from a judgment that it was aspirational and achievable, illustrating the progress-over-perfection principle.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — Get specific: defining your North Star](../chapters/chapter-03-get-specific-defining-your-north-star.md)** (PDF pp. 23–33, confidence 0.95):

  > at one point in time, Amplitude's North Star Metric was Weekly Learning Users (WLUs), defined as the count of active Amplitude users who have shared a learning that is consumed by at least two other people in the previous seven days.

  > Learning consumed by two other people is great; three is even better; one is better than zero. We're more confident about some things and less confident about others.
