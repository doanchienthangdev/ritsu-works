# Amplitude North Star Weekly Reporting Culture

**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 5 — Making the North Star Framework stick and changing your North Star

## Summary

Amplitude itself reports on its North Star Metric weekly within product and leadership teams, shares progress quarterly at company All Hands alongside pipeline and revenue, and has Customer Success Managers report on it in quarterly business reviews as a measure of customer health. The metric is also surfaced in the product's own usage reports so customers can self-serve their team's adoption.
