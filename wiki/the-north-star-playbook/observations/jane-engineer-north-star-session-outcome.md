**Type:** observation · **From:** *The North Star Playbook* (Amplitude, Inc., John Cutler, Ibrahim Bashir, 2024), Ch 7 — Reap the benefits of the North Star Framework

## Summary

A frustrated senior engineer ('Jane') and her cross-functional team (VP product, PM, designer, data scientist) ran a 60-minute North Star session and produced candidate metrics. Months later, though not perfect, the team stopped 'making stuff up' and began learning from shipped features. Previously untouchable 'sacred cows' were challenged and yielded valuable customer insights.
