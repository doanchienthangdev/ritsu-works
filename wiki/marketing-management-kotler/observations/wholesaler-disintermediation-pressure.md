# Wholesalers Face Structural Disintermediation

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 1162–1191)

## Summary

Pressure from digital B2B platforms (Alibaba), demanding customers, direct-buying programs by large retailers. Manufacturers complain wholesalers act as order takers, carry insufficient inventory, lack competitive intel. Successful wholesalers respond by adding services (financing, inventory mgmt, parts-tracking software) beyond logistics. Vertical integration trend compounds.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Wholesaler-distributors have faced mounting pressure in recent years from new sources of competition, including digital platforms such as Alibaba, demanding customers, new technologies, and more direct-buying programs by large industrial, institutional, and retail buyers.
>
> The wholesaling industry remains vulnerable to one of the most enduring trends: fierce resistance to price increases and the winnowing out of suppliers based on cost and quality.
>
