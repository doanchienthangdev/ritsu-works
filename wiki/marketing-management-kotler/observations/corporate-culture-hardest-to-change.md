# Corporate Culture: Hardest Variable to Change, Often Key to Market Success

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 30–31)

## Summary

Structures+policies can change (with difficulty); culture is very hard. Yet often the deciding factor — Southwest's employee-first culture produced nation's largest carrier.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Whereas managers can change structures and policies (though with difficulty), the company's culture is very hard to change. Yet creating a viable corporate culture is often the key to market success.
>
> By creating an inclusive and fun culture where every team member feels responsible for the success of company, Southwest motivates employees to take pride in their work.
>
