# Observation: Inside Sales Is 10-20x Cheaper Per Contact Than Field Sales

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 14 — Personal Selling and Direct Marketing (pp. 591–596)

## Summary

Each contact made by an inside salesperson costs $25 to $30, versus $300 to $500 for a field salesperson including travel expenses. Inside selling is growing faster than field selling. Virtual meeting software (Webex, Skype) and social media tools (LinkedIn) have made effective selling with few or no face-to-face meetings viable. Inside sales frees outside reps to focus on the most profitable major accounts and new prospect development.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Inside selling is less expensive than in-person selling, and it is growing faster. Each contact made by an inside salesperson might cost a company $25 to $30, compared with $300 to $500 for a field staff person with travel expenses. Virtual meeting software such as Webex, communication tools such as Skype, and social media sites such as LinkedIn, Facebook, and Twitter make it easier to sell with few if any face-to-face meetings.
>
