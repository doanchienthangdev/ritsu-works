# CSR Participation Elevates Perceived Product Performance Beyond Direct Quality Signals

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 21 — Socially Responsible Marketing (pp. 317–322)

## Summary

Research shows corporate social responsibility activity increases perceived product performance — consumers attribute higher quality to products from socially responsible companies. Additionally, CSR reduces price sensitivity, increases brand commitment, and improves ability to recruit and retain employees. CSR creates a multi-sided positive externality: the same investment improves brand, product, and talent acquisition simultaneously.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Recent research suggests that the positive effect of corporate social responsibility can extend to perceptions of the performance of its products, such that products of companies engaged in charitable giving are likely to be perceived as having higher levels of performance.
>
> A company's reputation for social responsibility tends to enhance consumer loyalty, increase customer satisfaction, decrease consumer price sensitivity, and increase their brand commitment.
>
> Commitment to socially responsible causes is also likely to increase a company's ability to recruit, motivate, and retain key employees, for many of whom the social responsibility component can be as important as the financial compensation.
>
