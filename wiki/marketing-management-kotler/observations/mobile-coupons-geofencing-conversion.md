# Mobile Coupons + Geofencing: 10x Conversion

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 581–615)

## Summary

Mobile coupons = 10% redemption vs 1% paper. Geofencing targets customers within geographic zone at peak intent. Research: mobile promos drive longer in-store travel + more unplanned purchases. Neiman Marcus, North Face, Kiehl's deploy with loyalty data.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The redemption rate for mobile coupons (10 percent) far exceeds that for paper coupons (1 percent).
>
> Research has shown that mobile promotions can get consumers to travel greater distances within a store and make more unplanned purchases.
>
> Neiman-Marcus is using geofencing in its stores so that its salespeople will know when their more valuable customers are on the premises.
>
