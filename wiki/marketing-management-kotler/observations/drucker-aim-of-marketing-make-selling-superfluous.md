# Drucker: Aim of Marketing Is to Make Selling Superfluous

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 1 — Defining Marketing for the New Realities (pp. 4–4)

## Summary

Peter Drucker's canonical distinction: marketing at its best creates a product so well fitted to the customer that it sells itself — selling signals marketing failure, not success.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The aim of marketing is to make selling superfluous. The aim of marketing is to know and understand the customer so well that the product or service fits him and sells itself. Ideally, marketing should result in a customer who is ready to buy.
>
