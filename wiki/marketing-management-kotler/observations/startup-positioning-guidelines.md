# Positioning a Startup: Focus, Trial, Community, Secondary Associations

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 155–156)

## Summary

(1) Find demonstrable performance advantage; (2) build 1-2 brands on 1-2 key associations; (3) encourage trial by any means; (4) cohesive digital strategy; (5) buzz + brand community via WoM; (6) leverage secondary associations.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> When resources are limited, focus and consistency in marketing programs become critically important. Creativity is also paramount.
>
> Build one or two strong brands based on one or two key associations. Small businesses often must rely on only one or two brands and key associations as points of difference for them.
>
> Create buzz and a loyal brand community. Small businesses often must rely on word of mouth.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Directly applicable. Implications: (1) pick ONE association ('active learning in 30 seconds') + build consistently; (2) viral trial mechanics over paid acquisition — share-after-quiz, embed-in-LMS, study-group sharing; (3) educator/influencer secondary associations — one viral study-influencer endorsement > broad paid ads.
