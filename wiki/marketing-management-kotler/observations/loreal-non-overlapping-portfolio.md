# L'Oreal Non-Overlapping Brand Portfolio Model

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 137–138)

## Summary

L'Oreal: each brand on 'very precise segment which overlaps as little as possible with the others.' 4 divisions (Consumer, Luxe, Professional, Active) each distinct customer profile + price tier. Eliminates internal cannibalization.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Each brand is positioned on a very precise [market] segment, which overlaps as little as possible with the others.
>
> L'Oréal has a richly endowed portfolio of international brands that responds to the diverse needs of consumers the world over.
>

## Ritsu relevance

As Ritsu expands (Ritsu for Educators as separate tier), L'Oreal warns against letting offerings compete for same user. Educator vs Student mode may need distinct onboarding, pricing, brand expression.
