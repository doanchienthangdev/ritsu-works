# Retailer Consolidation Shifts Power Upstream

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 337–333)

## Summary

Top 10 US retailers = 80%+ of average manufacturer's business. Reverses historic mfr-led channel power. Retailers dictate what mfrs make, price, promotion, shipping. Root cause of channel conflict.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Greater retailer consolidation—the 10 largest U.S. retailers account for more than 80 percent of the average manufacturer's business—has led to increased retailer influence that often spawns channel conflict.
>
> In many cases, retailers hold the power. One estimate is that manufacturers offer the nation's supermarkets between 150 and 250 new items each week, of which store buyers reject more than 70 percent.
>
