# JIT Inventory: Cost Efficiency vs Supply-Chain Fragility

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 340–341)

## Summary

JIT reduces warehousing costs + frees cash but assumes uninterrupted distribution. COVID exposed structural fragility. Post-pandemic: securing extra warehouse + moving closer to population.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Despite its obvious cost advantages, the just-in-time inventory management approach has an important drawback in that it assumes uninterrupted distribution logistics.
>
> The limitations of just-in-time inventory management came prominently into focus during the coronavirus pandemic.
>
