# Marketing Plans Are Living Documents — Updated When Any 5 Cs Change

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 46–47)

## Summary

Any shift in customer/competitor/collaborator/company/context triggers plan update. Inattention to change makes formerly successful models obsolete.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Marketing plans are not static; they need updating in order to remain relevant. The same is true of marketing management, an iterative process that executes the company's strategy and tactics while monitoring the outcome.
>
> Companies that do not adapt their business models and market plans to the new market conditions tend to be supplanted by companies with superior business models.
>
