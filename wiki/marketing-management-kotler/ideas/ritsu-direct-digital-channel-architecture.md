# Ritsu Direct-Digital Channel as Structural Advantage

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 323–342)

## Summary

Zero-level digital channel = no intermediaries, no conflict, no dual compensation, no power imbalance, full control over UX. Risk: showrooming-in-reverse — institutional buyers evaluate Ritsu but buy competitors with stronger sales forces. Mitigation: B2C-first PLG → aggregate to institutional demand. Future B2B = selective distribution analog (Coursera/Udemy) not intensive.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Direct distribution arrangement.
>
> Companies designing online channels need to consider these conflicts as they expand into omnichannel marketing.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's direct-digital choice is deliberate structural advantage. Codify in 03-gtm strategy. As Ritsu grows, selective EdTech marketplace partnerships (not intensive across all) protect premium AI-tutor brand.
