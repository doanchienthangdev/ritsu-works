# Reframe Free Users as High-Strategic-Value Targets

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 123–124)

## Summary

MM strategic value framework reframes Ritsu free users: not funnel leakage, but (1) social referrals — students share Ritsu in group chats, study groups, Discord; (2) AI training signal — which activities, which phrasings fail.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Social value reflects the influence of target customers on other potential buyers. Customers might be as attractive to the company for their social networks and ability to impact the opinions of other buyers.
>
> A company might also target customers who are likely to be early adopters of the company's offering, preceding mass market adoption. These 'lead users' allow the company to glean feedback on how it can modify and enhance the offering.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Operations: (1) explicit share mechanics at quiz completion (social value); (2) instrument which activities free users attempt most (information value); (3) use lead-user free cohort for feature co-design before gating. Increases strategic attractiveness without raising CAC.
