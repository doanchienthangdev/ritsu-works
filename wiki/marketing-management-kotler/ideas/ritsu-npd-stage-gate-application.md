# Ritsu Idea: Apply Stage-Gate DFV Triad to Every New Activity Type Launch

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 18 — Developing New Market Offerings (pp. 354–354)

## Summary

Ritsu ships new activity types (crossword, timeline, diagram, etc.) rapidly but without explicit gate discipline. Applying DFV validation before each launch — does this activity type delight students (desirability), can AI generation reliably produce it (feasibility), does it drive retention or upgrade conversion (viability) — would reduce wasted engineering effort and focus roadmap on activity types that improve CLV.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The stage-gate approach encompasses three aims. Its goal is to develop: (1) a desirable offering that target customers will find attractive, (2) a technologically feasible offering that the company will find doable, and (3) a viable offering that will create value for the company and its collaborators.
>
