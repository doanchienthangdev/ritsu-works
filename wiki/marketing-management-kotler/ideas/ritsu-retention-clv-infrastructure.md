# Ritsu Idea: Build CLV Dashboard and Defection-Rate Metric as Primary Growth KPIs

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 19 — Building Customer Loyalty (pp. 420–421)

## Summary

Ritsu should instrument CLV per acquisition cohort and weekly defection rate as its primary retention KPIs, not just DAU/MAU. Given the 5% retention = 25-85% profit improvement finding, a 1pp improvement in Ritsu's 30-day retention is worth quantifying in dollar terms. The 5-A funnel maps directly to Ritsu's onboarding: awareness (signup), appeal (first document upload), ask (first AI interaction), act (first quiz completed), advocate (share/invite). Conversion between each step should be tracked and the weakest gate addressed first.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> a 5 percent reduction in the customer defection rate can increase profits by 25 percent to 85 percent, depending on the industry
>
> By calculating conversion rates—the percentages of customers at the different stages who move to the next stage—managers can identify any bottleneck stage or barrier to building a loyal customer base.
