# Build Full Tactical Persona for Ritsu's Primary Student Segment

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 124–126)

## Summary

Beyond 'college student' label: observable profile (where high-converters discover study tools — YouTube tutorials, Reddit, TikTok); behavioral signals on-platform predicting paid conversion; psychographic values driving WTP.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Tactical targeting links the (typically unobservable) value-based segments to specific observable and actionable characteristics. Such observable characteristics, also referred to as the customer profile, include demographic, geographic, psychographic, and behavioral factors.
>
> Photos, images, names, or short bios help convey how the target customer looks, acts, and feels.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Deliverable: 1-page persona doc per Best Buy GeekSquad model — name, photo, daily workflow, current tools, trigger for needing Ritsu, paying objections. Guides ad creative, landing page, upgrade prompt timing.
