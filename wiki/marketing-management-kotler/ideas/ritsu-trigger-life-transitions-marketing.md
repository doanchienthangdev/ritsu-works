# Idea: Target Life-Transition Moments as Ritsu Acquisition Triggers

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 3 — Analyzing Consumer Markets (pp. 56–57)

## Summary

MM 16e frames critical life transitions - first job, career change, relocation, return to study - as generating entirely new need sets. Marketers who position at these transition moments (the 'first in, first win' principle from the baby market example) capture durable loyalty before habits form. For Ritsu, life transitions like exam season, new course enrolment, career pivot, or framework adoption are high-intent, low-habit-competition windows. These map to the situational workflows already on the homepage (ace your exam in 3 days; learn a new framework fast).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Marketers should consider critical life events or transitions - marriage, childbirth, illness, relocation, divorce, first job, career change, retirement, death of a spouse - as giving rise to new needs.
>
> Recognizing the importance of reaching expectant parents early to win their trust - industry pundits call it a 'first in, first win' opportunity.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Exam-season and course-start campaigns are the EdTech equivalent of the baby-market 'second trimester' targeting window. Growth should calendar these moments explicitly.
