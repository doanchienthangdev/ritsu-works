# Activate Psychological Value in Ritsu CVP Messaging

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 141–143)

## Summary

Ritsu's CVP is 90% functional. 3-domain framework says psychological value (confidence, pride, anxiety relief, identity) is major WTP driver in categories where performance hard to observe before purchase — EXACTLY Ritsu's situation. Emotional AI is product-level psychological feature — needs to be front-and-center in brand positioning.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Psychological value encompasses the psychological benefits and costs associated with the offering. Psychological value extends beyond the functional benefits to create emotional benefits for target customers.
>
> Many marketing experts believe positioning should have both rational and emotional components. In other words, it should appeal to both the head and the heart.
>
> Companies often seek to build on their performance advantages to strike an emotional chord with customers.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Add one emotional hook to every acquisition touchpoint. 'Feel what it's like to actually understand, not just memorize.' / 'Your next exam — but this time you're ready.' / '4 of 5 students who used Ritsu felt more confident going in.' A/B vs functional headline. Hypothesis: emotional variant wins on signup, functional on activation.
