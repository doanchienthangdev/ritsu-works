# Ritsu: Experiential Digital Retail + Omnichannel EdTech

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 352–929)

## Summary

Experiential retail framework → Ritsu's onboarding + activation design. 30s onboarding + interactive activities = Dick's indoor golf range (lets users taste mastery before committing). Price image: avoid competing on price vs free tools — engineer high-perceived-value signal via premium UI + activity richness + 10 emotional personalities. Omnichannel = individual B2C app feeds into classroom B2B2C via educator-created courses.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Companies designing online channels need to consider these conflicts as they expand into omnichannel marketing.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's strategic opportunity: omnichannel integration layer — individual app (B2C) + classroom assignments (B2B2C) via educator-created courses. Analogous to Walmart ship-from-store: existing B2C infrastructure fulfills institutional demand.
