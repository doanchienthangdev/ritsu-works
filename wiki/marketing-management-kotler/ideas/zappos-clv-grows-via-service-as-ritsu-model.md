# Zappos Model: CLV Grows Through Service Quality

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 51–51)

## Summary

Zappos rejected fixed-CLV: excellent service lets CLV grow over time, yielding 75% repeat-buyer rate. EdTech lesson: invest in service depth (onboarding, human support) to expand CLV.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Instead of viewing the lifetime value of a customer as a fixed amount, Zappos's management believes that the value of a customer can grow because of excellent customer service.
>
> Zappos employees do not work from a script and use their own judgment to determine what is needed to make the customer satisfied. Employees are not forced to ask supervisors for permission about delivering the 'WOW' factor.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Collison install protocol (founder onboards first ~30 paying via Zoom) = Zappos 'WOW' for EdTech. Over-invest in first session for CLV growth trajectory, not fixed churn curve.
