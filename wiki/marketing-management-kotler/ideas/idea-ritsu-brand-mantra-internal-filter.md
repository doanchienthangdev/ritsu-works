# Ritsu Should Define a 3-Word Brand Mantra to Filter AI Workforce Output

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 10 — Building Strong Brands (pp. 215–216)

## Summary

Based on the Disney and Nike mantra examples, Ritsu's AI workforce (growth-orchestrator, content-drafter, support-agent) needs an internal brand mantra to produce consistently on-brand content. Candidate: 'active, personal, mastery' — filtering decisions like: does this feature promote active engagement or passive consumption? Does this copy reflect a personal tutor or a generic AI tool? Does this campaign lead toward true mastery or surface-level completion? The mantra would be embedded in the AI system prompts for every content-generating agent.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Brand mantras are powerful devices. By highlighting points of difference, they provide guidance about what products to introduce under the brand, what ad campaigns to run, and where and how to sell the brand.
>
> In effect, they create a mental filter to screen out brand-inappropriate marketing activities or actions of any type that may have a negative effect on customers' impressions.
>
> Unlike brand slogans meant to engage consumers, brand mantras are designed with internal purposes in mind.
>
