# Ritsu Idea: Freemium Plan as Built-In CSR — Education Access as Brand Narrative

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 21 — Socially Responsible Marketing (pp. 471–473)

## Summary

Ritsu's free plan already functions as implicit social marketing — democratizing AI-powered learning for students who cannot afford premium tutoring. This should be explicitly articulated as Ritsu's CSR narrative in brand communication. A cause-aligned framing (e.g., partnering with a non-profit or university to provide free Ritsu access to underserved students) would amplify brand equity, improve perceived product quality, reduce price sensitivity on the paid plan, and aid recruiting. Given that EdTech equity is a mainstream cause, the brand-cause fit is high and authentic.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A successful cause marketing program can improve social welfare, create differentiated brand positioning, build strong consumer bonds, enhance the company's public image, create a reservoir of goodwill, boost internal morale, galvanize employees, drive sales, and increase the firm's market value.
>
> One study showed that 90 percent of U.S. consumers have a more positive image of, are more loyal to, and have more trust in a company that supports a cause, and 54 percent have purchased a product because it was associated with a cause.
>
