# Ritsu Idea: Apply Ansoff to Map Current vs Recommended Growth Mode

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 17 — Driving Growth in Competitive Markets (pp. 374–374)

## Summary

Ritsu is in the market-penetration quadrant (current product, current market — EdTech AI tutor for students) but has underdeveloped market-development options (engineers, researchers, educators, professionals are listed personas but not yet targeted campaigns). The Ansoff lens clarifies that Ritsu should exhaust penetration (more student segments, more subject areas) before committing budget to market development (professionals, enterprise) or product development (new activity types).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)
