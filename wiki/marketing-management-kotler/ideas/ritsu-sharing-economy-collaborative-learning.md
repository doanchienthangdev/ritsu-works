# Sharing-Economy Capability Suggests Ritsu Collaborative Learning Feature

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 1 — Defining Marketing for the New Realities (pp. 10–10)

## Summary

MM 16e notes consumers extract value from what they already own via sharing — someone can be both consumer and producer. For EdTech: social/collaborative learning as feature moat.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Consumers share bikes, cars, clothes, couches, apartments, tools, and skills. As one sharing-related entrepreneur noted, 'We're moving from a world where we're organized around ownership to one organized around access to assets.' In a sharing economy, someone can be both a consumer and a producer.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Educators ICP: teachers upload curriculum + share Ritsu-generated study set with students — educators as content co-producers. B2B2C wedge + reduces content-generation cost.
