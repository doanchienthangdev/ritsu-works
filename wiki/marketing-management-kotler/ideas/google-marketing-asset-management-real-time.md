# Google's Marketing Asset Management: Campaigns Managed Like Portfolio Assets

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 50–50)

## Summary

AdWords framing — advertisers should treat ads as assets to optimize continuously, not plans locked months ahead. Precursor to growth-loop thinking.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Google called this approach 'marketing asset management,' implying that advertisements should be managed like assets in a portfolio depending on market conditions. Companies could use the real-time data that Google collected to adjust their campaigns to market conditions, instead of following marketing plans developed months in advance.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's growth-orchestrator should run SEO + paid as portfolio — high-performing situational workflows (exam-prep, YouTube-to-quiz) get budget reallocated to them real-time, not in quarterly cycles.
