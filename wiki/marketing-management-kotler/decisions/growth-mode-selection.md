# Decision: Which Ansoff Quadrant to Prioritize for Next Growth Phase

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 17 — Driving Growth in Competitive Markets (pp. 374–375)

## Summary

Leadership must periodically decide whether to invest in market penetration (safest, lowest risk), market development (new segments/geographies), product development (new offerings for existing customers), or diversification (highest risk, highest potential). The choice constrains resource allocation, partnership needs, and competitive exposure for the next 12-24 months.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The market-penetration strategy, which involves growing sales of the company's current offerings to its existing customers, is often the easiest to implement.
>
> Growth via a diversification strategy makes sense when good opportunities exist outside the present businesses.
>
