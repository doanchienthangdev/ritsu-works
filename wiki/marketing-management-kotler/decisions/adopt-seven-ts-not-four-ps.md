# Adopt 7-T (Not 4 Ps) for Ritsu's Offering Design

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 38–38)

## Summary

4-P framework conflates product/service, brand/product, incentives/communication — critical distinctions for freemium SaaS EdTech.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> One of its limitations is that it fails to distinguish between the product and service aspects of the offering, which is a key drawback in today's service-oriented business environment.
>
> The 7-T marketing mix represents a more refined version of the 4-P framework, offering a more accurate and actionable approach to designing a company's offering.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Document Ritsu's offering using 7-T canvas — separating AI-tutor service from product (activity library), brand (active-learning identity), and incentives (streak bonuses, referral) from paid promotion. Prevents pricing from degrading brand equity.
