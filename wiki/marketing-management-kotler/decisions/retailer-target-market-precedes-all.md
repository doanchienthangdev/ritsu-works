# Retailer: Target Market Precedes All Other Decisions

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 225–289)

## Summary

Without defined target, no consistent retail decisions possible — assortment, decor, ads, pricing, service. Zales (mass→luxury) lost traditional customers without winning new. Destination categories have greatest influence on where households shop.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Until it defines and profiles the target market, the retailer cannot make consistent decisions about product assortment, store decor, advertising messages and media, price, and service levels.
>
> Mistakes in choosing target markets can be costly. When historically mass market jeweler Zales decided to chase upscale customers, it replaced one-third of its merchandise... The move was a disaster.
>
> Destination categories may play a particularly important role because they have the greatest impact on where households choose to shop.
>

## Ritsu relevance

For Ritsu retail-equivalent (homepage, app onboarding): defining target precedes all product decisions. Currently spread across 6 personas → product design inconsistent. Pick one primary ICP first.
