# Decision: Ritsu's Priority Channel Stack for IMC Phase 1

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 13 — Designing an Integrated Marketing Campaign in the Digital Age (pp. undefined–undefined)

## Summary

Applying the 6 Cs and POE framework to Ritsu's situation: Phase 1 IMC stack should be: (1) SEO/content (owned, high contribution, zero marginal cost) — primary; (2) Publicity/PR outreach (earned, highest credibility) — secondary; (3) In-feed native ads and micro-influencer seeding (paid/earned hybrid) — tertiary when conversion funnel is instrumented. Social media community building is ongoing. Paid SEM activates only after landing page is conversion-validated. TV/display advertising is deferred until post-PMF.
