# Decision: Build Email Lifecycle Engine as Ritsu's Primary Direct Marketing Channel

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 14 — Personal Selling and Direct Marketing (pp. undefined–undefined)

## Summary

Applying RFM segmentation and the 3x conversion advantage of email over social ads: Ritsu's growth lever 1 is activating the existing free user base through RFM-segmented email sequences. Priority segments: (A) Signed up but never completed first quiz — reactivation sequence; (B) Active free users studying a specific subject — conversion sequence timed to their exam; (C) Past paying users who churned — win-back sequence. All email must be timely (triggered by behavioral signals), targeted (subject-specific), and relevant (personalized to their document). Blanket newsletters are lowest priority.
