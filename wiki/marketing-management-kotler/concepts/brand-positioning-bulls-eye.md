# Brand Positioning Bull's-Eye Framework

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 10 — Building Strong Brands (pp. 233–234)

## Summary

A systematic framework for designing brand positioning using four concentric layers. Innermost circle: brand mantra (essence and core promise). Second circle: points of difference (why the brand is better than competitors) and points of parity (what the brand must match to be credible in the category). Third circle: substantiators or reasons-to-believe — specific, factual attributes that support the PODs and POPs. Outermost circle: brand personality/character (intangible associations) and executional properties/visual identity (logo, colors, name). All layers must cohere.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The key to developing a meaningful brand positioning is using a systematic approach to design the different aspects of the brand in a way that is relevant and meaningful to the customers the firm is targeting.
>
> The inner circle of the bull's-eye is the brand mantra defining the essence of the brand and the core brand promise. It guides the actions of company employees and collaborators.
>
> Points of parity and points of difference should be made as specific as possible without being too narrow, and they should be constructed in terms of the benefits a customer can actually derive from the product or service.
>
