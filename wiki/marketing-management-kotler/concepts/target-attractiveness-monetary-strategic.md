# Target Attractiveness: Monetary + Strategic Value Duality

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 123–124)

## Summary

Strategic targeting criterion 2. Customers must CREATE value for company, not just receive. Monetary value (revenues - cost-to-serve) + strategic value (social/network influence, scale/platform, information/feedback). Companies under-index on strategic, missing influencer + early-adopter logic.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Target attractiveness reflects the ability of a market segment to create superior value for the company.
>
> Social value reflects the influence of target customers on other potential buyers. Customers might be as attractive to the company for their social networks and ability to impact the opinions of other buyers as for the revenues they offer to the company.
>
> Information value is the worth of the information that customers provide.
>
> Some highly influential customers who might never generate a dollar for the company directly may exert significant influence on broader and more profitable segments of the market.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Free users have HIGH strategic value: (1) social — students share study sets; teachers recommend; (2) information — feedback signal for AI + which activities drive mastery. Currently under-invests in free-tier experience because misses strategic value framing.
