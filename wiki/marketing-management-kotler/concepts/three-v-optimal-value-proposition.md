# 3-V Market Value Principle

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 36–36)

## Summary

Viability test: business model sustainable only when creates value for target customers, collaborators, AND company. Failure for any one entity makes model unsustainable.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The market value principle is also referred to as the 3-V principle because it underscores the importance of creating value for the three key market entities — target customers, collaborators, and the company itself.
>
> Failure to create superior value for any of the three market entities inevitably leads to an unsustainable business model and dooms the business venture.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu check: Customer value = mastery faster than alternatives; Collaborator value = educators get course-creation tool; Company value = freemium converts >3% + LTV > CAC. All 3 must hold.
