# Product Design as Strategic Differentiator

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 8 — Designing and Managing Products (pp. 163–165)

## Summary

Design is the totality of features affecting how consumers perceive a product's look, feel, and function. It is not merely a phase in creation but a way of thinking that permeates all aspects of the marketing program. Design thinking uses three phases — observation, ideation, implementation — grounded in ethnographic studies. Good design makes a product easy to manufacture, distribute, open, install, use, repair, and dispose of.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Design is the totality of features that affect the way consumers perceive a product's look, feel, and function. Design offers functional and aesthetic benefits and appeals to both our rational and emotional sides.
>
> Design is not just a phase in creating a product, service, or application. It's a way of thinking that penetrates all aspects of the marketing program so all design aspects work together.
>
> In a crowded marketplace, aesthetics is often the only way to make a product stand out.
>
