# Marketing Audit: Comprehensive, Systematic, Unbiased, Periodic

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 46–47)

## Summary

Structured G-STIC-aligned review. Distinct from marketing plan: audit examines past+present to correct forward.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A marketing audit is a comprehensive examination of the marketing aspect of an offering or a company's marketing department. It is intended to identify overlooked opportunities and problems areas and to recommend a plan of action.
>
> The marketing audit consolidates the company's past, present, and future by examining the company's current and past performance to determine the right course.
>
