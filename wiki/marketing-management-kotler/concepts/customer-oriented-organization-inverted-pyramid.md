# Customer-Oriented Organization: Inverted Pyramid Model

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 1 — Defining Marketing for the New Realities (pp. 22–24)

## Summary

High-centricity firms flip the org chart: customers top, frontline next, service managers below, top management last. Contrasts 6 low-vs-high centricity dimensions.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Successful marketing companies transform the traditional organization-hierarchy chart... A company's top priority are customers; next in importance are the frontline people who meet, serve, and satisfy these customers; then come service managers, whose job is to support the frontline people so they can serve customers well; and finally, there is the top management.
>
> A customer-centric company must be market driven rather than product driven, it must aim to cater to individual customer needs rather than mass market needs, and it must strive to make the competition irrelevant rather than merely reacting to competitors' actions.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's 6 ICP personas each deserve persona-specific onboarding paths — market-driven, not one-size-fits-all product-driven.
