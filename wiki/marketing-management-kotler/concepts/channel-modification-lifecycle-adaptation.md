# Channel Modification + Lifecycle Adaptation

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 804–799)

## Summary

No channel strategy effective across whole PLC. Must periodically review + modify as buying patterns shift, competition intensifies, technology creates new channels. Incremental analysis governs add/drop decisions. Early buyers value high-service channels; later buyers prefer lower-cost.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> No channel strategy remains effective over the whole product life cycle. In competitive markets with low entry barriers, the optimal channel structure will inevitably change over time.
>
> Small office copiers were sold first by manufacturers' direct sales forces, later through office equipment dealers, still later through mass merchandisers, and more recently by mail-order firms and internet marketers.
>
> Early buyers might be willing to pay for high-value-added channels, but later buyers will switch to lower cost channels.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Pre-PMF Ritsu = direct web (high-touch onboarding, founder-led Collison install). As Ritsu scales, lower-cost channels (mobile app self-serve, LMS embedded) take over. Plan channel modification in advance.
