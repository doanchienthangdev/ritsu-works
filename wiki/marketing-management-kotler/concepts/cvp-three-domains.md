# Customer Value Proposition: 3-Domain Value Architecture

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 141–143)

## Summary

Functional (performance, reliability, ease, customization) + Psychological (emotional, self-expressive, status) + Monetary (price, fees, cash-back, TCO). Customer value = total benefit minus total cost vs perceived alternatives. CVP = 'whole cluster of benefits promised' — broader than positioning.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Depending on the needs of customers, an offering can create value across three domains: functional, psychological, and monetary.
>
> The value proposition consists of the whole cluster of benefits the company promises to deliver; it is more than the core positioning of the offering.
>
> Customer value is the difference between the prospective customer's evaluation of all the benefits and costs of an offering and her or his evaluation of the costs and benefits of the perceived alternatives.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's current CVP messaging is 90% functional (17 activities, 30s, multi-format). Psychological value — exam anxiety relief, pride of actually understanding, identity as serious learner — almost entirely absent. Significant gap; WTP for EdTech is often psychological.
