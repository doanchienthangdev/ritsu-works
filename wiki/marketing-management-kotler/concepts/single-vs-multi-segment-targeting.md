# Single vs Multi-Segment: Portfolio Logic

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 127–128)

## Summary

Single-segment (concentrated) = deep expertise + lower costs but tech-substitution risk. Multi-segment = portfolio of distinct offerings tailored to each. Critical error: developing offerings on production capacity not segment needs → cannibalization + confusion.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> With single-segment concentration, the firm markets to only one particular segment. Through concentrated marketing, a firm gains deep knowledge of the segment's needs and achieves a strong market presence.
>
> A direct consequence of the decision to target multiple customer segments is the need to develop unique offerings that satisfy the disparate requirements of each segment.
>
> Unless the company is clear on how its individual offerings will address the needs of each segment targeted, the offerings may end up competing for the same customer segment(s) while the needs of other segments are ignored.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu at PMF stage = single-segment focus. 6 ICPs risk multi-segment dilution. MM prescription: pick single segment with highest compatibility + attractiveness → achieve deep PMF → expand. 'Students with exam in 3 days' is best candidate given homepage emphasis.
