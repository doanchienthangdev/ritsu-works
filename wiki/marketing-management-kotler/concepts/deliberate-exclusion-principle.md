# Deliberate Exclusion: Ignoring Some to Serve Others Better

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 122–123)

## Summary

MM's sharpest targeting insight: viable strategy requires deliberately choosing NOT to serve certain customers. Targeting without exclusion criteria is not targeting. Meaningful assessment of who NOT to serve is strategically as important as who to serve.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Targeting is not based solely on identifying customers that the company intends to serve: It must also be based on a meaningful assessment of customers it deliberately chooses not to serve, and without such an assessment, a viable market strategy is impossible.
>
> Companies have failed because of their unwillingness to sacrifice market breadth and focus only on customers for whom their offering could create superior value.
>
> Strategic targeting is based on the deliberate choice to ignore some customers to better serve other customers.
>
