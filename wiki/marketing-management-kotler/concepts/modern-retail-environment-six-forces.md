# Modern Retail Environment: 6 Structural Forces

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 348–349)

## Summary

(1) New retail forms (pop-ups, Amazon brick-and-mortar); (2) Retailer consolidation power asymmetry; (3) Mobile retailing growth (>50% Google searches mobile); (4) Omnichannel as unified system; (5) Fast retailing (Zara, H&M supply-chain speed); (6) Technology remaking ops (cashierless, RFID, electronic shelf labels, AI displays).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The retail marketing environment is dramatically different today from what it was just a decade ago.
>
> In addition to gaining a retail footprint by acquiring Whole Foods, Amazon has launched its own brick-and-mortar stores.
>
> Amazon has opened cashier-less stores that use cameras and artificial intelligence to detect what items customers put in their carts, and charge them accordingly, without their having to go through a check-out process.
>
