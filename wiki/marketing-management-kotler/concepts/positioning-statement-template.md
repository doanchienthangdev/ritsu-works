# Positioning Statement Template

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 153–154)

## Summary

Template: 'For [target] [brand] offers [value proposition].' 3 ways to convey category membership: announcing benefits, comparing to exemplars, product descriptor. Lead with benefits, not attributes — attributes play supporting 'reason to believe' role.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A positioning statement clearly articulates the offering's target customers and the key benefit that will provide customers with a reason to choose the company's offering.
>
> For safety-conscious upscale families (target customers), Volvo offers the safest, most durable automobile in which your family can ride (value proposition).
>
> Many marketers tend to focus on the benefits as the pillars of the offering's positioning.
>
