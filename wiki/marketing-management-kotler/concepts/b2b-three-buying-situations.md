# Three B2B Buying Situations — Straight Rebuy, Modified Rebuy, New Buy

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 4 — Analyzing Business Markets (pp. 81–82)

## Summary

MM 16e presents three structured buying situations that determine the number of decision participants and process length. Straight rebuy: routine reorder from approved list, minimal participants. Modified rebuy: change in specs/price/terms, additional negotiation, possible supplier change. New buy: first-time purchase, maximum risk, most participants, longest process. New-buy purchasing moves through awareness-interest-evaluation-trial-adoption stages; mass media matters at awareness, salespeople at interest, technical sources at evaluation. Critically, 'over time, new-buy situations become straight rebuys and routine purchase behavior' - the journey from new to habitual is a key loyalty lever.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> There are three types of business-buying situations: the straight rebuy, the modified rebuy, and the new buy.
>
> New-buy purchasing is the marketer's greatest opportunity and challenge. The buying process passes through several stages: awareness, interest, evaluation, trial, and adoption.
>
> Over time, new-buy situations become straight rebuys and routine purchase behavior.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's institutional/Educator channel starts as a new buy (high friction, requires demo, approval). Goal is to move institutions to modified rebuy (annual renewal with scope expansion) and eventually straight rebuy (auto-renew SaaS). Product and CS design should accelerate this journey.
