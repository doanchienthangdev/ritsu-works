# Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Customization, Empowerment, Word-of-Mouth, Complaints

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 19 — Building Customer Loyalty (pp. 428–432)

## Summary

CRM is the process of carefully managing detailed information about individual customers across all touch points to maximize loyalty. Four enhancement strategies: customization (personalizing offering and service), customer empowerment (enabling customers to control interaction), managing word-of-mouth (online reviews contribute 10-30% of retailer sales), and proactive complaint management (95% of dissatisfied customers don't complain but just leave; resolving complaints quickly retains up to 95% of complainers).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Customer relationship management (CRM) is the process of carefully managing detailed information about individual customers and all customer touch points to maximize loyalty.
>
> One source estimated that recommendation systems contribute 10 percent to 30 percent of an online retailer's sales.
>
> Studies show that even though customers are dissatisfied with their purchases about 25 percent of the time, only about 5 percent of dissatisfied customers complain... The figure goes up to a staggering 95 percent if the customer feels the complaint was resolved quickly.
>
