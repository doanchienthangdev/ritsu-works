# Personas: Vivifying the Abstract Segment

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 125–126)

## Summary

Detailed hypothetical customer profiles (demographic + psychographic + behavioral + attitudinal). Unilever's 'Katie' for Sunsilk; Best Buy's 5 GeekSquad personas. Warning: don't overgeneralize — multiple personas often needed.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Personas are detailed profiles of one, or perhaps a few, hypothetical target consumers, imagined in terms of demographic, psychographic, geographic, or other descriptive attitudinal or behavioral information.
>
> Although personas provide vivid information to aid marketing decision making, it's important not to overgeneralize. Any target market may have a range of consumers who vary along a number of key dimensions, so marketers often account for these differences by developing multiple personas.
>
> Characterizing the target segment with a representative individual, however, makes it easier to visualize the company's target customers and better understand how likely they are to respond to the company's offering.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's 6 ICPs are demographic labels, not full tactical personas. MM standard: each persona should specify how they search, channels they trust, trial triggers, objections. Currently not codified in Ritsu ICP docs.
