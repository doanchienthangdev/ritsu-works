# Market Follower Typology: Cloner, Imitator, Adapter

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 17 — Driving Growth in Competitive Markets (pp. 376–377)

## Summary

Followers must grow without triggering competitive retaliation. Three archetypes: Cloner (near-identical duplication at lower price), Imitator (copies core but differentiates on packaging/price/location), Adapter (improves the leader's product and often grows into a future challenger). Followership can be highly profitable because it avoids innovation expense.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Although it may not overtake the leader, the follower can achieve high profits because it did not bear any of the innovation expense.
>
> Cloner. The cloner emulates the leader's products, name, and packaging with slight variations.
>
> Adapter. The adapter takes the leader's products and adapts or improves them. The adapter may choose to sell to different markets, but often it grows into a future challenger, as many Japanese firms have done after improving products developed elsewhere.
>
