# Corporate Credibility — Three Pillars: Expertise, Trustworthiness, Likability

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 4 — Analyzing Business Markets (pp. 92–93)

## Summary

MM 16e defines corporate credibility as customers' belief that a firm can design and deliver products satisfying their needs. It rests on three distinct factors: (1) Corporate Expertise - seen as able to make and sell; (2) Corporate Trustworthiness - seen as motivated to be honest, dependable, and sensitive to needs; (3) Corporate Likability - seen as likable, attractive, prestigious, dynamic. A credible firm is 'good at what it does; it keeps its customers' best interests in mind and is enjoyable to work with.' Trust is a prerequisite for B2B relationship health. Online settings require extra trust-building investment.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Corporate credibility is the extent to which customers believe a firm can design and deliver products and services that satisfy their needs and wants.
>
> Corporate expertise reflects the extent to which a company is seen as able to make and sell products or conduct services. Corporate trustworthiness reflects the extent to which a company is seen as motivated to be honest, dependable, and sensitive to customer needs. Corporate likability reflects the extent to which a company is seen as likable, attractive, prestigious, and dynamic.
>
> A firm is more likely to be seen as trustworthy when it provides full, honest information, when employee incentives are aligned with customer needs, when it partners with customers to create market value, and when it offers valid comparisons with competitive products.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's credibility with Educators requires all three pillars: expertise (AI education research citations), trustworthiness (transparent about hallucination risk and data handling), and likability (warm, educator-friendly UI and support). 'Nobody gets fired for buying IBM' applies — Ritsu must earn institutional trust before the B2B channel scales.
