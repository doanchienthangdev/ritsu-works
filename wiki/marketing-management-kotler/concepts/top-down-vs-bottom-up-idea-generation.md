# Top-Down (Market-Driven) vs Bottom-Up (Invention-Driven) Idea Generation

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 18 — Developing New Market Offerings (pp. 404–405)

## Summary

Top-down begins with an identified market need and engineers an offering to address it — the preferred approach because success hinges on delivering value. Bottom-up begins with a technological invention seeking a market application; can succeed but requires finding a matching unmet need. Both are valid; top-down is more reliably connected to commercial success.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Top-down idea generation begins with identifying a market opportunity followed by developing an offering specifically designed to address this opportunity.
>
> Bottom-up idea generation is the opposite of top-down generation: It starts with an invention and then seeks to identify an unmet market need.
>
> Even though successful products can spring from technological invention, top-down innovation is the preferable method of idea generation. The ultimate success of an offering hinges on its ability to deliver value; therefore, a company can enhance its chances of generating a product destined for success by identifying value-creation opportunities in the marketplace at the outset.
>
