# Six Market-Leader Defensive Strategies

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 17 — Driving Growth in Competitive Markets (pp. 383–384)

## Summary

Market leaders defend position through six strategic postures: position defense (occupying the most desirable mental territory), flank defense (protecting weak fronts), preemptive defense (attacking first), counteroffensive defense (meeting attackers frontally), repositioning defense (broadening domain), and contraction defense (strategic withdrawal from weak markets).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A dominant firm can use six main defense strategies. Decisions about which strategy to adopt will depend in part on the company's resources and goals and on its expectations about how competitors will react.
>
> In a repositioning defense, the leader stretches its domain over new territories through market broadening and market diversification. Market broadening shifts the company's focus from the current product to the underlying generic need.
>
> In strategic withdrawal, they give up weaker markets and reassign resources to stronger ones.
>
