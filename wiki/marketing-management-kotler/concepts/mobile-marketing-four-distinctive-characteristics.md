# Mobile Marketing: Four Distinctive Characteristics + Personalization Advantage

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 13 — Designing an Integrated Marketing Campaign in the Digital Age (pp. 686–716)

## Summary

Mobile devices have four characteristics that no other medium shares: (1) uniquely tied to one user (personal identity); (2) virtually always 'on' — carried everywhere; (3) immediate consumption possible — a channel of distribution with a payment system; (4) highly interactive — enables geotracking, image/video capture. These properties make mobile marketing uniquely powerful for timely, location-sensitive, personalized messages. Smart mobile is more than display: apps delivering social value, convenience, incentives, and entertainment drive loyalty better than banner ads.

> Wharton's David Bell notes four distinctive characteristics of a mobile device: (1) It is uniquely tied to one user. (2) It is virtually always on since it is typically carried everywhere. (4) It is highly interactive, given that it allows for geotracking and picture and video taking.
