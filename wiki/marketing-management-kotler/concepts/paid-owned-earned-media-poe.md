# POE Media Framework: Paid / Owned / Earned

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 12 — Managing Marketing Communications (pp. 271–272)

## Summary

Three categories of communication options: (1) Paid media — TV, display ads, paid search, sponsorships; company pays for placement; (2) Owned media — brand's own channels: website, blog, packaging, email list, social accounts; (3) Earned media — unsolicited word-of-mouth, press mentions, viral sharing, social commentary generated by others. An integrated campaign strategically sequences and combines all three. Earned media is highest-trust but hardest to control; owned media is the anchor for scalable, zero-marginal-cost reach.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> We can group communication options into three categories. Paid media include TV, magazine and display ads, paid search, and sponsorships, all of which allow marketers to show their ad or brand for a fee. Owned media are communication channels that marketers actually own, such as a company or brand brochure, product packaging, website, blog, Facebook page, or Twitter account. Earned media are streams in which consumers, the press, or other outsiders voluntarily communicate something about the brand via word of mouth, buzz, or viral marketing methods.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's current owned media (the product itself, viral share loops) is underexploited. Every session end is an earned-media trigger: 'Share your quiz score.' The company has near-zero paid media; POE framework clarifies that owned + earned must be the primary engine pre-PMF.
