# Forecasting Methods — Three Information Bases: Say, Do, Have-Done

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 5 — Conducting Marketing Research (pp. 111–112)

## Summary

MM 16e organizes all demand forecasting methods under three information bases: (1) What people say - buyer intention surveys, sales force opinions, expert opinion (Delphi method); (2) What people do - market tests, in-market experiments; (3) What people have done - time-series analysis, exponential smoothing, statistical demand analysis, econometric models. Advanced ML techniques are now 'revolutionizing marketing by automating and speeding up tasks that range from analyzing sales and revenue to spotting industry trends.' The conjoint analysis technique (how consumers value attribute bundles) is named as a key survey-based technique for B2B demand forecasting.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> All forecasts are built on one of three information bases: what people say, what people do, or what people have done.
>
> Advanced machine learning techniques are revolutionizing marketing by automating and speeding up tasks that range from analyzing sales and revenue to spotting industry trends.
>
> One popular survey-based statistical technique used in market research is conjoint analysis, which helps determine how consumers value different attributes (product features, service benefits, and price) that make up a particular offering.
>
