# VMS + Channel Stewardship

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 334–335)

## Summary

VMS (corporate/administered/contractual) coordinate channel as unified system. Channel steward accomplishes coordination without commands, persuading partners to act in collective interest. VMS now serve 70-80% of US consumer market.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A channel steward might be the maker of the product or service (Procter & Gamble), the maker of a key component (Intel), the supplier or assembler (Arrow Electronics), or the distributor (W.W. Grainger) or retailer (Walmart).
>
> Stewards accomplish channel coordination without issuing commands or directives by persuading channel partners to act in the best interest of all.
>
> Vertical marketing systems have become the dominant mode of distribution in the U.S. consumer marketplace, serving 70 percent to 80 percent of the market.
>

## Ritsu relevance

If Ritsu becomes the 'AI tutor layer' inside multiple LMS platforms, it becomes channel steward of that ecosystem — persuading LMS partners to align on student-outcome metrics without commanding them. New competition is between networked VMS systems, not individual firms.
