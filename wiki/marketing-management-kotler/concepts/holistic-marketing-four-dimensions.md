# Holistic Marketing: Four-Dimension Framework

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 1 — Defining Marketing for the New Realities (pp. 12–16)

## Summary

MM 16e's integrative lens: marketing succeeds only when relationship, integrated, internal, and performance dimensions operate together.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Holistic marketing recognizes and reconciles the scope and complexities of marketing activities and offers an integrated approach to managing strategy and tactics.
>
> The market value concept calls for a holistic approach to marketing that is focused on building relationships, rather than on generating transactions; on integrated marketing that is both automated and creative, rather than on manually managed piecemeal marketing actions; on internal marketing that reflects a strong corporate culture rather than disengaged employees; and on performance-focused marketing that is driven by science rather than intuition.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu must treat learning engagement (relationship), product+comms consistency (integrated), AI workforce alignment on active-learning mission (internal), retention/NPS metrics (performance) as one system — not separate initiatives.
