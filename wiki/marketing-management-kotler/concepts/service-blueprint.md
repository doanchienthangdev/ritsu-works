# Service Blueprint

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 9 — Designing and Managing Services (pp. 187–188)

## Summary

A service blueprint maps the service process, points of customer contact, and evidence of service from the customer's point of view. It separates the front stage (customer-visible actions) from the back stage (invisible support processes) using a line of visibility, and separates internal from customer-facing interactions using a line of internal interaction. Blueprints help identify potential pain points for customers, develop new services, support a zero-defects culture, and devise service recovery strategies.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)
