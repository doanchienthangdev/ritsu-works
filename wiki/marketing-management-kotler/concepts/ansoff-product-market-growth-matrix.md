# Ansoff Product-Market Growth Matrix

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 17 — Driving Growth in Competitive Markets (pp. 374–375)

## Summary

Four-cell strategic framework mapping growth options across current/new products and current/new markets: market penetration, market development, product development, and diversification — in increasing order of risk and resource commitment.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The company first considers whether it could gain more market share with its current products in their current markets using a market-penetration strategy. Next, it considers whether it can find or develop new markets for its current products using a market-development strategy. Then it considers whether it can develop new products for its current markets with a product-development strategy. Later, the firm will also review opportunities to develop new products for new markets through a diversification strategy.
>
> Growth via a diversification strategy makes sense when good opportunities exist outside the present businesses—i.e., the industry is highly attractive, and the company has the right mix of business strengths to succeed.
>
