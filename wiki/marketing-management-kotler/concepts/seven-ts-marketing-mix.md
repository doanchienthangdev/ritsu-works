# 7-T Marketing Mix: MM 16e's Upgrade of 4 Ps

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 36–39)

## Summary

7 Ts: Product, Service (ownership vs access), Brand (outsourceable), Price, Incentives (enhance value), Communication (informs but doesn't enhance), Distribution.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Marketing managers have seven tactics at their disposal to develop an offering that creates market value: product, service, brand, price, incentives, communication, and distribution.
>
> The four Ps can be easily mapped onto the seven attributes of the 7-T framework: The first P (product) comprises product, service, and brand; price remains the second P; the third P (promotion) is expanded to incentives and communication; and distribution replaces the fourth P (place).
>

## Ritsu relevance

For Ritsu: Product = AI activities; Service = continuity + explanations + personalization; Brand = 'active learning beats passive'; Price = freemium; Incentives = streaks + referral; Communication = homepage + social; Distribution = web + mobile app + educator sharing.
