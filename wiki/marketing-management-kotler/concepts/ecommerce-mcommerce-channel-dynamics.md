# E-Commerce + M-Commerce as Distribution Channels

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 323–342)

## Summary

Digital channels = structural element in channel architecture. Multichannel customers outspend single-channel (Nordstrom: 4x). Showrooming = consumers research in-store, buy online = structural disintermediation requiring integrated digital-physical response.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> With the advent of e-commerce (selling online) and m-commerce (selling via mobile devices), customers are buying in ways they never have before.
>
> Nordstrom found that its multichannel customers spend four times as much as those who shop only through one channel.
>
> Showrooming lets consumers physically examine a product and collect information in a store but make their actual purchase from the retailer later online.
>

## Ritsu relevance

For Ritsu, the analogue: students may discover Ritsu via educator (offline-ish channel) but sign up directly via mobile (online channel). Integrated cross-channel attribution + experience continuity matters.
