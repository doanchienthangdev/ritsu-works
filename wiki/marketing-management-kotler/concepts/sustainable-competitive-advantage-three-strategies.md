# 3 Strategies for Sustainable Competitive Advantage

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 151–153)

## Summary

(1) Differentiate on existing attribute (hardest — convergence erodes); (2) Introduce new attribute (TOMS BOGO, Uber cash-free, Nest ML — copyable); (3) Build strong brand (most durable — brand meaning transcends product attributes).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Three core strategies are integral to designing a value proposition that makes an offering stand out from the competition. These are to differentiate on an existing attribute, introduce a new attribute, and build a strong brand.
>
> A valuable source of sustainable competitive advantage is a powerful brand that gives customers a reason to choose the company's offering.
>
> Top-of-mind brand awareness also creates a competitive advantage in that the brand considered first often becomes a reference point for consumers, the default option.
>
