# Aligning Frame + POD + POP: Straddle Positioning

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 148–150)

## Summary

Multi-frame brands either find single robust positioning addressing all frames OR prioritize most important. Straddle positioning: PODs in one frame = POPs in another. BMW achieved POD on performance vs luxury cars + POD on luxury vs performance cars.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Occasionally, a company will be able to straddle two frames of reference with one set of points of difference and points of parity.
>
> One crucial consideration is not to try to be all things to all people; this leads to 'lowest common denominator' positioning.
>
> BMW was able to simultaneously achieve: (1) a point of difference on luxury and a point of parity on performance with respect to U.S. performance cars and (2) a point of difference on performance and a point of parity on luxury with respect to U.S. luxury cars.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu straddle: 'smarter than flashcard apps' (POD: AI-generated, multi-modal; POP: easy to start) + 'more affordable than tutor' (POD: $0 to start, instant; POP: personalized, subject-specific). Doubles addressable audience.
