# PODs: Desirability, Deliverability, Differentiability

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 145–147)

## Summary

PODs = attributes consumers strongly associate with brand + positively evaluate + believe couldn't be found elsewhere. 3 criteria must ALL be met. Brand authenticity is increasingly important POD dimension.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Points of difference (PODs) are attributes or benefits that differentiate the company's offering from the competition. These are attributes or benefits that consumers strongly associate with a brand, that they positively evaluate, and that they believe could not be found to the same extent with a competitive brand.
>
> Three criteria determine whether a brand association can truly function as a point of difference: desirability, deliverability, and differentiability.
>
> Creating strong, favorable, and unique associations is a real challenge.
>

## Ritsu relevance

3-criteria test on Ritsu PODs: '30s AI tutor from any source' — desirable + deliverable + differentiating vs Anki, but not vs NotebookLM. 'Emotional AI 10 personalities' — desirable + deliverable + differentiating (unique). Emotional/personality angle may be Ritsu's most defensible POD.
