# SPIN Selling: Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff Question Framework

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 14 — Personal Selling and Direct Marketing (pp. 263–268)

## Summary

SPIN is a structured approach for managing complex sales conversations: (S) Situation questions — establish context and facts; (P) Problem questions — uncover difficulties, dissatisfactions, and concerns; (I) Implication questions — explore consequences and effects of the problem; (N) Need-payoff questions — ask about the value or usefulness of a proposed solution, getting the buyer to articulate the benefit themselves. The framework shifts selling from feature-pushing to customer-need revelation.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A popular approach for managing the sale process is SPIN, an acronym derived from the types of questions that a salesperson should ask prospects: situation questions, problem questions, implication questions, and need-payoff questions.
>
