# Frame of Reference: Category Membership as Positioning Lever

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 7 — Crafting a Customer Value Proposition and Positioning (pp. 144–145)

## Summary

Positioning starts with competitive frame — product category or set of alternatives. Strategic choice, not given. Brands should choose frames that make advantages most salient. AA as 'fourth emergency service'; US Armed Forces as 'leadership career.'

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A good starting point in defining a competitive frame of reference for brand positioning is category membership—the products or sets of products with which a brand competes and that function as close substitutes.
>
> Firms should choose their competitive frame to evoke more advantageous comparisons.
>
> Consumers determine the value of an offering relative to a reference point used to assess its benefits and costs.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's frame-of-reference choice underdecided. Options: AI chatbots (ChatGPT frame — unfavorable); flashcard apps (Anki frame — favorable); tutors (personal tutor frame — highly favorable, Ritsu wins on cost+availability). Personal-tutor frame is currently underexploited.
