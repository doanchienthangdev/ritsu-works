# Product Differentiation Dimensions

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 8 — Designing and Managing Products (pp. 161–164)

## Summary

Products can be differentiated across nine primary attributes: core functionality, features, performance quality, conformance quality, durability, reliability, form, style, and customization. Well-differentiated products create competitive advantages that are difficult for rivals to copy. Design is treated as an additional, increasingly important tenth differentiator.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Attributes on the basis of which to differentiate include core functionality, features, performance quality, conformance quality, durability, reliability, form, style, and customization.
>
> Well-differentiated products can create significant competitive advantages. Crafting a distinctive aura for a product that helps distance it from competitors can involve moves that range from impressive technological advances like Intuitive Surgical's da Vinci robotic system for minimally invasive surgery to simple tweaks like putting a Chiquita sticker on a banana.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's 17+ activity types (quiz, flashcard, mindmap, etc.) are product features differentiating it from ChatGPT and Anki; each activity type maps to a feature dimension. Core functionality = AI tutoring that generates structured learning from any document — must be the non-negotiable baseline before differentiation via style or customization.
