# Target Compatibility: Company Resources vs Customer Needs Fit

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 6 — Identifying Market Segments and Target Customers (pp. 122–123)

## Summary

Strategic targeting criterion 1. Reflects company's ability to create superior value for segment — outperforming competition. Requires core competencies: source of competitive advantage, wide applicability, hard to imitate.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Target compatibility is a reflection of the company's ability to outdo the competition in fulfilling the needs of target customers—in other words, to create superior customer value.
>
> A core competency has three characteristics: (1) it is a source of competitive advantage and makes a significant contribution to perceived customer benefits, (2) it has applications in a wide variety of markets, and (3) it is difficult for competitors to imitate.
>
> The key to success is to own and nurture the resources and competencies that make up the essence of the business.
>
