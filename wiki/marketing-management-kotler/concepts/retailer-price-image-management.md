# Price Image: Beyond Actual Prices

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 1200–1288)

## Summary

Price image = qualitative consumer perception of price level, distinct from actual price. Drivers: known-value (signpost) items, price range, promotion frequency (HiLo can yield LOWER price image than EDLP at same average), price communication, store attributes, service, policies (price-match signals competitive confidence).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Price image reflects the general perception that consumers have about the level of prices at a given retailer. Price image differs from price, which is quantitatively expressed; price image is qualitative in nature.
>
> HiLo pricing can often elicit a lower price image than EDLP, even when the average price level is the same.
>
> Thoughtful alignment of all factors that affect price image with the retailer's overall strategy and tactics will go far toward building a meaningful price image.
>
