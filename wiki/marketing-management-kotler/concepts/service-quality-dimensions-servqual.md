# SERVQUAL: Five Determinants of Service Quality

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 9 — Designing and Managing Services (pp. 201–202)

## Summary

Research identified five determinants of service quality in descending order of importance: (1) Reliability — ability to perform the promised service dependably and accurately. (2) Responsiveness — willingness to help customers and provide prompt service. (3) Assurance — knowledge, courtesy, and ability of employees to convey trust and confidence. (4) Empathy — caring, individualized attention. (5) Tangibles — appearance of physical facilities, equipment, and communication materials. These five dimensions form the basis of the 21-item SERVQUAL scale. A 'zone of tolerance' exists between minimum acceptable and ideal service.
