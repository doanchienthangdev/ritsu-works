# Private Labels as Retailer Strategic Weapons

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 16 — Managing Retailing (pp. 940–953)

## Summary

Store brands = 1 in 5 US supermarket items; up to 40% in EU/Canada. Retailers sponsor for: (1) higher margins; (2) differentiation; (3) bargaining power vs national brands. 1% shift national→private = $5.5B revenue for supermarket chains. National counter-strategies: fight selectively, partner, innovate, value propositions with symbolic imagery.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A private label (also called a reseller, store, or house brand) is a proprietary brand that retailers and wholesalers develop.
>
> A one-percentage-point shift from national brands to private labels in food and beverages is estimated to add $5.5 billion in revenue for supermarket chains.
>
> Retailers also develop exclusive store brands to differentiate themselves from competitors.
>
