# Market Value Map: Business Model Visual

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 2 — Marketing Planning and Management (pp. 39–40)

## Summary

Single-page canvas: Target Market (5Cs) | Value Proposition (3Vs) | Market Offering (7Ts). Strategy = left 2; Tactics = right. Enables separate customer/collaborator/company value maps.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> The market value map mirrors the structure of the business model and contains three key components that define the company's strategy and tactics — the target market, the value proposition, and the market offering.
>
> The ultimate purpose of the value map is to facilitate the development of a viable business model that can enable the company to achieve market success.
>
