# Personal Selling 6-Stage Process: Prospecting → Pre-approach → Presentation → Persuasion → Closing → Servicing

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 14 — Personal Selling and Direct Marketing (pp. 139–250)

## Summary

The standard B2B/direct selling process has six stages: (1) Prospecting and qualifying — BANT criteria (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline); (2) Pre-approach — researching the prospect's purchasing process and decision structure; (3) Presentation — FABV framework (Features, Advantages, Benefits, Value) with emphasis on benefits and value over product features; (4) Persuasion — handling psychological resistance (inertia, existing brand loyalty, apathy) and logical resistance (price, specs, delivery); (5) Closing — reading buyer signals, offering inducements; (6) Servicing — follow-up, installation, issue resolution, relationship maintenance. Modern selling is relationship-oriented, not transaction-oriented.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Most sales training programs agree on the major steps in any effective sales process. These steps are prospecting, pre-approach, presentation, persuasion, closing, and servicing.
>
> A common way of telling the product story to the buyer is captured by the FABV approach, which focuses on articulating the features, advantages, benefits, and value of the company's offering. Salespeople often spend too much time on product features (a product orientation) and not enough time stressing benefits and value (a customer orientation).
>
> Personal selling is the most effective tool at later stages of the buying process, particularly in influencing buyer preferences, conviction, and action.
>
