# Three-Dimensional Market Position: Market Share, Mind Share, Heart Share

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 17 — Driving Growth in Competitive Markets (pp. 377–377)

## Summary

Market position is properly measured on three dimensions simultaneously: share of market (unit/revenue), share of mind (top-of-mind recall), and share of heart (preferred brand intent). Gains in mind and heart share predict future market-share and profitability gains.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> A company's market position can be defined on three dimensions: Share of market. Market share is measured by the company's sales revenue or the number of company units sold relative to the total revenue or total units sold in a specific market. Share of mind. This is defined by the percentage of customers who regard the company as the first company that comes to mind in a specific industry. Share of heart. The percentage of customers who name the company as the company from which they would prefer to buy a specific product.
>
> Companies that make steady gains in mind share and heart share will inevitably make gains in market share and profitability.
>
