# The 10 Deadly Marketing Sins

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 1 — Defining Marketing for the New Realities (pp. 23–24)

## Summary

MM 16e diagnostic checklist: 10 failure modes (not market-focused, poor customer understanding, weak competitor monitoring, poor stakeholder mgmt, no new opportunities, deficient planning, product bloat, weak brand-building, poor org, underuse of tech) with signs + solutions.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Deadly Sin #1: The company is not sufficiently market focused and customer driven.
>
> Deadly Sin #6: The company's marketing planning process is deficient. Signs: The marketing plan format does not have the right components, there is no way to estimate the financial implications of different strategies, and there is no contingency planning.
>
> Deadly Sin #10: The company has not made maximum use of technology. Signs: There is evidence of minimal use of the internet, the sales automation system is outdated, and there are no market automation, no decision-support models, and no marketing dashboards.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Pre-PMF audit tool: Ritsu should self-check against Sin #1 (segments truly differentiated?), Sin #5 (wedge discovery cadence), Sin #10 (LLM analytics + retention dashboards live?).
