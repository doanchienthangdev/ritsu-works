# Performance Marketing + Triple Bottom Line

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 1 — Defining Marketing for the New Realities (pp. 15–16)

## Summary

4th holistic marketing dimension: track financial returns, brand equity, CLV, societal impact (people-planet-profits). Science-driven, not intuition.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> Performance marketing requires understanding the financial and nonfinancial returns to business and society from marketing activities and programs.
>
> Organizations recognize that much of their market value comes from intangible assets, especially brands, the customer base, employees, distributor and supplier relations, and intellectual capital.
>

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's PMF metric ('100 paying who love') = performance marketing construct (NPS + retention + revenue, not vanity signups). Build composite scorecard now, not at scale.
