# Channel Mgmt: Select/Train/Motivate/Evaluate

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Marketing Management* (Kotler/Keller/Chernev 16e SE), Ch 15 — Designing and Managing Distribution Channels (pp. 329–335)

## Summary

4 pillars: (1) Select members on years, finance, cooperativeness, reputation; (2) Train for capability + loyalty; (3) Motivate via channel power (coercive, reward, legal, expert, referent) + incentives + partnership; (4) Evaluate against quota, inventory, delivery, promotion cooperation.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

> To customers, the channels are the company. Consider the negative impression customers would get of McDonald's, Shell Oil, or Mercedes-Benz if one or more of their outlets or dealers consistently appeared dirty.
>
> A company needs to view its intermediaries the same way it views its end users.
>
> Channel power is the ability to alter channel members' behavior so they take actions they would not have taken otherwise.
>

## Ritsu relevance

For future B2B/educator distribution partners (LMS providers, course marketplaces), Ritsu must apply 4-pillar framework: selection criteria, training/onboarding, motivation via rev share + co-marketing, evaluation against student activation outcomes.
