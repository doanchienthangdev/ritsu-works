# Use Heuristics Before Complex Models

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

The chapter prescribes starting every analysis with heuristics and summary statistics to size the problem levers before investing in complex models or large data sets. Jumping straight to big models without a scoping reconnaissance wastes resources and can obscure rather than illuminate the key drivers. Only after heuristic sizing should teams escalate to the statistical and simulation tools introduced in Chapter 6.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses](../chapters/chapter-05-conduct-analyses.md)** (PDF pp. 141–164, confidence 0.95):
