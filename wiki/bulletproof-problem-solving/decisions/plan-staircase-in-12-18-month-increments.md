# Plan Staircase Steps in 12–18-Month Increments and Revise

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

Rather than committing to a full multi-year roadmap upfront, plan staircase steps in detail only for the next 12–18 months, then take stock and replan as market and capability information arrives. The end goal or its timing should be revised to reflect competitive and market realities. This keeps strategy responsive to learning while maintaining a coherent directional vision.
