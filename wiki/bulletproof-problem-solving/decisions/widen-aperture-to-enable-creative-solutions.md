# Widen the Aperture to Enable Creative Solutions

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 2 — Define the Problem

## Summary

The chapter advises deliberately relaxing problem constraints and broadening scope to make room for breakthrough ideas. Narrowly scoped problems keep conventional blinders on; widening the aperture invites novel framing and diversity of perspective. This is framed as an explicit step: 'Bring creativity into problem definition by widening the aperture, relaxing constraints, and adding diversity to your team.'
