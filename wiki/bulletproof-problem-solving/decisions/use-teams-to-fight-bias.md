# Use Teams to Fight Bias

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 10 — Becoming a Great Problem Solver

## Summary

Teams should be used wherever possible because they bring diversity of thinking and experience that increases creativity and counteracts confirmation bias. Even solo problem solvers should form virtual teams. Specific techniques — team voting to overcome authority bias, red-team/blue-team competitions, and mock trials — are recommended to surface alternative perspectives.
