# Sequence Heuristics and Root-Cause Analysis Before Advanced Tools

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

The chapter prescribes a mandatory sequencing discipline: always start with first-cut heuristics, simple statistics, and root-cause analysis before considering sophisticated tools. Initial order-of-magnitude analysis often reveals direction of causality and size of impact — making the choice of advanced tools more targeted and the interpretation of their results more robust. Skipping this step risks expensive, misdirected analysis.

- **From [Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis](../chapters/chapter-06-big-guns-of-analysis.md)** (PDF pp. 165–208, confidence 0.95):
