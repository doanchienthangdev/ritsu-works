# Synthesize and Tell a Story to Drive Action

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

Problem solving is not complete until analysis is synthesized into a compelling narrative that convinces someone to act. Both synthesis (combining findings into a coherent conclusion) and storytelling (communicating it persuasively) are explicit steps in the cycle. The school levy case illustrates how structured analysis moved from an emotional reaction to a reasoned position that could be clearly articulated.
