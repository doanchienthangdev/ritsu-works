# Match Action Type to Uncertainty Level

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

Before taking strategic action under uncertainty, first classify the uncertainty level (1–5) and then select an action from the corresponding portion of the response spectrum. Lower uncertainty allows bigger, more committed moves; higher uncertainty demands information buying, hedging, or low-cost options. Deferring irreversible commitments is appropriate at Level 2+ in the absence of competitive preemption risk.
