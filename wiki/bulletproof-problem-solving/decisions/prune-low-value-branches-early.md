# Prune Low-Value Branches Early

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

After initial disaggregation, actively remove branches of the logic tree that are either not differentiating between alternatives, are not actionable, or where the answer is already clear enough. Pruning focuses analytical effort on the critical path and avoids wasted work. In the solar panel case, the CO2 branch could have been pruned once the financial case was clear; in the where-to-live case, crime and repetitive variables were pruned.
