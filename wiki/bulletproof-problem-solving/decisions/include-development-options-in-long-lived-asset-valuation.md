# Include Development Options in Long-Lived Asset Valuation

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

When evaluating investments with long time horizons, explicitly model the value of development options — future projects made easier or harder by the decision at hand. These options, particularly when they share infrastructure with existing assets, can represent a substantial fraction of total value (22% in the BHP case) and must not be treated as afterthoughts. Ignoring them systematically undervalues strategic moves.

- **From [Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty](../chapters/chapter-08-problem-solving-with-long-time-frames-and-hig.md)** (PDF pp. 225–264, confidence 0.95):

  > Whenever you are valuing long-lived assets in uncertain situations, think about what future projects are made easier or harder by the moves you make today—sometimes these can have more value than the immediate decision at hand.
