# Test Multiple Cleaving Frames to Find the Most Insightful Cut

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 3 — Problem Disaggregation and Prioritization

## Summary

Expert problem solvers should try two or three alternative disaggregations of any problem, applying different theoretical frames, to discover which yields the most insight. No single frame is universally correct. Anchoring on an initial frame — especially one that worked before — risks misleading the analysis when the problem is genuinely novel. Constructive challenging and 'what you'd have to believe' questions help break attachment.
