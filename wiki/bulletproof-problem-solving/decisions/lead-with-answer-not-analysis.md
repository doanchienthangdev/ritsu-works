# Lead With the Answer, Not the Analysis

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story

## Summary

The default recommendation is to open the pyramid with the governing thought (the 'What should I do?' answer) and then support it, rather than walking the audience through the analytical journey first. This should be varied only when the audience is resistant, in which case the revealed decision-tree approach is appropriate.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story](../chapters/chapter-07-synthesize-results-and-tell-a-great-story.md)** (PDF pp. 209–224, confidence 0.95):

  > our bias in most circumstances is to lead by answering the question 'What Should I Do?' and then summarize the situation and key observations that support action.
