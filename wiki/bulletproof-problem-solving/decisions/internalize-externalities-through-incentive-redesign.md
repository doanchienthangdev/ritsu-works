# Internalize Externalities Through Incentive Redesign

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

When individual incentives diverge from collective welfare, a key problem-solving move is to redesign incentives so that actors bear the true cost or benefit of their actions. For obesity, this means health insurers sharing savings with individuals who reduce BMI; for fisheries, it means quota systems and permit conditions that make conservation economically rational for fishers. This is preferable to relying solely on regulatory prohibitions.
