# Set Ambitious Targets Rather Than Waiting for a Silver Bullet

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

The chapter recommends against declaring a wicked problem unsolvable and waiting for a perfect solution. Instead, set ambitious quantified targets (e.g., 20% reduction in five years) and implement a portfolio of evidence-based interventions now. The MGI call to action explicitly urged decision makers to act on the portfolio rather than wait for an unlikely single solution.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 9 — Wicked Problems](../chapters/chapter-09-wicked-problems.md)** (PDF pp. 265–282, confidence 0.95):

  > the report set out a call to action to urge decision makers to act now on the portfolio of interventions, rather than waiting for an unlikely silver bullet solution.

  > We favor the reverse approach: setting ambitious targets and employing good problem solving approaches at the societal level with multiple intervention paths.
