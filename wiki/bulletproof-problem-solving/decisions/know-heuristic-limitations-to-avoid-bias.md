# Know Heuristic Limitations to Avoid Bias

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

The chapter recommends explicitly understanding the failure modes of each heuristic before applying it. Particular dangers include availability and confirmation biases, incorrectly identified reference classes in analogical reasoning, and applying S-curves or growth rules when the underlying rate has changed. The antidote is testing every assumption behind the heuristic against the specific problem before relying on it.

- **From [Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses](../chapters/chapter-05-conduct-analyses.md)** (PDF pp. 141–164, confidence 0.95):
