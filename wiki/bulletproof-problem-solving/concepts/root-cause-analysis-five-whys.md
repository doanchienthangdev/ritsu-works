# Root Cause Analysis and Five Whys

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

Root cause analysis uses iterative 'why' questioning—the Five Whys technique developed at Toyota—to push through proximate symptoms to the fundamental cause of a problem. It is often visualized as a fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram. While originally developed for production and operations problems, the chapter shows it applies equally to business issues like market-share loss and social problems like homelessness, where the technique uncovers whether the root lies in domestic violence, mental health, addiction, or financial distress.
