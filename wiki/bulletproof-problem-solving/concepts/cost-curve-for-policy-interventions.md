# Cost Curve for Policy Interventions

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

A cost curve organizes a portfolio of interventions in descending order of cost and ascending order of impact, enabling policy makers to identify and prioritize low-cost, high-impact 'small wins' before tackling more divisive and expensive measures. The MGI obesity analysis used this approach — evaluating 74 interventions across 18 groups using cost per disability-affected life year (DALY). The curve is dynamic and updates as information on cost and impact is gathered.
