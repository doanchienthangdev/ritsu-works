# S-Curve Adoption Model

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

The S-curve heuristic describes the typical adoption trajectory of a new product or market: slow early uptake, rapid acceleration through the middle, then saturation. Plotted as percent of full adoption potential over time, the shape varies by reference class and context-specific adoption drivers. The chapter notes that early Internet forecasters overestimated short-term penetration but underestimated long-term adoption—a classic S-curve misread.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses](../chapters/chapter-05-conduct-analyses.md)** (PDF pp. 141–164, confidence 0.95):

  > A useful heuristic if you are involved in estimating the adoption rate for a new innovation is the S-curve, which shows a common pattern of sales with a new product or a new market.
