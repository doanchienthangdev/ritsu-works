# MECE Problem Structure

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive decomposition: logic trees should capture everything relevant (holistic) without overlap between branches. The chapter demonstrates this through multiple cases where problems are cleaved at clean joints (supply vs. demand, financial payback vs. timing vs. carbon footprint) such that branches are non-overlapping and together cover the full problem space.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach](../chapters/chapter-01-learn-the-bulletproof-problem-solving-approac.md)** (PDF pp. 31–60, confidence 0.8):

  > Done correctly, they are holistic in the sense that everything relevant is captured in the tree.

  > In this case, the simplest possible way to cleave the problem is to define airport capacity as supply (of landing slots) less demand.
