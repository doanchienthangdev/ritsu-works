# Knock-Out Analysis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

Knock-out analysis involves estimating the importance of a variable and the influence you can have on it before committing resources. Lines of enquiry that can't materially move the answer are abandoned early. This directly implements the 80/20 rule: focus on the 20% of the problem yielding 80% of the benefit.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes](../chapters/chapter-04-build-a-great-workplan-and-team-processes.md)** (PDF pp. 117–140, confidence 0.95):

  > Knock-out analysis involves making estimates of the importance of a variable and the influence you can have on it, an estimate of expected value, the first cut of which comes out of our prioritization matrix from Chapter 3.

  > Focus your work on the 20% of the problem that yields 80% of the benefit.
