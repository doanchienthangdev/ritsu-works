# Heuristics and Rules of Thumb

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

Heuristics are analytic shortcuts that help problem solvers quickly size the scale and direction of problem levers before committing to complex models. The chapter treats heuristics, rules of thumb, and shortcuts as interchangeable. They are powerful for scoping but dangerous if applied blindly or in settings where rare, outsized events dominate. The chapter presents a toolkit of heuristics applicable across business, social, and personal decisions.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses](../chapters/chapter-05-conduct-analyses.md)** (PDF pp. 141–164, confidence 0.95):

  > Heuristics are powerful tools that act as shortcuts in analysis. They help you size the different elements of the problem to determine the efficient path in further analysis.

  > Start all analytic work with simple summary statistics and heuristics that help you see the size and shape of your problem levers.
