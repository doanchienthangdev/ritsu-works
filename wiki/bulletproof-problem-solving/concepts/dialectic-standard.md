# Dialectic Standard (Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

A team norm where every idea or hypothesis must be met with its antithesis and actively challenged before the learnings are joined in synthesis. This classical argument form is recommended as the default team brainstorming process to prevent confirmation bias from taking hold.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes](../chapters/chapter-04-build-a-great-workplan-and-team-processes.md)** (PDF pp. 117–140, confidence 0.95):

  > Establish the classical argument form as your team norm: thesis, antithesis, synthesis. This means every idea or hypothesis must be met with its antithesis and challenged, before joining the learning together in synthesis.

## Ritsu relevance

The /evolve outside-voice step and /cla Muse panel are direct instantiations of this dialectic — they exist precisely to generate the antithesis before the synthesis is written to Tier 1.
