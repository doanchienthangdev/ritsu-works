# Team Bias Mitigation Practices

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

A suite of team-process techniques to reduce cognitive biases and promote creativity: building diverse teams, always trying multiple logic-tree cleaves, using question marks instead of assertions to keep minds in discovery mode, obligation to dissent, role-playing, dialectic thesis-antithesis-synthesis, perspective taking, constructive confrontation ('what would you have to believe?'), team distributed voting (sticky-note votes), and soliciting outside views beyond conventional experts.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes](../chapters/chapter-04-build-a-great-workplan-and-team-processes.md)** (PDF pp. 117–140, confidence 0.95):

  > Always try multiple trees/cleaves: Even when a problem seems perfectly designed for your favorite framework, try multiple cleaving frames to see what different questions and insights emerge.

  > A core value of McKinsey is the obligation to dissent and it is baked into even the greenest business analysts from induction.
