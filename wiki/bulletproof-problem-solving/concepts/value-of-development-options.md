# Value of Development Options

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

In long-lived asset valuation, future development opportunities enabled (or blocked) by a current decision can represent a material fraction of total value. The chapter illustrates this with infrastructure-sharing: a mining acquisition's attached development options were estimated at 22% of NPV because new expansion could piggyback existing rail and port infrastructure. This concept is the strategic analogue of real options theory and reinforces the importance of preserving future flexibility when making irreversible capital commitments.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty](../chapters/chapter-08-problem-solving-with-long-time-frames-and-hig.md)** (PDF pp. 225–264, confidence 0.95):

  > Whenever you are valuing long-lived assets in uncertain situations, think about what future projects are made easier or harder by the moves you make today—sometimes these can have more value than the immediate decision at hand.
