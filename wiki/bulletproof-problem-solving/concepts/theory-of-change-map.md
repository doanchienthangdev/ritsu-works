# Theory of Change Map

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

For large, multi-year social or environmental problems, a theory of change (TOC) is a strategic map — an evolved logic tree — that visualizes how a portfolio of initiatives connects to a desired long-term outcome. In the Pacific salmon case, the TOC organized 300+ grants across 100 organizations into four high-level strategies. The TOC serves both as a planning instrument and a communication tool for trustees and decision makers.
