# Market-Based Conservation Easement

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

An analogy from land conservation applied to fisheries: instead of outright prohibition, a third party acquires property rights (permits, quotas) and leases them back with usage conditions. This confers limited property rights that can be bought and sold, creating economic incentives aligned with conservation goals. The community quota fund (CQF) structure in Morro Bay extended this into collective community ownership, replacing individual competitive exploitation with cooperative stewardship.
