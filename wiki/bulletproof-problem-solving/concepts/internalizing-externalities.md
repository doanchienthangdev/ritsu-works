# Internalizing Externalities

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

A defining characteristic of wicked problems is the presence of externalities — costs or benefits that fall outside the actors making decisions. The chapter argues that innovative problem-solving can internalize these externalities through mechanisms like market transactions (permit purchases), quotas, and incentive redesign. This transforms the problem structure rather than fighting it with conventional regulatory approaches alone.
