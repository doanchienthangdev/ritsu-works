# SMART Problem Statement Criteria

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 2 — Define the Problem

## Summary

A mnemonic the authors cite — Specific, Measurable, Action-oriented, Relevant, and Timely — as a useful but incomplete checklist for evaluating problem statements. The chapter cautions that SMART covers most but not all required characteristics: it must be supplemented with an outcomes focus and a highest-level framing.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — Define the Problem](../chapters/chapter-02-define-the-problem.md)** (PDF pp. 61–78, confidence 0.95):

  > Teams we worked with sometimes used a mnemonic—SMART—to remember these characteristics: whether the problem statement was specific, measurable, action oriented, relevant, and timely. SMART covers most but not all of these factors—make sure to have an outcomes focus and work at the highest level.
