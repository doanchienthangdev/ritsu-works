# Problem-Solving Organization

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century

## Summary

An organisational archetype that prioritises working on the right problems, addressing root causes, using short-duration work plans, and assigning clear accountability. The authors argue that organizational capability has evolved across three distinct eras: strategy (1970s–80s), execution (1990s–2000s), and complex problem solving (current era). Problem-solving organisations replace a purely execution-focused culture with continuous structured inquiry.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century](../chapters/chapter-00-introduction-problem-solving-for-the-challeng.md)** (PDF pp. 15–30, confidence 0.95):

  > organizations seek to become clever and agile to address this new world, they take on the persona of problem solving ­organizations—a drive to be working on the right problems, addressing root causes, engaging teams around short duration work plans, and allocating responsibilities and timelines with accountability.
