# Synthesis of Findings

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story

## Summary

Synthesis is the step that draws together individual branch-level analyses from the logic tree into a unified picture. It frequently yields emergent insights not visible at the branch level. The authors recommend team synthesis sessions where each member presents their analyses, enabling cross-branch insight and pressure-testing of alternative hypotheses. Visual representation of findings mapped back onto the tree structure is the recommended method.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story](../chapters/chapter-07-synthesize-results-and-tell-a-great-story.md)** (PDF pp. 209–224, confidence 0.95):

  > As you move to final synthesis, you draw together the individual findings of the work on each branch and twig of your logic tree into an overall picture.

  > This is often the stage where a big insight emerges that wasn't obvious in the branch-level analysis.

  > The quality of your synthesis will determine the quality of your decision making.
