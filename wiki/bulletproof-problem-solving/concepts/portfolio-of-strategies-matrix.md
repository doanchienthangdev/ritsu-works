# Portfolio of Strategies Matrix

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

A visualization tool for complex, long-term strategy portfolios that maps initiatives across two axes: stage of investment (Seed → Cultivation → Harvest) and level of aspiration (incremental vs. transformational). The matrix helps decision makers spot imbalance — e.g., an over-concentration of high-risk bets or an absence of transformational initiatives — and communicate the aggregate risk-return profile to governance bodies.

- **From [Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty](../chapters/chapter-08-problem-solving-with-long-time-frames-and-hig.md)** (PDF pp. 225–264, confidence 0.95):

  > One of the ways the team managed and communicated about the large portfolio of strategies and investments was to visualize them in a matrix that captures both the stage of investment and the level of aspiration, expressed as incremental or transformational change
