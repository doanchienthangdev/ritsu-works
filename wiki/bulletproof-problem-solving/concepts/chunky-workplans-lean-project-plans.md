# Chunky Workplans and Lean Project Plans

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

Short, highly specific workplans covering two to three weeks of the most important initial analyses, constantly revised as new insights arrive. These are paired with leaner project plans in Gantt-chart format that cover fixed milestone dates. Encyclopedic workplans are rejected because they become outmoded as soon as early analyses point to new avenues.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes](../chapters/chapter-04-build-a-great-workplan-and-team-processes.md)** (PDF pp. 117–140, confidence 0.95):

  > Our approach is to do short, but highly specific, workplans that focus on the most important initial analyses, perhaps stretching out two to three weeks, and constantly revise them as new insights come from the team's work.

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's sprint cadence for /cla capabilities mirrors this: short-horizon sprints with explicit deliverables per sprint, not a 12-week waterfall plan.
