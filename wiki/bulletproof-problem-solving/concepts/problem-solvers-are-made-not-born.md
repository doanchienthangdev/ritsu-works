# Problem Solvers Are Made Not Born

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 10 — Becoming a Great Problem Solver

## Summary

The authors' central conviction is that great problem solving is a learnable, practiceable skill — not an innate talent. With systematic investment in the seven-step process, almost any personal, business, or societal problem becomes tractable. They describe effective problem solving as feeling like a superpower once mastered.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 10 — Becoming a Great Problem Solver](../chapters/chapter-10-becoming-a-great-problem-solver.md)** (PDF pp. 283–288, confidence 0.95):
