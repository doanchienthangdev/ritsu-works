# Pre-Mortem Analysis and Downside Scenario Modeling

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

Pre-mortem analysis requires the team to work through all implications of even unlikely failure cases before committing to a solution. Complementing this is explicit downside scenario modeling that accounts for long-tailed distributions and black-swan events rather than assuming normal distributions. Both practices are recommended to counter overoptimism bias.
