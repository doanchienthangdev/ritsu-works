# Revealed Decision-Tree Storyline

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story

## Summary

An alternative to the top-down pyramid when the audience is resistant or the conclusion is uncomfortable. Instead of stating the governing thought first, the presenter reveals the answer step-by-step through a branching yes/no decision tree, providing evidence at each branch to gradually lead the audience toward the conclusion. The authors illustrate this with the Oilco refinery case where local management resented the foreign owner's recommendations.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story](../chapters/chapter-07-synthesize-results-and-tell-a-great-story.md)** (PDF pp. 209–224, confidence 0.95):

  > In circumstances like this, it can make sense to use a revealed approach to your arguments, employing a decision tree structure rather than the traditional pyramid.

  > With a decision tree final storyline structure, you provide evidence for each yes/no branch in your tree, slowly working the decision maker toward your conclusion. You reveal the answer rather than leading with it.
