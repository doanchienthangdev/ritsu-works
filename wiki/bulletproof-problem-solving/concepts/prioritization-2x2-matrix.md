# Prioritization 2x2 Matrix (Impact vs. Ability to Affect)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 3 — Problem Disaggregation and Prioritization

## Summary

After building a MECE logic tree, teams use a 2x2 matrix to prune branches: one axis measures the lever's potential impact on the overall problem; the other measures whether the team can actually affect it. Branches that are high-impact but immovable (e.g., climate conditions affecting salmon) are pruned, as are low-impact branches. Only high-impact, actionable levers survive into the workplan.
