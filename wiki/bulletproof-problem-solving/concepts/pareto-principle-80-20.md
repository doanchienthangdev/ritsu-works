# Pareto Principle (80:20 Rule)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

The 80:20 rule, named after economist Vilfredo Pareto, describes the common phenomenon that 80% of outcomes derive from 20% of causes. Its purpose in analysis is to focus effort on the most important factors rather than treating all inputs equally. The chapter illustrates it in healthcare (20% of the population accounts for 80% of costs) and in energy-fishery trade-offs in Myanmar, where 95% of potential hydropower was achievable with only a 20% reduction in fishery support.
