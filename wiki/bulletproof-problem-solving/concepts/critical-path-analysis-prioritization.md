# Critical Path Analysis and Prioritization

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

The critical path is the continuation of the prioritization process from logic-tree building. It means high-grading the analysis so you are always working on the parts with the highest probability of yielding insight. Workplanning and frequent iterations keep you on the critical path by ordering analyses so knock-out analyses come first.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes](../chapters/chapter-04-build-a-great-workplan-and-team-processes.md)** (PDF pp. 117–140, confidence 0.95):

  > The critical path is the continuation of the prioritization process we described toward the end of Chapter 3. It means high-grading the analysis you use to solve problems so that you are always working on the parts that have the highest probability of yielding insight.

  > To be on the critical path in problem solving, make sure you get the order of your analyses right and do the knock-out analyses first.
