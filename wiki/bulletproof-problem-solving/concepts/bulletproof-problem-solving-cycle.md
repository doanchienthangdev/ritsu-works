# Bulletproof Problem Solving Cycle

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

A seven-step iterative process for solving nearly every type of problem, from simple linear ones to those with complex interdependencies. The cycle involves defining the problem, disaggregating it, prioritizing analyses, work planning, analysis, synthesis, and storytelling. It is iterative rather than linear — at each stage understanding improves and early answers are refined. The process can be completed over any timeframe and repeated to draw out deeper insight.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach](../chapters/chapter-01-learn-the-bulletproof-problem-solving-approac.md)** (PDF pp. 31–60, confidence 0.95):

  > It sets out a simple but rigorous approach to defining problems, disaggregating them into manageable pieces, focusing good analytic tools on the most important parts, and then synthesizing findings to tell a powerful story.

  > The bulletproof problem solving process is both a complete process and an iterative cycle. This cycle can be completed over any timeframe with the information at hand.
