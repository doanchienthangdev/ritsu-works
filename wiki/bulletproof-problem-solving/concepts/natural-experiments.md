# Natural Experiments (Quasi-Experiments)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

When constructed experiments are infeasible due to cost, ethics, or time, natural experiments exploit existing real-world discontinuities — policy cutoffs, geographic accidents, arbitrary institutional rules — that mimic the treatment/control structure of a proper experiment. The method requires careful attention to potential confounders because randomization is not guaranteed. It provides high-quality causal inference in settings that are otherwise impossible to study experimentally.
