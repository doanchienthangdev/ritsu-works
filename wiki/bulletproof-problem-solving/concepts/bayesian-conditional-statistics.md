# Bayesian / Conditional Statistics

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Bayesian statistics are described as the right tool when data is incomplete and when the probability of an outcome is conditional on another probabilistic condition. The approach combines a prior probability with new evidence to produce a posterior probability. The chapter argues a 'Bayesian thinking' mindset — always asking about conditional probabilities — is valuable even when formal Bayesian calculations are not performed.

- **From [Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis](../chapters/chapter-06-big-guns-of-analysis.md)** (PDF pp. 165–208, confidence 0.95):
