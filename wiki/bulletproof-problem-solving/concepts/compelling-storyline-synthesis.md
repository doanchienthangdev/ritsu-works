# Compelling Storyline Synthesis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century

## Summary

The sixth common pitfall the chapter identifies is failing to link conclusions into an actionable narrative. Analytically oriented teams often declare 'done' when analysis is complete, without synthesising findings into a story that resonates with diverse audiences. The chapter illustrates with the ecosystem-services example: technical language about bees and trees fails to move decision-makers, whereas framing the same findings around human respiratory health compels action. Synthesis means finding the 'hook' that connects the analysis back to what audiences care about.
