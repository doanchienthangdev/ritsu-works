# Uncertainty Response Toolkit

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

A spectrum of six strategic actions calibrated to uncertainty level: (1) buying information/time, (2) hedges — cost-effective moves countering downside, (3) low-cost strategic options — betting on multiple horses, (4) buying insurance against catastrophic outcomes, (5) no-regrets moves that build capabilities regardless of outcome, and (6) big bets taken when you have a conviction not shared by others. Actions are matched to the level of uncertainty faced.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty](../chapters/chapter-08-problem-solving-with-long-time-frames-and-hig.md)** (PDF pp. 225–264, confidence 0.95):

  > The spectrum of actions you can take when faced with level 2 to level 4 uncertainty range from buying time (which you can also call do nothing, wait for more information), all the way through to taking big bets and no regrets moves

  > No regrets moves come into play when you are comfortable with the level of uncertainty, or can edge out into a competitive space with incremental learning moves; this often involves the ­capability building you'll need no matter what the outcome.

## Ritsu relevance

The toolkit maps directly to Ritsu's tactical decisions: no-regrets moves include building the activation funnel and content engine regardless of which channel wins; hedges include testing multiple acquisition channels before committing ad spend.
