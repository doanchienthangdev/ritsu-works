# Crowdsourcing Algorithms and Competitions

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Crowdsourcing platforms like Kaggle allow organisations to outsource hard analytical problems to a diverse global pool of solvers competing for prizes. The 'wisdom of the crowd' (Philip Tetlock's Good Judgment Project) shows that aggregating many independent forecasts cancels individual biases and outperforms single expert judgment. The decision to crowdsource versus hire experts depends on problem clarity, achievability, available resources, and risk tolerance.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis](../chapters/chapter-06-big-guns-of-analysis.md)** (PDF pp. 165–208, confidence 0.95):

  > Enter crowdsourcing as a way to pair enterprises searching for solutions with teams of individuals that have ideas for implementation.

  > If enough people weigh in on a particular forecast, they'll cancel out any individual contributor's biases and come to more accurate conclusions than a single forecaster would.
