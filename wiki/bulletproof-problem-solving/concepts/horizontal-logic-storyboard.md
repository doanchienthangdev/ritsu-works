# Horizontal Logic and Storyboarding

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story

## Summary

After building the pyramid structure, the authors recommend storyboarding the full presentation by dividing a page into nine boxes and sketching each exhibit with its storyline lead across the top. Reading across the top lines of all slides ('horizontal logic') checks that the argument flows and is coherent as a standalone narrative without requiring the reader to descend into the supporting detail.

- **From [Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story](../chapters/chapter-07-synthesize-results-and-tell-a-great-story.md)** (PDF pp. 209–224, confidence 0.95):
