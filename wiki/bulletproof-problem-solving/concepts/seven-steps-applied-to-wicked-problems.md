# Seven-Steps Framework Applied to Wicked Problems

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

The chapter argues that the book's seven-step problem-solving process — problem definition, cleaving, analysis, and synthesis — is applicable even to the most complex wicked problems. Wicked problems are harder but not categorically different; the same systematic approach can generate insights and prioritized portfolios of interventions.
