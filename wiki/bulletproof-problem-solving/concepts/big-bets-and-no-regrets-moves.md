# Big Bets and No-Regrets Moves

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

Two poles of the uncertainty response spectrum. No-regrets moves are incremental, capability-building actions that pay off regardless of how uncertainty resolves. Big bets are large, concentrated commitments taken when an actor has a conviction about an outcome not shared by the market. Big bets require strong cash generation and low debt and are only available to a few organizations.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty](../chapters/chapter-08-problem-solving-with-long-time-frames-and-hig.md)** (PDF pp. 225–264, confidence 0.95):

  > Big bets are taken when you have a level of confidence about an outcome not shared by others.

  > Big bets are open only to a few, as Hugh Courtney pointed out—those with business models that generate a lot of cash and have low debt.
