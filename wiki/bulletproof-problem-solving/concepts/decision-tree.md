# Decision Tree

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 3 — Problem Disaggregation and Prioritization

## Summary

A decision tree is a logic tree that guides action by sequencing threshold questions with clearly ordered hierarchy, leading to specific remedies or choices at leaf nodes. The chapter derives a decision tree for contested historical monuments by starting inductively (surveying specific cases) and then organizing questions by priority. Decision trees are suited for problems that need consistent, principled action at scale.
