# Heuristics Before Big Guns

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 10 — Becoming a Great Problem Solver

## Summary

The authors recommend always starting analysis with summary statistics, heuristics, and rules of thumb before reaching for sophisticated tools like machine learning or Monte Carlo simulations. This exploratory phase reveals data quality, key magnitudes and directions, and often proves sufficient to close many problems without heavier analytics.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 10 — Becoming a Great Problem Solver](../chapters/chapter-10-becoming-a-great-problem-solver.md)** (PDF pp. 283–288, confidence 0.95):

  > Start your analysis with summary statistics, heuristics, and rules of thumb to get a feel for the data and the solution space.

  > one-day answers, supported by good logic and simple heuristics, are often sufficient to close the book on many problems, allowing you to move on to the more difficult ones.
