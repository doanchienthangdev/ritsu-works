# One-Day Answers

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

A one-day answer is a concise, three-part synthesis of current best understanding: (1) the situation at the outset, (2) observations or complications creating the tension, and (3) the best current resolution or implication. Updated iteratively as analysis results come in, it acts as a strawman for pressure-testing and guides where to direct remaining analytical effort.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes](../chapters/chapter-04-build-a-great-workplan-and-team-processes.md)** (PDF pp. 117–140, confidence 0.95):

  > We call this a one-day answer, and they convey our current best analysis of the situation, complications or insightful observations, and our best guess at the solution, as we iterate between our evolving workplans and our analysis.

  > One-day answers clarify where you are and what work is left to do—and provide a strawman for pressure-testing your work.
