# Break-Even Analysis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses

## Summary

Break-even analysis calculates the sales level at which revenue covers all cash costs, expressed as fixed costs divided by (unit price minus unit variable cost). The chapter emphasizes that start-ups often know their cash runway but not their break-even point, and that the tricky element is how fixed costs behave as volume scales—particularly step-fixed costs where capacity must be added in lumps. Understanding break-even and cost behavior illuminates pricing, scaling, and competitive dynamics.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 5 — Conduct Analyses](../chapters/chapter-05-conduct-analyses.md)** (PDF pp. 141–164, confidence 0.95):

  > Not enough of them really know their break-even point, the level of sales where revenue covers cash costs.

  > The break-even point in sales dollars or units equals fixed costs/unit price less unit variable costs.
