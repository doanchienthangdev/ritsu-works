# Wicked Problems

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

Wicked problems are a class of problems that defy easy definition and solution because they are embedded in complex systems. They typically involve multiple causes, major values disagreements among stakeholders, unintended consequences, and require substantial behavior change. Classic examples include terrorism, environmental degradation, obesity, and poverty.
