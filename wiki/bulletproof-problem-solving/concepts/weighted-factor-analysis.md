# Weighted Factor Analysis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

A decision-making approach where the elements of a choice problem are broken into measurable indicator variables, each assigned a relative weight reflecting its importance. All data is normalized to a common scale, weights are applied, and the results are summed to rank alternatives objectively. It transforms subjective multi-criteria decisions into a structured, transparent, and auditable comparison.
