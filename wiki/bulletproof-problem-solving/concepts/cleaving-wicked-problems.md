# Cleaving Wicked Problems

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

Cleaving is the technique of disaggregating a complex problem along a chosen dimension — demand/supply, incidence/severity, behavioral/clinical, financial/non-financial — to reveal actionable intervention paths. For obesity, MGI chose demand-and-supply as the cleaving axis, producing a cost curve similar to carbon-abatement analysis. Choosing the right cleaving axis can surface entirely different solution sets.
