# Design Thinking Integration with Seven Steps

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 2 — Define the Problem

## Summary

The chapter positions design thinking (empathize, define, ideate, build, test, deliver) as complementary to the seven-step analytic process, especially when user experience and creative solutions are critical. The 'empathize' stage maps to enhanced problem definition, 'ideate' maps to hypothesis generation, and 'build/deliver' maps to work planning. The integration is recommended particularly for consumer/product problems.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 2 — Define the Problem](../chapters/chapter-02-define-the-problem.md)** (PDF pp. 61–78, confidence 0.9):

  > We believe it is both consistent and complementary, especially in cases where an understanding of the user experience is essential.

  > A user-centered approach is fundamental to this kind of problem solving, where a significant amount of time is spent empathizing with and understanding the user and their 'pain points.'
