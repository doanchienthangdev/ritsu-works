# Hypothesis-Driven Approach

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

Starting analysis by stating an explicit hypothesis to test rather than beginning with an open-ended investigation. The hypothesis is not a conclusion — it surfaces the arguments that would either disprove or support it. Criteria for supporting the hypothesis are defined up front, making the problem boundaries clear and the analysis more accurate and speedy.
