**Type:** idea · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 2 — Define the Problem

## Summary

The chapter's framework suggests that Ritsu's 'north star' metric — 100 paying who love — should be tested against problem-statement quality criteria: Is it outcome-focused? Time-bound? Addressing decision-maker values? Does it allow sufficient scope for creative solutions?
