# Ritsu Career Decision Tool as Product Feature

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

The chapter's career-choice matrix (economic outlook × personal ability × interest × risk tolerance, mapped to big-bet / no-regrets / hedge strategies) is a structured decision framework that Ritsu could operationalize as a product activity type. Students uploading career exploration documents or job market research could receive an AI-tutor that applies exactly this matrix, surfacing personalized strategy recommendations — a high-value use case that maps to the 'self-learners' and 'professionals' user personas.
