# Ritsu as the Missing Common Framework for Problem-Solving Pedagogy

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century

## Summary

The chapter documents that despite growing awareness of the problem-solving skill gap in education, 'we have not seen a common framework or process emerge yet.' Ritsu — which turns any document into structured active-learning activities — could be positioned as the delivery vehicle for exactly this kind of structured framework: a student uploads a problem-solving textbook or case study, and Ritsu generates exercises that practise disaggregation, hypothesis testing, and synthesis rather than passive re-reading. This is a content-type and ICP-extension idea worth exploring.
