# Obligation to Dissent as a Norm in AI Workforce Multi-Role Sessions

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

Ritsu's multi-agent /cla sessions (GPS + CTO + CPO + Muse panel) could formally encode an obligation-to-dissent norm: any agent producing a synthesis step must also be required to generate the strongest possible antithesis before the founder approves. This guards against the AI workforce's own version of confirmation bias when proposing capability builds.
