# Use One-Day Answer Structure for Ritsu's PMF State

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

Ritsu's weekly founder review could adopt the situation-complication-resolution format as a standing template: the situation (current 100-paying-who-love progress), complication (what friction or data surprised us this week), resolution (what we're doing differently next sprint). This creates a living strawman that can be attacked and updated each Monday rather than a status dashboard.
