# Climate Change Economic Impact Simulation — Hsiang et al.

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Hsiang, Kopp et al. combined 44 separate climate models with economic models to simulate county-level economic impacts of climate change in the US. The simulation mapped outcomes across labour, violent crime, mortality, electricity consumption, and CO2 at the county level. The finding was that negative impacts are likely to be disproportionately felt by counties that are already poor — demonstrating both the power of large-scale simulation and the use of sensitivity analysis to explore parameter ranges.
