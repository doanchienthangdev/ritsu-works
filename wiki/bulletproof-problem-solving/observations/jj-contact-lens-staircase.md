# J&J Global Contact Lens Business Built via Staircase

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

Johnson & Johnson built a global contact lens business in a little over a decade through a series of initially small steps followed by larger, more expansive moves. This case is cited as an archetypal staircase strategy — capabilities and market positions were accumulated sequentially, with each step enabling the next. The case demonstrates that large-scale new business creation under uncertainty is achievable through disciplined capability sequencing.
