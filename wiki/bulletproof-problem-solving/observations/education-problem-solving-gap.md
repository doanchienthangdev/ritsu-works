# Education System's Problem-Solving Gap

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century

## Summary

The chapter cites OECD Director Andreas Schleicher and the PISA assessments to argue that schools and universities are not adequately developing problem-solving skills. The OECD's PISA began testing individual problem-solving skills in 2012 and collaborative problem-solving in 2015. A Wall Street Journal report on the CLA+ test found that some well-respected colleges showed little improvement in critical thinking between freshman and senior years. The conclusion: problem-solving pedagogy is in early stages with no common framework or process yet.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century](../chapters/chapter-00-introduction-problem-solving-for-the-challeng.md)** (PDF pp. 15–30, confidence 0.95):

  > we have not seen a common framework or process emerge yet.

## Ritsu relevance

This is the core problem Ritsu exists to solve at the individual level: bridging the gap between knowing content and being able to apply it. The 'do with what they know' framing maps exactly to Ritsu's active-learning philosophy (quizzing, explaining, applying = mastery, not passive re-reading).
