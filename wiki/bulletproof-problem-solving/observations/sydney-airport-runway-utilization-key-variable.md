# Sydney Airport: Runway Utilization as the Actionable Variable

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

In the Sydney Airport capacity case used as a McKinsey recruiting exercise, the most productive analytical path was to focus on runway utilization — specifically hours of operation, aircraft spacing, and people per plane — because it is one of the few variables actively manageable by transportation planners. Other variables like number of runways and runway capacity are fixed in the short term. Sydney later built a third runway and managed traffic growth through these key variables.
