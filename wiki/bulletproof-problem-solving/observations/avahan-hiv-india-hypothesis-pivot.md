# Avahan HIV India Project: Hypothesis Pivot to Female Sex Workers

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

In the Avahan HIV project in India, the team's initial hypothesis was directionally useful but provided no leverage for action. Rather than persisting, they took a new tack based on what they had learned, focusing on female sex workers and the communities they lived in — a dimension previously unexplored as part of a disease problem — and this ultimately yielded insight and a course of action of profound impact.
