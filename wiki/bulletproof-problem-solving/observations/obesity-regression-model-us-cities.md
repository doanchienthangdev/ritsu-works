# Obesity Regression Model — 68 US Cities

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

A research assistant ran multivariate regression on 68 US cities using data on obesity prevalence, education, household income, walkability, and a climate comfort score. Income alone explained 71% of the variance in obesity between cities. The full four-variable model (income, education, comfort, walkability plus an income/education interaction term) explained 82% of variance. Crucially, walkability showed no significant correlation with obesity when tested alone but became significant after controlling for climate comfort.
