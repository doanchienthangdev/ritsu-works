# McKinsey Global Institute Obesity Analysis

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

The MGI 2014 report estimated obesity's global social burden at $2 trillion, similar to smoking, armed violence, and terrorism. It modeled 74 interventions across 18 groups for the UK, selecting 44 as highly cost-effective, at an implementation cost of $40 billion, targeting a 20% reduction in overweight/obese individuals within five years. The analysis demonstrated how systematic problem-solving methods — scope definition, disaggregation, cost-curve synthesis — can be applied to wicked societal problems.
