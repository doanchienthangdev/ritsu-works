# California Groundfish Fishery Reform (TNC / Chuck Cook)

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 9 — Wicked Problems

## Summary

The US West Coast Groundfish fishery collapsed from $110M catch in 1987 to $35M in 2003. The Nature Conservancy (TNC) intervened from 2004 by purchasing over 50% of trawl permits for $7M, leasing them back with conservation restrictions, and establishing a marine protected zone of 3.8 million acres. A subsequent catch-share and individual transferable quota system was introduced in 2011. Morro Bay's catch value recovered from $2M (2003) to $8.3M (2014), demonstrating that market-based collective-action solutions can succeed where conventional top-down regulation fails.
