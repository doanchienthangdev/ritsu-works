# Kaggle Competitions — Crowdsourced Algorithm Examples

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

The chapter cites five Kaggle competitions as evidence of crowdsourcing scale: DHS passenger screening ($1.5M prize, 518 teams), Zillow home valuation ($1.2M prize, 3779 teams), borrower default prediction ($5K prize, 924 teams, 250K records), fish species identification for The Nature Conservancy ($150K prize, 293 teams), and Titanic survival prediction (10,000+ teams). Together these illustrate how prize-incentivised competitions can rapidly generate superior algorithms at lower cost than traditional consulting.
