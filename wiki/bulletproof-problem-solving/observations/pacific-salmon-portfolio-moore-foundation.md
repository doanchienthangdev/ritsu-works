# Pacific Salmon Conservation: Moore Foundation Portfolio Strategy

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

Charles Conn managed a 15-year, North Pacific-scale conservation portfolio for the Moore Foundation, involving 300+ grants to nearly 100 organizations. The portfolio employed a theory of change map and a portfolio-of-strategies matrix balancing seed/cultivation/harvest stages against incremental/transformational ambition. Individual regional strategies (e.g., Skeena watershed) used shared-vision framing to bridge warring stakeholders toward science-based governance. The case illustrates full deployment of long-horizon problem-solving tools for Level 3–4 uncertainty.
