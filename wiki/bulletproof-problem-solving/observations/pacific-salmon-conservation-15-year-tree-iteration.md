# Pacific Salmon Conservation: 15-Year Logic Tree Iteration

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 3 — Problem Disaggregation and Prioritization

## Summary

A foundation's 15-year, $275 million Pacific salmon conservation effort began with a rudimentary component tree built after days of research. The initial tree was non-MECE and lacked magnitude signals. Through iterative refinement — including expert meetings, science commissions, and regional analysis — the team evolved to a hypothesis tree, pruned low-impact/high-difficulty branches, and achieved focused outcomes including a ban on salmon farming expansion in British Columbia.
