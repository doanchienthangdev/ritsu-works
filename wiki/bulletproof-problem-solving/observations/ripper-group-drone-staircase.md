# Ripper Group UAV Emergency Services Staircase

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 8 — Problem Solving with Long Time Frames and High Uncertainty

## Summary

Ripper Group, an Australian drone company co-founded by Kevin Weldon and Paul Scully-Power, built its staircase starting with beach lifesaving (shark-spotter algorithm), then expanding to flood rescue, bushfire detection, mine tailings inspection, and utility asset inspection. They chose a software-over-hardware focus and collaborated with regulators on BVLOS and night-flight approvals. The case illustrates how a start-up with a single paying customer uses the staircase to sequence capability building and manage Level 2–3 regulatory uncertainty.
