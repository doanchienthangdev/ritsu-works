# Steel Company Capital Investment Problem Redefinition

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 2 — Define the Problem

## Summary

A McKinsey team asked to evaluate a $6 billion capital equipment investment plan discovered the real problem was an inability to generate cash due to an uncompetitive cost position. By redefining the problem at a higher level — free cash flow and shareholder confidence — the team convinced the client to attack costs first and select only a fraction of planned investments, enabling the business to survive.
