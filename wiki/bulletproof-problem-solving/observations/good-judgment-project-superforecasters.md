# Good Judgment Project — Crowd Wisdom Outperforms Expert Forecasters

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Philip Tetlock's Good Judgment Project demonstrated that pooling many independent individual forecasts cancels out individual biases and produces more accurate predictions of world events than single expert judgment — including military invasions. The best contributors are called 'superforecasters'. The project operationalises 'wisdom of the crowd': individual deviations are random and cancel, driving the aggregate toward the correct answer.
