# Sleep Apnea Prediction — Oxford ML Model Outperforms Doctors by 20%

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Oxford doctoral students Logan Graham and Brody Foy developed a machine-learning model using a simple questionnaire (demographics, BMI, co-morbidities) that consistently outperformed clinicians by over 20% in predicting whether children would develop sleep apnea within 6–12 months. The model succeeded on a relatively small dataset of just over 400 patient records. The key caveat: the training data came from patients already flagged as at-risk, so a different, larger dataset would be needed for general population use.
