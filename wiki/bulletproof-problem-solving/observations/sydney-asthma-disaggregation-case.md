# Sydney Asthma Disaggregation Case

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 0 — Introduction: Problem Solving for the Challenges of the Twenty-First Century

## Summary

A team studying the burden of asthma in Sydney found the critical insight only after disaggregating by incidence versus severity. Western Sydney had only 10% higher incidence than Northern Sydney, but 54–65% greater deaths and hospitalisation. Further disaggregation revealed that socioeconomic status was significantly lower, tree cover was about half, and daily maximum particulate matter (PM 2.5) was 50% higher in Western Sydney. This led to a proposal to increase tree cover as a natural solution to respiratory health — an insight invisible in aggregate data.
