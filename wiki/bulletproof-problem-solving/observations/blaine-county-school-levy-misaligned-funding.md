# Blaine County School Levy: Funding Misaligned with Root Cause

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 1 — Learn the Bulletproof Problem Solving Approach

## Summary

Charles analyzed whether to support a $50M school bond in Blaine County, Idaho. Research showed K-12 underperformance in the US is best explained by teacher quality and school environment, not per-student funding levels (higher in the US than most countries). The proposed levy directed most funds at clean energy and school facilities — low-impact factors — with little allocation to teacher recruitment, pay, and training. Charles voted against the first levy; a later levy that included early childhood education and teacher training passed.
