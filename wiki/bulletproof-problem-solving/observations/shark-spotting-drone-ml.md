# Ripper Company — Drone + ML Shark Detection at 90.4% Precision

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

The Ripper Company (with the University of Technology Sydney) developed a shape-interpreting algorithm that identifies sharks from drone video, achieving 90.4% mean average precision. An operator confirms algorithmic detections before beach patrols clear the water. The case illustrates deep learning applied to image recognition for public safety, replacing expensive physical netting while reducing species by-catch. Results are expected to improve further with additional training data.
