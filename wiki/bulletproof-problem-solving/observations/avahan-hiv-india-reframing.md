# Avahan HIV Program India — Demand-Side Reframing

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 2 — Define the Problem

## Summary

The Avahan HIV program in India initially followed a traditional public health (supply-side) approach, but breakthrough results came only after reframing to a demand-side perspective that involved sex workers in developing the solution. This reframing reduced HIV infections by 600,000 over a decade. The chapter uses it to show how widening the aperture of problem definition — bringing in previously excluded voices and perspectives — can unlock dramatically better outcomes.
