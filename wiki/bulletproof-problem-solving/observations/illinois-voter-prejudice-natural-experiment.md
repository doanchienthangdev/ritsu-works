# Illinois Republican Primary — Voter Prejudice Natural Experiment

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Evan Soltas and David Broockman exploited an obscure feature of Illinois Republican delegate voting — where voters cast ballots for multiple delegates supporting the same candidate — as a natural experiment for racial prejudice. Because delegate identities are nearly unknown to voters, differences in vote counts within a slate can only be attributed to name-based racial inference. Analysis of over 800 such experiments found that delegates with minority-sounding names received about 10% fewer votes than their white counterparts.
