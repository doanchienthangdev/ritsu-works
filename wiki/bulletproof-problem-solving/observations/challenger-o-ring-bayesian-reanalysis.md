# Space Shuttle Challenger O-Ring — Bayesian Reanalysis

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Researchers reanalysed the 1986 Challenger disaster data using Bayesian statistics and found that the engineering team's pre-launch analysis was fatally flawed: they examined only flights where O-ring damage occurred at temperatures of 53–81°F, ignoring all undamaged flights. Including all 24 flights, 100% of flights below 65°F had O-ring incidents versus 15% above 65°F. Combining this with a 30% prior probability of failure, the posterior probability of failure at 31°F was 99.8%.
