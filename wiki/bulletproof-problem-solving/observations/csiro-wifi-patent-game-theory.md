# CSIRO Wifi Patent — Game Theory Yields $400M from Tech Giants

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

CSIRO, a small Australian government research agency, used game-theory thinking to win $400M in patent royalties from Microsoft, Intel, Dell, HP, AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile over its wifi technology. Key strategic moves: choosing a weak initial adversary (Buffalo Technology) in a favourable court jurisdiction (Eastern District of Texas, 55% plaintiff win rate versus 33% nationally), demonstrating the patent, then suing larger players from a position of strength. CSIRO also maintained an abandonment option (selling the claim) to cap downside.
