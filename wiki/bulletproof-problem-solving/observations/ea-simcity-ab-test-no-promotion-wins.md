# EA SimCity 5 — A/B Test Shows No Promotion Drives 43% More Sales

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

Electronic Arts ran an A/B/C/D test on the SimCity 5 pre-order page. The version without any promotional offer led to 43.4% more purchases than the version with a promotional banner in a headline position. The finding revealed that SimCity customers' purchase decisions were not contingent on a promotion — counter-intuitive to the team's prior assumption.
