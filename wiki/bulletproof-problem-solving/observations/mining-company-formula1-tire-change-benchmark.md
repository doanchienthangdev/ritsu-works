# Mining Company Learns Tire Changes from Formula 1

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

A mining company exploring how to change large-vehicle tires more quickly asked who had the most experience with fast tire changes. The answer was Formula 1. The mining team visited F1 racing pits to learn process improvements — a vivid example of seeking breakthrough thinking through radical reference-class benchmarking rather than incremental industry improvement.
