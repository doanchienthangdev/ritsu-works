# Hechinger vs. Home Depot: One-Day Answer Case Study

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 4 — Build a Great Workplan and Team Processes

## Summary

The chapter illustrates the situation-complication-resolution structure with a hardware retail case: Hechinger, a dominant regional player, faces a new warehouse-superstore competitor (Home Depot) with substantially lower pricing driven by sourcing economies and higher asset productivity. The one-day resolution concludes Hechinger must reform inventory management, logistics, and sourcing — or face an existential threat even in its core markets.
