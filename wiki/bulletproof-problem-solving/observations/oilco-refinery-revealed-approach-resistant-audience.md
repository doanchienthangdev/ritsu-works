# Oilco Refinery Case: Revealed Approach Overcomes Resistant Local Management

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 7 — Synthesize Results and Tell a Great Story

## Summary

In a refinery in a remote island location owned by an Australian company, local management resented foreign ownership and resisted change. The consulting team used a decision-tree revealed approach, presenting competitor data layer by layer, to gradually bring the local management to accept a difficult conclusion (cost cuts and niche positioning). The case demonstrates that audience psychology, not just analytical rigor, determines the optimal storyline structure.
