# Bay Area Nursing Outcomes: Deductive Tree Saved 1,000 Lives/Year

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 3 — Problem Disaggregation and Prioritization

## Summary

A foundation team used a deductive logic tree to focus nursing investment across two axes: increasing the number of skilled graduates and improving existing RN practices. After 12 years, the effort added over 4,500 RNs in the Bay Area, improved nursing school curricula, reduced bloodstream infections and readmission rates, and saved an estimated 1,000 lives per year from sepsis.
