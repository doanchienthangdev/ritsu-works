# Boston Public Schools — MIT Bus Routing Algorithm Saves $5M

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (Charles Conn, Robert McLean, 2018), Ch 6 — Big Guns of Analysis

## Summary

MIT researchers developed an algorithm that optimised school bus routes for 30,000 Boston students, reducing the fleet from 650 to fewer buses across 230 schools and saving the system $5 million annually. The algorithm runs in minutes versus the weeks required by manual human planning. The case illustrates when automation is clearly superior: an objective external criterion exists (cost/time minimisation), the process is highly repetitive, and human intuition cannot compete with computational scale.
