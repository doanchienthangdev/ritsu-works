# Elevator Pitch for Recommendations

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline

## Summary

The chapter reframes the governing thought as an 'elevator pitch' — the 30-second answer the analyst gives when unexpectedly asked for their recommendation. A good elevator pitch takes a clear stand, delivers the core message and one or two supporting pillars, and invites dialogue. It prepares the problem owner for the formal presentation rather than closing off discussion.

- **From [Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline](../chapters/chapter-10-sell-the-solution-core-message-and-storyline.md)** (PDF pp. 211–235, confidence 0.9):
