# Extreme Users

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path

## Summary

Extreme users are people at the tails of a user distribution whose needs, behaviors, attitudes, and emotions are atypical—experts or complete novices, rabid fans or rejectors, heavy users or those who refuse the product on principle. The chapter argues designers should seek out extreme users during the Empathy phase because they reveal workarounds and hacks invisible in average users, and solutions that work for extremes will also work for mainstream users. IDEO's Tim Brown warns that concentrating on the 'bulge at the center of the bell curve' confirms what you already know rather than revealing what is new.

## Ritsu relevance

For Ritsu, extreme users include students who completed entire textbooks using the platform in one session (power learners) and students who signed up, uploaded a file, and never returned (extreme avoiders). Studying both cohorts would sharpen the activation funnel and surface unarticulated friction the average user silently accepts.
