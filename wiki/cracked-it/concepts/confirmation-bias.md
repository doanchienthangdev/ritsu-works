# Confirmation Bias

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

The cognitive tendency to seek out and give disproportionate weight to evidence that confirms existing hypotheses while ignoring disconfirming evidence. The chapter identifies confirmation bias as the primary pitfall of hypothesis-driven problem solving in corporate settings, made worse by political pressure, groupthink, and the lack of the structural safeguards consulting firms maintain (outsider neutrality, obligation to dissent, continuous PSAC training).

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — The 4S Method](../chapters/chapter-03-the-4s-method.md)** (PDF pp. 52–69, confidence 0.95):

  > Because of confirmation bias, we are much more likely to seek evidence that confirms our hypotheses and much more likely to believe such evidence once we find it, than we are to search for, and pay attention to, disconfirming evidence.

  > Man prefers to believe what he prefers to be true.
