# Story-of-the-Search Anti-Pattern

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline

## Summary

The most pervasive communication failure identified in the chapter: a presenter structures their report following their problem-solving process (first the analyses, then the conclusion at the end) rather than leading with the solution. This buries the core message, forces the audience through seesaw arguments, and fails to persuade. The fix is to restructure around the pyramid principle: solution first, then supporting rationale.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline](../chapters/chapter-10-sell-the-solution-core-message-and-storyline.md)** (PDF pp. 211–235, confidence 0.95):

  > It's tempting to tell the story of the search instead of telling the story of the solution.

  > The underlying flaw is typical: the writer is reporting his problem-solving process instead of explaining the recommendation and its rationale to the problem owner.
