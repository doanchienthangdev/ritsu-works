# Reservation Price Valuation

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis

## Summary

A corporate finance concept used in divestiture analysis: the minimum price at which a seller should be willing to transact, defined as the net present value of cash flows the seller can generate by retaining the asset. In the Solar/Uranus example, Solar's reservation price = NPV of keeping Uranus under its own realistic business plan. This prevents selling below intrinsic value and anchors negotiation rationally.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis](../chapters/chapter-07-solve-the-problem-eight-degrees-of-analysis.md)** (PDF pp. 133–154, confidence 0.95):

  > Solar doesn't face cash constraints and has no reason to sell Uranus for less than the value the business would generate if it kept it. Tracy believes there are buyers prepared to pay more for Uranus than this reservation price.
