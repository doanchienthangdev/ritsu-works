# Groupthink in Hypothesis Testing

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

The social pressure mechanism that silences lone skeptics in corporate problem-solving teams, compounding the dangers of hypothesis-driven thinking. Even someone who privately doubts the prevailing hypothesis will typically 'go with the flow' rather than challenge team-wide confirmation bias. The chapter positions groupthink as one of five interlocking pressures that make hypothesis testing in corporate settings structurally harder than in consulting firms.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — The 4S Method](../chapters/chapter-03-the-4s-method.md)** (PDF pp. 52–69, confidence 0.8):

  > even if you were the lone skeptic on a team of true believers, you'd probably quickly decide that it's not your job to challenge other team members' confirmation biases, and just "go with the flow" despite your doubts—a powerful phenomenon sometimes called groupthink.
