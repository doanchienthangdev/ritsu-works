# Creative Peers in Concept Evaluation

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

The chapter identifies two systematic biases in concept evaluation: insiders (idea generators) produce false positives by falling in love with their own ideas; outsiders (uninvolved managers) produce false negatives by undervaluing novelty and overvaluing familiarity. Creative peers — people outside the project team who are themselves actively generating solutions to similar problems — correct both biases by being neither over-invested nor overly risk-averse.

- **From [Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking](../chapters/chapter-09-structure-and-solve-the-problem-using-design.md)** (PDF pp. 184–210, confidence 0.95):

  > One way to overcome these challenges is to involve creative peers: people from outside the project team actively working on creating solutions to similar problems.

  > we risk falling in love with our ideas and overestimating their chances of success, resulting in 'false positives' (i.e., approving poor concepts). In contrast, outsiders who are not involved in the idea generation process... are more likely to underestimate the value of novel solutions
