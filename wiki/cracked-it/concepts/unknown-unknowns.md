# Unknown Unknowns (Unk-Unks)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

As problem complexity increases, solvers face uncertainties they do not even know exist — 'unknown unknowns' or 'unk-unks.' Unlike known gaps that can be filled by research, unk-unks are invisible to the solver because they lack the frames to see them. The chapter argues that many unknowns are only unknown because solvers fail to invest in discovering them, and that deliberate ethnographic-style immersion (as in the Banco Davivienda case) can surface them.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned](../chapters/chapter-01-the-most-important-skill-you-never-learned.md)** (PDF pp. 18–31, confidence 0.95):

  > an unknown unknown simply means that a problem solver faces uncertainties in solving a problem she is unaware of. Faced with complex problems, we rarely know the right questions to ask.

  > Just because something is unknown, however, doesn't mean it's unknowable.
