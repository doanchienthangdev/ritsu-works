# Actors (TOSCA Component)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 4 — State the Problem: The TOSCA Framework

## Summary

Actors are stakeholders other than the owner who have a stake in the problem and its resolution. Unlike constraints (which are stable), actors are reactive — they can deliberately respond to recommendations in supportive or detrimental ways. Stakeholder analysis is the primary technique: identify each actor and their objectives. The chapter also stresses empathy and immersion as ways to understand actors whose needs they cannot articulate.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — State the Problem: The TOSCA Framework](../chapters/chapter-04-state-the-problem-the-tosca-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 70–85, confidence 0.95):

  > Stakeholder analysis is a useful technique to identify, systematically, the stakeholders and their objectives.

  > Without empathizing with all the actors, the problem statement can't be complete.
