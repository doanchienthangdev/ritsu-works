# Empathize Phase (Design Thinking)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path

## Summary

The Empathize phase is the foundation of design thinking, in which designers deeply understand the people facing the problem: their physical and emotional needs, constraints, existing workarounds, and the full meaning of their experience. It comprises three activities: participant observation (watching users in natural settings), engagement (semi-structured interviews), and immersion (living like a user). Crucially, the chapter recommends focusing on extreme users—those at the tails of the distribution—rather than the average user, because outliers reveal unexpected workarounds and unarticulated needs that will also improve solutions for mainstream users.

  > Empathizing can help you develop novel insights about users that traditional research tools can't.

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's SOP-PRODUCT-002 (N=10 strangers observed) is the institutionalized Empathize phase gate. Extending it toward extreme users—power learners and extreme avoiders—could surface unanticipated pain points in the onboarding-to-first-quiz flow that surveys will miss.
