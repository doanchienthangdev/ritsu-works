# Intermediary Checkpoints

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 11 — Sell the Solution: Recommendation Report and Delivery

## Summary

Scheduled check-ins with the problem owner throughout the problem-solving process, before the final presentation. They serve three purposes: reconfirm shared understanding of the problem, share intermediary findings and collect early reactions, and test draft slides. Checkpoints can range from formal update meetings to short phone calls or messages, depending on complexity and relationship.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 11 — Sell the Solution: Recommendation Report and Delivery](../chapters/chapter-11-sell-the-solution-recommendation-report-and-d.md)** (PDF pp. 236–262, confidence 0.95):

  > To avoid surprises, schedule intermediary checkpoints with the problem owner during the problem-solving process. Checkpoints allow you to update the problem owner on the status of your progress and get feedback.

  > They serve three crucial purposes. First, they provide an opportunity to reconfirm with the problem owner that you remain aligned on how you understand and define the problem, which can change over time.
