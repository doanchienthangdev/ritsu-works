# Analogical Reasoning and Its Limits

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

Analogical reasoning — 'I've seen something like this before, so what worked there may work here' — can be a legitimate source of insight, but it fails when solvers map on the basis of superficial similarities rather than deep causal traits. Experts with strong domain knowledge are especially prone to this: salient prior experience makes similar-looking features in a new problem easy to recall, causing them to overlook critical differences and generate inappropriate solutions.
