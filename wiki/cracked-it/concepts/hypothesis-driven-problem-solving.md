# Hypothesis-Driven Problem Solving

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

An approach that starts from a candidate hypothesis about the solution, then tests it by disaggregating it into conditions that must be true and gathering evidence to prove or disprove them. It is logically efficient—avoiding the trap of 'boiling the ocean'—but carries a serious risk of confirmation bias, especially in corporate settings where hypotheses have proponents, precedents, and political weight. The corresponding structure in the 4S method is the hypothesis pyramid.
