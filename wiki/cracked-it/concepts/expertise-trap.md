# The Expertise Trap

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

Experts develop rich mental models within their domain that help them recognize and solve familiar problems, but these same models become liabilities when problems fall outside their expertise or when conditions change. Cognitive entrenchment makes experts resistant to revising their worldview. Experts are also overconfident about problems outside their domain and prone to superficial analogical reasoning — pattern-matching on surface similarities rather than deep causal structure, leading to poor solutions.
