# Five Phases of Design Thinking

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path

## Summary

The design thinking process consists of five iterative phases: Empathize (discover how users think and feel), Define (reframe the problem from users' perspectives), Ideate (generate many potential solutions), Prototype (represent promising solutions in tangible form), and Test (gather user feedback on prototypes). The phases are not strictly linear—there are feedback loops between all five, and each phase is itself iterative. The process is presented linearly for simplicity but practiced as a cycle.
