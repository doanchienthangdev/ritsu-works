# Grouping Storyline Pattern

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline

## Summary

In a grouping, every key-line point independently supports or details the governing thought; points can be reordered without breaking the logic. Groupings are the 'workhorses' of storylines: robust (the audience can disagree with one reason and still accept the conclusion), easy to construct, and best suited to receptive audiences or simple recommendations. The chief weakness is tactical — listing reasons can feel blunt or confrontational when the audience initially disagrees.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline](../chapters/chapter-10-sell-the-solution-core-message-and-storyline.md)** (PDF pp. 211–235, confidence 0.95):

  > In a grouping pattern, the key line consists of points of the same kind that collectively support or detail the core message.

  > Groupings are the workhorses of storylines. They're robust: if your audience disagrees with one of several reasons you're giving for your governing thought, it may still agree with the overall message.
