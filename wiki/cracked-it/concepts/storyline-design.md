# Storyline Design

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline

## Summary

A storyline is the complete plan of a recommendation report — core message, key line, and all elementary components — designed before any slides or visuals are created. The chapter prescribes writing the storyline as a one-pager (memo or executive summary) first, using a word processor rather than presentation software, to force logical coherence before visual production begins. The storyline can be built top-down when the governing thought is clear, or bottom-up by clustering findings when it is not.

- **From [Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline](../chapters/chapter-10-sell-the-solution-core-message-and-storyline.md)** (PDF pp. 211–235, confidence 0.95):

  > The overall plan of the recommendation report you're putting together is called the "storyline." A complete storyline includes the core message, the key line, and all the elementary components of the presentation.

  > The simplest advice we can offer on this issue is: forget the presentation software for a while, and turn instead to your word processor.
