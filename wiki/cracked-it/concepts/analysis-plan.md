# Analysis Plan

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis

## Summary

A structured mapping from each elementary hypothesis (or issue) to the specific analysis required to confirm or disprove it, and the source of information for that analysis. The analysis plan bridges the hypothesis pyramid or issue tree to actual work execution. It forces analysts to explicitly state what they need to know and where they will get the information before starting work.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis](../chapters/chapter-07-solve-the-problem-eight-degrees-of-analysis.md)** (PDF pp. 133–154, confidence 0.95):

  > Your task as a problem solver is straightforward. You must identify, for each elementary hypothesis: (1) what you must know to test the hypothesis, and (2) how you will get that information. These two steps are the "analyses" and the "sources"

  > The resulting "analysis plan" tells you what you must do to confirm, disprove, or modify the hypotheses.
