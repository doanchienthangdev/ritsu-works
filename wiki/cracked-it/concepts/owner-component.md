# Owner (TOSCA Component)

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 4 — State the Problem: The TOSCA Framework

## Summary

The Owner is the person or entity whose problem it is — and who will judge whether the problem definition and solution are 'good enough.' Choosing the owner shapes the solution space: a narrow owner constrains options; a broader owner opens them. Being explicit about ownership is essential because the owner is the ultimate arbiter of success.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 4 — State the Problem: The TOSCA Framework](../chapters/chapter-04-state-the-problem-the-tosca-framework.md)** (PDF pp. 70–85, confidence 0.95):

  > The identity of the problem owner shapes the potential solution space, and hence the problem definition.

  > If you don't know who's on the hook to solve the problem, you can never define or solve it.
