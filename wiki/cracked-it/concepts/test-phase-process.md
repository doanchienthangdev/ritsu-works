# Test Phase Process

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

Testing is a six-step process following prototype creation: (1) choose a realistic setting and representative sample of users (neither fans nor cynics; small qualitative samples early, hundreds for high-fidelity tests); (2) develop a feedback capture grid (what worked, what didn't, what wasn't understood, how to improve); (3) show the prototype rather than explain it; (4) collect feedback by observing and asking users to think aloud; (5) interpret feedback against hypotheses while staying open to unexpected insights; and (6) reflect with the team to plan the next iteration. The goal is to test as if you will be wrong, embracing disconfirming feedback.

- **From [Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking](../chapters/chapter-09-structure-and-solve-the-problem-using-design.md)** (PDF pp. 184–210, confidence 0.95):

  > Be careful not to defend your prototype when you collect feedback about it... We must embrace disconfirming or unexpected feedback because it can reveal unanticipated limitations of our solutions and provide opportunities to refine them. Test as if you will be wrong.

  > Testing prototypes gives serendipity a chance because it creates opportunities for unexpected feedback.
