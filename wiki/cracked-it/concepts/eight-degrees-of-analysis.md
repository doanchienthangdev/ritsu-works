# Eight Degrees of Analysis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis

## Summary

A continuum of analytical rigor from accepted facts to irreducible judgment calls. The eight types, in increasing complexity, are: (1) given facts requiring no analysis, (2) hard numbers easy to identify, (3) non-numerical qualitative facts, (4) fact-based simple analysis, (5) assumption-based analysis, (6) internal plans and forecasts, (7) expert input, and (8) judgment calls. The framework prevents analysts from treating all analyses as equally reliable or equally uncertain.
