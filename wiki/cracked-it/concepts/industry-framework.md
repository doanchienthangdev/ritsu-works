# Industry Framework

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 6 — Structure the Problem: Analytical Frameworks

## Summary

Industry frameworks are MECE problem breakdowns specific to how a particular industry creates value—its value drivers. Unlike functional frameworks learned in classrooms, industry frameworks are typically learned on the job and embedded in the tools, templates, and decision rules of the industry. The chapter illustrates this with the retail LFL (like-for-like) versus new-store breakdown for Starbucks, which captures the real levers of a retail chain's profitability far better than a generic revenues-vs-costs split.
