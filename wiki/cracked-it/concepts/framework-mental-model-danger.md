# Framework as Mental Model — The Danger of Unquestioned Assumptions

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 6 — Structure the Problem: Analytical Frameworks

## Summary

Every analytical framework embeds a worldview and a set of assumptions about what drives value or what the problem is. When applied routinely and unquestioningly, practitioners lose sight of those embedded assumptions. The chapter contrasts a stock analyst's efficient-market pricing model with a PE investor's active-value-creation model: both address the same question but reflect incompatible assumptions. A further danger is the 'hammer-in-search-of-a-nail' tendency—applying a mastered framework to every problem regardless of fit.
