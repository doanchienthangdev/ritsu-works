# Owner-Centric Problem Definition

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action

## Summary

The chapter emphasizes identifying the single decision-maker whose approval or rejection determines success, rather than satisfying all stakeholders equally. In the Kangaroo case the CEO and CFO are already convinced; the real 'owner' is Brenda, the skeptic. The entire scope and framing of the study is oriented toward giving Brenda a convincing basis for her decision.

- **From [Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action](../chapters/chapter-12-the-4s-method-in-action.md)** (PDF pp. 263–281, confidence 0.95):
