# Sensitivity Analysis

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis

## Summary

A technique for testing assumption robustness by asking whether conclusions would change if key inputs varied by a given percentage, or inversely, by how much inputs would need to change for conclusions to be wrong. The chapter emphasizes that analysts systematically underestimate their own uncertainty (overprecision / miscalibration) and that sensitivity testing is especially critical when sources are likely to be biased.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis](../chapters/chapter-07-solve-the-problem-eight-degrees-of-analysis.md)** (PDF pp. 133–154, confidence 0.95):

  > Even when you're very confident in your assumptions, you must ask yourself, "If I'm wrong, will my conclusions change?" That's the purpose of a sensitivity analysis.

  > Even better, reverse the question and ask: by how much would your assumptions need to change, either individually or collectively, for your conclusion to be wrong?
