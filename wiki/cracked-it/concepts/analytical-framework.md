# Analytical Framework

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 6 — Structure the Problem: Analytical Frameworks

## Summary

An analytical framework is a prepackaged, MECE breakdown of a typical or generic problem. Frameworks serve as building blocks for issue trees and hypothesis pyramids, providing shortcuts to problem structuring by encoding established decompositions for recurring problem classes. The chapter distinguishes three styles: formula (connects a result to components), typology (lists MECE categories), and checklist (lists conditions that must all be simultaneously present).
