# Design Thinking Mindset

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path

## Summary

Design thinking requires a distinct mindset comprising five qualities: creative confidence (belief that everyone is creative and that a disciplined process will yield solutions), tolerance of failure (treating user feedback on ideas as experiments rather than failures), empathy (deep desire to understand people different from yourself), comfort with ambiguity (willingness to start without knowing the solution), and a beginner's mind (suppressing existing assumptions to learn from users with fresh eyes). This mindset distinguishes design thinking from conventional analytical problem-solving.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path](../chapters/chapter-08-redefine-the-problem-the-design-thinking-path.md)** (PDF pp. 155–183, confidence 0.95):

  > Creative confidence: the belief that everyone (including you) is creative; a belief that you can and will discover creative solutions to challenging problems if you have a disciplined process to follow.

  > Begin like a beginner: a willingness to suppress your assumptions and beliefs, enter the process with fresh eyes, and learn from users.
