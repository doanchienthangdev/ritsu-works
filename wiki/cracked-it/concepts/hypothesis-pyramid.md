# Hypothesis Pyramid

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

A top-down problem-structuring tool that starts with a leading hypothesis (candidate solution) and breaks it down into sub-hypotheses that are conditions for the hypothesis to be true. Sub-hypotheses are broken further until they are specific enough to be confirmed or disproven by analysis and data. The logic of the pyramid mirrors the story structure used to sell the solution, making it efficient for both problem solving and communication.
