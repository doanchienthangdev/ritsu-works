# Amazon Narrative Memo Format

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 11 — Sell the Solution: Recommendation Report and Delivery

## Summary

Jeff Bezos's practice of banning PowerPoint at Amazon and requiring executives to write 4-to-6-page prose narratives ("narratives") instead. The first 20 minutes of any meeting are spent reading the memo in silence, after which the presenter fields questions. The chapter uses this as evidence that a well-written storyline makes producing such a narrative straightforward, and that the narrative format deepens the flow and logic of ideas compared to slides.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 11 — Sell the Solution: Recommendation Report and Delivery](../chapters/chapter-11-sell-the-solution-recommendation-report-and-d.md)** (PDF pp. 236–262, confidence 0.95):

  > Amazon executives have ideas to communicate, they write up their views in a 4- to 6-page memo, which the company calls a "narrative." They take the memo to a team meeting, where the first 20 minutes are spent reading the narrative.

  > The advantage of this memo-based approach is that the narrative structure emphasizes the flow and depth of ideas, instead of juxtaposing piecemeal facts in a smorgasbord of slides.
