# The 'So What?' Question

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline

## Summary

A recurring synthesis prompt used by strategy consultants and decision-makers to demand that raw findings be converted into actionable conclusions. The chapter applies it explicitly in the Summit Water bottom-up exercise, where repeatedly asking 'so what?' converts a list of ten contradictory findings into a specific, nuanced governing thought about piloting at a 9:1 ratio. Summarising findings is insufficient; the 'so what?' forces genuine synthesis.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline](../chapters/chapter-10-sell-the-solution-core-message-and-storyline.md)** (PDF pp. 211–235, confidence 0.9):

  > The basic question that may come to mind after going over this list is, "So what?" This is a question strategy consultants—and decision-makers of all kinds—ask several times a day.

  > Summarizing isn't enough. You must synthesize findings and overcome contradictions by constantly asking the "So what?" question.
