# Design Thinking

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

Design thinking is a disciplined, iterative process for solving human-centered, complex problems that are poorly understood by solution developers. It places observation and discovery of human needs at the core, cycling through five phases: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test. The process alternates between divergent and convergent thinking, between concrete fieldwork and abstract modeling, and is inherently collaborative and interdisciplinary. It requires a specific mindset: creative confidence, tolerance of failure, empathy, comfort with ambiguity, and a beginner's mind.
