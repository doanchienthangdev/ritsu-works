# Field of Dreams Principle

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

The chapter names a common entrepreneurial failure pattern: 'if you build it, they will come.' Entrepreneurs, instead of investing in understanding unknown customer problems, often assume their intuition about the problem is correct and proceed directly to building. This is a form of WYSIATI applied to product development — assuming the problem is understood without actually discovering it.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned](../chapters/chapter-01-the-most-important-skill-you-never-learned.md)** (PDF pp. 18–31, confidence 0.9):

  > research shows that entrepreneurs often abide by the field of dreams principle: if you build it, they will come.

## Ritsu relevance

Ritsu's SOP-PRODUCT-002 (observe N=10 strangers before major feature builds) is the direct antidote to this principle — it institutionalizes problem discovery before solution construction.
