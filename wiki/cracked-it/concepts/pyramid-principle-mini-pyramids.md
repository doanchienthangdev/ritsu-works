# Pyramid Principle Mini-Pyramids

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action

## Summary

The chapter shows how the final report implements the pyramid principle at two levels: an executive summary at the top, each key-line point developed as a 'mini-pyramid' (a storyline page summarizing the segment plus content pages with elementary findings), and these mini-pyramids cohering into the full pyramidal structure. Each content page carries a dual headline: an action title ('so what?') and a descriptive chart headline.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action](../chapters/chapter-12-the-4s-method-in-action.md)** (PDF pp. 263–281, confidence 0.95):

  > The report follows the pyramid principle: each key-line point is developed as a mini-pyramid, with the storyline page summarizing the corresponding segment of the story (p. 3) and the content pages presenting the elementary findings (pp. 5–14).

  > All content pages have two headlines: the action title, which conveys the message (the "so what?") of the page as part of the storyline, and a capitalized headline that presents the chart content in purely descriptive terms.
