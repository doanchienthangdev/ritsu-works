# Master Problem-Solver Profile

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 13 — Conclusion: Becoming a Master Problem-Solver

## Summary

The authors characterise expert problem solvers not as exceptionally smart or creative, but as people who have internalised the 4S toolkit through high-volume practice. Their signature capabilities: holding multiple problem framings simultaneously, switching fluidly between hypothesis-driven and design-thinking approaches, juggling functional and industry frameworks, and distilling a clear governing thought while staying open to revision.
