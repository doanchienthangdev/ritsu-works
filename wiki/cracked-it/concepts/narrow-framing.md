# Narrow Framing

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 2 — The Five Pitfalls of Problem Solving

## Summary

The fourth pitfall occurs when a complex, poorly understood problem is mapped by superficial analogy onto a familiar problem type, producing an apparently quick solution that bypasses genuine investigation. This is especially dangerous when the solver is confident in their experience and does not pilot-test the solution before committing resources. Ron Johnson's application of the Apple Store playbook to J.C. Penney without understanding Penney's actual customers illustrates the disastrous consequences.
