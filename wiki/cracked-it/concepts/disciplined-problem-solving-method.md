# Disciplined Problem-Solving Method

**Type:** concept · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

The chapter's central argument is that expertise and raw intelligence alone are insufficient for complex, ill-defined, non-routine problems. What is needed is a systematic, generalizable problem-solving method — a set of explicit steps and tools that counteract cognitive biases, structure thinking, and harness expertise without being constrained by it. Research on training effects confirms that method-following demonstrably improves problem-solving outcomes; this is the foundation for the 4S framework introduced in later chapters.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned](../chapters/chapter-01-the-most-important-skill-you-never-learned.md)** (PDF pp. 18–31, confidence 0.95):

  > What we need is a disciplined and generalizable problem-solving method and a set of useful tools for each step of the process.

  > A disciplined method can help. Strategy consultants turn rookies into trusted advisors and then into CEOs partly by teaching them robust, general-­purpose problem-solving techniques.

  > Following a method matters for problem-solving performance.
