# Separate Solving from Selling to Prevent Contamination

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

The chapter prescribes keeping problem-solving and solution-selling as distinct activities, even while recognizing that communication should be integrated throughout the process. Jumping to 'how will I explain this?' before fully testing a candidate solution leads to selective use of evidence and poor or harmful recommendations. The rule is to ensure you are confident the problem is solved before switching to solution-selling mode.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 3 — The 4S Method](../chapters/chapter-03-the-4s-method.md)** (PDF pp. 52–69, confidence 0.95):

  > Finding the best solution is one task, selling it is another, and shouldn't start before you're sure the problem is solved.

  > Letting the need for a good story pollute the search for a solution frequently traps business problem solvers.
