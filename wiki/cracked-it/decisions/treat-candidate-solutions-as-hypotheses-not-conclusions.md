# Treat Candidate Solutions as Hypotheses to Be Tested, Not Conclusions

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 2 — The Five Pitfalls of Problem Solving

## Summary

Whenever a candidate solution exists at the start of problem-solving, whether from prior experience, organizational assets, or intuition, it must be treated as a working hypothesis requiring factual validation, not as a given. Skipping this discipline and proceeding directly to implementation converts a reasonable heuristic into an unchallenged belief, producing the solution-confirmation pitfall.
