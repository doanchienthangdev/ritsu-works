# Focus on the Skeptical Owner, Not the Already Convinced

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action

## Summary

When multiple stakeholders exist but only one is undecided, orient the entire problem-solving effort toward that skeptic's information needs. In the Kangaroo case, the CEO and CFO were already favorably disposed; all analytical effort was directed at giving Brenda, the skeptic, a defensible basis for her decision. Wasting effort on the already-convinced is a misallocation.
