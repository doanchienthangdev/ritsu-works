# Test Early with Rough, Cheap Prototypes

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

Prototyping and testing early and often, even with rough low-fidelity prototypes, speeds up overall solution development by identifying and eliminating weak solutions before they reach expensive advanced stages. IDEO CEO Tim Brown notes that prototypes 'slow us down to speed us up' — a paradox where short-term iteration investment prevents much larger late-stage rework costs. Small qualitative samples (around ten users) are appropriate for early rough prototypes.
