# Decouple Idea Generation from Evaluation

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

Teams must separate the divergent phase (idea generation) from the convergent phase (idea evaluation). Judging ideas during generation leads to self-censorship, reducing both the number and variety of ideas. The rule is explicit: withhold judgment until ideas are more developed and the generation phase is complete. An experienced facilitator helps enforce this discipline in group settings.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking](../chapters/chapter-09-structure-and-solve-the-problem-using-design.md)** (PDF pp. 184–210, confidence 0.95):

  > Resist the temptation to critically evaluate your team members' ideas and censor your own. While you'll eventually do this in the selection phase, you should decouple idea evaluation from generation.

  > judging ideas as they're generated leads to self-censoring, which reduces the number and variety of ideas produced.
