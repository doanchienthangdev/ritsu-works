# Involve Creative Peers, Not Just Managers, for Concept Review

**Type:** decision · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

To avoid both false positives (insider bias toward own ideas) and false negatives (manager bias toward familiar solutions), concept evaluation should include creative peers — people outside the team who are actively solving similar problems. This role is distinct from both the generating team (too invested) and uninvolved managers (too risk-averse). Creative peers provide objective appraisal of novel ideas without the conservatism of organizational gatekeepers.
