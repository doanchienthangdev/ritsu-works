# Label Certainty Degree on AI-Generated Learning Content

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis

## Summary

Ritsu's AI-generated quizzes, explanations, and concept maps span a wide certainty spectrum — from direct document excerpts (degree 1–2) to inferences and analogies drawn by the model (degree 5–8). Applying the eight-degrees framework to tag generated content with its epistemic basis (cited fact vs. model judgment) would strengthen the SERVQUAL reliability pillar and differentiate Ritsu from tools that present AI confabulation as fact.
