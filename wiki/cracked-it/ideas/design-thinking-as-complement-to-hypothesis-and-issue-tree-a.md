# Design Thinking as Complement to Hypothesis and Issue-Tree Approaches

**Type:** idea · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

The chapter explicitly positions design thinking (abductive: immerse in problem → infer solution) as complementary to hypothesis-driven (deductive) and issue-driven (inductive) problem solving from earlier chapters. Ritsu's /cla and /think workflows currently lean heavily on hypothesis pyramids and logic trees; explicitly incorporating design thinking phases (empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test) for user-facing product problems — especially new feature designs — could surface solution directions the analytical approaches miss.

## Ritsu relevance

The 4S method's Structure step can be implemented via issue trees OR design thinking, depending on whether the problem is analytical or creative. Ritsu's product team faces many creative problems (how to teach X concept, how to make quizzes feel rewarding) where design thinking's abductive loop fits better than MECE decomposition.
