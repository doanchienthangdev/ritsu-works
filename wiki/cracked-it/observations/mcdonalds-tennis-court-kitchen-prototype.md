# McDonald Brothers' Tennis Court Kitchen Prototype

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

In 1948, Dick and Maurice McDonald closed their successful San Bernardino drive-in to reinvent their kitchen operations. Dick McDonald hypothesized that kitchen layout affected preparation speed. To test this cheaply and quickly, the brothers drew the exact kitchen dimensions in chalk on their home tennis court and ran simulations with kitchen staff. After multiple layout iterations observed from a ladder, they identified a highly efficient configuration. The resulting 'Speedee Service System' delivered a full meal in 30 seconds, revolutionizing the restaurant industry and founding the fast-food category.
