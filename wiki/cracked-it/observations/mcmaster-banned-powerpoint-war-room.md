# H.R. McMaster Banned PowerPoint from War-Room Briefings

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 11 — Sell the Solution: Recommendation Report and Delivery

## Summary

US General H.R. McMaster banned PowerPoint presentations from all war-room briefings, citing "slideware" as the cause of an insidious erosion of strategic vision. The authors cite this as evidence that bullet-pointed slides break logical depth, flatten the relative importance of findings, and oversimplify interconnections — exactly the failure mode of strategic problem-solving presented without a solid storyline.
