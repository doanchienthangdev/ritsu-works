# OXO Good Grips Developed from Extreme User (Arthritis Sufferer) to Benefit All

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path

## Summary

Sam Farber observed his wife struggling to use a vegetable peeler due to mild arthritis during a vacation, prompting him to question why kitchen tools were uncomfortable. He partnered with Smart Design to research and develop new tools, resulting in the OXO Good Grips line (1990)—15 ergonomically designed tools with soft black plastic handles. The tools, initially conceived for people with physical limitations, inspired fierce brand loyalty across the full spectrum of kitchen utensil users, demonstrating that designing for extreme users yields solutions that outperform for everyone.
