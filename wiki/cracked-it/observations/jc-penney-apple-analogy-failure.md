# J.C. Penney: Ron Johnson Applied Apple Playbook Without Understanding Penney's Customers

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 2 — The Five Pitfalls of Problem Solving

## Summary

Ron Johnson, hired as CEO of J.C. Penney in 2011 following success at Apple retail, eliminated promotions and discounts, introduced boutique-style branded sections, and rebranded to 'jcp', all without pilot testing or investigating actual customer preferences. In 2012, revenues fell $4.3 billion, same-store sales dropped 25 percent, the company recorded a $1 billion loss, and Johnson was ousted after 18 months. The core assumption that Penney's customers resembled Apple Store customers was never tested.
