# General Intelligence Explains Only ~20% of Complex Problem-Solving Effectiveness

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

A meta-analysis of 47 studies (nearly 14,000 participants) found that general intelligence accounted for approximately one-fifth of variance in complex problem-solving effectiveness, leaving over 80% explained by other factors. This empirical finding is the chapter's key evidence that intelligence alone is insufficient and that trainable method and process matter independently of IQ.
