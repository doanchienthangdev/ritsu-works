# CB Insights: 40%+ of Startup Failures Caused by Insufficient Market Acceptance

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

A CB Insights postmortem of over 200 failed startups found the leading cause of failure — over 40 percent — was insufficient market acceptance: the ventures built solutions that didn't effectively address customers' actual problems. The chapter uses this to illustrate the 'field of dreams principle' — the entrepreneurial tendency to build without first deeply understanding the problem — and as evidence that unknown unknowns matter at scale.
