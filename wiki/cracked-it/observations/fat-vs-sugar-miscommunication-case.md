# Sugar Hypothesis Suppressed for 40 Years Due to Communication Asymmetry

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 2 — The Five Pitfalls of Problem Solving

## Summary

British scientist John Yudkin correctly identified sugar (not fat) as the primary driver of obesity and heart disease in the late 1950s, but his theory was dismissed while Ancel Keys's incorrect fat hypothesis spread globally for four decades. Keys was brilliant at scientific communication, institutional positioning, and political persuasion; Yudkin was scientifically rigorous but an ineffective communicator. Robert Lustig's 2009 YouTube video 'Sugar: The Bitter Truth', garnering 7.6 million views, finally surfaced the correct story through masterful scientific communication.
