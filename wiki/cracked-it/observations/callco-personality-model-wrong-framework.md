# CallCo Exit Interviews Revealed Framework Was Wrong Before the Tool Was Adopted

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 2 — The Five Pitfalls of Problem Solving

## Summary

Lisa, HR director of a large call-center operator with 30 percent staff turnover, nearly adopted a machine-learning personality-matching vendor (BigHRData) before exit interviews revealed that leavers unanimously cited low pay, poor working conditions, and brutal management as the cause of attrition. The personality model, had it been adopted, would have selected for workers who had no better alternative, optimizing for retention of dissatisfied employees rather than improving performance or conditions.
