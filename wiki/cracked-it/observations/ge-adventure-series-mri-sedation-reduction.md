# GE Adventure Series Reduces Pediatric MRI Sedation from 80% to 10–27%

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 8 — Redefine the Problem: The Design Thinking Path

## Summary

Doug Dietz at GE Healthcare transformed pediatric MRI suites into adventure-themed environments (pirate ships, rocket ships) using decals, scripts for staff, soundtracks, and aromatherapy—without changing the internal MRI technology. The result: sedation rates at Pittsburgh Children's Hospital dropped from 80% to 27%, with some locations reaching as low as 10%. Patient satisfaction scores increased by up to 90%, and the number of scans per day increased significantly. The intervention came from reframing the problem from 'how to maximize scans while minimizing sedation costs' to 'how might we turn MRI scans for children into an adventure.'
