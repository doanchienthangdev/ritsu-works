# Kangaroo Case — Expansion Plan Overoptimism Finding

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action

## Summary

A benchmark of comparable small and medium companies that attempted brand-umbrella product-line extensions revealed that Kangaroo's five-year growth targets were overly ambitious. Most comparables had not achieved similar targets. Consumer focus groups confirmed the plans seemed 'oversold,' though interviews suggested the expansion effort might create positive spillovers for the core brand even if it fell short.

- **From [Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action](../chapters/chapter-12-the-4s-method-in-action.md)** (PDF pp. 263–281, confidence 0.95):
