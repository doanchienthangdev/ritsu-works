# Great Ormond Street Hospital F1 Pit Stop Analogy

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 9 — Structure and Solve the Problem Using Design Thinking

## Summary

Professor Martin Elliott and Dr. Allan Goldman at Great Ormond Street Hospital in London noticed by accident while watching TV that Formula 1 pit stops structurally resembled the critical and error-prone 15-minute handover of post-cardiac-surgery infants from the operating theater team to the ICU team. They collaborated with the McLaren and Ferrari F1 teams to redesign the handover protocol, resulting in significant improvements across all aspects of the handover process. This case exemplifies distant analogical thinking — borrowing from a domain (motorsport) that appears superficially unrelated.
