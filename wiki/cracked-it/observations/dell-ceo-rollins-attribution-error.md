# Dell / Kevin Rollins: Attribution Error in CEO Problem Diagnosis

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 1 — The Most Important Skill You Never Learned

## Summary

When Kevin Rollins's tenure as Dell CEO coincided with slowing growth, declining market share, and stock decline, observers quickly attributed the problems to his leadership. But deeper examination revealed multiple confounding factors: HP's acquisition of Compaq, flattening enterprise demand, declining value of Dell's customization model, faulty Sony batteries, and accounting issues originating under Michael Dell. The case illustrates how WYSIATI and System 1 shortcuts produce confident but wrong causal attributions.
