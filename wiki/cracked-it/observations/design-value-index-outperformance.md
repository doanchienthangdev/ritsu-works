# Design-Centric Companies Outperformed S&P 500 by 228%

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 3 — The 4S Method

## Summary

The Design Management Institute's Design Value Index tracked a portfolio of 16 design-centric companies and found it outperformed the S&P 500 by 228 percent over 2006–16. The chapter cites this as evidence that the extensive use of design thinking improves financial performance, supporting its inclusion in the 4S method alongside analytical approaches.
