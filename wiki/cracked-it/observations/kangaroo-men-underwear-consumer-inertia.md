# Kangaroo Case — Men's Underwear Consumer Inertia

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action

## Summary

Primary research (retailer and expert interviews) showed that men's underwear buying is characterized by exceptionally high brand loyalty and low purchasing commitment. Buyers who find a comfortable brand rarely switch. The market was flat in volume since 2012, with average unit prices stable due to a category mix shift from briefs to boxer shorts — not from price increases.
