# Barclays / Lehman — Excel Hidden Rows Cause Unintended Asset Acquisition

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 7 — Solve the Problem: Eight Degrees of Analysis

## Summary

After the Lehman Brothers bankruptcy, an analyst at Barclays' law firm failed to delete hidden rows in an Excel spreadsheet before converting it to PDF, accidentally including toxic assets Barclays had not intended to acquire in its bid. The case illustrates that plain calculation and formatting errors remain frequent even in high-stakes settings, and the only antidote is independent double-checking.
