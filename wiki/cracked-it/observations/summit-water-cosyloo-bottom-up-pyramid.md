# Summit Water–Cosyloo Bottom-Up Pyramid (SCR)

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline

## Summary

A real-life case (company names disguised) where a utility's strategy team had ten contradictory findings about a portable-toilet startup partnership. The team built the pyramid bottom-up using 'so what?' iteration, discovering the governing thought: pilot the service with a 9:1 ratio of septic-tank to portable-toilet customers. The final storyline uses an SCR argument pattern, acknowledging both the strategic fit and the implementation hurdles before the resolution.

- **From [Ch 10 — Sell the Solution: Core Message and Storyline](../chapters/chapter-10-sell-the-solution-core-message-and-storyline.md)** (PDF pp. 211–235, confidence 0.9):
