# Kangaroo Case — Private Label Plateau Finding

**Type:** observation · **From:** *Cracked It! How to Solve Big Problems and Sell Solutions Like Top Strategy Consultants* (Bernard Garrette, Corey Phelps, Olivier Sibony, 2018), Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action

## Summary

Field research in the Kangaroo case found that mass retailers' private labels had grown significantly but had probably reached a plateau in men's underwear. Multiple retailer interviews supported stabilization, and panel data showed that private labels had not hurt the best-established brands. The Alligator brand launch was retrospectively validated as a smart counter-move.

## Raw quotes (verbatim, for citation)

- **From [Ch 12 — The 4S Method in Action](../chapters/chapter-12-the-4s-method-in-action.md)** (PDF pp. 263–281, confidence 0.95):

  > The team concludes that launching the Alligator brand, with its value-for-money positioning, was a smart move to counterattack on this front, and that private-label products have probably reached a plateau and are unlikely to gain additional market share in the future.

  > In addition, the rise of private labels has not hurt the best-established brands, which is consistent with past experience in other categories
