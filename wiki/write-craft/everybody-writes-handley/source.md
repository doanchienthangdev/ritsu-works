## What this book gives /write

- Reframe every claim with the 'So what? / Because' ladder until you hit the reader's real felt stake — then pin that one sentence at the top of the page as your map.
- Make the customer the hero, not the product: the Rudolph fill-in-the-blank template forces the why-now 'foggy Christmas Eve' moment and gives the credit to the reader.
- 'Tell Me Without Telling Me' — dramatize the benefit with a concrete image or named specific instead of describing a feature; specific is also funnier (minivan > car).
- Write the Ugly First Draft fast and badly — writing IS editing, so reserve all judgment for the rewrite where voice, humor, and clarity actually arrive.
- A reusable copy-edit pass for any piece: important words first, active voice + action verbs, real words over buzzwords, every word earning its keep, white space as oxygen.
