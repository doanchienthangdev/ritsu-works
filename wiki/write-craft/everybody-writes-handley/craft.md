# Writing craft distilled — Everybody Writes (Ann Handley)

## Core thesis on writing
Writing is a skill, not a talent — therefore learnable, and a daily habit rather than an art. The whole craft bends toward one north star: **serve the reader, not the writer.** High-quality content = **Utility × Inspiration × Empathy** — it helps an audience, is grounded in data or creativity, and is built on near-pathological empathy for the reader's point of view. Grammar matters but comes *second*; thinking, rewriting, and relentless reader-focus come first.

## Frameworks it gives /write
- **Writing GPS (Go · Push · Shine)** — a 17-step process in 3 phases (research → write/rewrite → polish/publish) for any piece that matters; 7 steps are mandatory, 10 add the magic.
- **So What? / Because Ladder** — interrogate any claim until you hit the reader's real felt stake; pin it as your map sentence.
- **Tell Me Without Telling Me** — show-don't-tell for copy: dramatize the benefit with an image, don't describe the feature.
- **Name Names (Specificity Ladder)** — trade generic nouns for specific true instances ('a fat pug named Carl'); specific is more vivid AND funnier.
- **Rudolph Framework** — an 8-blank fill-in template that makes the *customer* the hero and the product merely the enabler; find the 'foggy Christmas Eve' why-now.
- **Six Elements of a Marketing Story** — true · human · original · customer-as-hero · emotional · strategy-aligned (strategy last).
- **Hermit Crab Content** — pour your message into a borrowed, familiar format (recipe, game show, People profile).
- **Lede + Kicker** — open with a punch, close with a kick; ten opening moves, four closing moves.
- **Fifteen Angles to Frame a Piece** — rotate the frame (quiz, explainer, contrarian, how-to…) on a fixed topic.
- **Headline Recipe (90% clear, 10% clever)** + **Lift the A in CTA** + **Landing Page Five-Things** + **Brand Voice in Four Words**.

## Craft principles
- **Serve the reader, not the writer** — please one person (the customer); if they love it, the boss will too.
- **Pathological empathy** — engineer the 'It's-Me Minute' where the reader feels mirrored.
- **Good writing is good teaching** — explain the *why*, bring rigor AND joy.
- **Embrace the Ugly First Draft** — writing is editing; brilliance comes on the rewrite.
- **Important words first** — lead with subject+verb; cut 'According to…', 'It is important to note…'.
- **Active voice + action verbs** — make sentences throb with a pulse; ditch invisible 'thinking' verbs.
- **Keep it simple, not simplistic** — write for scrollers; white space is oxygen; no one complains it's too clear.
- **Use real words** — kill buzzword bloat; we all sell to people, not companies.
- **Voice is constant, tone flexes** — one recognizable voice across every touchpoint.
- **Make every word earn its keep** — cut clichés, 'really/very,' and adverbs a stronger verb replaces.
- **Break school rules with purpose** — fragments, one-sentence paragraphs, starting with And/But — when they add clarity or voice.
- **Cite as you write** — ground in credible, current sources; check twice, create once.
