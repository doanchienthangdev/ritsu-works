## What this book gives /write

- Clutter is the disease: cut every word doing no new work, then cut the draft to half — most first drafts shrink 50% with no loss of meaning or voice.
- Clear thinking IS clear writing — if a sentence is fuzzy, the thought is fuzzy; fix the idea, not just the words.
- Rewriting is the writing: the newborn sentence is almost always wrong, and clarity is the residue of relentless tinkering done by ear, read aloud.
- Leave the reader with exactly ONE provocative thought; bite off a small corner, cover it well, and stop at the nearest exit (no 'In sum…' recap).
- Voice is the product — be yourself, use 'I/you' and active verbs, prune every 'kind of'; readers buy who you are, not the subject.
