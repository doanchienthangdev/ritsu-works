## What this book gives /write

- Open every piece with an Inciting Incident — knock the reader's world off balance in the first lines; never ease in with context.
- The Gap is the engine of attention: make reality contradict expectation in every beat, and ruthlessly cut any section where nothing's value-charge changes (the 'non-event').
- Dramatize, never explain — prove your idea through what the reader experiences, because understanding informs but only dramatization convinces.
- Convert exposition to ammunition and withhold secrets till last: you hold attention by withholding information, not by giving it.
- Lock meaning to one sentence (Value + Cause) and make the ending inevitable — 'Meaning Produces Emotion,' not spectacle or volume.
