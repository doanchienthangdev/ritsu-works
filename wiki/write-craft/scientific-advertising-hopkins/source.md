## What this book gives /write

- Kill every superlative: "best/fastest/most effective" persuades no one — "recall 2× more in a week" does, because a specific claim reads as tested fact.
- Lead with service, not the ask: rewrite every "sign up for Ritsu" as the result the reader gets at zero risk — the purchase becomes the natural consequence.
- Spend hours on the headline, minutes admiring it: its only job is to hail the exact reader you can help, and a new headline can multiply response 5–10×.
- Write to sell, not to be applauded — the test for any clever line is "would it help a salesman sell this face-to-face?"
- Let buyers, not opinions, decide: run small honest variants, measure cost-per-customer, and scale only the proven winner.
