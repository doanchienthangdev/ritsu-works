## What this book gives /write

- Every sentence is a story: put your characters in the subjects and their actions in the verbs — the one move that fixes most 'dense' prose.
- 'Flow' is engineered at the seams: open each sentence on old/familiar information and end it on the new — then chain them.
- The end of a sentence is its stress position; relocate the idea you most want felt to the last slot and cut whatever trails after it.
- Concision is a six-step delete-and-compress pass (meaningless, doubled, inferable, phrase→word, negatives→affirmatives, useless modifiers) — and it's manual labor, not a skim.
- Clarity is an ethical, reputation-building act: write to others as you would have others write to you.
