## What this book gives /write

- Readers run on 'click, run' shortcuts — lead with the single trigger cue that carries your case, not a wall of evidence.
- Frame stakes as a loss, not a gain: loss aversion pulls harder than the identical benefit, and scarcity must be real or it destroys trust.
- Earn a small, active, public yes first — it rewrites the reader's self-image and 'grows its own legs' toward the bigger ask.
- Prove with the many-like-you (a concrete count of similar peers) and aim for shared identity ('one of us'), never mere similarity.
- Never make a bare request — attach a genuine 'because' reason, and give real value before you ask.
