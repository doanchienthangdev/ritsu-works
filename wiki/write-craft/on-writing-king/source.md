## What this book gives /write

- Show, don't tell: replace every 'X was [adjective]' with a depicted behavior the reader decodes — 'If I have to tell you, I lose.'
- Make revision a quota, not a vibe: 2nd Draft = 1st Draft − 10%, cutting needless words, adverbs, and stage directions first.
- Write any lesson or story from a one-sentence 'What if ___?' situation and let it grow, instead of pre-plotting the ending.
- Describe with three or four well-chosen sensory details and stop — description should finish in the reader's imagination, not the writer's.
- Aim every draft at ONE Ideal Reader and watch where they put it down; that scene is the boring part that has to go.
