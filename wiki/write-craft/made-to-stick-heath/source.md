## What this book gives /write

- The SUCCESs checklist (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories) gives /write a six-point audit to make any draft memorable and action-changing — not just readable.
- The Curse of Knowledge is the master failure mode: experts write 'taps' and forget the reader can't hear the tune — every /write piece should be re-read cold as a novice.
- Write to the one, not the mass — a single named person beats statistics, because readers are wired to feel for people, not abstractions (and calculating kills caring).
- Open a curiosity gap before you fill it ('here's what you know; now here's what you're missing') and translate every statistic into a human-scale relationship.
- Show, don't tell, by making it a simulation: a concrete story is a flight simulator that rehearses the reader for the real moment and teaches them to spot the problem in the wild.
