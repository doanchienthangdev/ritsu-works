## What this book gives /write

- 'Omit needless words' is a checklist, not a vibe: cut 'the fact that', collapse 'who is/which was', swap padding phrases for single words — a deterministic compression pass /write can run on any draft.
- Default to active voice and positive form — name the actor, and say what IS true, not what isn't ('did not remember' → 'forgot'); hedged negatives read as both tame and vague.
- Build every expository paragraph as topic-sentence → develop → loop back, and never let the last sentence wander into a digression.
- Engineer emphasis by position: the most important word goes at the END of the sentence — and the rule scales to paragraphs and whole pieces.
- Make form mirror meaning — parallel ideas in parallel grammar, related words kept physically together — so the reader grasps structure without effort.
