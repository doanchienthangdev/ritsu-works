## What this book gives /write

- Write the one-line FIRST and pitch it to strangers — if you can't say 'what it is' in a sentence, you don't have it yet.
- Bookend every piece with opposite open/close images; the gap between them is your proof that change actually happened.
- Make the Midpoint a false-up and the All-Is-Lost its false-down inverse — and slip a 'whiff of death' into the low point so the turn lands.
- Name your moves to make them stick ('Save the Cat', 'Pope in the Pool', 'Is It Primal?') — a labeled rule is a remembered rule.
- Anchor every stake to a primal drive (survival, protection, fear of failure) so a caveman — and the whole world — gets it.
