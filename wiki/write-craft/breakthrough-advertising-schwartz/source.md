## What this book gives /write

- Lead with the desire, not the product — rank candidate desires by Urgency × Staying-power × Scope and let only ONE drive the headline (turns 'Ritsu has 17 activity types' into 'Ace your exam in 3 days').
- Belief is built, not asserted: open with what the reader already accepts, stack a 'Habit of Agreement,' and bridge up to the big claim last — one believed promise outsells ten doubted ones.
- Meet the reader where they are: match the headline to their State of Awareness AND Market Sophistication — this book is the canonical source of both, and a headline that wins at one stage dies at the next.
- Don't argue objections — redefine them (concept-judo: 'repairs' → 'minor adjustments'); innovation without acceptance is worthless.
- A new structure is a new ad: when copy goes dead, re-sequence the same material instead of rewording, and interweave promise + image + logic + emotion into every single sentence.
