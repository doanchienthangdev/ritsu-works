# Writing craft distilled — Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwartz)

## Core thesis on writing
You cannot create desire — you can only **channel** desire that already exists in millions of people onto your product. Great persuasive writing is therefore not invention but **analysis**: choose the one strongest desire, meet the reader at the exact point of awareness and market-sophistication they've reached, and then build **belief brick by brick** until the claim feels inevitable. The words are only half the ad; the other half is the **architecture of reactions** they trigger in the reader's mind.

## Frameworks it gives /write
- **Gradualization (the Belief-Bridge)** — open with what the reader already accepts, stack small agreements, lead them up to the big claim last.
- **Mass-Desire Selection** — rank candidate desires by Urgency × Staying-power × Scope; only one may lead.
- **Intensification** — stack fresh, never-repeated fulfillment images until desire overpowers price.
- **Mechanization (Name → Describe → Feature)** — answer "how does it work?" at the depth the market needs.
- **Redefinition (Concept-Judo)** — dissolve an objection by giving the thing a new definition ("repairs" → "adjustments").
- **Concentration (Bad/Good Interweave)** — destroy the alternatives, but never attack a weakness without supplying the cure.
- **Rule-and-Example Campaign** — one fixed central idea, endlessly fresh examples, across a whole series.
- **States of Awareness** + **Market Sophistication** (this book is the *canonical source* of both) — the more aware/saturated the market, the more you must shift from claim → mechanism → identification.

## Craft principles
- The **headline's only job is to earn the second sentence** — every sentence then sells the next.
- **Sell what it does, not what it is** — specs only justify price and document the promise.
- **One fully-believed promise beats ten half-believed ones** — strengthen structure, don't pile on claims.
- **Interweave every sentence** with promise + image + logic + emotion — "1 + 1 can equal 10."
- **Position increases power** — deliver proof exactly when the reader silently demands it.
- **A new structure is a new ad** — when copy dies, re-sequence the same material, don't just reword.
- **Hide your emotion-definers** and **read it aloud** — mood works only while invisible.
- **Research first**; know the reader better than they know themselves, or the believability-net shatters.
- **No formula works twice** — analyze the unique product-market-timing situation; copying produces lifeless "Echo Ads."

## Signature lines
- "Copy cannot create desire for a product. It can only… focus those already-existing desires onto a particular product."
- "If you demand that your prospect jump across a believability-chasm, your ad will fail. If… you build a bridge of ideas… then he will let you lead him almost anywhere."
- "One fully-believed promise has ten times the sales power of ten partially-believed promises."
- "Analysis is the art of asking the right questions and letting the problem dictate the right answers."
