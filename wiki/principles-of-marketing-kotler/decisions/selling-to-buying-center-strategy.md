# How to Sell to a Buying Center

> **Source.** [Chapter 6](../chapters/chapter-06-business-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 186–191, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The business marketer must learn who participates in the decision, each participant's relative influence, and what evaluation criteria each decision participant uses."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

B2B sellers map all 5 buying center roles, assess each participant's influence and evaluation criteria, tailor outreach accordingly. New task: reach as many key buying influences as possible, provide help/info proactively. Proposals = marketing documents, not just technical specs.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "In the new task situation, the marketer not only tries to reach as many key buying influences as possible but also provides help and information."
>
> "Proposals should be marketing documents, not just technical documents. Presentations should inspire confidence and should make the marketer's company stand out from the competition."
>
> "Sometimes, even the people in the buying center are not aware of all the buying participants."
>
