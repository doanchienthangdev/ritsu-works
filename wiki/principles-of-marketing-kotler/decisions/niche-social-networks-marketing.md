# Target Niche Social Networks Over Mass Platforms

> **Source.** [Chapter 17](../chapters/chapter-17-direct-online-social-media-mobile.md), book pp. 505–506, chunk 6 (confidence 0.85, auto-accepted).
>
> "Countless niche and interest-based social media have also emerged. These online social networks cater to the needs of smaller communities of like-minded people, making them ideal vehicles for marketers who want to target special-interest groups."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Mass platforms (Facebook 2.4B, YouTube 1.3B, Instagram 1B+) get headlines. Niche networks (Goodreads 65M readers, Doximity 1M+ MDs, CafeMom mothers) offer higher targeting precision. For defined ICPs, niche may deliver better engagement than fighting mass platforms.
