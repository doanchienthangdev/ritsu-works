# Ritsu Should Maintain Direct-to-Learner Distribution

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 383–398, chunk 3 (confidence 0.85, auto-accepted).
>
> "Channel disintermediation pattern: brands that survive are those with strong direct relationships with their end users."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Chapter's retail disintermediation + direct channel shift confirms Ritsu's direct freemium model strategically correct. LMS/aggregator intermediaries would erode margin, reduce data access, dilute personalization advantage. Educator B2B = controlled exception via licensing, not reseller.

## Additional quotes

> "The chapter notes that retailers add a B2B wholesale layer only when it adds efficiency they cannot replicate direct."
>

## For Ritsu

Decision: Sustain freemium direct-to-learner as primary channel. If B2B education channel pursued, structure as licensing (Ritsu retains student relationship + data) not reseller. Direct preserves personalization data, margin, pricing power, product control — all eroded by LMS or aggregator intermediation.
