# Ritsu Should Not Build Traditional Sales Force Pre-PMF

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 464–479, chunk 2 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Field B2B sales call cost: ~$600/call. For B2C SaaS with ARPU below $20/month, field sales is never ROI-positive at scale."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Chapter's personal selling machinery is B2B-optimized + high-fixed-cost. B2B field call $600 vs B2C ARPU $20/mo — never ROI-positive. Pre-PMF: founder is sales force for institutional inquiries. 02-sales pillar stays LITE. B2C conversion = sales promotion + product design, not sales force.

## Additional quotes

> "Personal selling shines in 'complex selling situations' where 'salespeople can probe customers to learn more about their problems and adjust the marketing offer to fit each customer's special needs.'"
>

## For Ritsu

Decision: founder IS sales force for institutional inquiries. 02-sales remains LITE per pillar architecture — enters DEEP only at ≥$500/mo deal or 3 enterprise leads. B2C is sales promotion + product design. Trigger to reassess: first inbound from school/company/platform with ≥50 potential users.
