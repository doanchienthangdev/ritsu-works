# Market Leaders Must Continuously Innovate

> **Source.** [Chapter 18](../chapters/chapter-18-creating-competitive-advantage.md), book pp. 538–540, chunk 8 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "But the best defense is a good offense, and the best response is continuous innovation. The market leader refuses to be content with the way things are."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Leaders cannot be complacent. Lose position to: product innovation (Netflix unseating Blockbuster), arrogance (Sears losing to Walmart), looking old-fashioned (Abercrombie vs Zara). Best defense = continuous innovation: new products/services/distribution/promo/cost cutting. P&G 5K+ diaper patents.

## Additional quotes

> "A leader's life is not easy. It must maintain a constant watch. Other firms keep challenging its strengths."
>
> "A product innovation may come along and hurt the leader (as when Netflix's direct marketing and video streaming unseated then-market leader Blockbuster)."
>
