# GDPR as Default: Ask Only What You Need, Use Responsibly, Don't Share Without Permission

> **Source.** [Chapter 4](../chapters/chapter-04-managing-marketing-information.md), book pp. 132–143, chunk 7 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The European Union recently enacted a General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which sets strict standards for how companies around the world collect, process, and protect personal data on citizens of the European Union. Companies that fail to comply with GDPR face substantial fines, up to 20 million euros or 4 percent of their global sales, whichever is larger."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

GDPR applies to any organization holding EU citizen data regardless of company location. Penalties up to €20M or 4% global sales. Decision rule: only collect what you need, use it to provide customer value, never share without permission.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The best approach is for researchers to ask only for the information they need, use it responsibly to provide customer value, and avoid sharing information without the customer's permission."
>
