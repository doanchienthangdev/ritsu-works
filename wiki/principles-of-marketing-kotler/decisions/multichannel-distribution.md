# Multichannel Distribution — When + How to Add Channels

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 352–353, chunk 6 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Today, with the proliferation of customer segments and channel possibilities, more and more companies have adopted multichannel distribution systems."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Single firm sets up 2+ channels for 1+ segments. Benefits: expanded reach, tailored to diverse needs. Risks: harder to control, generates conflict as channels compete. John Deere routes all online sales through dealers to avoid conflict with existing dealer network.

## Additional quotes

> "With each new channel, the company expands its sales and market coverage and gains opportunities to tailor its products and services to the specific needs of diverse customer segments. But such multichannel systems are harder to control, and they can generate conflict as more channels compete for customers."
>
> "To avoid such conflicts in its online marketing channels, the company routes all of its online sales to John Deere dealers."
>
