# Adopt Proactive Rather Than Reactive Stance Toward Marketing Environment

> **Source.** [Chapter 3](../chapters/chapter-03-analyzing-the-marketing-environment.md), book pp. 109–111, chunk 7 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Many companies view the marketing environment as an uncontrollable element to which they must react and adapt. They passively accept the marketing environment and do not try to change it."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Reactive companies treat environment as uncontrollable. Proactive firms shape the environment through lobbying, media engagement, partnerships, competitive actions. Whenever possible, smart marketers take proactive stance.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Other companies take a proactive stance toward the marketing environment. Rather than assuming that strategic options are bounded by the current environment, these firms develop strategies to change the environment."
>
> "Whenever possible, smart marketing managers take a proactive rather than reactive approach to the marketing environment."
>
> "There are three kinds of companies: those who make things happen, those who watch things happen, and those who wonder what's happened."
>
