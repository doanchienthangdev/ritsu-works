# Decision Framework: Which Global Markets to Enter

> **Source.** [Chapter 19](../chapters/chapter-19-the-global-marketplace.md), book pp. 562–564, chunk 5 (confidence 0.93, auto-accepted).
>
> "After listing possible international markets, the company must carefully evaluate each one."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Evaluate foreign markets across demographic, geographic, economic (GDP, income distribution), sociocultural, political-legal. Rank on size, growth, cost, competitive advantage, risk. Seek greatest long-run ROI. Netflix India case: 1.4B population vs fierce local competition (Hotstar, 35 streaming services), pricing barriers ($8 vs $4 Hotstar), content gaps.

## Additional quotes

> "Possible global markets should be ranked on several factors, including market size, market growth, the cost of doing business, competitive advantage, and risk level."
