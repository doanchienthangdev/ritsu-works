# Ritsu Should Set Prices Value-Based, Not Cost-Plus

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 298–298, chunk 3 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Value-based pricing reverses this process. The company first assesses customer needs and value perceptions. It then sets its target price based on customer perceptions of value. The targeted value and price drive decisions about what costs can be incurred and the resulting product design."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ritsu's pricing anchor must be customer-perceived value (time saved, exam performance, mastery), not Anthropic API cost. Cost-plus creates moving floor + no WTP signal. Value-based requires per-persona WTP understanding.

## For Ritsu

Action: before setting/changing any pricing tier, run ICP-specific WTP surveys or experiments. Measure outcome proxies (quiz scores, retention, time-to-mastery) and frame price in terms of those outcomes. Don't anchor on competitor prices or token costs as primary inputs.
