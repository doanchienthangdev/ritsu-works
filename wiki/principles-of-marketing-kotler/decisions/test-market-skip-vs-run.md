# When to Run vs Skip Test Marketing

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 587–612, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "When introducing a new product requires a big investment, when the risks are high, or when management is not sure of the product or its marketing program, a company may do a lot of test marketing."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Test market when investment large, risk high, mgmt uncertain. Skip when costs+risks low, mgmt confident, competitive windows closing. Starbucks: 20yr dev + months test for VIA; shipped imperfect mobile app for speed.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "We're willing to innovate and have speed to market trump a 100 percent guarantee that it will be perfect."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu features: ship feature flags for incremental additions (no test market). Run closed-beta test for major repositioning (e.g., adding institutional tier). Don't perfectly polish before shipping — Starbucks mobile lesson applies to AI product velocity.
