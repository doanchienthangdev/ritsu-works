# Actionable: Seed New Product Adoption via Innovators + Opinion-Leader Early Adopters First

> **Source.** [Chapter 5](../chapters/chapter-05-consumer-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 173–174, chunk 9 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "This adopter classification suggests that an innovating firm should research the characteristics of innovators and early adopters in their product categories and direct initial marketing efforts toward them."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Innovators (2.5%) + early adopters (13.5%) are the mechanism by which innovations reach mainstream. Direct initial marketing resources to these segments, especially early adopters who function as opinion leaders.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Early adopters are guided by respect—they are opinion leaders in their communities and adopt new ideas early but carefully."
>
> "New-product marketers must think about how to help consumers move through these stages. If a company finds that many consumers are considering its products but are still tentative about buying one, it might offer sales prices or special promotions that help get consumers over the decision hump."
>
