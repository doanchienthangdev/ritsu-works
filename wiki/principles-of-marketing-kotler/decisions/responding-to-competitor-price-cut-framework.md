# Framework for Responding to Competitor Price Cut

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 1043–1064, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The firm needs to consider several issues: Why did the competitor change the price? Is the price change temporary or permanent? What will happen to the company's market share and profits if it does not respond?"
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 options: (1) hold price, (2) reduce price, (3) raise perceived value, (4) improve quality + raise price, (5) launch fighter brand. Don't match reflexively — diagnose first.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The company might maintain its price but raise the perceived value of its offer."
>

## For Ritsu

If ChatGPT Plus drops to $10/mo or adds free tier: don't reflexively match. Diagnose intent. Response options: (a) hold + reinforce active-learning differentiation; (b) raise perceived value via new outcome metrics ('Ritsu users improve quiz scores 40%'); (c) reposition educator/enterprise tier as 'serious learners' segment. Match only if price war is unavoidable + Ritsu cost structure allows.
