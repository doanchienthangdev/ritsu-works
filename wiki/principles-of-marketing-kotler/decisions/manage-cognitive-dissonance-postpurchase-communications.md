# Actionable: Reduce Postpurchase Dissonance via After-Sale Evidence and Support

> **Source.** [Chapter 5](../chapters/chapter-05-consumer-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 171–172, chunk 8 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "To counter such dissonance, the marketer's after-sale communications should provide evidence and support to help consumers feel good about their brand choices."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Since dissonance is near-universal after major purchases, marketers should proactively send after-sale communications validating decision, providing evidence, surfacing social proof. Convert potentially dissatisfied customers into advocates before bad word-of-mouth spreads.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "A dissatisfied consumer responds differently. Bad word of mouth often travels farther and faster than good word of mouth."
>
> "a company should measure customer satisfaction regularly. It should set up systems that encourage customers to complain. In this way, the company can learn how well it is doing and how it can improve."
>
> "Satisfied customers buy a product again, talk favorably to others about the product, pay less attention to competing brands and advertising, and buy other products from the company."
>
