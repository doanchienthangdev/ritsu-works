# Marketing Control: Evaluating Results and Taking Corrective Action

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 77–77, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Marketers must practice constant marketing control — evaluating results and taking corrective action to ensure that the objectives are attained. Marketing control involves four steps."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ongoing cycle: set specific goals, measure performance, diagnose gaps, take corrective action. Two levels: operating control (check against annual plan) and strategic control (reassess whether strategy still fits opportunity).

## Additional quotes

> "Strategic control involves looking at whether the company's basic strategies are well matched to its opportunities. Marketing strategies and programs can quickly become outdated."
>
