# Position Ritsu as Specialty Product, Not Convenience Tool

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 237–237, chunk 2 (confidence 0.85, auto-accepted).
>
> "Specialty products are consumer products and services with unique characteristics or brand identifications for which a significant group of buyers is willing to make a special purchase effort. Buyers normally do not compare specialty products."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Applying consumer-products classification: convenience positioning = suppresses WTP, commoditizes vs ChatGPT. Specialty positioning = motivated learners seek out, supports premium pricing, exclusive brand identity, lower price sensitivity.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Customer buying behavior [for specialty]: Strong brand preference and loyalty; special purchase effort; little comparison of brands; low price sensitivity."
>

## For Ritsu

Direct implication for pricing page + onboarding copy. Avoid 'easy AI study tool' framing (convenience). Frame as 'the tutor serious learners use' (specialty). Low price sensitivity in specialty = don't race to bottom on freemium limits; premium tier priced confidently.
