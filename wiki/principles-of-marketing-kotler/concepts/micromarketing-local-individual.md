# Micromarketing: Local + Individual-Level Personalization

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 217–219, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Micromarketing is the practice of tailoring products and marketing programs to suit the tastes of specific individuals and local customer segments. Rather than seeing a customer in every individual, micromarketers see the individual in every customer."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Tailoring products/messages to local segments or individual customers — enabled by data, mobile, AI. Most granular targeting end.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "In the extreme, micromarketing becomes individual marketing — tailoring products and marketing to the needs and preferences of individual customers."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's core product IS individual marketing by design — each user's upload creates a personalized tutor. Strong positioning vs Anki/Quizlet (segment-level) and ChatGPT (generic). Marketing should name: 'Not a generic study app. Your document, your tutor, your learning path.'
