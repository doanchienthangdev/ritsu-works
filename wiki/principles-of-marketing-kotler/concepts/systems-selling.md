# Systems Selling (Solutions Selling)

> **Source.** [Chapter 6](../chapters/chapter-06-business-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 185–186, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Many business buyers prefer to buy a complete solution to a problem from a single seller rather than buying separate products and services from several suppliers and putting them together."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

B2B strategy: seller provides complete packaged solution from single source, avoiding need for buyer to assemble multiple vendor components. Wins/retains accounts by reducing buyer coordination burden.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Such systems selling (or solutions selling) is often a key business marketing strategy for winning and holding accounts."
>
> "The sale often goes to the firm that engages business customers deeply and provides the most complete system for meeting a customer's needs and solving its problems."
>

## For Ritsu
