# Disintermediation — Cutting Out Intermediaries

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 353–354, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Disintermediation occurs when product or service producers cut out intermediaries and go directly to final buyers or when radically new types of channel intermediaries displace traditional ones."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

When producers cut intermediaries to go direct, OR new intermediary types displace traditional ones. Examples: iTunes/Amazon displaced music retailers; Spotify disintermediated downloads; Uber/Lyft taxis; Netflix Blockbuster. Two-directional: incumbents must innovate or face displacement.

## Additional quotes

> "Channel innovators who find new ways to add value in the channel can displace traditional resellers and reap the rewards."
>
> "In turn, traditional intermediaries must continue to innovate to avoid being swept aside."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu IS a disintermediator — replacing tutor + flashcard app + quiz platform + note-taking tool with single AI layer. Netflix/Blockbuster parallel: must continuously add activity types + distribution channels before specialized competitor disintermediates a niche (e.g., pure-exam-prep).
