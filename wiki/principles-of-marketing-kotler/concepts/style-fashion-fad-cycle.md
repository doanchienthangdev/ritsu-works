# Style, Fashion, and Fad — Special PLC Variants

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 932–950, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A style is a basic and distinctive mode of expression."
>

## Summary

3 special PLC shapes. Styles recur over generations (multi-peak). Fashions grow slowly + decline slowly. Fads spike rapidly + collapse equally fast.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Fads are temporary periods of intense sales driven by short-term consumer enthusiasm."
>
> "By early May of that year, the gizmos accounted for an amazing 17 percent of daily online toy sales."
>

## For Ritsu

AI tutoring could be a fad (peak hype 2023-25, then commoditizes), fashion (decade-long enterprise adoption), or style (durable category like productivity software). Ritsu's positioning + execution determines which. Active-learning science is durable; 'AI quiz generator' is faddish.
