# Wholesaling — 8 Functions

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 398–403, chunk 7 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Wholesalers are firms that sell goods to those buying for resale or business use."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Wholesalers sell to those buying for resale/business use. 8 functions: selling+promoting, buying+assortment, bulk-breaking, warehousing, transportation, financing, risk-bearing, market info. McKesson ($208B), Arrow ($30B), Grainger ($10.4B). 3 types: merchant wholesalers (take title), brokers/agents (don't), manufacturer branches.

## Additional quotes

> "Wholesalers justify their margin by performing channel functions that manufacturers and retailers cannot perform as efficiently alone."
>
> "Merchant wholesalers take title (and therefore risk); brokers and agents do not."
>

## For Ritsu

Wholesale layer typically not applicable to Ritsu's direct-digital model. Exception: future API/SaaS resellers (white-label Ritsu for tutoring companies) would function as merchant wholesalers — they'd take title, repackage, resell. Per the warning from Ch 12, very long-term commitment with control loss risk.
