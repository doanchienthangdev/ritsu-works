# Direct and Digital Marketing — Definition and Core Model

> **Source.** [Chapter 17](../chapters/chapter-17-direct-online-social-media-mobile.md), book pp. 494–496, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Direct and digital marketing involve engaging directly with carefully targeted individual consumers and customer communities to both obtain an immediate response and build lasting customer relationships."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Engaging directly with carefully targeted individual consumers + customer communities to get immediate response + build lasting relationships. For some companies, a complete business model not just supplemental channel.

## Additional quotes

> "For many companies today, direct and digital marketing are more than just supplementary channels or advertising media — they constitute a complete model for doing business."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu operates as direct-digital-first — no retail intermediaries, freemium funnel, app-based onboarding. 'Complete model for doing business' framing validates Ritsu's D2C approach.
