# Break-Even Analysis

> **Source.** [Chapter 22](../chapters/chapter-22-appendix-2-marketing-by-the-numbers.md), book pp. 619–623, chunk 7 (confidence 0.97, auto-accepted).
>
> "Break-even volume = fixed costs / (price - unit variable cost)"
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Volume needed for profitability given price + cost. Break-even volume = Fixed costs / (Price - Unit variable cost). Break-even $ sales = Fixed costs / Contribution margin. With profit goal: Unit volume = (Fixed costs + Profit goal) / (Price - Variable cost).

## Additional quotes

> "The denominator (price - unit variable cost) is called unit contribution. It represents the amount that each unit contributes to covering fixed costs."
>
> "Break-even sales = fixed costs / contribution margin"
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's freemium has fixed costs (infra, Anthropic baseline, founder salary) + variable per-user cost (tokens). Break-even tells how many paying subs at each price tier cover fixed costs. Foundation of 08-finance/ model.
