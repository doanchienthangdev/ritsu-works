# Market-Centered Company — Balance Customer + Competitor Orientation

> **Source.** [Chapter 18](../chapters/chapter-18-creating-competitive-advantage.md), book pp. 543–544, chunk 9 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A competitor-centered company is one that spends most of its time tracking competitors' moves and market shares."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 orientations: product-oriented, customer-oriented, competitor-oriented, market-oriented. Competitor-centered = too reactive. Customer-centered = identifies emerging opportunities. Market-centered = balanced — watch both, never let competitor-watching blind to customer-focusing.

## Additional quotes

> "A customer-centered company, by contrast, focuses more on customer developments. Clearly, the customer-centered company is in a better position to identify new opportunities."
>
> "In practice, today's companies must be market-centered companies, watching both their customers and their competitors. But they must not let competitor watching blind them to customer focusing."
>
