## Summary

Fastest-growing sales trend. Doesn't replace salespeople — extends reach. 68% of B2B buyers prefer to research independently online; 62% develop selection criteria before contacting rep. Cost: B2B field call $600 vs telemarketing/online $25-75.

## Additional quotes

> "Industrial telemarketing/online contact: $25–75 vs B2B field call ~$600."
