# POES Framework — Paid, Owned, Earned, Shared Media

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 412–414, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Paid media: promotional channels paid for by the marketer."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 media types: Paid (TV/print/digital ads), Owned (website/blog/brand community), Earned (PR pickup, reviews), Shared (consumer-distributed social, viral). Effective IMC leverages all 4 in combination. Single TV ad now seed content moving across O+E+S channels.

## Additional quotes

> "Owned media: promotional channels owned and controlled by the company — company websites, corporate blogs."
>
> "Earned media: PR channels — TV coverage, newspapers, blogs — not directly paid for but featuring content because of viewer interest."
>
> "Shared media: media shared by consumers with other consumers — social media, blogs, mobile, viral channels."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's POES split pre-PMF: 70% owned (SEO content, product as content), 20% earned (founder PR, community AMAs), 10% shared (user testimonials, UGC), 0% paid. As PMF approaches: add paid amplification of best-performing organic content.
