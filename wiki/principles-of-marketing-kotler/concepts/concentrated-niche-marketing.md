# Concentrated (Niche) Marketing: Large Share of Small Segment

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 215–217, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Through concentrated marketing, the firm achieves a strong market position because of its greater knowledge of consumer needs in the niches it serves and the special reputation it acquires."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Niche strategy: deep segment knowledge + strong reputation by focusing on one or few segments rather than spreading thin.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Many companies start as nichers to get a foothold against larger, more resourceful competitors and then grow into broader competitors. For example, Southwest Airlines began by serving intrastate, no-frills commuters in Texas but is now one of the nation's largest airlines."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu fits niche-to-broad arc (Southwest, Amazon) — start with students/self-learners, prove model, expand. Risk: larger competitor entering niche → ChatGPT/Notion AI adding quiz/flashcard features. Depth of segment knowledge = Ritsu's moat.
