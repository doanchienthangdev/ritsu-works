# Treacy-Wiersema Value Disciplines — 3 Paths to Customer Leadership

> **Source.** [Chapter 18](../chapters/chapter-18-creating-competitive-advantage.md), book pp. 749–752, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Companies can pursue any of three strategies — called value disciplines — for delivering superior customer value."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3 disciplines: (1) Operational excellence (best price + convenience — Walmart, IKEA, Trader Joe's). (2) Customer intimacy (precise segmentation + tailoring — Nordstrom, Amazon, Ritz-Carlton). (3) Product leadership (continuous leading-edge products — Apple). Excel at ONE, meet industry standards on other two.

## Additional quotes

> "Most excellent companies focus on and excel at a single value discipline while meeting industry standards on the other two."
>
> "By trying to be good at all value disciplines, a company usually ends up being best at none."
>
