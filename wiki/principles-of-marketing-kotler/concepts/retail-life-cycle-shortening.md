# Retail Life Cycle Shortening

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 394–395, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Of the top 10 U.S. discount retailers in 1962 — the year Walmart, Kmart, Target, and Kohl's each opened their first store — not one still exists today."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

New retail formats reach maturity faster. Dept stores: ~100 years. Warehouse clubs: ~10 years. Online flash sales: <5 years. Of 1962's top 10 discount retailers, NONE survive today. Continuous innovation required.

## Additional quotes

> "Department stores took roughly 100 years to reach the mature stage. Warehouse stores reached maturity in approximately 10 years."
>
