# Service Profit Chain

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 249–251, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Successful service companies focus their attention on both their customers and their employees. They understand the service profit chain, which links service firm profits with employee and customer satisfaction."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

5-link causal model: internal service quality → satisfied/productive employees → greater service value → satisfied/loyal customers → healthy profits + growth.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Internal marketing means that the service firm must orient and motivate its customer-contact employees and supporting service people to work as a team to provide customer satisfaction."
>
> "Customer retention is perhaps the best measure of quality; a service firm's ability to hang onto its customers depends on how consistently it delivers value to them."
>
