# Contribution Margin + Net Marketing Contribution (NMC)

> **Source.** [Chapter 22](../chapters/chapter-22-appendix-2-marketing-by-the-numbers.md), book pp. 622–630, chunk 8 (confidence 0.97, auto-accepted).
>
> "Contribution margin = (price - variable cost) / price"
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Unit contribution = Price - Unit variable cost. Contribution margin % = unit contribution / selling price. NMC measures marketing profitability (only marketing-controlled costs): NMC = Net sales - COGS - Marketing expenses. Marketing ROS = NMC / Net sales. Marketing ROI = NMC / Marketing expenses.

## Additional quotes

> "Net marketing contribution (NMC) measures marketing profitability. It includes only components of profitability that are controlled by marketing."
>
> "NMC = net sales - cost of goods sold - marketing expenses"
>
> "Marketing ROI = net marketing contribution / marketing expenses"
>
