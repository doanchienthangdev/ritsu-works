# Marketing Microenvironment

> **Source.** [Chapter 3](../chapters/chapter-03-analyzing-the-marketing-environment.md), book pp. 86–89, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The microenvironment consists of the actors close to the company that affect its ability to engage and serve its customers—the company, suppliers, marketing intermediaries, customer markets, competitors, and publics."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

The actors close to the company that directly affect its ability to engage and serve customers: internal departments, suppliers, marketing intermediaries, customer markets, competitors, and publics — collectively forming the value delivery network.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Marketing success requires building relationships with other company departments, suppliers, marketing intermediaries, competitors, various publics, and customers, which combine to make up the company's value delivery network."
