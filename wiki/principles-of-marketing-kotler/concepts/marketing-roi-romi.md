# Marketing ROI (ROMI) + Marketing ROS

> **Source.** [Chapter 22](../chapters/chapter-22-appendix-2-marketing-by-the-numbers.md), book pp. 629–630, chunk 10 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Marketing return on sales (or marketing ROS) shows the percent of net sales attributable to the net marketing contribution."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

ROMI = NMC / Marketing expenses (can exceed 100%). Marketing ROS = NMC / Net sales. Both require computing NMC first. Increase NMC + decrease marketing expense for high ROMI. Compare vs prior periods + competitors, not isolation.

## Additional quotes

> "Marketing return on investment measures the marketing productivity of a marketing investment."
>
> "Marketing ROI = net marketing contribution / marketing expenses"
>
