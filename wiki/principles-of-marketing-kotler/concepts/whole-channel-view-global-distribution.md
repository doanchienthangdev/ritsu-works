# Whole-Channel View for Global Distribution

> **Source.** [Chapter 19](../chapters/chapter-19-the-global-marketplace.md), book pp. 572–573, chunk 7 (confidence 0.93, auto-accepted).
>
> "An international company must take a whole-channel view of the problem of distributing products to final consumers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Global distribution = manage channels BETWEEN nations (production→border) + channels WITHIN nations (border→consumer). Infrastructure varies enormously — India kirana shops, China fragmented rural (15% of GDP logistics cost vs 7.5% US), Africa hand-pulled trolleys.

## Additional quotes

> "To compete well internationally, the company must effectively design and manage an entire global value delivery network."
>
> "Distribution systems in developing countries may be scattered, inefficient, or altogether lacking."
>

For Ritsu (digital): 'distribution' = app store presence, payment gateway support, CDN latency per market. Vietnam mobile-first internet + India UPI payments = local digital distribution infrastructure to navigate.
