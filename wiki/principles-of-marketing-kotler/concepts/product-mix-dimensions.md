# Product Mix Dimensions: Width, Length, Depth, Consistency

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 246–247, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A company's product mix has four important dimensions: width, length, depth, and consistency."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 dimensions of company's product lines. Width: # of distinct product lines. Length: total items across lines. Depth: # versions per product. Consistency: how related lines are in end use/production/distribution.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Product mix width refers to the number of different product lines the company carries."
>
> "Product line depth refers to the number of versions offered of each product in the line."
>
> "The consistency of the product mix refers to how closely related the various product lines are in end use, production requirements, distribution channels, or some other way."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu has single product line. Width = 1. Considering educator tools, API access, enterprise tiers → must decide: widen (new lines) or deepen (more activities, more file formats). Consistency risk: educator offering vs individual learner = different distribution + customer job — could dilute brand clarity pre-PMF.
