# Competition-Based Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 304–306, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Competition-based pricing involves setting prices based on competitors' strategies, costs, prices, and market offerings. Consumers will base their judgments of a product's value on the prices that competitors charge for similar products."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Set prices based on competitor strategies, costs, prices, offerings. Consumers judge value RELATIVE to competitors. Goal not to match — set prices according to relative value delivered.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The goal is not to match or beat competitors' prices. Rather, the goal is to set prices according to relative value."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu operates in monopolistic competition (differentiated AI tutors). Don't match ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Notion AI ($10/mo) reflexively. If Ritsu delivers more learning outcome value, price accordingly (Caterpillar lesson: $80K premium = $150K added value). 'No matter what price you charge — be certain to give customers superior value for that price.'
