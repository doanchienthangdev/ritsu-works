# Cause-Related Marketing

> **Source.** [Chapter 3](../chapters/chapter-03-analyzing-the-marketing-environment.md), book pp. 104–106, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Cause-related marketing has become a primary form of corporate giving. It lets companies 'do well by doing good' by linking purchases of the company's products or services with benefiting worthwhile causes or charitable organizations."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Linking purchases or brand identity to worthwhile causes. Distinguished from charity by commercial intent. 64% of global consumers are 'belief-driven' buyers. U.S. spending grew from $120M (1990) to $2.2B (2019).

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "According to one recent study, 64 percent of global consumers are now 'belief-driven' buyers who will choose or avoid a brand based on its stand on societal issues."
>
> "Spending on cause-related marketing in the United States skyrocketed from only 120 million dollars in 1990 to 2.2 billion dollars in 2019."
>
