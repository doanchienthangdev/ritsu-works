# Big Data

> **Source.** [Chapter 4](../chapters/chapter-04-managing-marketing-information.md), book pp. 119–119, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The term big data refers to the huge and complex data sets generated by today's sophisticated information generation, collection, storage, and analysis technologies. Every day, the people and systems of the world generate nearly 2.5 quintillion new bytes of data."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Huge complex data sets generated by sophisticated info technologies. ~2.5 quintillion bytes/day generated globally; 90% of world data created in past 2 years. Opportunities + challenges: marketers don't need more info, they need better info.

## Additional quotes from the chapter
