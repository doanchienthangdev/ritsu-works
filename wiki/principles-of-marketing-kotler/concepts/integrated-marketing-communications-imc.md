# Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC)

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 411–416, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Carefully integrating and coordinating the company's many communications channels to deliver a clear, consistent, and compelling message about the organization and its products."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Carefully integrate all channels so every touchpoint delivers clear/consistent/compelling brand message. Digital fragmentation makes IMC harder + more necessary. Without IMC: confused company images, brand positions, customer relationships.

## Additional quotes

> "Consumers do not distinguish between message sources. A Super Bowl ad, an in-store display, a friend's social media post, and a mobile app notification all merge into a single brand impression."
>

## For Ritsu

For small founder-led Ritsu team, IMC discipline = consistent brand voice across landing page, blog, social, support replies, in-product copy. Risk: as workforce scales (AI subagents producing content), drift in tone or positioning. Style guide + brand voice doc become critical.
