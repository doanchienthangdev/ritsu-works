# Product-Market Expansion Grid (Ansoff Matrix)

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 65–66, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "One useful device for identifying growth opportunities is the product/market expansion grid. We apply it here to Starbucks."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Portfolio-planning tool: 4 growth strategies based on existing vs new products and existing vs new markets — market penetration, market development, product development, diversification. Companies typically exhaust lower-risk quadrants before diversifying.

## Additional quotes

> "Starbucks management might consider whether the company can achieve deeper market penetration — making more sales to current customers without changing its original products."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu near-term growth = market penetration (more activities for existing students) + market development (engineers, researchers, educators). Product development = the 17+ activity type roadmap. Diversification = educator-created course kits sold to institutions — furthest quadrant, post-PMF only.
