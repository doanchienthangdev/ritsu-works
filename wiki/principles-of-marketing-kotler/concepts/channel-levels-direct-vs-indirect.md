# Channel Levels — Direct vs Indirect

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 347–348, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A layer of intermediaries that performs some work in bringing the product and its ownership closer to the final buyer is a channel level."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Channel level = layer of intermediaries performing work in moving product toward final buyer. Direct = no intermediary (producer→consumer). Indirect = one or more intermediaries. More levels = less control + greater complexity, but greater reach.

## Additional quotes

> "Channel 1, a direct marketing channel, has no intermediary levels — the company sells directly to consumers."
>
> "From the producer's point of view, a greater number of levels means less control and greater channel complexity."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu = direct channel (Channel 1): user→ritsu.ai→signup→use. Future LMS integrations (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard) or app-store distribution would introduce intermediary levels — each adds reach but reduces control over pricing, UX, user data.
