# Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Providers

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 368–369, chunk 9 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Third-party logistics (3PL) providers such as Ryder, Penske Logistics, BAX Global, DHL Logistics, FedEx Logistics, and UPS Business Solutions."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Independent logistics provider performing client product-to-market functions. 90% of Fortune 500 now use 3PL (vs 46% in 2001). 3PL market ~$1.5T by 2025. Reasons: (1) 3PLs execute more efficiently at lower cost, (2) outsourcing frees company for core business, (3) 3PLs understand complex logistics.

## Additional quotes

> "90 percent of Fortune 500 companies now use 3PL services, compared to only 46 percent in 2001."
>
> "Companies use third-party logistics providers for several reasons. First, because getting the product to market is their main focus these providers can often do it more efficiently and at lower cost. Second, outsourcing logistics frees a company to focus more intensely on its core business."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's 3PL analogs: cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Vercel for compute+CDN), payment processors (Stripe), AI APIs (Anthropic). Deliberately outsourcing these 'logistics' lets Ritsu focus on core differentiation — AI pedagogy + activity generation engine — vs building infra from scratch.
