# Advertising Objectives: Informative, Persuasive, Reminder

> **Source.** [Chapter 15](../chapters/chapter-15-advertising-and-public-relations.md), book pp. 437–438, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "An advertising objective is a specific communication task to be accomplished with a specific target audience during a specific period of time."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3 types. Informative: build primary demand (new categories). Persuasive: build selective demand + brand preference as competition rises (incl. comparative). Reminder: maintain mature-product relationships. Objectives flow from target/positioning/marketing-mix decisions.

## Additional quotes

> "Informative advertising is used heavily when introducing a new product category. In this case, the objective is to build primary demand."
>
> "Persuasive advertising becomes more important as competition increases. Here, the company's objective is to build selective demand."
>
