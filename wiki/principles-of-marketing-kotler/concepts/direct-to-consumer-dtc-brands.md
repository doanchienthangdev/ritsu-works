# Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Brands

> **Source.** [Chapter 17](../chapters/chapter-17-direct-online-social-media-mobile.md), book pp. 496–497, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands avoid direct competition with established traditional brands by selling and shipping directly to consumers only through online and mobile channels."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

DTC brands avoid traditional competition by selling/shipping directly via online + mobile channels only. Eliminating intermediaries: lower costs, lower prices, greater convenience, deeper relationships, more personalization. Dollar Shave Club, Peloton, Casper.

## Additional quotes

> "By eliminating intermediaries, DTC companies can cut costs and lower prices, offer greater convenience, build direct relationships with customers, and deliver more personalized offerings."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu = structurally DTC EdTech brand. Understanding DTC dynamics (personalization advantage, cost structure, relationship depth) informs Ritsu's GTM vs legacy EdTech intermediaries.
