# Integrated Social Media Marketing

> **Source.** [Chapter 17](../chapters/chapter-17-direct-online-social-media-mobile.md), book pp. 507–508, chunk 7 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "More than making scattered efforts and chasing Likes and retweets, companies that use social media successfully are integrating a broad range of diverse media to create brand-related social sharing, engagement, and customer community."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Beyond scattered posts chasing Likes. Successful companies integrate diverse media for brand-related sharing, engagement, customer community. Starbucks manages dozens of accounts across 5 platforms with dedicated teams, driving both online engagement + in-store/purchase behavior.

## Additional quotes

> "Social media are not just about engaging and telling a story and connecting. They can have a material impact on the business."
