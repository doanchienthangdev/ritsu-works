# Marketing Data Science as Distinct Function

> **Source.** [Chapter 23](../chapters/chapter-23-appendix-3-careers-in-marketing.md), book pp. 646–647, chunk 14 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "A marketing data scientist's job is to analyze marketing data to gain actionable customer insights. Data scientists collect big data sets and apply marketing analytics to discover actionable insights."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Analyzes marketing data for actionable customer insights. Collect big data sets, apply analytics for insights/solutions, share with marketing managers. Required: math, stats, analytical, computer science + marketing strategy understanding. Most have master's+. Bridges quantitative analysis + marketing strategy.

## Additional quotes

> "Being a marketing data scientist often requires math, statistics, analytical, and computer science skills, along with an understanding of marketing strategy."
>
