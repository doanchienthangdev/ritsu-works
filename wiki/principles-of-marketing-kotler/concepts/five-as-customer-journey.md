# Five As — Customer Journey

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 418–419, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The five customer journey stages on the path from awareness of a brand to advocating it to others: awareness, appeal, ask, act, and advocacy."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Maps communication objectives to journey stages: Awareness (I know about), Appeal (I like), Ask (I want to know more), Act (I buy), Advocacy (I tell others). Updated from older AIDA. Integrates post-purchase advocacy dimension.

## Additional quotes

> "The goal is to create content experiences that will keep customers on the path from brand awareness and preference to purchasing the brand and advocating it to others."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu 5As mapping: Awareness (SEO + organic social) → Appeal (landing page demos, peer testimonials) → Ask (free signup) → Act (first quiz completion = activation) → Advocacy (shareable score cards, referral mechanic). Funnel design must hit each stage explicitly.
