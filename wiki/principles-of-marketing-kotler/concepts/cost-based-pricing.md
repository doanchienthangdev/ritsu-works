# Cost-Based Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 301–304, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Cost-based pricing involves setting prices based on the costs of producing, distributing, and selling the product plus a fair rate of return for the company's effort and risk."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Set price based on production+distribution+selling costs plus fair return. Costs set price FLOOR. Methods: cost-plus (markup) and break-even/target-return. Product-driven, not customer-driven — structural weakness vs value-based.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The drop in the average per-unit production cost that comes with accumulated production experience is called the experience curve (or the learning curve)."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu shouldn't anchor on Anthropic API cost. Value-based first, then check cost floor. Use experience curve: as Ritsu's user base + data grow, marginal cost per session decreases. Future model fine-tuning could collapse costs further.
