# Customer-Centered New Product Development

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 668–678, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Customer-centered new product development focuses on finding new ways to solve customer problems and create more customer-satisfying experiences."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

NPD that begins+ends with deep customer understanding rather than internal R&D assumptions. Companies engaging customers in innovation: 2x ROA + 3x growth in operating income.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Companies that directly engage their customers in the new product innovation process had twice the return on assets and triple the growth in operating income of firms that did not."
>
