# Marketing Research Process (4 Steps)

> **Source.** [Chapter 4](../chapters/chapter-04-managing-marketing-information.md), book pp. 123–136, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Marketing research is the systematic design, collection, analysis, and reporting of data relevant to a specific marketing situation facing an organization."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4-step process: (1) define problem and research objectives, (2) develop research plan, (3) implement plan, (4) interpret and report findings. Step 1 is hardest and most important.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The marketing research process has four steps: defining the problem and research objectives, developing the research plan, implementing the research plan, and interpreting and reporting the findings."
>
> "Defining the problem and research objectives is often the hardest step in the research process."
>
