# Social Media Marketing — Engagement Over Interruption

> **Source.** [Chapter 17](../chapters/chapter-17-direct-online-social-media-mobile.md), book pp. 503–507, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Marketers can't simply muscle their way into consumers' digital interactions — they need to earn the right to be there."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

User-controlled, brands must earn presence not intrude. Advantages: targeting, interactivity, immediacy, real-time, cost-effectiveness, engagement/sharing. Biggest advantage: engagement + sharing — getting customers involved with brand + each other.

## Additional quotes

> "Perhaps the biggest advantage of social media is their engagement and social sharing capabilities. Social media are especially well suited to creating customer engagement and community."
>
> "You're going into the consumer's backyard. This is their place."
>
