# SWOT Analysis in Marketing Plan Context

> **Source.** [Chapter 21](../chapters/chapter-21-appendix-1-marketing-plan.md), book pp. 614–616, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "NutriWater has several powerful strengths on which to build, but its major weakness is lack of brand awareness and image."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Identifies internal S+W and external O+T as inputs to objective-setting. NutriWater: Strengths (quality, expertise, social proof, brand positioning); Weaknesses (lack of awareness, limited budget); Opportunities (growth, distribution gaps, trend alignment); Threats (shelf-space competition, image risks). Directly informs objectives.
