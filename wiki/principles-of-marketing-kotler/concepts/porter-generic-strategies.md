# Porter's 4 Generic Competitive Strategies

> **Source.** [Chapter 18](../chapters/chapter-18-creating-competitive-advantage.md), book pp. 534–535, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The three winning strategies are as follows: Overall cost leadership... Differentiation... Focus."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3 winning + 1 losing. (1) Overall cost leadership: lowest costs to price lower. (2) Differentiation: highly differentiated product preferred at acceptable price. (3) Focus: serve few segments exceptionally. (4) Middle-of-the-roaders try to be good at all — end up best at nothing.

## Additional quotes

> "Companies that pursue a clear strategy — one of the above — will likely perform well."
>
> "Middle-of-the-roaders try to be good on all strategic counts but end up being not very good at anything."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu = differentiation (17+ activities, emotional AI, personalized AI tutor) + focus (B2C students/self-learners, not enterprise). MUST avoid middle-of-roader — can't be cheapest (loses to free ChatGPT) or most generic. Active learning mastery = defensible differentiation.
