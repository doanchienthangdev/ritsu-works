# Global Product + Communication: Standardize vs Adapt

> **Source.** [Chapter 19](../chapters/chapter-19-the-global-marketplace.md), book pp. 566–571, chunk 6 (confidence 0.97, auto-accepted).
>
> "Five strategies are used for adapting product and marketing communication strategies to a global market."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

5 strategies. (1) Straight extension (same product, same message). (2) Product adaptation (change product, keep message). (3) Communication adaptation. (4) Dual adaptation. (5) Product invention. 'Think globally, act locally' — balance brand standardization with market-level adaptation.

## Additional quotes

> "Straight product extension means marketing a product in a foreign market without making significant changes to the product."
>
> "Product invention consists of creating something new to meet the needs of consumers in a given country."
>
> "Most international marketers suggest that companies should 'think globally but act locally.'"
>
