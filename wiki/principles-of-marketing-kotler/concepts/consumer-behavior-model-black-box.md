# Model of Consumer Behavior (Stimulus-Response / Black Box)

> **Source.** [Chapter 5](../chapters/chapter-05-consumer-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 152–153, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "This figure shows that marketing and other stimuli enter the consumer's 'black box' and produce certain responses."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Marketing and environmental stimuli enter the consumer's 'black box' (buyer characteristics + decision process), producing responses: brand choice, purchase timing/location, brand engagement. The 'why' inside the box is fundamentally hard to observe.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "the buyer's characteristics influence how he or she perceives and reacts to the stimuli. These characteristics include a variety of cultural, social, personal, and psychological factors. Second, the buyer's decision process itself affects his or her behavior."
>
> "learning about the whys behind consumer buying behavior is not so easy—the answers are often locked deep within the consumer's mind. Often, consumers themselves don't know exactly what influences their purchases."
>

## For Ritsu
