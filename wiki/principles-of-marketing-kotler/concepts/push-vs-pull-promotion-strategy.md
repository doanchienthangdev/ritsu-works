# Push vs Pull Promotion Strategy

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 425–426, chunk 7 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Producer's marketing activities (personal selling, trade promotion) target retailers/wholesalers, who in turn promote to consumers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Push: producer → channel intermediaries → consumer (personal selling, trade promotion). Pull: producer → consumer (advertising, digital, consumer promotion) creates demand pulling product through channel. B2C = pull-heavy. B2B = push-heavy.

## Additional quotes

> "Producer directs activities (advertising, consumer promotion, digital content) at final consumers to induce purchase."
>
> "Business-to-consumer companies usually pull more. Business-to-business marketers tend to push more."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu = pure pull (B2C direct-to-consumer, no channel partners to push through). All promotion energy → end users. Push only applies if Ritsu adds B2B channel (universities, LMS) — different motion, different team.
