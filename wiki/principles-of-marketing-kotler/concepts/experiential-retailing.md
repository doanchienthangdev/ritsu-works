# Experiential Retailing

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 389–397, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Customers today are not necessarily just looking for products. They are also buying experiences, so creating emotional attachment is important."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Store = environment to be experienced, not warehouse. Multi-sensory engagement difficult for online competitors to replicate. adidas Fifth Ave (gait analysis, customization stations), Lowe's Holoroom (VR design), Amazon Go (checkout-free).

## Additional quotes

> "Bloomingdale's varies scent by department. Hard Rock Hotel Orlando's sugar-cookie scent at stairwell top drove 45 percent ice cream sales increase in six months."
>

## For Ritsu
