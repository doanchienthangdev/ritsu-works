# Vertical Marketing Systems (VMS) — Corporate, Contractual, Administered

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 349–351, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A vertical marketing system (VMS) consists of producers, wholesalers, and retailers acting as a unified system. One channel member owns the others, has contracts with them, or wields so much power that they must all cooperate."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

VMS = channel where producers/wholesalers/retailers act as unified system under coordinated leadership. 3 types: corporate (single ownership), contractual (independent firms linked by contracts — franchise), administered (coordination via dominant member's size+power).

## Additional quotes

> "A corporate VMS integrates successive stages of production and distribution under single ownership."
>
> "In an administered VMS, leadership is assumed not through common ownership or contractual ties but through the size and power of one or a few dominant channel members."
>
