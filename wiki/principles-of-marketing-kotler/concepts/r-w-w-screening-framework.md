# R-W-W Idea Screening Framework

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 273–273, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "One marketing expert describes an R-W-W ('real, win, worth doing') new product screening framework that asks three questions."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3-question filter: Is it Real? Can we Win? Is it Worth doing? All three must be 'yes' before proceeding to concept development.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The company should be able to answer yes to all three R-W-W questions before developing the new product idea further."
>

## For Ritsu

Apply to every feature decision: Is the user need REAL? Can Ritsu WIN against ChatGPT/Notion on this feature? Is it WORTH investing pre-PMF resources? E.g., 'social study groups' — real demand, but win vs Discord? Worth pre-PMF? R-W-W is the gate.
