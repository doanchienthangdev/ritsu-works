# Relationship Marketing vs Transaction-Oriented Selling

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 478–479, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Transaction orientation: each stage of the 7-step process is aimed at getting an order."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Transaction = close specific sale. Relationship = build long-term profitable partnership. Companies want value selling (demonstrate superior customer value) not price-cutting. Shift requires transforming salespeople from customer advocates for price cuts INTO company advocates for value.

## Additional quotes

> "Relationship orientation: the goal is mutual profitability over time, not a single close."
>
> "Value selling (Rockwell example): instead of matching competitor's lower price, the rep quantified pump downtime costs and presented a precise ROI of $16,268 savings per pump."
>
> "Salespeople who cut price are being customer advocates at the company's expense."
>
