# The 8-Stage New Product Development Process

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 271–277, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A company must carry out strong new product planning and set up a systematic, customer-driven new product development process for finding and growing new products."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Systematic NPD: (1) idea generation, (2) idea screening, (3) concept dev+testing, (4) marketing strategy dev, (5) business analysis, (6) product development, (7) test marketing, (8) commercialization.
