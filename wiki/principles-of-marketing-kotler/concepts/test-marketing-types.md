# Three Test Marketing Types

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 276–277, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Test marketing gives the marketer experience with marketing a product before going to the great expense of full introduction."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Standard (full realistic launch in select regions), controlled (curated panels of shoppers/stores), simulated (lab/virtual environments). Cost+time trade-offs differ significantly.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Companies can use controlled test markets or simulated test markets."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's lightweight equivalents: simulated = closed beta with 5-10 power users; controlled = invite-only feature flag for top decile of users; standard = full feature flag rollout. A/B testing IS modern test marketing.
