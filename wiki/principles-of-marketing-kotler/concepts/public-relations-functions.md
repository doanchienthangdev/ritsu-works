# Public Relations: Functions, Tools, Lower-Cost Brand Impact

> **Source.** [Chapter 15](../chapters/chapter-15-advertising-and-public-relations.md), book pp. 454–456, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Public relations (PR) consists of activities designed to engage the company's various publics and build good relations with them."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

PR = build relations with multiple publics. Functions: press relations, product publicity, public affairs, lobbying, investor relations, development. Tools: news, special events, written/audiovisual, corporate identity, public service. Can achieve advertising-equivalent impact at fraction of cost.

## Additional quotes

> "Public relations can have a strong impact at a much lower cost than advertising can. Interesting brand stories, events, videos, or other content can be picked up by different media or shared virally by consumers."
>
> "PR pros are an organization's master storytellers. In a word, they do content."
>

## For Ritsu

PR = Ritsu's most cost-effective channel pre-PMF. Press relations (EdTech reporters), product publicity (Product Hunt, Reddit AMAs in r/learnmath/r/medicalschool), public affairs (university study group partnerships) — all near-zero budget.
