# International Trade System: Barriers, WTO, Regional Blocs

> **Source.** [Chapter 19](../chapters/chapter-19-the-global-marketplace.md), book pp. 554–557, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT), established in 1947 and modified in 1994, was designed to promote world trade by reducing tariffs and other international trade barriers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Governments impose tariffs, quotas, exchange controls, nontariff barriers. Countervailing: WTO (164 members), EU (28 countries, 513M consumers), NAFTA/USMCA, CPTPP (11 nations, 495M people). Understanding which treaties apply = prerequisite for market entry.

## Additional quotes

> "The first seven rounds of negotiations reduced the average worldwide tariffs on manufactured goods from 45 percent to just 5 percent."
>

## For Ritsu

Digital EdTech has low physical-trade friction but data-localization rules + regional data-protection treaties (GDPR EU, PDPA SEA) = nontariff barriers. Understanding EU + ASEAN treaty frameworks matters for Ritsu's user acquisition in those regions.
