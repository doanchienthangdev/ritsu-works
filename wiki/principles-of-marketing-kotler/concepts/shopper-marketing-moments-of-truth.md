# Shopper Marketing + Moments of Truth (ZMOT)

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 376–377, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "P&G coined the 'First Moment of Truth': the critical 3-7 seconds a shopper considers a product on a shelf."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

P&G 'First Moment of Truth': 3-7s shopper considers product at shelf. Google's 'Zero Moment of Truth' (ZMOT): the research moment BEFORE store, via mobile. Micro-moments. 90% of consumers have no brand in mind during micro-moments; 73% decide based on most useful brand.

## Additional quotes

> "Google extended this to the 'Zero Moment of Truth' (ZMOT) — the research moment that occurs before a shopper enters a store, primarily via mobile."
>
> "90 percent of consumers have no brand in mind during their micro-moments; 73 percent make a purchase decision based on which brand is most useful during retail research micro-moments."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu: ZMOT = the moment a student searches 'how to study for [exam]' on Google or YouTube. SEO + content marketing must show up here. Micro-moments = 5-min commute practice. Ritsu wins by being the most useful brand during research, not the most-advertised.
