# Market-Skimming Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 318–319, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Many companies that invent new products set high initial prices to skim revenues layer by layer from the market."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Set high initial price for new product to skim revenues layer by layer from segments willing to pay premium. Lower price as product matures. Apple iPhone pattern.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Market skimming makes sense only under certain conditions. First, the product's quality and image must support its higher price, and enough buyers must want the product at that price."
>

## For Ritsu

Not Ritsu's strategy. AI tutor category too price-sensitive for skimming. Skimming requires durable quality+image moat (Apple) — Ritsu doesn't have it yet.
