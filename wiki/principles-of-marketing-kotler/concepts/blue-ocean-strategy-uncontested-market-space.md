# Blue-Ocean Strategy — Create Uncontested Market Space

> **Source.** [Chapter 18](../chapters/chapter-18-creating-competitive-advantage.md), book pp. 559–561, chunk 4 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Called a 'blue-ocean strategy,' the goal is to make competition irrelevant."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Instead of red-ocean competition over shrinking profits, create new market spaces where competition is irrelevant. Value innovation = powerful leaps in value for firm AND buyers, rendering rivals obsolete. Apple iPod/iPhone/App Store, Redbox, Keurig, DTC brands.

## Additional quotes

> "Such strategic moves — termed value innovation — create powerful leaps in value for both the firm and its buyers, creating all-new demand and rendering rivals obsolete."
>
> "Tomorrow's leading companies will succeed not by battling competitors but by creating 'blue oceans' of uncontested market space."
>
