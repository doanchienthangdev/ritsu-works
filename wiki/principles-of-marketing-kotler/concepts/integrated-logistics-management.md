# Integrated Logistics Management

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 367–368, chunk 8 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Integrated logistics management. This concept recognizes that providing better customer service and trimming distribution costs require teamwork."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Better customer service + lower distribution costs require teamwork — internally (across functions) and externally (suppliers/resellers/customers). Individual functions optimize locally creating trade-offs. Goal: harmonize all logistics for high market satisfaction at reasonable cost.

## Additional quotes

> "The goal of integrated supply chain management is to harmonize all of the company's logistics decisions."
>
> "The company must coordinate its logistics, inventory investments, demand forecasting, and marketing activities to create high market satisfaction at a reasonable cost."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu (digital, no physical logistics): integrated content + infra management. AI pipeline (ingestion→activity generation), CDN/edge delivery, billing/growth systems must coordinate. 'Inventory' = preprocessed document vectors; 'delivery' = latency of first activity generation. Cross-functional AI-ops + product + infra = Ritsu's integrated logistics.
