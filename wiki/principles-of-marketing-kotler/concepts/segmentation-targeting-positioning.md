# Segmentation, Targeting, Differentiation, Positioning (STP)

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 69–70, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The process of dividing a market into distinct groups of buyers who have different needs, characteristics, or behaviors and who might require separate marketing strategies or mixes is called market segmentation."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Four-step process for deciding which customers to serve: divide market into segments, evaluate and select most attractive, differentiate offer for superior value, position product in target consumer minds.

## Additional quotes

> "Positioning is arranging for a product to occupy a clear, distinctive, and desirable place relative to competing products in the minds of target consumers."
>
