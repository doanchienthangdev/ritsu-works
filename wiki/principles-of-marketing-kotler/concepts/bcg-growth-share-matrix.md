# BCG Growth-Share Matrix

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 63–64, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Using the now-classic Boston Consulting Group (BCG) approach, a company classifies all its SBUs according to the growth-share matrix. On the vertical axis, market growth rate provides a measure of market attractiveness. On the horizontal axis, relative market share serves as a measure of company strength in the market."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Portfolio-planning tool classifying SBUs on market growth rate (attractiveness) vs relative market share (strength). Four quadrants: Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, Dogs. Invest cash from Cash Cows to build Stars and Question Marks.

## Additional quotes

> "Stars are high-growth, high-share businesses or products. They often need heavy investments to finance their rapid growth. Eventually their growth will slow down, and they will turn into cash cows."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu lens on activity types: quiz/flashcard generation are proven Cash Cows (high usage, low marginal cost); mindmap and code exercises are Question Marks needing investment to become Stars. Freemium tier is a Question Mark — cash-light, high growth potential.
