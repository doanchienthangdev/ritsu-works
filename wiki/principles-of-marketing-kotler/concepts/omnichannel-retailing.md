# Omni-Channel Retailing

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 377–385, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Omni-channel retailing — creating a seamless cross-channel buying experience that integrates in-store, online, and mobile shopping."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Seamless cross-channel buying experience integrating in-store, online, mobile, and social. Eliminates physical/digital boundary. Mobile drives 50%+ of all retail; social influences 58% of purchases. Best Buy + CarMax archetypes.

## Additional quotes

> "Mobile devices now influence more than 40 percent of in-store sales."
>
> "60 percent of shoppers use smartphones while shopping; 54 percent prefer retailers who send in-store special offers."
>
