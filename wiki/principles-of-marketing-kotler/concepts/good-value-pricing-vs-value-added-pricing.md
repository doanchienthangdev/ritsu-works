# Good-Value Pricing vs Value-Added Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 299–300, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Good-value pricing — offering the right combination of quality and good service at a fair price."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

2 sub-types of customer value-based pricing. Good-value = right quality+service at fair (often lower) price (EDLP). Value-added = differentiate with additional features+services to support HIGHER prices.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Value-added pricing strategies. Rather than cutting prices to match competitors, they add quality, services, and value-added features to differentiate their offers and thus support their higher prices."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu freemium = good-value tier (core capability at zero price, quality high enough to genuinely learn). Paid tier = value-added (adaptive modes, emotional AI, unlimited uploads, progress heatmap). Conversion trigger = users hitting value ceiling, not paywalling core function.
