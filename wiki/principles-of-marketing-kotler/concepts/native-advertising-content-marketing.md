# Native Advertising + Content Marketing as Clutter-Busters

> **Source.** [Chapter 15](../chapters/chapter-15-advertising-and-public-relations.md), book pp. 442–443, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Native advertising is advertising or other brand-produced online content that looks in form and function like the other natural content surrounding it on a web or social media platform."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Native ads match form/feel of surrounding content. Bypasses ad blockers. 53% more attention than banner ads. Grew 25%+ YoY to ~2/3 of digital display spending. JWT: 'Advertising needs to stop interrupting what people are interested in and BE what people are interested in.'

## Additional quotes

> "One recent study found that viewers pay 53 percent more attention to native advertising than to banner ads."
>
> "Advertising needs to stop interrupting what people are interested in and be what people are interested in."
>
