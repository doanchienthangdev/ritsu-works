# Marketing Mix — Four Ps vs Four As

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 70–73, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The marketing mix is the set of tactical marketing tools that the firm blends to produce the response it wants in the target market."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion) = seller toolkit. 4 As reframe from buyer's view: Acceptability (exceeds expectations), Affordability (willing+able to pay), Accessibility (ease of acquisition), Awareness (informed, persuaded, reminded). Design 4 As first, then 4 Ps.

## Additional quotes

> "Under this more customer-centered framework, acceptability is the extent to which the product exceeds customer expectations; affordability the extent to which customers are willing and able to pay the product's price; accessibility the extent to which customers can readily acquire the product; and awareness the extent to which customers are informed about the product's features, persuaded to try it, and reminded to repurchase."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu freemium scores high on Affordability (free tier) + Accessibility (web + mobile, no install). Drop-any-file = Acceptability proof. Awareness is weakest A — making growth pillar's SEO and content work the highest-leverage lever.
