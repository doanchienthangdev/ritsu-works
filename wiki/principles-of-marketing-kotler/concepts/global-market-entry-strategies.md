# Global Market Entry: Exporting/Joint Venturing/Direct Investment

> **Source.** [Chapter 19](../chapters/chapter-19-the-global-marketplace.md), book pp. 564–566, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Once a company has decided to sell in a foreign country, it must determine the best mode of entry. Its choices are exporting, joint venturing, and direct investment."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3 modes by risk-return. Exporting (indirect/direct) = least change, lowest investment. Joint venturing (licensing/contract mfg/management contract/joint ownership) = shared risk + local expertise. Direct investment = most control + profit but max exposure.

## Additional quotes

> "Each succeeding strategy involves more commitment and risk but also more control and potential profits."
>
> "Licensing is a simple way for a manufacturer to enter international marketing."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu (digital SaaS, no manufacturing): 'exporting' = direct cross-border subscription sales; 'licensing' = white-label or API partnerships with EdTech platforms in SEA (local LMS providers).
