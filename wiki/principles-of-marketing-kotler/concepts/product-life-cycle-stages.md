# Product Life Cycle (PLC) — 5 Stages

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 867–878, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Figure 9.2 shows a typical product life cycle (PLC), the course that a product's sales and profits take over its lifetime. The PLC has five distinct stages."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Product dev → introduction → growth → maturity → decline. Each stage requires distinct marketing strategies. PLC is descriptive, not deterministic — skilled marketers can defy it via repositioning or reinvigorating.
