# Customer Value-Based Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 297–299, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Customer value-based pricing uses buyers' perceptions of value as the key to pricing. Value-based pricing means that marketers cannot design a product and marketing program and then set the price. They must consider price along with all other marketing mix variables before they set a marketing program."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Setting price based on buyer perceptions of value rather than seller costs. Process: assess customer needs+value → set target price matching perceived value → engineer costs to fit. Reverse of cost-based.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Value-based pricing reverses this process. The company first assesses customer needs and value perceptions. It then sets its target price based on customer perceptions of value. The targeted value and price drive decisions about what costs can be incurred and the resulting product design."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu pricing anchor = customer-perceived value (time saved, exam performance, mastery), not Anthropic API cost. Cost-plus creates floor that moves with model costs + provides no WTP signal. Value-based requires understanding what each ICP persona is willing to pay for proven learning outcomes.
