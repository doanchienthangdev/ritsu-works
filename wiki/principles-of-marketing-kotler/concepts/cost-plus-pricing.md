# Cost-Based Pricing + Price Elasticity

> **Source.** [Chapter 22](../chapters/chapter-22-appendix-2-marketing-by-the-numbers.md), book pp. 619–621, chunk 11 (confidence 0.93, auto-accepted).
>
> "Cost-plus pricing simply adds a standard markup to the cost of the product."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Cost-plus: unit cost + standard markup. Unit cost = Variable cost + Fixed costs/Unit sales. Markup price = Unit cost / (1 - desired return on sales). ROI pricing = Unit cost + (ROI × Investment / Unit sales). Demand elastic when price drop causes proportionally larger volume increase.

## Additional quotes

> "Markup price = unit cost / (1 - desired return on sales)"
>
> "Demand factors set the price ceiling. The company's costs set the price floor."
>
> "Demand for most types of consumer electronics products is elastic — percentage increase in quantity demanded is greater than percentage decrease in price."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu sets subscription prices. Cost-plus = floor (API cost per active user + infra + dev amortized). Value-based ceiling = student/professional WTP. Price-elasticity matters for freemium→paid conversion: trial extension reducing barrier must not collapse contribution below break-even.
