# Market-Oriented Mission Statement

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 59–60, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Mission statements should be market oriented and defined in terms of satisfying basic customer needs. Products and technologies eventually become outdated but basic market needs may last forever."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

A mission statement defines what an organization wants to accomplish, framed in terms of satisfying basic customer needs rather than products or technologies. Market-oriented missions endure because needs outlast specific products.

## Additional quotes

> "A clear mission statement acts as an 'invisible hand' that guides people in the organization."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's mission should be framed around the need to truly understand and master knowledge, not around the PDF-to-quiz pipeline. The charter tagline 'Turn Raw Materials Into True Mastery' is already market-oriented; any internal SOP language should reinforce learning outcomes, not the technology.
