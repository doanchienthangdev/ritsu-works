# Four Targeting Strategies: Undifferentiated, Differentiated, Concentrated, Micromarketing

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 214–216, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Figure 7.2 shows that companies can target very broadly (undifferentiated marketing), very narrowly (micromarketing), or somewhere in between (differentiated or concentrated marketing)."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Targeting breadth ranges from mass marketing → multi-segment differentiation → niche concentration → individual-level micromarketing.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Using a concentrated marketing (or niche marketing) strategy, instead of going after a small share of a large market, a firm goes after a larger share of one or a few smaller segments or niches."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu uses quasi-undifferentiated approach — one product for 6 personas. As PMF accrues, decide: stay undifferentiated (faster to 100 paying) or pivot differentiated (separate onboarding per persona). Concentrated on students first may be fastest to PMF.
