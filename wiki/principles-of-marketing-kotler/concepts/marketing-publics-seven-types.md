# Seven Types of Marketing Publics

> **Source.** [Chapter 3](../chapters/chapter-03-analyzing-the-marketing-environment.md), book pp. 88–90, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A public is any group that has an actual or potential interest in or impact on an organization's ability to achieve its objectives. We can identify seven types of publics."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Any group with actual or potential interest in or impact on company objectives. Seven types: financial, media, government, citizen-action, internal, general, local publics.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Financial publics. This group influences the company's ability to obtain funds."
>
> "Media publics. This group carries news, features, editorial opinions, and other content. It includes television stations, newspapers, magazines, and blogs and other social media."
>
> "Citizen-action publics. A company's marketing decisions may be questioned by consumer organizations, environmental groups, minority groups, and others."
>
