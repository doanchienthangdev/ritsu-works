## Summary

Span advertising (copywriter, art director, account exec, content manager, media planner), brand/product management (brand manager, product manager, market analyst), sales+sales management, marketing research, data science, digital/social media, retailing, new product planning, logistics, PR, not-for-profit. Brand/product mgmt = best training ground for future corporate officers. Sales has more CEO origins than any entry-level function.
