# Consumerism + Environmentalism

> **Source.** [Chapter 20](../chapters/chapter-20-sustainable-marketing.md), book pp. 592–596, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Consumerism is an organized movement of citizens and government agencies to improve the rights and power of buyers in relation to sellers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Consumerism = organized citizen+gov movement to improve buyer rights vs sellers. Beyond traditional rights: to be well informed, protected from questionable practices, influence products, consume sustainably. Environmentalism = protect+improve living environment. 3 generations: pollution prevention → product stewardship → clean tech → sustainability vision.

## Additional quotes

> "Environmentalism is an organized movement of concerned citizens, businesses, and government agencies designed to protect and improve people's current and future living environment."
>
> "The marketing system's goal should not be to maximize consumption, consumer choice, or consumer satisfaction but rather to maximize life quality."
>

## For Ritsu

'Right to be well informed' = directly applies to EdTech. Ritsu users have right to know when AI may be wrong, what data is collected, expected outcomes. Transparency UX (confidence indicators on AI output, clear opt-out) = strategic asset, not legal afterthought.
