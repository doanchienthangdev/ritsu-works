# Intermarket (Cross-Market) Segmentation

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 213–213, chunk 2 (confidence 0.85, auto-accepted).
>
> "Using intermarket segmentation (also called cross-market segmentation), marketers form segments of consumers who have similar needs and buying behaviors even though they are located in different countries."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Targeting segments with shared needs and behaviors across national borders, bypassing geographic constraints on market definition.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Today's technologies let fast-fashion retailer Zara target like-minded style-conscious but value-seeking consumers anywhere in the world they live."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu is inherently cross-market — 'student studying for exams' exists in Vietnam/US/India/Brazil identically. Validates global launch from day 1; marketing content (situational workflows) is globally reusable without geo-specific localization for core ICP.
