# Positioning Statement Template

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 226–226, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Company and brand positioning should be summed up in a positioning statement. The statement should follow the form: To (target segment and need) our (brand) is (concept) that (point of difference)."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Format: 'To [target segment and need] our [brand] is [concept] that [point of difference]' — anchors brand identity in category with clear differentiator.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Note that the positioning statement first states the brand's membership in a category and then shows its point of difference from other members of the category."
>
