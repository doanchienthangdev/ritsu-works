# Brand Equity and Brand Value

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 252–254, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Brand equity is the differential effect that knowing the brand name has on customer response to the product and its marketing. It's a measure of the brand's ability to capture consumer preference and loyalty."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Brand equity = differential effect of knowing brand name on customer response. Measures preference/loyalty beyond unbranded equivalent. Brand value = total financial worth of equity. BrandAsset Valuator: differentiation, relevance, knowledge, esteem.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The fundamental asset underlying brand equity is customer equity — the value of customer relationships that the brand creates."
>
> "Products are created in the factory, but brands are created in the mind."
>
