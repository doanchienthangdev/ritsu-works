# Market-Penetration Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 219–241, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Companies set a low initial price to penetrate the market quickly and deeply — to attract a large number of buyers quickly and win a large market share."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Low initial price to enter market quickly + win large share. Conditions: market price-sensitive, costs fall with volume (scale/learning curve), low price keeps competitors out. Amazon Echo Dot + Prime Video.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The low price must help keep out the competition, and the penetration pricer must maintain its low-price position. Otherwise, the price advantage may be only temporary."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's free tier IS market-penetration pricing at the limit (price = $0). Conditions hold: market price-sensitive (students), costs fall with model improvements, free tier creates switching cost barrier as user uploads accumulate.
