# E-Procurement (Online Purchasing)

> **Source.** [Chapter 6](../chapters/chapter-06-business-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 192–192, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Online purchasing, often called e-procurement, has grown rapidly in recent years. Virtually unknown two decades ago, online purchasing is standard procedure for most companies today."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Purchasing through electronic connections. Methods: reverse auctions, online trading exchanges, company buying sites, extranet links. Benefits: reduced transaction costs, faster order-to-delivery, freed purchasing staff. Risk: internet enables buyers to pit suppliers against each other on price.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Business-to-business e-procurement yields many benefits. First, it shaves transaction costs and results in more efficient purchasing for both buyers and suppliers. E-procurement reduces the time between order and delivery."
>
> "The rapidly expanding use of e-procurement, however, also presents some problems. Many buyers now use the power of the internet to pit suppliers against one another and search out better deals."
>
