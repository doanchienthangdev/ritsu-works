# Markup Pricing + Markup Chains

> **Source.** [Chapter 22](../chapters/chapter-22-appendix-2-marketing-by-the-numbers.md), book pp. 621–622, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Dollar markup = selling price - cost"
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Markup = selling price - cost. % on cost or on selling price. Distribution channels: each layer takes margin = markup chain. To find manufacturer's price: start MSRP, subtract reseller margins working backward. Mfg price = MSRP × (1 - retail margin) × (1 - wholesale margin).

## Additional quotes

> "Markup percentage on selling price = dollar markup / selling price"
>
> "Markup chain: Suggested retail $300, minus retail margin (30%): -$90, Retailer's cost/wholesaler's price: $210, minus wholesaler's margin (20%): -$42, Wholesaler's cost/Mfg price: $168"
>

## For Ritsu

App store distribution takes 30% from Ritsu's revenue. Markup math: $10/mo via App Store = $7 net. Pricing tiers must account for this. Web-direct channels capture more margin — chain analysis quantifies how much.
