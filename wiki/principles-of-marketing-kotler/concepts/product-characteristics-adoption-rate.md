# Product Characteristics Influencing Rate of Adoption

> **Source.** [Chapter 5](../chapters/chapter-05-consumer-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 174–175, chunk 9 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Relative advantage. The degree to which the innovation appears superior to existing products."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

5 characteristics determine adoption speed: relative advantage, compatibility (fit with existing values/practices), complexity (ease of use), divisibility (try on limited basis), communicability (visible/describable results).

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Compatibility. The degree to which the innovation fits the values and experiences of potential consumers."
>
> "Complexity. The degree to which the innovation is difficult to understand or use."
>
> "Divisibility. The degree to which the innovation may be tried on a limited basis."
>
> "Communicability. The degree to which the results of using the innovation can be observed or described to others."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's adoption scorecard: Relative advantage = high (replaces 4+ tools in one upload). Compatibility = MEDIUM RISK (active learning is unfamiliar vs passive highlighting; must be messaged carefully). Complexity = low (30s onboarding). Divisibility = high (free tier, no credit card). Communicability = high (shareable quiz results, progress heatmaps). Weak point is compatibility — must position against passive re-reading habit, not just against Anki.
