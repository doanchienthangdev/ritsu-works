# Media Selection: Reach, Frequency, Impact, Engagement

> **Source.** [Chapter 15](../chapters/chapter-15-advertising-and-public-relations.md), book pp. 447–449, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Reach is a measure of the percentage of people in the target market who are exposed to an ad campaign during a given period of time."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 dimensions. Reach: % target market exposed. Frequency: avg exposures/person. Impact: qualitative value of medium for message. Engagement: depth of consumer connection. Engagement now primary metric. Relevance > raw reach.

## Additional quotes

> "The advertiser also must determine the desired media impact — the qualitative value of message exposure through a given medium."
>
> "The relevance of ad content for its audience is often much more important than how many people it reaches."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu organic strategy: prioritize engagement + impact over reach. Single YouTube video on 'how I used Ritsu to pass orgo' reaching 5K relevant students > 500K display impression broad campaign.
