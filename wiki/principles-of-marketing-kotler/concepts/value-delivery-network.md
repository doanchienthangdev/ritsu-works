# Value Delivery Network

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 344–345, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A value delivery network is made up of the company, suppliers, distributors, and, ultimately, customers who 'partner' with each other to improve the performance of the entire system."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Company + suppliers + distributors + customers partnering to improve performance of entire system. Supersedes 'supply chain' (make-and-sell) and 'demand chain' (sense-and-respond). Companies don't compete — entire networks do.

## Additional quotes

> "Individual companies and brands don't compete; their entire value delivery networks do."
>
> "The term supply chain may be too limited, as it takes a make-and-sell view of the business."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's value delivery network is direct-digital: product team → Ritsu platform → learner. Knowing network boundaries helps product-ops identify which partners (LMS, app stores, content providers) are inside vs outside and what value each adds.
