# Product Line Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 263–295, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The price steps should take into account cost differences between products in the line. More important, they should account for differences in customer perceptions of the value of different features."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Deliberate price steps across product line reflecting cost differences, perceived value differences, competitive positioning. Each tier serves distinct segment. Microsoft Surface ladder ($399-$2999).

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Microsoft's task is to establish perceived value differences that support price differences for different buyers and uses."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's free → Pro → Team/Educator tiers ARE product-line pricing. Steps must feel proportional to value jumps. Each tier should solve meaningfully larger problem for meaningfully different buyer. Microsoft Surface = mental model.
