# Personal vs Non-Personal Communication Channels

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 420–421, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "83% of consumers say a recommendation from a friend or family makes them more likely to purchase."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Personal (face-to-face, phone, chat, WoM) = adaptive feedback, high persuasion. Non-personal (broadcast, print, digital, events, atmospheres) = scale without feedback. WoM bridges: mass media seeds opinion leaders → amplify to peers. 83% of consumers more likely to buy after friend/family rec.

## Additional quotes

> "50% of Americans would choose word of mouth over any other single information source if forced to pick one."
>
> "Buzz marketing: cultivating opinion leaders and getting them to spread product information."
>

## For Ritsu

WoM is Ritsu's #1 acquisition channel. EdTech fits 'risky/expensive-in-time/visible' = perfect WoM conditions. Buzz marketing applies: seed Red Bull-style ambassador program with student opinion leaders (study YouTubers, Discord mods). Cost: near-zero. ROI: highest of any channel.
