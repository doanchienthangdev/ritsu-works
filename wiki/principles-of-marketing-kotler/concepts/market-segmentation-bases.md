# Four Bases for Segmenting Consumer Markets

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 205–208, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "There is no single way to segment a market. A marketer has to try different segmentation variables, alone and in combination, to find the best way to view market structure."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Consumer markets segmented along 4 major variables: geographic, demographic, psychographic, behavioral. Each reveals different facets of buyer identity.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "For consumer marketing, the major segmentation variables are geographic, demographic, psychographic, and behavioral."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's 6 personas (students, self-learners, engineers, researchers, educators, professionals) are implicitly a psychographic+behavioral segmentation. Formalizing with these 4 lenses could sharpen ICP targeting and inform which activity types to surface first per persona.
