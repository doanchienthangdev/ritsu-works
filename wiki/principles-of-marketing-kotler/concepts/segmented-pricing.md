# Segmented Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 441–484, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "In segmented pricing, the company sells a product or service at two or more prices, even though the difference in prices is not based on differences in costs."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Different prices to different segments (customer/product-form/location/time) not based on cost differences. Forms: customer-segment, product-form, location-based, time-based.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Segmented prices should reflect real differences in customers' perceived value."
>

## For Ritsu

Direct Ritsu opportunity. Customer-segment: student discount (with .edu). Time-based: exam-season pricing premium. Product-form: educator plan vs individual. Conditions all hold for Ritsu — segmentable, different elasticities, low segmentation cost, legal.
