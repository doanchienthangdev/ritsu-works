# Distribution Intensity — Intensive, Exclusive, Selective

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 357–358, chunk 7 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Producers of convenience products and common raw materials typically seek intensive distribution."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3 strategies for # of intermediaries per channel level. Intensive: stock in as many outlets as possible (convenience goods, P&G/Coca-Cola). Exclusive: limited dealers with territorial rights (luxury — Breitling). Selective: more than one but fewer than all (STIHL).

## Additional quotes

> "Exclusive distribution is often found in the distribution of luxury brands."
>
> "Selective distribution — the use of more than one but fewer than all of the intermediaries who are willing to carry a company's products."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu freemium web = intensive (anyone with browser). If launching LMS integrations: intensive (all LMS), selective (Canvas + Moodle), or exclusive (one university system) — each changes brand positioning + price leverage differently.
