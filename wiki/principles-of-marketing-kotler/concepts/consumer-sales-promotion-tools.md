# Consumer Sales Promotion Tools (9 Types)

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 481–484, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Samples: trial amounts, free or subsidized. Most effective/most expensive introduction tool."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Samples, coupons, rebates, price packs, premiums, advertising specialties, POP promotions, contests/sweepstakes/games, event marketing. Each different objective: trial, repeat, switch, loyalty. Sampling = most effective + most expensive. Digital coupons = 28x newspaper redemption.

## Additional quotes

> "Coupons: certificates reducing purchase price. Digital coupons redeem at ~8% vs 0.28% for newspaper insert coupons — 28x uplift."
>
> "Event marketing: Red Bull's event engine is the archetype — hundreds of events/year, 2.5B YouTube views, most-viewed U.S. brand 9 of 12 months."
>

## For Ritsu

Direct Ritsu applications: (1) Free tier = sample. (2) Referral promotions = digital coupons (zero cost, high targeting). (3) Exam-season contests = sweepstakes. (4) Founder-led events (webinars, AMAs) = event marketing. Sampling is the #1 mechanic for Ritsu's freemium.
