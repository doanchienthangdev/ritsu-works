# Marketing ROI and Marketing Dashboards

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 77–78, chunk 6 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Marketing ROI is the net return from a marketing investment divided by the costs of the marketing investment. It measures the profits generated by investments in marketing activities."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Marketing ROI = net return from marketing investment ÷ cost. Because intangibles (brand equity, engagement) are hard to monetize, companies also use marketing dashboards — curated performance measures on a single display — and customer-centered metrics (lifetime value, retention, customer equity).

## Additional quotes

> "Many companies are assembling such measures into marketing dashboards — meaningful sets of marketing performance measures in a single display used to monitor strategic marketing performance."
>
