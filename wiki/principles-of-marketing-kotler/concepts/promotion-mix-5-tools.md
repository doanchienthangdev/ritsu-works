# The Promotion Mix — 5 Tools

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 410–411, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "the specific blend of advertising, public relations, personal selling, sales promotion, and direct and digital marketing tools that the company uses to engage consumers, persuasively communicate customer value, and build customer relationships."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

5 communication tools: advertising (paid, nonpersonal, high reach), sales promotion (short-term incentives), personal selling (highest persuasion per contact), public relations (credibility-building, news framing), direct/digital marketing (targeted, interactive, immediate).

## Additional quotes

> "The product's design, its price, the shape and color of its package, and the stores that sell it — all communicate something to buyers."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu pre-PMF promotion mix: heavy direct/digital (SEO, content, social), zero personal selling (B2C self-serve), zero sales promotion (no discount culture), heavy PR (founder-led), zero traditional advertising. Aligns with structural constraints.
