# Marketing Channel Functions — How Intermediaries Add Value

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 346–347, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Producers use intermediaries because they create greater efficiency in making goods available to target markets."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Channel members add value via 8 functions: info, promotion, contact, matching, negotiation (transaction-completing) + physical distribution, financing, risk-taking (transaction-fulfilling). Functions must be performed by someone; assign to member adding most value per cost.

## Additional quotes

> "The question is not whether these functions need to be performed — they must be — but rather who will perform them."
>
> "In making products and services available to consumers, channel members add value by bridging the major time, place, and possession gaps that separate goods and services from those who use them."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu (digital direct) performs all 8 functions itself: info (matching docs to paths), promotion (onboarding), contact (SEO/ads), matching (AI-generated activities to user level), negotiation (pricing tiers), distribution (instant digital), financing (freemium), risk-taking (infra). Clarifies which functions LMS or app store partners would absorb + what margin they'd demand.
