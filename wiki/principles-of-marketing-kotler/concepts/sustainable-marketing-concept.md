# Sustainable Marketing

> **Source.** [Chapter 20](../chapters/chapter-20-sustainable-marketing.md), book pp. 582–584, chunk 2 (confidence 0.97, auto-accepted).
>
> "Sustainable marketing calls for socially and environmentally responsible actions that meet the present needs of consumers and businesses while also preserving or enhancing the ability of future generations to meet their needs."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Socially + environmentally responsible actions meeting present consumer/business needs while preserving future generations' ability to meet needs. Beyond marketing concept (current needs) + societal marketing concept (future welfare). McDonald's arc from fat-laden Happy Meals → salads + grilled chicken = central case.

## Additional quotes

> "Truly sustainable marketing requires a smooth-functioning marketing system in which consumers, companies, public policy makers, and others work together."
>
