# Sales Force Structure: Territorial/Product/Customer/Complex

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 467–468, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Territorial: each rep owns an exclusive geographic area, sells the full product line."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 structures. Territorial: rep owns geographic area, full product line. Product: reps specialize by product (GE aviation/healthcare/etc). Customer: reps specialize by industry/customer type (P&G Walmart team in Bentonville). Complex: combinations. No universal best — fit company's customers + strategy.

## Additional quotes

> "Product: reps specialize by product line. GE has separate sales forces for aviation, power, healthcare imaging, life sciences."
>
> "Customer (market): reps specialize by industry or customer type. P&G Walmart CBD team (200+ people embedded in Bentonville)."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu's hypothetical future B2B sales force: customer structure (educator vertical vs corporate L&D vs platform partners) likely fits better than territorial — Ritsu's institutional clients don't cluster geographically.
