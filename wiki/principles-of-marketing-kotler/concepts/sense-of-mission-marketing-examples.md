# Sense-of-Mission Marketing

> **Source.** [Chapter 20](../chapters/chapter-20-sustainable-marketing.md), book pp. 597–603, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Sense-of-mission marketing means that the company should define its mission in broad social terms rather than narrow product terms. When a company defines a social mission, employees feel better about their work and have a clearer sense of direction."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Define company mission in broad social terms, not narrow product terms. Pedigree (not dog food but 'loving + caring for dogs'). TOMS buy-one-give-one. CVS Health dropped tobacco ($2B revenue sacrifice), revenues grew 10% next 2 years. Purpose-driven missions can drive revenue.
