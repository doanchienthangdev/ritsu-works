# Four Service Characteristics: Intangibility, Inseparability, Variability, Perishability

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 247–249, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A company must consider four special service characteristics when designing marketing programs: intangibility, inseparability, variability, and perishability."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Services differ from goods: intangible (can't see/taste before purchase), inseparable (produced+consumed simultaneously), variable (depends on who/when/where), perishable (can't be stored).

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Service intangibility means that services cannot be seen, tasted, felt, heard, or smelled before they are bought."
>
> "Service variability means that the quality of services depends on who provides them as well as when, where, and how they are provided."
>
> "Service perishability means that services cannot be stored for later sale or use."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu is pure service. Intangibility response: product screenshots, sample quizzes, free tier, '30 seconds' promise tangibilize the offering. Inseparability: AI tutor quality IS the service — weak generation = service failing. Variability: LLM output varies by doc quality + model state — needs guardrails. Perishability: session not started = revenue lost, hence freemium onboarding urgency.
