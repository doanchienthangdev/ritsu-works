# Marketing Plan Structure + Purpose

> **Source.** [Chapter 21](../chapters/chapter-21-appendix-1-marketing-plan.md), book pp. 609–610, chunk 1 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A marketing plan has a more limited scope. It serves to document how the organization's strategic objectives will be achieved through specific marketing strategies and tactics, with the customer as the starting point."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Marketing plan documents how strategic objectives achieved through specific marketing strategies/tactics with customer as starting point. Scope limited vs business plan. Standard sections: executive summary, current situation (market/product/competitive/channels review), SWOT, objectives+issues, marketing strategy, action programs, budgets, controls.

## Additional quotes

> "For effective implementation and control, the marketing plan should define how progress toward objectives will be measured."
>
