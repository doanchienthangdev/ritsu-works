# Brand Development: Line Extension, Brand Extension, Multibrands, New Brands

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 259–261, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Line extensions occur when a company extends existing brand names to new forms, colors, sizes, ingredients, or flavors of an existing product category."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 options on 2x2: existing/new brand name × existing/new category. Line extension = same brand, same category, new variant. Brand extension = same brand, new category. Multibrand = new brand, same category. New brand = both new.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "A brand extension extends a current brand name to new or modified products in a new category."
>
> "Multibranding offers a way to establish different features that appeal to different customer segments, lock up more reseller shelf space, and capture a larger market share."
>
> "A company might believe that the power of its existing brand name is waning so a new brand name is needed."
>
