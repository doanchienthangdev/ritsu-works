# The 7-Stage Personal Selling Process

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 476–479, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The selling process consists of seven stages."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Prospecting+Qualifying → Preapproach → Approach → Presentation+Demonstration → Handling Objections → Closing → Follow-Up. Each stage transaction-oriented but must be understood inside broader context of building long-term profitable customer relationships.
