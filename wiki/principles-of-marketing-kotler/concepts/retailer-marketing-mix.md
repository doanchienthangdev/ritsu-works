# Retailer Marketing Mix

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 386–392, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Retailers face the same marketing mix decisions as any other marketer, applied to the retail context."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 decisions: product+services assortment (narrow targeting, exclusive merchandise, atmosphere), price (EDLP vs high-low), promotion (personalization), place (location aligned to positioning). Together create value proposition for target segment.

## Additional quotes

> "Strategies include: (a) highly targeted narrow assortment; (b) exclusive merchandise."
>
> "Specialty stores pursue high markup / low volume; mass merchants pursue low markup / high volume."
>
