# Behavioral Segmentation: Occasions, Benefits, User Status, Usage Rate, Loyalty

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 208–210, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Behavioral segmentation divides buyers into segments based on their knowledge, attitudes, uses, or responses to a product. Many marketers believe that behavior variables are the best starting point for building market segments."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Most predictive segmentation: knowledge, attitudes, usage patterns, loyalty. Specifically occasions, benefits sought, user status, usage rate, loyalty.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Benefit segmentation requires finding the major benefits people look for in a product class, the kinds of people who look for each benefit, and the major brands that deliver each benefit."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's freemium funnel maps to user status (nonuser → free → paid) + usage rate (light vs heavy). Benefit segmentation reveals why engineers want code exercises while students want flashcards — same upload, different benefit. Loyalty segmentation identifies power users to turn into brand advocates.
