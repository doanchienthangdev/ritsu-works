# Marketing Plan Controls + Performance Standards

> **Source.** [Chapter 21](../chapters/chapter-21-appendix-1-marketing-plan.md), book pp. 610–618, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Managers typically use budgets, schedules, and performance standards for monitoring and evaluating results."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Define how progress monitored. (1) Budgets compare planned vs actual by period. (2) Schedules track on-time completion. (3) Performance standards: market share, sales volume, profitability, customer satisfaction. Contingency plans for fast environmental changes. Monthly sales by segment + channel + monthly expenses = early-warning signals.

## Additional quotes

> "Some examples of performance standards are market share, sales volume, product profitability, and customer satisfaction."
>
> "Other early warning signals that will be monitored for signs of deviation from the plan include monthly sales (by segment and channel) and monthly expenses."
>
