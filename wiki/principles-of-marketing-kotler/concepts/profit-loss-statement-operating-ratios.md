# Pro Forma P&L + Operating Ratios

> **Source.** [Chapter 22](../chapters/chapter-22-appendix-2-marketing-by-the-numbers.md), book pp. 625–629, chunk 9 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "A pro forma profit-and-loss statement shows projected revenues less budgeted expenses."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Projected P&L = revenues less budgeted expenses → net profit for planning period. Line items: Net sales, COGS, Gross margin, Marketing expenses, G&A, Net profit before taxes. Operating ratios: Gross margin %, Net profit %, Operating expense %. Inventory turnover. ROI = Net profit / Investment.

## Additional quotes

> "Gross margin percentage = gross margin / net sales"
>
> "Net profit percentage = net profit / net sales"
>
> "Return on investment = net profit before taxes / investment"
>
