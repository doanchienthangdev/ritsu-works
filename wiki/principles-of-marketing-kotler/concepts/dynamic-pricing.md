# Dynamic and Personalized Pricing

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 685–720, chunk 4 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Dynamic pricing — adjusting prices continually to meet changing conditions and situations in the marketplace."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Continuously adjust prices in real-time for changing conditions, demand, competitors, individual customers. Amazon: 80M item-price changes/day → 14% below competitors avg. Risk: consumer trust loss when unfair.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Dynamic and personalized pricing make sense in many contexts — they adjust prices according to market forces and consumer situations. However, done poorly, they can trigger margin-eroding price wars and damage customer relationships and trust."
>
