# Consumer Products: Convenience / Shopping / Specialty / Unsought

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 236–237, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Convenience products are consumer products and services that customers usually buy frequently, immediately, and with minimal comparison and buying effort."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

4 categories by purchase behavior. Convenience: frequent, minimal effort. Shopping: deliberate comparison. Specialty: unique brand ID, special purchase effort. Unsought: unknown/unconsidered until marketed.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Shopping products are less frequently purchased consumer products and services that customers compare carefully on suitability, quality, price, and style."
>
> "Specialty products are consumer products and services with unique characteristics or brand identifications for which a significant group of buyers is willing to make a special purchase effort."
>
> "Unsought products are consumer products that a consumer either does not know about or knows about but does not normally consider buying."
>
