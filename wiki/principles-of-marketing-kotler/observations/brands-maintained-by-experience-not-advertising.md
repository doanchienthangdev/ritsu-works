# Brands Built Through Customer Experience, Not Advertising

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 261–262, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "The fact is that brands are not maintained by advertising but by customers' engagement with brands and customers' brand experiences. Today, customers come to know a brand through a wide range of contacts and touch points along the customer journey."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Advertising creates awareness/preference. Brand equity sustained by cumulative quality of customer interactions across all touchpoints (use, WoM, social, apps, service). 'Thousand small gestures' model.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "A brand is a living entity, and it is enriched or undermined cumulatively over time, the product of a thousand small gestures."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu has no ad budget yet — actually an advantage. Every dollar into product quality = durable brand-builder. 'Delight on first use' IS marketing spend. 30-second setup, quality of first quiz, emotional AI persona moment = brand touchpoints Ritsu controls today.
