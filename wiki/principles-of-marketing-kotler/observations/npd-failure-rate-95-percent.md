# 95% of New Products Fail

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 207–218, chunk 1 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Of the more than 30,000 new products introduced every year, 95 percent fail."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

By one estimate, 95% of 30,000+ new products launched annually fail. Causes: overestimated market, poor design, wrong positioning, bad timing, mispriced, inadequate ads, exec bias, cost overruns, stronger competition.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "A company must understand its consumers, markets, and competitors and develop products that deliver superior value to customers."
>
