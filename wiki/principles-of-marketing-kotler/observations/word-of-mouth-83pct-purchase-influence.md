# WoM Is #1 Purchase Influencer — 83% of Consumers

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 420–420, chunk 5 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "83 percent of consumers said that a recommendation from a friend or family member makes them more likely to purchase a product or service."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

83% of consumers worldwide more likely to buy after friend/family rec. 50% of Americans would choose WoM over any other source. Strongest for expensive/risky/visible products — EdTech fits perfectly.

## Additional quotes

> "Fifty percent of Americans would choose online or offline word of mouth if they had to pick one source of information."
>
> "Few consumers buy a big-ticket item before checking out what existing users have to say."
>

## For Ritsu

WoM = highest-ROI channel for Ritsu. EdTech is risky (uncertain outcomes), expensive in time, visible in peer groups. Invest in referral mechanics, shareable quiz results, study group features, ambassador program. Single student saying 'I aced my exam thanks to Ritsu' = more valuable than 1000 ad impressions.
