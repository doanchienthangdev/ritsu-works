# PR Consistently Outperforms Paid Advertising on ROI

> **Source.** [Chapter 15](../chapters/chapter-15-advertising-and-public-relations.md), book pp. 1332–1392, chunk 5 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "A simple statue with a big message, backed by an imaginative PR campaign, has had a larger and more lasting impact than even the most memorable Super Bowl ad, probably at a lower cost."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

State Street Fearless Girl: 10B impressions across 6 continents from statue + PR. CVS Quits for Good: 218M media impressions + 9.2% stock jump from one principled business decision. Framework: PR earns attention; advertising buys it. Structural advantage for cash-constrained orgs.

## Additional quotes

> "PR professionals have an edge because they have always had to earn attention, while [ad people] have bought attention."
>
> "Public relations can have a strong impact at a much lower cost than advertising can."
>

## For Ritsu

Directly validates Ritsu's pre-PMF strategy: no paid ads, focus on PR. Ritsu needs genuinely newsworthy moments (feature ship as learning-science story, university partnership, '10K student session study') that earn media pickup without buying placement.
