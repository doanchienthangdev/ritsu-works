# Channel Conflict Is Inevitable + Must Be Managed Actively

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 348–349, chunk 5 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Disagreements over goals, roles, and rewards generate channel conflict."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Disagreements among members on goals/roles/rewards. Horizontal = among firms at same channel level. Vertical = between channel levels. Some conflict healthy; severe/prolonged disrupts effectiveness. McDonald's franchisee satisfaction 1.81/5.0 illustrates real cost.

## Additional quotes

> "Horizontal conflict occurs among firms at the same level of the channel."
>
> "Vertical conflict, conflict between different levels of the same channel, is even more common."
>
> "Severe or prolonged conflict can disrupt channel effectiveness and cause lasting harm to channel relationships."
>

## For Ritsu

If Ritsu adds LMS integrations or white-label partnerships, vertical conflict = real risk (LMS partner wanting exclusive data, demanding pricing control). McDonald's franchisee case = cautionary pattern. Pre-negotiate economic alignment (revenue share, data rights, pricing authority) BEFORE conflict emerges.
