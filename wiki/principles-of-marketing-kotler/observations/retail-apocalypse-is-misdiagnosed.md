# 'Retail Apocalypse' Misdiagnosed: Weak Retailers Die, Experience Retailers Thrive

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 387–395, chunk 6 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "a never-ending stream of deep discounts damaged margins; cost-cutting eliminated knowledgeable sales staff."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Mass closure narrative conflates category failure with format failure. Ulta Beauty doubled revenues + grew 874→~1,200 stores while Radio Shack/Sports Authority/Toys R Us died. Retailers competing on assortment+price (Amazon territory) lose; experience+expertise+exclusivity retailers grow. Macy's 'death by discount.'

## Additional quotes

> "Best Buy embraced showrooming: matched Amazon prices to neutralize price as a factor, then competed on in-store experience, expert associates, integrated omni-channel services. Sales grew 7 percent in the survey year; holiday season grew 9 percent, the best in 15 years."
>
