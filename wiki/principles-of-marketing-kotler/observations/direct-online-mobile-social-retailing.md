# Direct/Online/Mobile/Social Channels Fragment Purchase Journey + Outgrow Retail

## Summary

Online retail growth 16.5% vs overall 5%. 46% holiday online purchases on mobile. 58% of purchase decisions influenced by social media. 30% made purchases via social. Purchase journey starts online, may pass through social, closes either online or in-store.

## Additional quotes

> "58 percent of shoppers report social media influences their purchase decisions."
>

## For Ritsu

For B2C EdTech, social discovery (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube shorts) = top-of-funnel channel competitive with paid search. Mobile-first design of purchase flows not optional — mobile initiates majority of retail purchase journeys. Grocery's 20%-online by 2025 = even low-consideration categories migrate digital.
