# Price Is the Only Marketing Mix Element That Generates Revenue

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 297–297, chunk 2 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Price is the only element in the marketing mix that produces revenue; all other elements represent costs. Price is also one of the most flexible marketing mix elements."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Among 4 Ps, price is unique — only one producing revenue. Product/place/promotion = costs. Small % improvement in price → disproportionate profitability improvement. High leverage, under-attended.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "A small percentage improvement in price can generate a large percentage increase in profitability."
>
