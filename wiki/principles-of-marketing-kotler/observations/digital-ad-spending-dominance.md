# Digital Ad Spending >50% of All U.S. Ad Spending

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 287–288, chunk 2 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Total digital ad spending now captures more than 50 percent of all U.S. ad spending and will grow to an estimated 67 percent of total ad spending by 2023."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

By 2021 digital >50% of all U.S. ad spending; 67% by 2023 projected. Mobile alone exceeds total TV. Unilever 40% of $9B on digital. Adidas abandoned TV entirely.
