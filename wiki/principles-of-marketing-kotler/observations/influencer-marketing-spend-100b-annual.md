# Influencer Marketing Annual Spend Exceeds $100 Billion

> **Source.** [Chapter 5](../chapters/chapter-05-consumer-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 158–158, chunk 5 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Influencer marketing is hot these days—companies are spending nearly 100 billion dollars annually on influencer campaigns. Brands love influencer marketing because it builds on existing relationships between influencers and their followers. In a digital media world crammed with clutter, influencer marketing seems less intrusive than many other forms of marketing content at the same time that it's less likely to be ignored."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Companies spend ~$100B annually on influencer campaigns. Brands favor influencer marketing for trust/intrusion benefits in cluttered digital environments.

## For Ritsu

EdTech influencer marketing (study-with-me YouTubers, productivity TikTokers) is a direct channel to Ritsu's student ICP. $100B figure underscores this is proven, not experimental. Budget should favor micro-influencers with authentic study audiences over celebrity mega-influencers.
