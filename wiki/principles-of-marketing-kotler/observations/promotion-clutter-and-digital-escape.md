# Promotion Clutter Erodes Effectiveness — Digital Personalization = Escape

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 480–482, chunk 5 (confidence 0.85, auto-accepted).
>
> "The heavy use of sales promotion has resulted in promotion clutter."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Heavy promotion creates 'promotion clutter' — given promotion gets lost. Escape: digital + mobile with individual targeting. Digital coupons redeem 28x newspaper. Total coupon volume dropped 11.7% while redemption held steady — fewer, better-targeted > volume blasting.

## Additional quotes

> "Digital coupon redemption: traditional insert = 0.28%; digital/print-at-home = ~8%."
>

## For Ritsu

Any Ritsu promotional outreach (upgrade nudges, referral offers, re-engagement emails) should be behaviorally triggered (just-completed quiz, just-hit streak milestone, just-uploaded second doc) — NOT batch-blasted. Behavioral triggers = Ritsu's digital coupons.
