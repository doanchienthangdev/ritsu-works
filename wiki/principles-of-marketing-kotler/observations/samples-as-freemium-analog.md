# Sampling = Closest Physical Analog to Digital Freemium

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 481–481, chunk 5 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Sampling is the most effective — but most expensive — way to introduce a new product."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Kotler: 'most effective — but most expensive — way to introduce new product.' 7-Eleven Free Slurpee Day (9M units) + 7-day follow-on loyalty deals = sample drives acquisition, loyalty mechanic drives upgrade. Maps to freemium-to-paid funnel.

## Additional quotes

> "7-Eleven Free Slurpee Day = 9 million units. Immediately followed by 7Rewards 'seven deals in seven days' — restricts follow-on value to loyalty members."
>
> "Sampling works best when sample delivers enough value to demonstrate full product's benefit, but leaves visible gap that paid tier closes."
>
