# Service Recovery Can Exceed Original Satisfaction in Driving Loyalty

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 251–252, chunk 4 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Good service recovery can turn angry customers into loyal ones. In fact, good recovery can win more customer purchasing and loyalty than if things had gone well in the first place."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Marriott study: guests whose complaint was resolved showed higher return intent (94%) than guests with no problem (89%). Unresolved complaints: 69% intent. Successful recovery > problem-free experience.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The percentages of these three groups that intended to return were 89 percent, 94 percent, and 69 percent, respectively. Thus, the best outcomes were those where Marriott turned a negative into a positive."
>
