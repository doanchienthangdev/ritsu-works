# Team-Based NPD Outpaces Sequential Development

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 733–745, chunk 4 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Under this approach, company departments work closely together in cross-functional teams, overlapping the steps in the product development process to save time and increase effectiveness."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Cross-functional teams overlapping NPD stages simultaneously outperform sequential dept handoffs on speed + effectiveness in fast-changing markets. Customer-centered + team-based = major competitive edge.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "In rapidly changing industries facing increasingly shorter product life cycles, the rewards of fast and flexible product development far exceed the risks."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's small founder-led team already cross-functional by default. As workforce scales (more roles), maintain team-based discipline via the AI workforce pillar architecture — pillar-orchestrators own their domain end-to-end, no sequential handoffs across pillars.
