# Consumer-Generated Content Outruns Brand Content at Scale

> **Source.** [Chapter 17](../chapters/chapter-17-direct-online-social-media-mobile.md), book pp. 493–494, chunk 1 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Coca-Cola estimates that of the hundreds of millions of views of Coca-Cola-related content on YouTube each year, only about 18 percent are from content created by Coca-Cola. The other 82 percent are from content created by engaged consumers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Coca-Cola: only 18% of hundreds of millions of YouTube views from own content — 82% from engaged consumers. Empowered consumers generate more brand content than companies. Goal: 'expressions' (comments/shares/uploads/advocacy) not just impressions.

## Additional quotes

> "In addition to creating impressions, Coca-Cola now seeks to create consumer 'expressions' — brand-related exchanges and responses such as comments, retweets, photo uploads, advocating the brand, and sharing brand content."
>
