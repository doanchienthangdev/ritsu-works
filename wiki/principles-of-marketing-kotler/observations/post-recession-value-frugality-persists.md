# Post-Recession Value Frugality as Durable Consumer Norm

## Summary

Even post-recession recovery, consumers retain 'back-to-basics sensibility' — seeking value, practicality, durability rather than free-spending. Each generation carries its own financial burden reinforcing frugality.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "In today's post-recession era, consumer spending is again on the rise. However, even as the economy has strengthened, rather than reverting to their old free-spending ways, Americans are retaining an enthusiasm for frugality."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's freemium model is structurally aligned with durable frugality norm. 'Free forever' tier reduces activation barrier. For paid conversion, Ritsu must demonstrate clear value-per-dollar vs Anki Premium/Quizlet Plus/tutoring — not luxury positioning. Millennial educators and Gen X professionals with student debt are especially value-sensitive.
