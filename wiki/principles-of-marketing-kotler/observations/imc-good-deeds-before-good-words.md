# Good Comms Can't Save Bad Product — 'Good Deeds Before Good Words'

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 418–420, chunk 4 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Marketing communications alone cannot create positive engagement, preference, and purchases."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Outstanding comms can ACCELERATE demise of poor product. The faster buyers learn about a poor product, the faster they become aware of its faults. Product quality is prerequisite; communication amplifies whatever reality exists.

## Additional quotes

> "Outstanding marketing communications can actually speed the demise of a poor product. The more quickly potential buyers learn about a poor product, the more quickly they become aware of its faults."
>
> "Good marketing communications call for good deeds followed by good words."
>
