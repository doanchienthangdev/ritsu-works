# Customer Value Analysis as Key to Competitive Advantage

> **Source.** [Chapter 18](../chapters/chapter-18-creating-competitive-advantage.md), book pp. 531–532, chunk 4 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "The aim of customer value analysis is to determine the benefits that target customers value and how customers rate the relative values of various competitors' offers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Customer value analysis determines benefits customers value + rates competitors' offers. Identify major attributes, assess own performance vs competitors, find where you deliver greater value than rivals. Competitive advantage = performing better on dimensions customers actually care about.

## Additional quotes

> "The key to gaining competitive advantage is to examine how a company's offer compares to that of its major competitors in each customer segment."
>
