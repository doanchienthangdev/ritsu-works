# Store Brand Growth Driven by Experience, Not Just Price

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 256–258, chunk 5 (confidence 0.85, auto-accepted).
>
> "Gone are the days when store brands were little more than cheap, no-name knockoffs. Today's store brands often equal national brands in quality, and they are backed by retailers with reputations that match or exceed those of national brand manufacturers."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Store brands (private labels) moved beyond 'cheap generic'. Trader Joe's, Target, Amazon built billion-dollar private brands by controlling end-to-end experience + leveraging direct customer relationships national brands can't access.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "When it comes to marketing their brands, retailers have another big advantage over name-brand marketers — direct customer access and control over the customer experience."
>
