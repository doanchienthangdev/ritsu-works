# Strategic Planning Decentralizing to Cross-Functional Teams

> **Source.** [Chapter 2](../chapters/chapter-02-company-and-marketing-strategy.md), book pp. 64–65, chunk 3 (confidence 0.95, auto-accepted).
>
> "Unlike former strategic planning efforts that rested mostly in the hands of senior managers at company headquarters, today's strategic planning has been decentralized."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Formal portfolio methods (BCG matrix) increasingly abandoned for customized approaches by cross-functional divisional teams close to markets. Digital age gives rich real-time data; centralized headquarter planning becomes a competitive disadvantage.

## Additional quotes

> "In this digital age, such managers have rich and current data at their fingertips and can adapt their plans quickly to meet changing conditions and events in their markets."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu's AI workforce architecture mirrors this: instead of single GPS orchestrator planning everything, pillar-specific roles (product-orchestrator, gtm-orchestrator, customer-lead) own their planning loops close to domain data. Operationally sound per Kotler — maintain as workforce scales.
