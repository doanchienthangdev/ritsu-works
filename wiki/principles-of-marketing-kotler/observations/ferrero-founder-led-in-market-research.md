# Ferrero: Founder-Led In-Market Research Outperformed Outsourced Research

> **Source.** [Chapter 4](../chapters/chapter-04-managing-marketing-information.md), book pp. 117–118, chunk 1 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Ferrero did not hire any market research firm when it was test-marketing Rocher but decided to approach the market on its own to better understand the Indian customer."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ferrero refused to hire market research firms in India test-marketing Rocher; instead internal customer insights team + management did in-person ethnographic field work. Result: 14% box chocolate share by 2014, created India's premium chocolate segment.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The Ferrero customer insights team as well as the management traveled to the metro cities as well as out of them. They also visited consumer homes to understand consumer habits and aspirations."
>
> "Ferrero not only realized that there was a market for an expensive box of chocolates, even if it was sold in the local kirana store, but they also discovered that consumers would buy expensive chocolates mostly during festivals."
>
