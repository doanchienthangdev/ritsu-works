# Emotion Plays Significant Role in B2B Buying Decisions

> **Source.** [Chapter 6](../chapters/chapter-06-business-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 187–187, chunk 4 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Today, most B-to-B marketers recognize that emotion plays an important role in business buying decisions."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Despite assumption that business buyers are rational/cost-driven, emotional appeals (fear, aspiration, identity) work even in performance-based categories. USG Corporation case demonstrates this.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Far from being cold, calculating, and impersonal, business buyers are human and social as well. They react to both reason and emotion."
>
> "This and other USG ad recognize that emotions can play a role even in heavily performance-based decisions."
>

## For Ritsu

For Ritsu's educator persona (B2B-adjacent buyer): emotional drivers matter as much as ROI. Educator identity ('I'm a great teacher who cares about student outcomes') and aspiration ('my students will master this material') are stronger purchase triggers than pure cost-savings pitches.
