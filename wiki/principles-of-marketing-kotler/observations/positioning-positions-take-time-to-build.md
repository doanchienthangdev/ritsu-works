# Positions Take Years to Build, Can Be Lost Quickly

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 227–227, chunk 4 (confidence 0.9, auto-accepted).
>
> "Establishing a position or changing one usually takes a long time. In contrast, positions that have taken years to build can quickly be lost. Once a company has built the desired position, it must take care to maintain the position through consistent performance and communication."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Position is long-term commitment requiring consistency in performance + communication. Abrupt repositioning confuses consumers.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Instead, a product's position should evolve gradually as it adapts to the ever-changing marketing environment."
>

## For Ritsu

Ritsu is early enough that position is not yet fixed in users' minds — window to set correctly. 'Active learning > passive learning' is right early stake. Every product decision should check: 'does this reinforce or dilute that position?'
