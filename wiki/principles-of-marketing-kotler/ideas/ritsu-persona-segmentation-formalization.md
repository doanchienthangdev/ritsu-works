# Formalize Ritsu ICP Segmentation Using Behavioral + Psychographic Bases

> **Source.** [Chapter 7](../chapters/chapter-07-customer-value-driven-marketing-strat.md), book pp. 208–208, chunk 2 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).
>
> "Behavioral segmentation divides buyers into segments based on their knowledge, attitudes, uses, or responses to a product. Many marketers believe that behavior variables are the best starting point for building market segments."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ritsu's 6 personas should be grounded in behavioral (usage rate, benefit sought, occasion) and psychographic (motivation, identity) — not just job title — to enable sharper product/marketing decisions.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Benefit segmentation requires finding the major benefits people look for in a product class, the kinds of people who look for each benefit, and the major brands that deliver each benefit."
>

## For Ritsu

Current personas (students, engineers, etc.) are demographic. Behavioral re-framing: 'exam-deadline-driven user' vs 'ongoing-curiosity self-learner' vs 'skill-gap closer' maps tightly to usage occasions, benefit sought, urgency — drives which activity types and onboarding flows to build first. Recommended as 01-marketing ICP sub-pillar input.
