# Referral Program as Sweepstakes + Premium Combination

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 481–483, chunk 5 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).
>
> "Premium: goods offered free or at low cost as an incentive."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Combine 2 Ch 16 tools. Premiums = immediate reward for referral (1 free month Pro + premium activity type unlock for both referrer + referred). Sweepstakes = monthly draw for users who referred ≥1 active user (3 months Pro free, student-relevant prize). Student-specific: 'invite study partner' with SHARED premium (co-study mode).

## Additional quotes

> "Sweepstakes: consumers submit their names for a drawing."
>

## For Ritsu

Design principles: (1) Incentive size > minimum threshold (1-day extension = not enough). (2) Clear conditions (referral must activate + complete one session). (3) Rolling monthly cadence (no urgency drift). (4) Evaluate referral-to-activation rate, referral-to-paid rate, LTV vs organic signups. Student social context = key — frame as 'invite study partner.'
