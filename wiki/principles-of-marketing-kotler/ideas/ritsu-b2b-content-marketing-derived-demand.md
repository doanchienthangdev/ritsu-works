# Ritsu B2B Content Marketing via Derived-Demand Logic

> **Source.** [Chapter 6](../chapters/chapter-06-business-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 183–189, chunk 3 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).
>
> "Business demand is derived demand—it ultimately derives from the demand for consumer goods."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Gore-Tex marketing to end consumers to pull B2B demand has direct Ritsu analogue. If students request Ritsu from their institutions, creates pull-through demand. Ritsu B2B content marketing should focus on educator identity + student outcomes (emotion + aspiration) more than procurement ROI.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "So to boost demand for Gore-Tex, Gore advertises to final consumers to educate them on the benefits of Gore-Tex fabrics in the brands they buy. As a result, consumers around the world have learned to look for the familiar Gore-Tex brand label."
>
> "B-to-B marketers know that they aren't really targeting businesses, they are targeting individuals in those businesses who affect buying decisions."
>

## For Ritsu

Strategy: focus B2C Ritsu growth in academic communities to generate organic pull-through to institutions. Educator-targeted content should emphasize identity ('the teacher who actually changes student outcomes') + emotion (aspiration) over economic ROI.
