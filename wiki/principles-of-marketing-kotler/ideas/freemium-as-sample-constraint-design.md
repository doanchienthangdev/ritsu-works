# Design Free Plan as Product Sample, Not Crippled Product

> **Source.** [Chapter 16](../chapters/chapter-16-personal-selling-and-sales-promotion.md), book pp. 481–481, chunk 5 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Sampling is the most effective — but most expensive — way to introduce a new product."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Sampling principle: deliver enough value to prove full product's benefit, leave visible gap for paid. Free plan should maximize 'wow' on first use (full quiz quality, AI tutor depth) but constrain breadth (# docs, # activity types, sessions/week) not depth. Upgrade should feel 'I want more' not 'I hit a wall.'

## Additional quotes

> "For digital products, marginal cost of sample is near zero — constraint is strategic, not economic."
>

## For Ritsu

Constraint design logic: don't constrain output quality (quiz difficulty, AI tutor explanation depth, persona quality) — prove core value. DO constrain breadth: 3 docs/month, 5 of 17 activity types, 2 AI tutor sessions/day. Constraint hit AFTER 'wow' event. Measure gap between first wow + first limit hit — if limit hit too early, churn rises not conversion.
