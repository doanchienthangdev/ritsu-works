# Exam-Season Dynamic + Student-Segment Pricing as Ritsu Revenue Levers

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 444–469, chunk 4 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).
>
> "Using time-based pricing, a firm varies its price by the season, the month, the day, and even the hour. For example, movie theaters charge matinee pricing during the daytime, and resorts give weekend and seasonal discounts."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ritsu's demand time-concentrated around exams → time-based segmented pricing (premium urgency window) + student-segment discount (volume from price-sensitive primary ICP).

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Under customer-segment pricing, different customers pay different prices for the same product or service. For example, museums, movie theaters, and retail stores may charge lower prices for students, people in the military, and senior citizens."
>

## For Ritsu

(1) Time-based: 'Crunch Mode' or 'Exam Prep' time-limited premium ($12 for 2-week intensive vs $8/mo regular). (2) Student-segment: verified .edu discount expands TAM from this elastic segment, while professional+educator plans hold higher prices. Risk: deal-prone users waiting for exam-season promos — mitigate by making standard monthly the clear default anchor.
