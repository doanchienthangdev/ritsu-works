# Ritsu Augmented Product Layer: Community, Certificates, Cross-Session Memory as Retention Moats

> **Source.** [Chapter 8](../chapters/chapter-08-products-services-and-brands.md), book pp. 235–236, chunk 3 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Product planners must build an augmented product around the core benefit and actual product by offering additional consumer services and benefits."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

3-product-levels framework reveals Ritsu's under-developed augmented layer. Core product (activities, AI tutor) strong; augmented product (services beyond core) thin. EdTech incumbents use streaks, leaderboards, certificates, cross-session progress as retention drivers.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Consumers see products as complex bundles of benefits that satisfy their needs."
>

## For Ritsu

Near-term roadmap input: identify top-3 augmented features that reduce churn without core-model investment. Candidates: (1) cross-session progress continuity with visible mastery score per doc, (2) shareable 'I mastered this' moments (social proof + virality), (3) email/push nudges tied to forgetting-curve predictions. Each augments without changing 17+ activity-type core.
