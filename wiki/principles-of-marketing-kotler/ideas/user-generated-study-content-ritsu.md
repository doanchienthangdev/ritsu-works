## Summary

Apply UGC framework to Ritsu: invite users to share study sessions, quiz scores, before/after using #StudiedWithRitsu. UGC outperforms polished brand content (West Elm 2.6x CTR). For EdTech, student success stories = highest-credibility acquisition signal.

Medium-term initiative (post first 50 paying users). Prerequisite: in-product sharing affordances (score share button, session summary card). Immediate proxy: monitor what students already post about Ritsu, amplify best examples.
