# Ritsu as Omni-Channel Learning Platform

> **Source.** [Chapter 13](../chapters/chapter-13-retailing-and-wholesaling.md), book pp. 377–385, chunk 2 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Today's omni-channel consumer makes no clean distinction between in-store and online shopping."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Apply omnichannel retail model to Ritsu: learner uploads desktop → quizzes on mobile commute → reviews flashcards offline → resumes mindmap desktop — all synchronized state. Educational equivalent of seamless retail. Mobile-first practice layer = Ritsu's ZMOT.

## Additional quotes

> "The shift to seamless cross-device experience changes user expectations across all categories."
>

## For Ritsu

Product implications: (1) state sync across devices seamless, (2) offline mode for flashcards + saved content, (3) push notifications with learning micro-moments, (4) mobile-first design of practice layer. ZMOT for Ritsu = student opens app during 5-min break, not when they sit down at computer.
