# Idea: Cause-Related Marketing for Ritsu — Education Access as Brand Mission Amplifier

> **Source.** [Chapter 3](../chapters/chapter-03-analyzing-the-marketing-environment.md), book pp. 104–106, chunk 6 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).

## Summary

64% of consumers are belief-driven. Ritsu's freemium already provides free access, but a formal cause-related program ('for every paid sub, one student in under-resourced school gets free premium') could turn business necessity into brand differentiator.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "According to one recent study, 64 percent of global consumers are now 'belief-driven' buyers who will choose or avoid a brand based on its stand on societal issues."
>

## For Ritsu

Pre-condition: Ritsu must hit stable free-tier unit economics before formalizing the program. At current pre-100-paying stage, cause framing can live in positioning copy and founder narrative without a formal program. Test hypothesis: does 'active learning for everyone' mission framing improve free-to-paid conversion among educator and professional ICP segments who are more values-driven than price-sensitive?
