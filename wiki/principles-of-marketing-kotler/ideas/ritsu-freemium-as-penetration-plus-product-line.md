# Ritsu Freemium = Penetration Pricing + Product-Line Pricing Combined

> **Source.** [Chapter 11](../chapters/chapter-11-pricing-strategies-additional.md), book pp. 219–263, chunk 3 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Companies set a low initial price to penetrate the market quickly and deeply — to attract a large number of buyers quickly and win a large market share."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ritsu's free tier = market-penetration (price=0 maximizes adoption, builds user base for network effects). Paid tier structure = product-line pricing (capture WTP variation across 6 ICP personas).

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "The price steps should take into account cost differences between products in the line. More important, they should account for differences in customer perceptions of the value of different features."
>

## For Ritsu

Two Ch 11 frameworks justify Ritsu freemium design. Free tier removes adoption friction (penetration). Paid tier ladder captures persona WTP variation (product-line). Conversion = users hitting value ceiling, not paywall core. PMF bottleneck = first paid tier feature set.
