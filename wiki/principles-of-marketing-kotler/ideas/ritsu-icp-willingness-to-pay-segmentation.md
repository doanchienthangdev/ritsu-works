# WTP Varies Sharply Across Ritsu's 6 ICP Personas

> **Source.** [Chapter 10](../chapters/chapter-10-pricing-understanding-customer-value.md), book pp. 309–309, chunk 4 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).
>
> "Marketers also need to know price elasticity — how responsive demand will be to a change in price."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Students (exam-pressure, budget) = elastic. Self-learners moderate elastic. Engineers/researchers = inelastic (ROI, expensable). Educators = institution-price-sensitive but volume. Professionals = inelastic if career ROI. Freemium-to-paid mechanics should be persona-specific.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "If demand hardly changes with a small change in price, we say demand is inelastic."
>

## For Ritsu

Persona-specific triggers: (1) Students — paywall adaptive quiz + spaced-repetition near exam dates; seasonal pricing OK if time-bounded. (2) Engineers/researchers — token depth (full-doc ingestion, code exercises) as upgrade hook; price $15-20/mo (inelastic). (3) Educators — seat-based classroom plan; WTP frame shifts to institutional. (4) Professionals — anchor vs coaching session or online course cost. Avoid single 'student discount' cannibalizing higher-WTP segments — use feature gating, not blanket discounts.
