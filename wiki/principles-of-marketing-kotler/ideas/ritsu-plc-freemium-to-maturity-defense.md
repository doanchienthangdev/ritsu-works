# Ritsu PLC Risk: Freemium AI Tutor Features Commoditize Fast — Apply Maturity-Stage Defense Early

> **Source.** [Chapter 9](../chapters/chapter-09-developing-new-products-plc.md), book pp. 983–1110, chunk 5 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "The slowdown in sales growth results in many producers with many products to sell. In turn, this overcapacity leads to greater competition."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ritsu's core feature set is replicable. PLC theory: maturity → price competition + feature parity. Apply maturity-stage defensive strategies (market modification, product modification, marketing mix modification) BEFORE competitors force it.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Rather than sitting on its successful products, it systematically reinvents them before competitors can."
>

## For Ritsu

Playbook: (1) Market mod — activate underserved ICP segments (professionals, educators, researchers) before competitors. (2) Product mod — deepen personalization (adaptive difficulty, spaced repetition, Feynman-mode depth) → raise switching costs. (3) Marketing mix — lock educators+institutions at early-adopter pricing. Reinvigorate pre-decline, not reactively. Intuit model = target posture.
