# The Free Trial IS the Marketing Message — Product-Led Awareness

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 418–420, chunk 4 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Personal communication channels have highest persuasion, especially at preference/conviction/action stages."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Freemium trial is primary communication vehicle, not just conversion tactic. User uploads PDF + generates quiz in 30s = lived value proposition (more vivid than ad). The 30s onboarding IS the ad. Apply Kotler's communication model: trial = encoding + media + decoding combined.

## Additional quotes

> "A user completing their first quiz has decoded the message through experience, not inference. Zero decoding error, zero noise."
>

## For Ritsu

Design implications: (1) Free tier generous enough to produce full 'Ritsu moment' before paywall. (2) Onboarding copy frames trial as 'try your document' not 'sign up.' (3) Track 5As through trial: land → demo seen → upload → quiz complete → share/invite. Conversion funnel IS communication funnel. (4) Frictionless sharing = shared media at zero marginal cost.
