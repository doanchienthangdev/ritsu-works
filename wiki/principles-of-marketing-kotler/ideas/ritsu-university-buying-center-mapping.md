# Ritsu University/L&D Sale: Mapping the Buying Center

> **Source.** [Chapter 6](../chapters/chapter-06-business-markets-and-buyer-behavior.md), book pp. 185–186, chunk 3 (confidence 0.65, pending founder review).
>
> "A company buying a product or service for the first time faces a new task situation. In such cases, the greater the cost or risk, the larger the number of decision participants and the greater the company's efforts to collect information."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

For Ritsu's eventual university/L&D licenses: Users = students + instructors. Influencers = course directors, instructional designers. Buyers = procurement/finance. Deciders = Dean / L&D VP. Gatekeepers = IT (SSO/LMS) + legal (FERPA/GDPR). Educator pilot = 'new task' purchase.

## Additional quotes from the chapter

> "Users are members of the organization who will use the product or service. In many cases, users initiate the buying proposal and help define product specifications."
>
> "Gatekeepers control the flow of information to others. For example, purchasing agents often have authority to prevent salespersons from seeing users or deciders."
>

## For Ritsu

Direct application of Ch 6 framework. Early Ritsu strategy should focus on turning educators into Users + Influencers (champion pathway) before approaching institutional Buyers/Deciders. Educator-led pilot programs lower perceived risk for new-task purchase.
