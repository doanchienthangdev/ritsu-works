# Ritsu Pre-PMF Communication Mix — Pull-First, WoM-Core

> **Source.** [Chapter 14](../chapters/chapter-14-engaging-consumers-imc.md), book pp. 420–425, chunk 7 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "Pull strategy: producer's marketing activities target final consumers."
>

## Summary

Pure pull (B2C direct, no channel partners). WoM-core (83% WoM stat + EdTech's risky/visible profile). Owned + earned + shared media lead; paid amplifies only after organic signal proven.

## Additional quotes

> "Word-of-mouth is most effective for expensive, risky, or highly visible products."
>

## For Ritsu

Mix (cost-efficiency order): (1) Shared/Earned — Reddit (r/learnprogramming, r/premed, r/MCAT), Discord study servers, YouTube study creators with free access. (2) Owned — SEO content targeting textbook+exam queries. (3) Paid — amplify only what organically outperforms. Sequencing gate: 'good deeds before good words' — don't scale comms before product loop is proven.
