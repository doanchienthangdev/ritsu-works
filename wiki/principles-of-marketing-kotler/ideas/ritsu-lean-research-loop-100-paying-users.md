## Summary

At sub-100-paying-users scale, right sequence is: (1) ethnographic observation of 5-8 users in their natural study context, (2) short descriptive survey, (3) A/B test single change derived from ethnographic insight. Don't build analytics stack before understanding qualitative 'why.'
