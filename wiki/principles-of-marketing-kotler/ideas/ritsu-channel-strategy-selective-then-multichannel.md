# Ritsu Channel Strategy — Selective Direct Now, Structured Multichannel at PMF

> **Source.** [Chapter 12](../chapters/chapter-12-marketing-channels.md), book pp. 355–358, chunk 7 (confidence 0.8, pending founder review).
>
> "For maximum effectiveness, however, channel analysis and decision making should be more purposeful. Marketing channel design calls for analyzing consumer needs, setting channel objectives, identifying major channel alternatives, and evaluating the alternatives."
>
> — Kotler/Armstrong/Opresnik, *Principles of Marketing* (18e Global, Pearson 2021)

## Summary

Ritsu is direct-channel, intensive-distribution digital product. Pre-PMF correct: maximum accessibility, no intermediary conflict, full control. Channel design sequence (analyze→objectives→alternatives→evaluate) suggests next channel decision triggered by segment saturation, not opportunity.

## Additional quotes

> "Each alternative should be evaluated against economic, control, and adaptability criteria."
>

## For Ritsu

Distribution roadmap. Phase 1 (now): direct web, intensive, freemium. Phase 2 (post-PMF, educator ICP): selective LMS integrations as contractual VMS — Canvas + Moodle first. Phase 3: mobile app stores for mobile-first student ICP. Each phase adds channel only when existing channel's reach ceiling provably hit.
