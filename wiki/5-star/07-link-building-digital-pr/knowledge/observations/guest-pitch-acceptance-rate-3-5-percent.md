# Guest pitch acceptance rate is 3-5%

Industry standard for guest-post outreach: you need to pitch roughly 20 publications to place 1-2 articles, which is a 3-5% acceptance rate. This benchmark is what should drive your pipeline sizing — to reliably place 3-5 articles in a quarter, you need to start with a target list of 30-50 publications, not 10. The same ratio applies whether you're pitching mainstream business publications or industry trade journals, because the bottleneck is editor inbox volume (publications receive 50-200+ pitch emails daily), not pitch quality alone.

The corollary: direct email to editors and writers has a 5-10x higher response rate than contact forms, and a strong first pitch can hit 50% response rate. So while overall placement is 3-5%, the "response" rate to a personalized pitch is much higher — most responses are "not now" or "wrong fit," not silence.
