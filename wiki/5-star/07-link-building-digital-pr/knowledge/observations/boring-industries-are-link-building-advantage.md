# Boring industries are a link-building advantage, not a handicap

The empirical proof point is Trelleborg, a Swedish polymer-seals manufacturer — about as "boring" a business as exists. They built 2 million backlinks from 8,700+ referring domains and rank #1 for industrial seal terms. The mechanism that produced that outcome is structural: most competitors in B2B, industrial, SaaS, and services don't do marketing at all, so the field is vacuum-empty of good content. Readers in boring industries are desperate for useful material precisely because it's so rare.

The reframe to internalize: "the industry is boring, but the problems aren't." Boring industries have customers facing real, expensive, urgent problems — water hardness for plumbers, labor shortages for HVAC, tax planning for accountants. Each of those problems is a content goldmine and a link magnet, but only if you actually solve it in the content rather than producing thin marketing fluff. The advantage compounds: low competition + desperate audience + easy differentiation if you simply try.
