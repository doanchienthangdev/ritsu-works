# Write guest posts as a journalist, not a marketer

The mindset shift that determines whether a guest article gets published and read: write as a journalist providing value to THEIR audience, not as a marketing manager promoting yourself. Readers should finish thinking "wow, this was helpful," not "this is an ad." Your credibility and the byline mention are the reward — not the main point of the article.

This rule translates into hard constraints on the draft: mention your company at most 1-2 times and only naturally ("As we've found at [Company], the key is..." — never "Our product does X and you should use it"); include at most 1-2 internal links to genuinely useful resources, not the homepage; and concentrate any self-promotion into the author bio (50-100 words), which is where the link payoff actually lives. The article body itself must read like editorial content the publication would have commissioned anyway. Editors will reject anything that smells like a press release, and readers will mentally discount it even if it slips through.
