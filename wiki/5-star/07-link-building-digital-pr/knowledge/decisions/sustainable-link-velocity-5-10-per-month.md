# Build links at sustainable 5-10/month velocity, not in spikes

Getting 100 backlinks in a single month and then zero for six months looks algorithmically spammy to Google and signals an unsustainable acquisition pattern. The better rule: 5-10 new backlinks per month, consistently, indefinitely. Consistency itself becomes a ranking signal because it suggests you're genuinely valuable rather than running a one-off campaign.

The operational implication for any link-building program: design it as an ongoing process with steady inputs (pitches per week, content per month, outreach per cycle) rather than a sprint or campaign. Realistic per-month yields by phase: early months 2-5 new links from guest posting alone; after 6 months, 5-15 links/month as multiple strategies compound; after 12+ months, authority in the space and strong rankings for target keywords. The compounding only happens if velocity stays steady — pause for a quarter and the engine cools off.
