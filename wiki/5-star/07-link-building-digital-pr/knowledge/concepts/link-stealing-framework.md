# Link stealing framework

A four-step playbook for actively redirecting backlinks from inferior competing articles to your own better version: (1) **Identify inferior competing articles** — search your target keyword and find a top-10 result that ranks well but is poorly written or outdated; (2) **Analyze their backlinks** — use Semrush to pull every site linking to that inferior article, filter by domain authority and anchor relevance; (3) **Create vastly better content** — write a version that is genuinely 2-3x better (more comprehensive, better structured, more recent, original data where possible); (4) **Reach out to linking sites** — email each linking site noting their existing link and suggesting your better resource as an updated alternative.

The framework rests on a simple psychological premise: many editors and bloggers genuinely want their content to be accurate, and updating a link to a better source is a small lift. The mechanism scales because it inverts the typical link-building funnel — instead of cold-pitching strangers about your content, you're approaching people who have *already demonstrated* they want to link to content about this topic. The constraint is honesty: your version must actually be better, or the outreach falls flat.
