# Links as votes of confidence

A backlink is not a numeric SEO signal so much as one website vouching for another's credibility. Each external link reads to Google like a peer endorsement, which is why one link from a high-authority domain (TechCrunch, Nature, Harvard Business Review) is worth more than 100 links from low-authority sites. The vote model has three corollaries: (1) quality trumps quantity because trusted voters carry more weight, (2) relevance matters because votes from topically related sources count for more, and (3) anchor text signals intent because the words around the vote tell Google what the linked page is about.

This vote framing also explains why links are the hardest SEO signal to fake: votes require genuine relationships and valuable content. That difficulty is precisely why search engines weight them so heavily as a ranking signal — and why building links functionally equals building brand authority in your industry.
