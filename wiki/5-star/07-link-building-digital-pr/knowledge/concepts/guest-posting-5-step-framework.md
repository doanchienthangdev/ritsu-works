# Guest posting 5-step framework

A repeatable system for placing articles on relevant publications and earning high-quality backlinks. The five steps are sequenced so each one feeds the next: (1) **Ideation** — generate compelling angles using trend research (Exploding Topics, Answer The Public, BuzzSumo) and produce 5-10 ranked topic angles; (2) **Find publications** — discover 30-50 targets via Google search, competitor backlink analysis (Semrush), and a quality checklist covering domain authority, spam score, mobile-friendliness, and subject-matter fit; (3) **Find contacts and pitch** — bypass contact forms, find authors/editors via author bios, Twitter/LinkedIn, and email-finder tools (Hunter.io, RocketReach), then send a personalized pitch with 3-5 topic options; (4) **Write the article** — match the publication's voice, structure with a hook + 3-5 sections + conclusion, mention your company naturally at most 1-2 times; (5) **After publication** — thank the editor, promote it, capture the link, and seed long-term relationships every 2-3 months.

The framework is calibrated for industry-standard yield: ~20 pitches placed at 1-2 articles (3-5% acceptance), so the input pipeline must always be larger than the desired output. Each successful article makes the next pitch easier — published bylines become social proof that compound.
