# Leading vs lagging indicators for link building

Link-building metrics split into two categories with different operational meanings: **leading indicators** measure activity you control directly and can adjust this week — pitches sent, articles published, links acquired, outreach emails sent, content pieces shipped. **Lagging indicators** measure outcomes that result from sustained leading activity — organic traffic, keyword rankings, referring domain count, leads from organic search.

The discipline: track both, but manage off the leading indicators. Lagging metrics move slowly (3-6 months for ranking gains, 9-12 months for compound authority effects), so reading them weekly creates false signals and tempts you into reactive pivots. Leading metrics tell you whether the engine is running today; lagging metrics tell you whether the engine is producing — but only after a delay. Review leading indicators monthly; review lagging indicators quarterly.
