# Branded anchor text is the safest signal that still builds authority

Anchor text — the visible words wrapping a hyperlink — splits into a four-rung ranking by SEO value and spam risk: exact-match ("SEO services") is most valuable per-link but looks manipulative if overused; partial-match ("best SEO services company") is safer; **branded** ("Acme SEO") is the safest *and still builds authority*; naked URL ("example.com") is neutral; generic ("click here", "read more") barely helps.

The practical decision: optimize the bulk of your anchor-text mix for branded mentions. They build domain authority and brand recognition without ever tripping spam heuristics, and they're what naturally occurs when other writers actually like your brand. Treat exact-match anchors as a rare, deliberate tool — not a default. The same principle applies to Vietnamese-language link campaigns: the brand name in any language is safer than aggressive keyword anchors.
