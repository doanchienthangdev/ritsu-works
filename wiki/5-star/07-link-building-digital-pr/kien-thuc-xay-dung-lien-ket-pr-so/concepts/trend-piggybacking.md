# Trend piggybacking

You don't need to originate a unique trend to ride one — instead, identify large external trends tangentially related to your business and connect them to your offering. A houseplant business can piggyback on mental-health awareness (houseplants improve mental health), climate/sustainability (responsible sourcing), remote-work culture (better home offices with plants), and biophilic design (nature in modern interiors) — four orthogonal trends, four content angles, all genuinely connected without being forced.

The constraint is honesty: the connection must be real, not contrived. An editor or reader who senses a forced trend-grab will reject the piece. But the universe of large trends is wide enough that most businesses can find 3-5 genuine angles per year — and large trends bring large audiences that small businesses can't generate on their own.
