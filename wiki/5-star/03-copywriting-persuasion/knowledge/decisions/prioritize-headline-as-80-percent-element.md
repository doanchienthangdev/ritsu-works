# Prioritize Headlines as the 80% Element

Studies show 80% of people read only the headline. Spend more time crafting and testing headlines than any other copy element. A great headline with mediocre body copy can still convert; a weak headline with brilliant body copy will not — the reader never gets past the first line. Headline is the leverage point.

What headlines must do: grab attention (stop the scroll), promise a specific benefit (not "Better Learning" but "Ace Your Exams Without Staying Up All Night"), create relevance for the target audience, arouse curiosity (open loop), and signal credibility through specifics or proof ("Why 10,000+ Students Are Ditching Textbooks for This"). Lead with the deepest benefit from the So-What chain, not the feature.

Operational rule: generate at least 5 headline variations per piece (different angles — curiosity, benefit, proof, contrast, story), A/B test on landing pages and email subject lines, measure click-through and conversion, let data — not opinion — pick the winner. Continuous headline testing compounds: 10% monthly improvement is 3x better in a year. For solo founders with no sales team, this is the highest-ROI copywriting discipline.
