# The oldest running ad in Facebook Ad Library is the proven control

Facebook Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) exposes every ad a business is currently running, plus how long each has been live. Advertisers kill creative that does not perform — so the **longest-running ad is the empirical winner** against everything they have tested against it. That ad is the highest-signal artifact for understanding what works in the category.

Operationally: when researching a competitor, find their oldest active ad and treat its hook, image, call-to-action, and disqualification copy as the benchmark to beat. The Ad Library also shows which channels they target (FB, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network), languages, geographic targeting, and which variations they are split-testing — a complete picture of competitor paid-social priorities for free.
