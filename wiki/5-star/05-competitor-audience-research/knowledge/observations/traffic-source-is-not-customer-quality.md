# Traffic source ≠ customer quality

Google Analytics reports the channel that drove a click, but not whether that click became a high-LTV customer. Channels that look weak on volume can produce the highest-quality customers, and the highest-volume channel can produce mostly bounces. Bounce rate, time on site, pages-per-session, and conversion rate vary substantially between channels — display traffic is usually lowest-quality (accidental clicks), referral and direct often highest.

The practical correction: post-purchase or lead-capture surveys asking "Where did you find us?" reveal where the **best** customers come from. Combining survey data with analytics gives the complete picture; either signal alone is misleading. If every channel shows a high bounce rate, the problem is the website, not the traffic mix.
