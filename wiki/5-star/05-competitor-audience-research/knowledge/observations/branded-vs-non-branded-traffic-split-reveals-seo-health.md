# Branded vs non-branded organic split reveals SEO health

When a competitor's organic search traffic is overwhelmingly branded (people typing the company name), their SEO is weaker than the raw traffic number suggests — that traffic would arrive via direct or social regardless. Non-branded organic traffic ("health tracking," "best AI tutor") is the signal of real SEO investment because it requires ranking against unaware users' intent queries.

Concrete example from the source: **Aura Ring** has a large site and high organic traffic, but most of it is branded — page headings like "Adapts and responds to you" waste SEO real estate that should hold non-branded target keywords like "health tracking." The competitor's branded dominance is therefore an opportunity for a challenger to capture non-branded intent at lower cost.
