# Analyze 3-5 competitors for pattern confidence, not one

A single competitor's strategy could be smart, accidental, or wasteful — you cannot tell from one data point. Looking at 3-5 competitors reveals **patterns**: if all four invest heavily in paid social and none in SEO, that's a signal about the market. If strategies diverge wildly, that signals viable alternatives still exist.

The discipline: spend 2-4 hours per competitor on the Semrush + Facebook Ad Library + Google Analytics proxy passes. Total 10-20 hours of research up-front saves months of misallocated channel spend. Looking at only one competitor is a common operator mistake that produces a single-source-of-truth illusion.
