# Traffic Lasso framework

The Traffic Lasso is Exposure Ninja's five-stage workflow for building a customer-acquisition stack: (1) **Locations** — qualitative and quantitative research on where customers hang out and how competitors reach them; (2) **Ad Channels** — match paid channels to audience location; (3) **Search** — choose target keywords; (4) **Social** — leverage organic social and community; (5) **Optimization** — improve what is already running.

The Locations stage is the foundation — before any spend, the operator must know **where** customers already are and **how** competitors are reaching them. Skipping this step is the most common cause of wasted ad budget, because traffic strategy without a location map is guessing.
