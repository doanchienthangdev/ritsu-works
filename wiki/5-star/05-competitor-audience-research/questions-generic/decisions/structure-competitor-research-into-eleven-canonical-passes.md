# Structure competitor research into 11 canonical passes covering Semrush, ads, social, analytics, and synthesis

The Exposure Ninja research checklist organizes competitor analysis into eleven sequential passes, each with its own question set: **(1) Semrush Domain Overview**, (2) Organic Search, (3) Backlinks, (4) Content, (5) Paid Search, (6) Social Media, (7) Facebook Ad Library, (8) YouTube Channel, (9) Google Analytics Traffic Channels, (10) Geographic Data + Customer Surveys, (11) Keyword Research, plus a final **Synthesis & Strategy** pass and a **Validation & Confidence** pass.

Using this ordered structure (rather than ad-hoc browsing) ensures every competitor profile covers the same surface area, makes 3-5-competitor pattern recognition possible (apples-to-apples comparison), and prevents the "I forgot to check their backlinks" gap that breaks the synthesis later. The 167 generic questions in the source operationalize each pass — when researching a new competitor, the operator works through the relevant pass and answers each question with a data point.
