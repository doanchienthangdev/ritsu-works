# Competitor & Audience Research Questions — Generic Framework

## SEMRUSH ANALYSIS: Domain Overview
1. What is the total estimated monthly organic traffic for this competitor?
2. What is the estimated monthly paid search traffic?
3. How does organic vs paid traffic break down (percentage)?
4. What countries/regions does the competitor target? Where is majority of traffic?
5. Should this analysis be desktop-focused, mobile-focused, or blended? Why?
6. How stable is the traffic trend over the past 12 months (growing, declining, stable)?
7. Are there seasonal patterns in traffic volume?
8. How does estimated traffic compare to annual revenue or business size?

## SEMRUSH ANALYSIS: Organic Search
9. What are the top 10 keywords this competitor ranks for?
10. How many keywords does the competitor rank for in the top 10 Google results?
11. What percentage of organic traffic is branded vs non-branded?
12. Which pages drive the most organic search traffic?
13. Is their organic search dominance intentional (strategy) or accidental (large site)?
14. What are their top-performing landing pages for organic traffic?
15. Do they have a blog strategy? If yes, how much organic traffic does it drive?
16. Which blog posts are their best performers in organic search?
17. Are there obvious content gaps in their organic strategy (topics they should rank for but don't)?
18. What search intent keywords are they missing (informational, commercial, transactional)?

## SEMRUSH ANALYSIS: Backlinks
19. How many total backlinks does this competitor have?
20. How many unique referring domains?
21. What is the authority score distribution of their backlinks (high authority vs low)?
22. Which referring domains have the highest authority scores?
23. Where are most of their high-authority backlinks coming from?
24. Can you identify PR/media links? Which publications are linking?
25. Do they have affiliate or partnership links? Which partners?
26. Are there Wikipedia links? What pages link from Wikipedia?
27. Do they have app store links? (iOS App Store, Google Play, etc.)
28. Can you identify podcast sponsorship links or guest appearance links?
29. Is there a digital PR strategy visible (data/survey links, original research)?
30. How many links are from educational or government domains (.edu, .gov)?
31. What link building opportunities are competitors using that you haven't exploited?
32. Are they acquiring links through partnerships, PR, or scalable content strategies?

## SEMRUSH ANALYSIS: Content
33. How much content does the competitor have on their main website?
34. Is their content stripped of SEO value? (designer-driven vs SEO-driven)
35. Do main page headings include target keywords or are they brand-only?
36. What is the primary keyword focus in their main copy and headings?
37. Do they optimize metadata (page titles, meta descriptions)?
38. How extensive is their blog? (100+ posts, 500+ posts, 1000+?)
39. What are the content pillars (main topics they focus on)?
40. Are there content gaps in competitor's strategy? (Topics they don't cover)
41. Do they have pillar pages and cluster content structure?
42. How deep is their content (5-minute reads vs comprehensive guides)?
43. Do they use multimedia (video, infographics, interactive content)?
44. What content types drive the most traffic (blog, guides, product pages, comparison)?

## SEMRUSH ANALYSIS: Paid Search
45. What keywords is this competitor bidding on in Google Ads?
46. What is their estimated monthly Google Ads spend?
47. What percentage of paid spend is on branded vs non-branded keywords?
48. How many ad variations are they running for each keyword?
49. What is the ad copy quality? (Are they using benefits, USPs, social proof?)
50. Do their ad copy match the landing page? (Relevance score indicator)
51. What is the estimated cost per click for their top keywords?
52. Which keywords appear to be most profitable (low CPC, likely high conversion)?
53. Which keywords appear to be least profitable (high CPC with low conversion)?
54. Are they spending heavily on branded keywords (defensive) or non-branded (growth)?
55. What landing pages are they driving paid traffic to?
56. Do different ad groups use different landing pages?
57. How often are they updating their paid ads? (Control vs testing)
58. What disqualification copy do they use in ads? (Who they want to exclude)

## SEMRUSH ANALYSIS: Social Media
59. Which social channels is this competitor active on?
60. What is their posting frequency on each channel?
61. What is their follower count on each channel?
62. Is posting consistent or sporadic?
63. What content types dominate? (Video, images, carousels, reels, text)
64. What is the engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) vs follower count?
65. Are they using UGC (user-generated content)?
66. Is content native to each platform or cross-posted everywhere?
67. What hashtag strategy are they using?
68. Do they use influencer partnerships or collaborations?
69. What is the tone/voice in social content? (Educational, entertaining, promotional)
70. Are they selling directly in social (DMs, shop features) or driving to website?
71. How active are they in community engagement (responding to comments)?
72. Do they run giveaways, contests, or interactive campaigns?

## FACEBOOK AD LIBRARY ANALYSIS
73. What ads are currently running on Facebook/Instagram (via Ad Library)?
74. How many active ad variations are they testing?
75. What is the age/demographic targeting?
76. What is the geographic targeting (countries, states, cities)?
77. What languages are ads running in?
78. Which placements are used (Facebook feed, Instagram feed, Stories, Reels, Audience Network)?
79. How long has their longest-running ad been active? (Control ad)
80. What messaging/hook does the control ad use?
81. How frequently are they updating/rotating ads?
82. What call-to-action is used in ads (Learn More, Shop Now, Sign Up)?
83. Are ads driving to landing pages or directly to product?
84. What creative assets are used (static images, video, carousel)?
85. Is there A/B testing visible in ad variations?

## YOUTUBE CHANNEL ANALYSIS
86. Do they have a YouTube channel? How many subscribers?
87. How many videos have they uploaded?
88. What is the posting frequency?
89. What video types dominate? (Product demos, educational, vlogs, testimonials)
90. Which videos have the most views? What do top performers have in common?
91. What is the average engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)?
92. Are they using YouTube Shorts or long-form content?
93. Do they run ads on YouTube? What do the ads look like?
94. What keywords/topics are they targeting with video titles and descriptions?
95. Do they have playlists organizing content by topic?
96. Are they optimizing for YouTube SEO (tags, timestamps, cards)?

## GOOGLE ANALYTICS: Traffic Channels
97. What is the traffic breakdown by source (organic, paid, social, direct, referral)?
98. Which channel drives the most traffic?
99. Which channel has the highest conversion rate?
100. Which channel has the highest bounce rate?
101. Which channel has the highest pages-per-session?
102. What is the quality difference between channels (bounce rate, time on site)?
103. Is display advertising being used? How much traffic from display ads?
104. Is there referral traffic? From which sources?
105. How has channel mix changed over the past 12 months?
106. If traffic shifted between channels, what changed? (Campaign launch, algorithm update)

## GOOGLE ANALYTICS: Organic Traffic Deep Dive
107. What percentage of organic traffic lands on homepage vs content pages?
108. What is the bounce rate for branded vs non-branded organic traffic?
109. What is the conversion rate for branded vs non-branded?
110. Which blog posts drive the most organic traffic?
111. Which blog posts have the highest conversion rate?
112. Which blog posts drive high traffic but low conversion? (Opportunity to improve)
113. Which blog posts drive low traffic but high conversion? (Opportunity to promote)
114. What is the typical customer journey (how many pages before conversion)?
115. Do branded and non-branded traffic have different user behaviors?
116. What is the search intent of top organic landing pages?

## GOOGLE ANALYTICS: Geographic Data
117. What countries drive the most traffic?
118. What countries have the highest conversion rates?
119. What states/regions drive the most traffic (if applicable)?
120. Are there geographic pockets of strong performance?
121. Do conversion rates vary significantly by geography?
122. Is there untapped geographic opportunity?
123. Which geographic regions have highest/lowest bounce rates?
124. Is geographic targeting reflected in ad spend and content strategy?

## CUSTOMER SURVEYS & QUALITATIVE DATA
125. Where do customers say they found the company? (vs what analytics shows)
126. Which traffic source leads to best quality customers?
127. Which traffic source leads to highest lifetime value?
128. Which traffic source leads to most referrals?
129. Do customers mention competitors? Which ones?
130. What problems were customers trying to solve when they found the company?
131. What objections did customers have? How does marketing address these?
132. What content/touchpoints influenced the purchase decision most?
133. Do customers discover through friends/word-of-mouth or paid channels?

## KEYWORD RESEARCH FOR AUDIENCE UNDERSTANDING
134. What keywords have highest search volume in this market?
135. What keywords have highest commercial intent (CPC)?
136. What keywords are growing in search volume?
137. What keywords are declining in search volume?
138. What are the main keyword clusters (topic areas)?
139. What pain points do keywords reveal about the customer?
140. What questions are people asking (question-based keywords)?
141. What buying stage keywords are available (awareness, consideration, decision)?
142. What long-tail opportunities exist (low volume, less competition)?
143. How many keywords have typos/variations that aren't being targeted?

## SYNTHESIS & STRATEGY
144. What is the competitor's primary traffic source? (Where they focus spend)
145. What is the competitor's secondary traffic source?
146. Where are they weak? (Low investment, poor execution, no presence)
147. What channels are they ignoring?
148. What is the competitive intensity in each channel?
149. Where could you differentiate with less competition?
150. What is their go-to-market motion? (How do they acquire first customers)
151. How has their strategy evolved over the past 1-2 years?
152. What worked for them that you should test?
153. What didn't work for them that you should avoid?
154. What unfair advantages do they have? (Brand, budget, distribution)
155. What unfair advantages could you build instead?
156. Where are their customers least satisfied? (Opportunity)
157. What is their customer acquisition cost estimate?
158. What would their lifetime value need to be for unit economics to work?
159. How would you position differently to avoid head-to-head competition?
160. What is your beachhead market where you can win first?

## VALIDATION & CONFIDENCE
161. How many competitors did you analyze? (Need 3-5 for pattern confidence)
162. Do all competitors follow similar strategies? (Indicates required strategy)
163. Do competitors follow different strategies? (Indicates viable alternatives)
164. What data points have highest confidence (measured vs estimated)?
165. What assumptions need validation from customer feedback?
166. How would you test your channel assumptions with small budget?
167. What is your lowest-risk, highest-potential channel to test first?
