# 2-4 hours of competitor research per competitor saves months of wasted spend

Most marketers skip the Locations research phase and spend money in the wrong channels — channels their target customer is not in, channels their competitor already dominates, or channels with structurally low intent. The remediation is small relative to the savings: 2-4 hours per competitor on Semrush + Facebook Ad Library + social audits + Google Analytics proxy work. Looking at 3-5 competitors (10-20 hours total) yields the channel map that prevents months of misallocated ad budget.

The framing is not "research is optional rigor"; it is "research is the cheapest insurance against burning the marketing budget on wrong channels." Competitor successes and failures draw the map; the operator follows data trails instead of guessing.
