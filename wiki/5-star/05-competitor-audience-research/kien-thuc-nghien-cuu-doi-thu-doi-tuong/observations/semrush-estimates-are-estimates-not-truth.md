# Semrush traffic and spend numbers are estimates, not measured truth

Semrush organic traffic estimates and paid search spend estimates are **modeled**, not observed. They can be 2-5x off in either direction, especially for sites with unusual distribution patterns (e.g. heavy direct or app traffic that bypasses crawl signals). Treating Semrush numbers as ground truth is one of the most common operator mistakes in competitor research.

The discipline: use Semrush for **relative comparison** (Competitor A vs B vs C) and **directional signal** (where is the spend concentrated), not for absolute targeting math. Cross-validate the strongest signals — high-CPC keywords, longest-running ads, top-referring domains — against other sources (Facebook Ad Library, Google Search Console proxies, customer surveys) before committing budget.
