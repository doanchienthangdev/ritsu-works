# Spend Pattern Diagnostic

Semrush's estimated monthly PPC spend trends — though directional, not exact — encode four diagnostic patterns for what's happening inside a competitor's account: (1) **consistently increasing spend** → PPC is profitable; they're confident in ROI and scaling what works (takeaway: validated strategy worth studying); (2) **big spike then drop** → they tested, found it didn't work, abandoned the campaign (takeaway: learn from their mistake, don't replicate); (3) **seasonal spikes** → e-commerce Black Friday/holiday or B2B Q4 budget-flush / Q1 new-budget patterns (takeaway: time your own campaigns to match); (4) **flat/low spend** → PPC isn't their core acquisition channel (takeaway: they're relying on SEO, content, or other channels).

The diagnostic flips the usual "what is competitor spending?" question into "what is competitor's spend curve telling us about their strategy phase?" — testing, scaling, retreating, or supplementing. Each pattern dictates a different competitive response.
