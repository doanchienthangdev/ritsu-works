# B2B vs B2C PPC has a fundamental divide

B2B and B2C paid search are structurally different across every dimension. B2B (HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp) targets software features / integrations / use cases; ad copy stresses ROI, efficiency, team collaboration; CPC is higher (commercial intent, valuable customers); free tools are very common (lead generation, nurture); landing pages are long-form with product tours and case studies; conversion goal is demo request or trial signup; decision-makers are multiple (buyer, end-user, CFO).

B2C (Cult Beauty, Airbnb, Amazon) targets product names, categories, deals; ad copy stresses value, lifestyle, convenience; CPC is lower (high volume, low AOV); free tools are less common (shipping / tracking instead); landing pages are short-form with product gallery and social proof; conversion goal is immediate purchase; decision-maker is the individual buyer. Most failed paid-ad campaigns mix these motions — a B2C creative format applied to a B2B funnel, or vice versa.
