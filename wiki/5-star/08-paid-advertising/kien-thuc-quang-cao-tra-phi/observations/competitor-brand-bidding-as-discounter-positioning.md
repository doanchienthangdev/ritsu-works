# Competitor brand bidding as discounter positioning

E-commerce aggregator Cult Beauty bids extremely aggressively on competitor brand names (Olaplex, The Ordinary, Charlotte Tilbury, Nars, Sephora) — beating those brands in position 1 for their own brand keywords. The tactic only economically works under one of two conditions: the aggregator has substantially better margins than the brand it intercepts, OR it accepts losing money on those clicks for brand-building reasons.

The positioning embedded in the ad copy is decisive: "Find [brand name] cheaper on Cult Beauty" turns competitor brand traffic into discounter-positioning conversions. This is fundamentally different from a direct competitor bidding on rivals (which usually fails without a clearly better offer). It works for aggregators because they're not a rival product — they're a retail alternative with a price hook.
