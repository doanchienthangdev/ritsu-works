# Limited budget defers paid ads to organic first

With limited budget against well-funded incumbents (Quizlet, Coursera, Notion), Ritsu's paid-ad strategy must invert the standard playbook. Rather than testing five channels at $500/month each, the constraint forces sequencing: build organic / word-of-mouth / referral mechanics first, then layer paid ads on the channels that already convert organically. The Ritsu-specific question set repeatedly returns to this: "Should Ritsu build organic/word-of-mouth before scaling paid ads?", "Should Ritsu invest in SEO instead of paid ads long-term? (Lower CAC)", "What's the role of content marketing? (Blog, YouTube tutorials, guides = organic growth)".

The operative principle for limited-budget advertisers: paid ads amplify a working funnel; they cannot create one. Without product-market fit signals (organic users, referrals, NPS), every paid dollar buys a click into a leaky bucket. The decision rule: validate retention organically before scaling acquisition paid.
