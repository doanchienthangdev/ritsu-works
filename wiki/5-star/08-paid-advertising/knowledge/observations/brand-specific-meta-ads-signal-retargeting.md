# Brand-specific Meta ads signal retargeting

Because Meta Ad Library hides exact targeting parameters, advertisers must deduce audience from creative content. Strong heuristics emerge: ads using brand-aware language ("Discover HubSpot CRM") without a hard CTA almost certainly retarget prior site visitors who already recognize the product. "Your next haul" language signals retargeting prior customers for repurchase. "15% off your first order" signals new-customer acquisition (or retargeting non-buyers). Generic unbranded product shots typically indicate broad prospecting.

The deduction framework reverses the targeting logic: ad copy and creative assume a certain audience prior state (knows brand vs doesn't), and that assumption tells you the targeting bucket. This makes Meta Ad Library still useful for competitive intelligence despite the privacy-driven targeting opacity.
