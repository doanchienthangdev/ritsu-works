# CPC tracks keyword intent

Cost-per-click rises monotonically with purchase intent in any given category. HubSpot's free email signature generator keyword costs ~$1.73 CPC; "CRM software" costs $14+. The 8x spread is not noise — it reflects auction dynamics where commercial-intent keywords attract more aggressive bidders (because conversion likelihood is higher), while free-tool/lead-magnet keywords get low competition (visitors are far from purchase).

Strategic consequence: bottom-of-funnel commercial keywords are where money is wasted unless your conversion rate justifies the spend; mid-funnel free tools are cheaper-per-click but require a longer nurture path before revenue. The sweet spot to hunt for is moderate-CPC keywords with high intent — niche commercial phrases that haven't attracted full auction competition. Brand keywords sit outside this dynamic: low CPC because competition is minimal.
