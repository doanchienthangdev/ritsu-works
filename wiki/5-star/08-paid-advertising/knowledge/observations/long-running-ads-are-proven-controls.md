# Long-running ads are proven controls

A Facebook/Instagram ad that has been running continuously for 13+ months in a competitor's Ad Library is, with very high confidence, their proven winner — the control that all new creative tests are measured against. Advertisers do not waste budget on durable ad placements; if it survives over a year, it has the lowest CAC and highest ROI in the account. HubSpot's 13+ month controls are studied as carefully as proprietary data because they encode validated audience-message fit.

Operational implication: when researching a competitor, sort their Ad Library by start date and study the oldest still-running variants first. These reveal the messaging, format, and audience hooks that have been validated against the most spend. New ads are noise; old running ads are signal. Budget is almost always concentrated on variants similar to these controls.
