# Competitive Moat Detection from Ad Patterns

A competitor's PPC + social ad mix reveals what kind of moat they are defending. Four diagnostic patterns: (1) always bidding on competitor names → moat is **brand trust**, not product (they substitute for known brands); (2) free tools entry + conversion funnel → moat is **conversion optimization** (cheap-acquisition + LTV play); (3) heavy content marketing + ads → moat is **education/authority** (own the answer to common questions); (4) pure discount messaging → moat is **scale/procurement, not defensible** (race to the bottom, easily commoditized).

The framework lets you read ad strategy as a public signal of strategic positioning. It also tells you what kind of moat you must build to compete: against a brand-trust moat, you need authentic trust signals; against a discount moat, you need differentiated value (not price-matching).
