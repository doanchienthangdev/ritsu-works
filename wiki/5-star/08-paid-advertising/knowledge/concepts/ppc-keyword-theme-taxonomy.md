# PPC Keyword Theme Taxonomy

Competitors organize Google Ads keyword bids into four distinct clusters, each with a different cost structure and funnel role: **branded** (own company name — defensive, high CTR), **free tools / lead generators** (downloadable resources, trials, webinars — low CPC entry point for awareness-stage audiences), **tripwire features** (individual product capabilities like "email automation" — captures pain-point searchers who may not know the full product), and **commercial / category** terms (broad category keywords like "CRM software" and competitor-name bids — highest intent, highest CPC, most aggressive).

HubSpot exemplifies the four-group strategy: free email signature generator at $1.73 CPC pulls awareness traffic; "CRM software" at $14+ CPC captures bottom-funnel intent. The taxonomy is diagnostic — by mapping a competitor's keywords into these four buckets you can read their funnel strategy (cheap entry → upsell vs pure bottom-funnel capture) and identify which cluster they over-invest in vs neglect.
