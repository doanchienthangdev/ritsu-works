# Disqualification Copy

Disqualification copy is the deliberate use of ad-copy language to filter out audiences the keyword targeting cannot exclude. Examples: "Best for teams of 10+" (filters out solopreneurs), "For your business" (disqualifies personal use), "For your marketing team" (narrows to a department). The principle: cast a wide net with keywords, then narrow audience qualification inside the ad creative itself.

The economic rationale is precise — every expensive click filtered out before reaching the landing page directly reduces cost per qualified lead. Disqualification copy turns the ad itself into a self-selection mechanism, exploiting the fact that ad impressions are cheap but clicks are paid. Particularly important when bidding on broad commercial keywords where audience fit varies wildly.
