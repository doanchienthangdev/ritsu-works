# Cast wide with keywords, narrow in copy

When running PPC campaigns, the rule is: target broad keyword sets (high volume, lower bid contention) but write ad copy that explicitly disqualifies the wrong audience. Targeting alone cannot exclude every poor-fit click, but a single phrase in the headline ("Best for teams of 10+", "For your marketing team") converts the ad creative into a free filter that ad keywords cannot provide.

The decision rule favors broader-than-comfortable keyword coverage, because the qualifying filter is moved downstream into copy where it costs nothing per impression. Narrow keywords + narrow copy is double-filtering and leaves volume on the table; broad keywords + permissive copy bleeds budget on unqualified clicks. The optimal posture is broad keywords with surgical copy.
