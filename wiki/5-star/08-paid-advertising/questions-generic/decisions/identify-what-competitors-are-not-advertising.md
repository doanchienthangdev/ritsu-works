# Identify what competitors are NOT advertising

The highest-leverage finding from competitive ad research is usually a negative observation: keywords, audiences, objections, creative formats, customer segments, or messaging angles that competitors are NOT addressing. These represent uncontested attention — markets where you can compete without bidding against an incumbent's optimized auction position. The synthesis checklist explicitly elevates this beyond "what to copy" by asking: which gap can you exploit?

Operating principle: read the absence as data. After cataloguing what competitors are doing, list 3-5 things they're conspicuously not doing in your market — that's where the opportunity for differentiation lives. The default analyst bias is to focus on the visible (what competitors do); the discipline is to also list the invisible (what they don't).
