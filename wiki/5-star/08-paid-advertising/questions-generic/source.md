# Paid Advertising — Competitor Analysis Questions (Generic)

## Section A: Google Ads / PPC Competitor Analysis

### A1: Targeting Strategy & Keywords

1. What are the primary keyword themes your competitors bid on? (Branded, free tools, tripwire features, commercial)
2. Which competitor keywords have the highest search volume?
3. Which keywords do your competitors bid on but you don't? Why might that be?
4. What is the average CPC your competitor is paying across all keywords?
5. Which keyword group has the highest CPC? Why?
6. Which keyword group has the lowest CPC? Why?
7. Are your competitors bidding on competitor brand names? Which ones?
8. How aggressively are competitors bidding on their own branded terms?
9. What free tools or lead generators are competitors using as entry-point keywords?
10. Are there specific product features competitors are targeting that you haven't considered?
11. What tripwire keywords (individual features as keywords) do competitors prioritize?
12. Do competitors' mobile keyword strategy differ from desktop? How?
13. Are competitors bidding on comparative keywords ("Alternative to X", "X vs Y")? Which ones?
14. What long-tail keywords are competitors neglecting? (Opportunity for you)
15. What is the search volume for each competitor keyword theme?

### A2: Landing Page Alignment & Relevance

16. When you click competitor ads, where do they send each keyword? (Same landing page for all, or unique pages?)
17. Is the landing page relevant to the keyword intent? (Or missed optimization opportunity?)
18. Are competitors using separate landing pages for mobile vs desktop?
19. Do competitors send branded keywords to comparison pages? (Or defensive/education page?)
20. Are there examples where competitor ads don't match landing page intent? (Your competitive advantage?)
21. What is on each competitor's landing page? (Product tour, testimonials, free trial, pricing?)
22. How quickly can you understand the value prop on each landing page?
23. Are landing pages optimized for specific demographics? (Enterprise vs SMB, technical vs non-technical?)

### A3: Ad Creative & Messaging

24. What are the top 3 USPs each competitor emphasizes in their ads?
25. Do competitors lead with features or benefits in ad copy? What's the split?
26. What objections are competitors trying to handle in their ad copy?
27. What disqualification language do they use? ("For your X", "Best for X+")
28. How many action buttons/CTAs does each ad have? (Single or multiple?)
29. What CTA button text do competitors use? ("Learn more", "Try free", "Get demo", "Buy now")
30. What social proof elements appear in competitor ads? (Reviews, user count, testimonials, awards)
31. What emotional triggers do competitors use? (Urgency, fear, aspiration, community)
32. Are competitors using video ads? What do the videos show? (Demo, testimonial, animation)
33. Do competitors emphasize pricing in their ads? Or do they hide it?
34. What pain points do competitor ads mention specifically?
35. How long is the average ad copy? (Short punchy vs detailed)
36. Do competitors use first-person ("I") or second-person ("You") or third-person language?
37. What guarantees or reassurances do they offer? ("Money-back", "No setup fee", "Easy migration")
38. Are competitor ads text-heavy or minimalist?

### A4: Ad Format & Platform

39. What ad formats are competitors using? (Text, expanded text, shopping, discovery, video)
40. Do they use single-image ads or carousel ads? What's the split?
41. Are they running Google Shopping ads? If so, which products?
42. Do they use responsive search ads? How many variations?
43. How often do competitor ads update? (Weekly, monthly, seasonally)

### A5: Spend & Budget Patterns

44. What is your estimate of each competitor's monthly PPC spend?
45. Is competitor spending increasing or flat over the last 6-12 months?
46. Are there seasonal spikes in competitor spending? (When and why?)
47. What percentage of their budget is branded vs non-branded keywords?
48. What percentage of budget is on free tools vs direct product keywords?
49. Have you seen competitor campaigns suddenly spike then drop? (Tested and abandoned?)
50. Do competitors spend more on desktop or mobile PPC?
51. Which products/services receive the most PPC budget from competitors?
52. If competitor spend is decreasing, what might they be shifting to? (SEO, content, other channels)
53. What does competitor spend tell you about product profitability? (Margins and ROI)
54. Are there products competitors bid on that might be high-margin/low-volume?

### A6: Synthesis & Strategic Insights (Google Ads)

55. What is the competitor's overall PPC strategy? (Acquisition, defense, education, product-specific)
56. Which competitor's PPC approach would you most want to replicate? Why?
57. Which competitor's PPC approach would you avoid? Why?
58. What is your biggest competitive advantage vs their PPC strategy?
59. What gaps exist in their keyword strategy that you could exploit?
60. If you had 20% of their PPC budget, where would you focus to compete?
61. Are they winning with expensive keywords, cheap keywords, or both?
62. What's one PPC tactic they use that's working for them that you should test?
63. What's one PPC tactic they're using that looks inefficient/wasteful?
64. How would you position against their messaging in PPC ads?

## Section B: Facebook & Instagram Ad Analysis

### B1: Finding & Accessing Competitor Ads

65. Where do you find competitor Facebook/Instagram ads? (Which tool?)
66. Can you see exact targeting parameters in Facebook Ad Library? (Or do you have to deduce them?)
67. What information CAN you see about competitor ads in Ad Library?
68. What information is HIDDEN from Facebook Ad Library?
69. How far back can you see historical ads in Facebook Ad Library?
70. Do different businesses have different transparency in Ad Library? (E.g., nonprofits vs for-profits)

### B2: Deducing Targeting from Ad Content

71. When you see a "brand-specific" ad (e.g., "Discover HubSpot CRM"), what audience are they likely targeting? (Cold, warm, retargeting?)
72. What does "Your next haul" language tell you about the audience? (Retargeting signal?)
73. What does "15% off your first purchase" tell you about the audience?
74. When you see ads for specific features (e.g., "LinkedIn integration"), what targeting audience does that suggest?
75. When you see competitor ads with competitor names in them, what audience are they targeting?
76. Are competitors running ads with explicit job titles? Which titles?
77. Are competitors mentioning specific industries in ads? Which ones?
78. What behavioral targeting might competitor ads suggest? (Career-focused, budget-conscious, tech-savvy)
79. Do you see different audiences for different product lines? (Or single audience for all products)
80. Are competitors running geographically targeted ads? How do you know?
81. What interests do you think competitors are using to target? (Based on ad content)
82. Are there lookalike audience signals in competitor ads? (Based on messaging)
83. Which competitor ads seem to be "cold" (broad) prospecting vs "warm" (retargeting)?

### B3: Understanding Platform & Format Diversity

84. Is the competitor running different ads on Facebook vs Instagram?
85. Are they running Stories ads? Reels ads? Feed ads? Combination?
86. How does the messaging differ between Stories and Feed ads? (If both exist)
87. Are they running messenger ads? What's the format?
88. Are they in Audience Network? How can you tell?
89. What does the prevalence of certain formats tell you about their audience? (E.g., heavy video = younger audience)

### B4: Ad Creative Analysis & Production Quality

90. Are competitor ads primarily UGC (user-generated) or professional photography?
91. If using UGC, how many variations do they have? (Suggests budget and testing)
92. What's the video/image split? Is one format dominant?
93. Do they use carousel ads? What's typically shown in carousels?
94. What's the design aesthetic? (Minimalist, busy, brand-heavy, clean)
95. Are images text-heavy or visual-only?
96. Do you see split testing signals? (Same message, different creative or vice versa)
97. How frequently are they updating creative? (Weekly, monthly, seasonal?)
98. What's the production quality level? (Stock photos, professional, custom design, UGC)
99. Are they using video? What type? (Demo, testimonial, lifestyle, animation)
100. How much text is typically overlaid on images?

### B5: Primary Messages & USPs

101. What are the top 3 USPs in competitor ads?
102. Do they emphasize features or benefits?
103. What pain points are explicitly mentioned?
104. What's the dominant positioning? (Lowest cost, easiest, most feature-rich, best support, fastest)
105. Are they using social proof? (User count, testimonials, reviews, awards)
106. Are they using urgency/scarcity messaging? ("Limited time", "Only X left")
107. What objections are they trying to overcome in ads?
108. Do different ads have different messaging, or is it consistent?
109. What value proposition is emphasized most? (Price, quality, ease, speed, results)
110. How does their messaging differ from what they say on their website?

### B6: Identifying Long-Running Controls

111. What's the oldest ad currently running for each competitor?
112. How long has it been running? (Month long-running ads are controls)
113. What's special about their long-running ads vs newer ones?
114. Why do you think this ad is still running after so long?
115. What messaging do the long-running ads have in common?
116. Should you replicate this ad's approach, or is it not applicable to you?

### B7: Ad Frequency & Testing Intensity

117. How many ad variations is each competitor currently running?
118. Are they updating ads daily, weekly, or monthly?
119. Do you see multiple variants testing the same angle? (Heavy testing = heavy budget)
120. What does the frequency of new ads tell you about their budget?
121. Are they seasonal in their testing? (More ads in certain months)
122. What new angles/messages are they testing right now?

### B8: Spend & Budget Estimation (Facebook/Instagram)

123. Based on creative quality and quantity, what's your estimate of their monthly social ad budget?
124. Are there visibility metrics that suggest budget? (Frequency of seeing ads, number of variants)
125. Do they have the budget to test extensively or are they optimizing limited budget?
126. Is this competitor's social budget likely their primary channel or supplementary?
127. Where do you think the majority of their advertising budget is spent? (PPC, social, traditional, email)

### B9: Retargeting vs Prospecting Analysis

128. What percentage of competitor ads appear to be prospecting vs retargeting?
129. Are they more aggressive on retargeting or prospecting?
130. What's their retargeting sequence/journey? (First touch → Second touch → Conversion)
131. Do they have different messaging for each retargeting step?
132. What's the likely CAC on prospecting ads vs retargeting ads? (Retargeting usually cheaper)
133. Are they using dynamic retargeting (showing products the user viewed) or static retargeting?

### B10: Channel-Specific Strategy

134. Are they prioritizing Facebook or Instagram?
135. What percentage of ads appear on each channel?
136. Why might they have that channel split? (Audience, goals, performance)
137. Are Stories ads a significant part of their strategy or minor testing?
138. Are they using Messenger ads? For what purpose?
139. How does messaging change between channels?

### B11: Synthesis & Strategic Insights (Facebook/Instagram)

140. What's the overall social advertising strategy? (Awareness, conversion, community, retention)
141. Are they customer acquisition-focused or retention-focused?
142. What's their primary customer lifecycle stage being targeted? (Awareness, consideration, decision)
143. Which competitor's social approach would you most want to replicate? Why?
144. Which competitor's social approach would you avoid? Why?
145. What's your biggest competitive advantage vs their social strategy?
146. What creative approach are they using that's working well?
147. What messaging angle would differentiate you from their approach?
148. If you had 1/10 of their social budget, where would you focus?
149. What's one social tactic they're using that you could test immediately?
150. What's one assumption about their audience that you could test against?

## Section C: B2B vs B2C Specific Analysis

### C1: B2B-Specific Questions

151. Are the competitors in a B2B space? How does that change their strategy?
152. What's the decision-making journey? (Who's the buyer vs end-user?)
153. Are they targeting specific job titles? Which ones?
154. Are they mentioning company size? ("For teams of X+")
155. Are they leading with ROI/business metrics or ease/simplicity?
156. How much content/education is in their ads vs direct product pitches?
157. Are they using case studies or customer testimonials in ads?
158. Are they targeting specific industries? Which industries are prioritized?
159. How long do you estimate their sales cycle to be, based on ad messaging?
160. Are they focusing on replacing a competitor or solving a new problem?

### C2: B2C-Specific Questions

161. Are the competitors in a B2C space? How does that change their strategy?
162. Is the purchase decision individual or household? What does that imply?
163. What's the impulse vs considered purchase dynamic? (How much urgency in ads?)
164. Are they focusing on new customer acquisition or repeat purchases?
165. What role does social proof play vs brand recognition?
166. Are they using discounts/promotions as primary motivation?
167. How much do they emphasize convenience/ease of purchase?
168. Are there seasonal patterns in their advertising? What drives them?
169. What's the likely average order value based on their messaging?
170. Are they driving to immediate purchase or email list capture?

## Section D: Competitive Positioning & Differentiation

### D1: Gaps & Opportunities

171. What keywords/audiences are competitors NOT targeting that might be opportunity?
172. What messaging angles aren't being used by competitors?
173. What creative formats aren't being used? (E.g., nobody using video - opportunity)
174. What price point are they establishing? (Premium, mid-market, budget)
175. What's the primary buying motivation in competitor ads? (Price, speed, quality, support, innovation)
176. What's NOT being emphasized? (Might be a customer priority they're ignoring)
177. Are there underserved customer segments in their ads?
178. What objection are they NOT addressing in ads? (Opportunity to address it)
179. What's the biggest gap between their ads and their actual product/service?
180. If a customer saw their ad but was skeptical, what would convince them? (Your opportunity)

### D2: Benchmarking Against Competitors

181. What's the baseline CPC in your space based on competitor spend?
182. What's the baseline CAC based on estimated spend and conversions?
183. What conversion rate do competitors likely need to justify their spend?
184. How does your CAC compare to what competitors are likely paying?
185. What's the minimum product price/margin that justifies their ad spend?
186. Are competitors' ads above or below the fold on most placements? (Budget tells this story)
187. How does your ad quality compare to competitors? Be honest.
188. How does your messaging clarity compare to theirs?
189. How does your targeting precision compare to theirs? (Or estimate)
190. How does your brand familiarity compare? (Affects conversion rates)

### E1: What to Copy

191. What's one PPC tactic from competitors that's clearly working well?
192. What's one social creative approach that's getting the most visibility?
193. What's one messaging angle that appears across multiple competitors? (Validated approach)
194. What's one landing page design approach worth replicating?
195. What's one funnel sequence you could test?

### E2: What to Avoid

196. What's a competitor tactic that looks expensive but unproven?
197. What's a competitor approach that doesn't align with your brand?
198. What's a messaging angle that seems off-brand for you?
199. What's a channel that competitors are on but you shouldn't be?
200. What's a customer segment competitors are targeting that you shouldn't?

### E3: Your Differentiation

201. What's a genuine advantage you have over competitors that you're not emphasizing?
202. What's a pain point competitors aren't addressing that customers care about?
203. What's a different approach to a problem competitors are solving?
204. What's your unique positioning statement vs how competitors position?
205. What's one thing you can offer that competitors can't?

### E4: Budget Allocation

206. Based on competitor spend, what's a realistic budget for your market?
207. If you started with 10% of competitor budgets, where would you allocate?
208. What's the priority: PPC or social for your product/service?
209. What percentage of budget should go to prospecting vs retargeting?
210. What percentage of budget should go to branded vs non-branded?

### E5: Measurement & Success

211. What KPI should you track based on competitor benchmarks?
212. What's a realistic CAC target based on competitors?
213. What's a realistic ROAS target based on competitors?
214. What conversion rate should you target?
215. How will you know if you're winning against competitors? (Which metrics matter most)
