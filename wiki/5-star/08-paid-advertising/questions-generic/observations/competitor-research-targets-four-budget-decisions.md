# Competitor research targets four budget decisions

Every paid-ad competitor research effort ultimately feeds four budget-allocation decisions: (1) realistic budget level for the market based on observed competitor spend; (2) PPC vs social split based on which channels competitors prioritize; (3) prospecting vs retargeting split based on observed retargeting volume; (4) branded vs non-branded split based on competitor's defensive vs growth posture. The 215-question generic competitor checklist is organized around these decision endpoints — each section either reads a competitor signal or maps that signal to your own allocation.

Practical consequence: don't research everything. If you already know your channel mix and just need a CAC target, jump directly to the spend/budget sections. Research scope should be sized to the decisions you actually need to make.
