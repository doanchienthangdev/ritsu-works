# Narrowing your audience increases your addressable market

The counter-intuitive claim: when a business narrows its audience, it often *expands* the people who actually buy, because the people attracted become passionate advocates who refer others similar to themselves. Trying to serve everyone produces bland messaging that resonates with no one; targeting a specific segment lets messaging speak directly to a person's fears, desires, and language, which compounds via word-of-mouth.

The mechanism is referral-loop driven: passionate niche advocates have high-leverage networks within their segment. Generic messaging cannot trigger that same loop because it has no specific person to mobilize. This makes narrowness a growth strategy, not a constraint.

Aura Ring is cited as a concrete instance: rather than positioning as "a wearable for anyone who wants to track their health," they positioned for "health-conscious, tech-savvy individuals who want invisible health tracking." The narrower positioning gave them dominance in that segment.
