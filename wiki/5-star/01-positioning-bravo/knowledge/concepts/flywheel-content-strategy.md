# Flywheel content strategy

The Flywheel concept is that each piece of positioning content compounds over time rather than being consumed once and discarded. Every piece simultaneously (1) provides immediate value to the audience, (2) builds the business's authority, (3) generates discovery via search/sharing/referrals, (4) builds trust, and (5) moves potential customers closer to buying. Over time the content gets more discoverable, authority increases, audience grows, and trust deepens — each piece accelerating the next.

This is positioned as the *opposite* of tactical, disposable content. Tactical content (weekly social posts, one-off promotions) generates noise. Flywheel content (ultimate guides, frameworks and mental models, case studies, original research, tutorials, thought leadership) generates signal. The discipline is to invest creator time in high-compound formats even when their short-term metrics underperform tactical posts.

The reason this lives inside the positioning module rather than a content module: content reveals what a business knows, how it thinks, whether it understands its audience, and what it actually values — so content either reinforces positioning or undermines it. The Flywheel is therefore positioning, not just distribution.
