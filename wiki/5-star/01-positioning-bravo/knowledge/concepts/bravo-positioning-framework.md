# BRAVO Positioning Framework

BRAVO is Exposure Ninja's five-component model of positioning, used as the first "star" of their 5-Star Marketing System. The five components are interconnected: Brand (identity, personality, promise), Reason for Existing (the "why" beyond profit), Audience (who you serve and who you don't), Voice (how you communicate), Offering (what you're actually selling). The framework treats positioning as a multiplier on every downstream tactic — content, ads, sales, retention — so positioning must be set deliberately before tactics scale.

The components must be aligned with each other. A luxury brand with a casual voice feels incongruent; a budget product with premium visual design creates confusion. The five letters are not a checklist to fill in separately — they are a single coherent posture the customer perceives.
