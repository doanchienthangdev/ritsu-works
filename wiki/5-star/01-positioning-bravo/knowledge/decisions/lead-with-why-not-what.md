# Lead positioning with WHY, not WHAT

Simon Sinek's Golden Circle prescribes ordering communication as WHY (purpose, belief), then HOW (process), then WHAT (product). Most businesses default to leading with WHAT ("We sell marketing software") because the product feels most concrete. The framework rules that this default is weak positioning. Strong positioning leads with WHY ("We help small businesses compete with larger competitors by making enterprise-level marketing accessible and affordable").

The reasoning is that customers don't buy what a business does — they buy why it does it. WHY-led communication recruits customers whose values align with the business, which produces higher loyalty, willingness to pay premium prices, and word-of-mouth referrals. WHAT-led communication invites pure feature comparison, which collapses into price competition.

Caveat from the source: the WHY cannot be fabricated. Customers detect inauthenticity immediately. A founder must already hold the WHY before leading with it.
