## Implementation Notes for Ritsu

**Landing page should launch with:**
- Hero with clear value prop + CTA
- How it works section (visual + text)
- 2-3 strong student testimonials (even if beta users)
- Simple signup form (email + password)
- Mobile-optimized (90%+ of traffic)
- Email nurture sequence (welcome, how to get started, tips, case study, re-engagement)

**Don't launch with:**
- Overpromising (claims not backed by data)
- Unclear CTAs or multiple competing goals
- Heavy content that requires reading paragraphs
- Complex onboarding before first use

**First month after launch:**
- Measure: conversion rate, bounce rate, scroll depth, form completion rate
- Test: headline, CTA button text, hero image
- Optimize: mobile experience if lagging
- Collect: student feedback (what confused them? what excited them?)

**Second month after launch:**
- Email nurture optimization based on engagement
- Viral loop testing (does share feature work?)
- Freemium boundary testing (is free tier attractive? are students upgrading?)
- Content additions (FAQ based on support questions, testimonials from active users)

The landing page is not your product—it's the first chapter of the student experience. Every promise made on the page should be matched by the product itself.
