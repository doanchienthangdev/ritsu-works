# CLI-to-Landing-Page Visual Incongruence Risk for Ritsu

A latent congruence trap specific to Ritsu's current state: the **product is CLI-based** (commands like `/quiz`, `/explain`, rich terminal-style visual output), but a typical "study app" landing page hero would show a student studying peacefully with coffee — a visually calm aesthetic that does not match the actual product surface.

The mismatch creates a double risk:
1. A student arrives via TikTok ad ("AI creates your study materials") expecting a polished mobile app, lands, sees CLI screenshots, and bounces (visual mismatch with ad).
2. A student who *does* sign up after a calming hero finds an actually technical CLI experience, feels deceived, and churns within the first session.

Two possible resolutions to test:
- **Match the CLI aesthetic**: lean into the terminal/CLI look on the landing page, position Ritsu as the "developer-grade AI tutor" (narrower but more honest).
- **Ship a polished UI before the landing page launches**: invest in a mobile/web wrapper before any paid acquisition so the landing page's visual promise matches what signup actually delivers.

This is filed as an **idea** rather than a decision because the resolution depends on Ritsu's roadmap (is a mobile/web client landing before or after the marketing site?). Worth surfacing during the next product-marketing alignment session.
