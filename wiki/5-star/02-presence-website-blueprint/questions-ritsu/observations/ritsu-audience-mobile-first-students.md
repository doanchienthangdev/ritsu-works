# Ritsu Audience is 80-90% Mobile-First Students

The Ritsu-specific question deck explicitly anchors landing-page strategy around the claim that **90%+ of Ritsu's student users are on mobile** (with a separate question framing 80%+). This is a sharper mobile dominance than the generic Blueprint baseline of "60%+" — meaning Ritsu cannot treat mobile as parity-with-desktop; mobile is **the** experience.

Direct consequences for the eventual ritsu.ai landing page:
- The "Sign Up Free" button must be at least 48px tap target and visible without scrolling on iPhone viewports.
- Any CLI screenshot carousel must not require horizontal scrolling.
- Pricing tables must reflow (not squish) on narrow viewports.
- Hero video must be optimized (not a 50MB file) — college WiFi and cellular can be slow.
- If mobile conversion lags desktop, mobile optimization is the #1 priority and "headline A/B testing" is premature optimization.

This observation is best read as a Ritsu-specific specialization of the generic "mobile is primary" observation in this same package.
