# Ritsu Landing Page Must Differentiate from ChatGPT-for-Students

**Ruling**: A primary student objection to any new AI study tool is *"Is this just ChatGPT?"* The Ritsu landing page must crystallize the differentiation in the hero — not bury it three sections down. Differentiators to surface:

- Ritsu **transforms textbooks/PDFs/lecture materials into interactive learning activities** (`/quiz`, `/explain`, `/summarize`) — it ingests the student's own material, not a generic prompt.
- Personalization angle: Ritsu **learns what each student struggles with** — a key difference from generic chat tools.
- Persistent study workflow versus one-off prompt-paste-answer.

The decision binds two adjacent concerns from the question deck:
- **Congruence**: positioning must answer "what makes Ritsu different from Quizlet, ChatGPT, or other study tools?" in the hero, consistently echoed in CTAs, testimonials, and FAQ.
- **Content**: the "How It Works" section should explain the **student outcome** (upload textbook → get quizzes → study smarter), not the technical method (AI analyzes content, generates quizzes).

If a student reads the landing page and walks away thinking "this is ChatGPT with a different skin," conversion fails regardless of how good the freemium offer is.
