# Every CTA Must Describe What Happens Next

**Ruling**: CTA button text must explicitly state what happens after the click — not "Submit," not "Next," not "Learn More" as a primary action. Use action + outcome language: "Start Learning Now" beats "Submit"; "Book Your Call" beats "Next"; "Click to get free trial" beats "Learn More."

Additional constraints:
- Every page must have at least one crystal-clear CTA, visible above the fold.
- Visually prominent (contrasting color, larger size).
- Multiple CTAs (top, middle, bottom) are acceptable but must direct to the same action — never compete with each other.
- CTA text should match visitor intent at that stage of the journey: early CTAs can be "learn more," later CTAs are "start now."

The principle: ambiguity at the conversion point is friction. A visitor who hesitates "what will pressing this button do?" is a visitor about to leave.
