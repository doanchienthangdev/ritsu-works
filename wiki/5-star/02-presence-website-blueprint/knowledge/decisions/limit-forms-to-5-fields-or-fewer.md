# Limit Conversion Forms to 5 Fields or Fewer

**Ruling**: Fewer fields equals higher completion rates. Each additional field costs roughly **10-20% of completions**, and target form-completion rate is **50%+**. Strip every field that is not strictly essential for the conversion you're driving.

Supporting tactics:
- Label fields clearly (above or inside the input box).
- Validation errors must be specific and helpful, not vague.
- Multi-step forms need progress indicators.
- Enable auto-fill; use mobile-friendly input types.
- No "required" fields that aren't truly required.
- Clear privacy statement near email fields.
- Confirmation page after submission + auto-responder with next steps.

The decision is harder than it looks: every internal stakeholder will lobby for "just one more field" (region, company size, source). Hold the line. If a field is valuable, capture it later in the lifecycle — never at the moment of conversion.
