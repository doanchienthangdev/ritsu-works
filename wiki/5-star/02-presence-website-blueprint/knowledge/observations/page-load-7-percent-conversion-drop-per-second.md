# Each Second of Page Load Time Reduces Conversion ~7%

Page speed has a measurable, linear cost on conversion: studies cited by the Heavy Lifting Website Blueprint show approximately a **7% conversion drop per additional second** of load time. The performance target is **under 3 seconds to full interactivity**, with a Google Lighthouse score of **80+**. Levers to hit that target: image optimization (compression + WebP), lazy loading for below-the-fold content, minimizing render-blocking JavaScript/CSS, a CDN for global speed, and browser caching for repeat visits.

The implication is that page speed is not a "technical hygiene" concern but a primary conversion lever — equal in business impact to copy, CTA design, or social proof. A site that takes 6 seconds to load is leaving roughly 21% of conversions on the table relative to a 3-second baseline.
