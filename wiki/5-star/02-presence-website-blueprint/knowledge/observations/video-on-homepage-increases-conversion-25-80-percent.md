# Video on Homepage Can Increase Conversion 25-80%

The Heavy Lifting Website Blueprint cites that **homepage video can lift conversion 25-80%** depending on quality. Mechanisms: video increases time on page; visitors see a face and hear a voice (trust); product demo videos reduce support questions and increase conversions; testimonial videos are materially more credible than text testimonials.

Constraints for the lift to actually materialize:
- Video must be **short** (under 2 minutes for most use cases).
- Auto-play must be muted (un-muted auto-play annoys visitors and inverts the effect).
- Video must have a **clear CTA** appended ("Click below to start your trial") — video without a next step is engagement waste.
- Hosted on CDN or YouTube (never unoptimized file uploads).
- YouTube transcripts double as SEO value.

The implication: for a B2C consumer product with a non-obvious value proposition, building a 60-90 second demo video is among the highest-ROI marketing investments available.
