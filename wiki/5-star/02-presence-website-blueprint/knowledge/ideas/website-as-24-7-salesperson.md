# Website as 24/7 Salesperson, Not Digital Brochure

The Blueprint's framing move is to reposition the website itself as the company's **most efficient salesperson** rather than a passive brochure. The salesperson framing yields specific design properties:

- Available around the clock (no time-zone gaps in the sales funnel).
- Never tired, never inconsistent (every visitor sees the same A-game pitch).
- Serves thousands simultaneously (no scaling cost per additional conversation).
- Continuously improving through data (A/B tests + heat maps + session recordings).
- Turns cold traffic into warm leads (lead magnets + email capture).
- Builds trust before any conversation begins (social proof + educational content).

This reframe matters because most founders allocate website investment as "marketing brochure budget" (one-time creative spend) rather than "sales hire investment" (ongoing optimization, like you'd train a real rep). The latter mental model produces websites that compound; the former produces digital pamphlets.

For Ritsu: the implication is that the eventual ritsu.ai landing page should be treated as a hired AE — measured against signup-to-active conversion, retrained quarterly, and never "finished."
