# Bridge Congruence (Ad-to-Landing-Page Message Match)

**Bridge congruence** is the specific congruence rule that the headline of a paid-ad landing page must **exactly match** the headline of the ad that brought the visitor. The rule extends to language choice: if an ad says "affordable," the page must say "affordable" — not "cost-effective." If the ad calls out a pain point, the page must address that exact pain point, not a different one.

The cost of bridge mismatch is high because it triggers cognitive dissonance at the exact moment of highest commercial intent (paid click landed). Each mismatch is a small friction point; multiple friction points accumulate into abandonment. The corollary: every ad creative needs its own purpose-built landing page (or a landing page section/variant), not a generic homepage.
