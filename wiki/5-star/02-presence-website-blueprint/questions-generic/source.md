# Heavy Lifting Website Blueprint — Generic Deep Questions

## Purpose
These questions are designed to probe deeply into all aspects of the 4 C's framework and website optimization. Use them for:
- Comprehensive website audits
- Strategic planning sessions
- Identifying bottlenecks
- Testing assumptions
- Designing solutions
- Measuring progress

Answer honestly and specifically (not yes/no). Scoring 70%+ on these indicates a strong website.

---

## CONGRUENCE — Consistency and Alignment (20 questions)

### Brand and Visual Congruence
1. **What is your core positioning (BRAVO format)?** How consistently does every element of your website reinforce this exact positioning?

2. **What is your visual brand identity** (colors, fonts, imagery style)? Are all pages using these elements consistently, or are there rogue pages with different colors/fonts?

3. **If a new visitor lands on page 3 of your site without seeing page 1, would they immediately recognize your brand?** What would make recognition instant?

4. **On a scale of 1-10, how "premium" does your website design feel?** How does this match your positioning? (Premium brand with cheap design = incongruence)

5. **How consistent is your brand across desktop, mobile, tablet, and dark mode?** Where is consistency breaking?

6. **Describe the typical customer's first impression** when landing on your site. Does their impression match your positioning promise?

### Messaging Congruence
7. **What is your single, most important promise** to customers? Does this promise appear in the same words on your homepage, about page, pricing page, and main CTA?

8. **For each paid ad campaign, does the landing page headline match the ad headline exactly?** Where is there mismatch?

9. **Do your testimonials and social proof reinforce your positioning, or do they tell a different story?** Example: if you position as "premium," do testimonials talk about price or about quality?

10. **If you describe your product as "simple" in ads, do website pages use the word "simple" or do they use "streamlined," "efficient," "frictionless," etc.?** Consistency?

11. **What pain points do your ads mention? Are these same pain points addressed on your landing pages?** Or are different problems discussed?

12. **Tone of voice: Is your brand voice consistent across all pages?** (Corporate → Corporate, conversational → conversational, technical → technical)

### Experience Congruence
13. **Does your mobile website experience deliver the same core message as desktop?** Or have elements been cut that weaken the message?

14. **Do all forms on your website behave the same way?** Same field labels, validation messages, success pages?

15. **If a visitor navigates your site, do they encounter any "surprises" (design inconsistencies, broken elements, different navigation)?** What would they notice?

16. **Is page load time consistent across pages?** Do some pages feel dramatically slower than others?

17. **Does every page maintain your website's information architecture** (header, footer, navigation in expected places)?

### Claim vs. Reality Congruence
18. **If you claim "24/7 support," how quickly do your contact forms actually get responses?** Is the reality matching the claim?

19. **If you claim "fastest in the industry," what is your actual page load time?** Is it actually fast, or is this positioning without backing?

20. **List 3 claims you make about your product (speed, ease, quality, price, etc.). For each one, what is the actual measurable reality?** Is there a gap?

## CONVENIENCE — Removal of Friction (22 questions)

### Calls to Action
21. **What is your primary conversion goal?** Is there a single, clear CTA that matches this goal on every page?

22. **Describe your primary CTA button.** Does the text describe what happens next (e.g., "Get Free Trial" vs. "Submit")?

23. **Is your primary CTA visible without scrolling** (above the fold on desktop and mobile)?

24. **How many different CTA variations exist on your site?** (E.g., "Start Free Trial" vs. "Begin Now" vs. "Claim Your Trial") Should they be unified?

25. **For each page, what is the visitor supposed to do next?** Is that action obvious?

### Navigation and Findability
26. **Count your main navigation items.** Is the list 5-7 items (good), under 5 (risky—important things hidden), or over 7 (overwhelming)?

27. **If you asked 5 random visitors to find [specific page], could they do it in 3 clicks or fewer?** What page is hardest to find?

28. **Is your navigation the same on mobile and desktop?** Where does mobile diverge?

29. **Do you have a site search function?** If yes, does it work well? If no, how many clicks to find a specific page?

30. **Are breadcrumb or progress indicators visible on nested pages?** Does the visitor know where they are in the site structure?

### Mobile Optimization
31. **What percentage of your traffic comes from mobile?** Is mobile optimization a priority, or is it afterthought?

32. **On mobile, can a visitor read text without zooming?** What's the minimum font size on your site?

33. **On mobile, are buttons at least 48px tall/wide?** Test tapping each button with your thumb—is it comfortable?

34. **Does horizontal scrolling ever occur on mobile?** Where?

35. **Are forms mobile-friendly?** Do they use appropriate input types (email keyboard, number pad, date picker)?

### Page Speed
36. **What is your current page load time?** (Measure with Google PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse, or GTmetrix)

37. **If page load increased by 1 second, what would be the estimated conversion impact?** (Usually -7% per second)

38. **Which pages load slowest, and why?** (Video, images, scripts, tracking codes?)

39. **Are images optimized?** (Correct dimensions, compressed, modern format like WebP?)

40. **Do you use a CDN to serve assets?** Does caching improve repeat-visitor load times?

### Contact and Support
41. **How many contact methods are available?** (Phone, email, form, live chat, social?) Are they all visible?

42. **What is your actual response time** for contact form submissions? Is it matching customer expectations?

43. **Is live chat available?** What are the business hours? Do visitors know them?

44. **Do you have a booking/calendar link** for scheduling calls or meetings?

45. **Does each contact method have a clear expectation set?** (E.g., "We'll respond within 2 hours" vs. ambiguous)

### Form and Conversion Friction
46. **What is your longest form on the site?** How many fields? Could any be removed or made optional?

47. **If you made your conversion form one field shorter, how would that impact completion rates?** Have you tested?

48. **Are form validations helpful** (clear error messages) or frustrating (vague errors)?

49. **Can visitors complete checkout as a guest**, or must they create an account?

50. **For checkout/payment, are all costs (shipping, tax) clear before final submission?**

51. **Do you have a confirmation page** after form submission, or do visitors wonder if it went through?

52. **Is there an auto-responder email** confirming form submission and setting expectations?

### Distraction Reduction
53. **List all the CTA buttons visible on your homepage.** Are they too many (confusing) or strategically placed?

54. **Are there external links on your conversion-focused pages?** Do they open in a new window or pull visitors away?

55. **Are there any auto-play videos, sounds, or pop-ups that might startle visitors?**

56. **On your blog, do sidebar widgets link to external sites**, or do they stay internal?

57. **Do your pop-ups feel helpful or intrusive?** What would make them better?

## CONTENT — Education and Persuasion (20 questions)

### Overall Content Strategy
58. **What is your #1 topic or core expertise area?** Is your website a clear authority on this topic?

59. **How much content does your site have?** (Pages, blog posts, guides, videos) Is it 50 pages+ or less?

60. **When was the oldest piece of content last updated?** Are you regularly refreshing and updating?

61. **What percentage of your traffic comes from organic search?** (Indicates content effectiveness for SEO)

62. **What are the top 5 questions customers ask about your product/service?** Does your website answer all 5?

### Content Quality and Depth
63. **What is the word count of your main product/service page?** (1000+ is typically better for ranking and persuasion)

64. **How comprehensive is your FAQ section?** List the FAQs. Are they addressing real questions or marketing questions?

65. **Do your product descriptions focus on features or benefits?** Example: "30-second load time" (feature) vs. "never waste time waiting" (benefit)

66. **For each major feature, do you explain not just what it does, but why it matters to the customer?**

67. **Is there video content on key pages?** (Homepage, product page, pricing page?) Does video increase engagement?

68. **Do you have case studies or before/after examples?** How many?

### Customer Language and Objections
69. **Record calls with customers. What language do they use?** Is this language reflected on your website?

70. **What are the top 3 objections customers raise?** Are these objections directly addressed on your website?

71. **If a customer is price-sensitive, how does your website justify the price?** Is the value case clear?

72. **If customers compare you to competitors, what is your competitive advantage?** Does your website make this clear (without directly bashing competitors)?

73. **What customer fears or concerns exist?** (Security, learning curve, commitment, quality?) Does your content address these?

74. **What transformations happen from using your product?** Are these transformations showcased (before/after)?

### Content Organization and SEO
75. **Is your content structured with proper headings (H1, H2, H3)?** Or is it just paragraph text?

76. **Do you have a content "hub and spoke" strategy** where one main topic links to related subtopics?

77. **Are there internal links connecting related content?** Or is each page isolated?

78. **Do your blog posts link back to product pages?** Is there a clear conversion path from content → product?

79. **What are your target keywords?** Are these keywords naturally woven into content, or keyword-stuffed?

80. **Do you have meta descriptions for key pages?** (Title: compelling, keyword-rich; Meta: click-worthy summary)

## CONTROL — Guiding the Journey (18 questions)

### Conversion Funnel and Journey
81. **Draw your ideal customer journey through your website.** Where should they land, what should they see, what action should they take?

82. **What is the actual customer journey** (based on analytics)? Does it match the ideal journey?

83. **Where do most visitors drop off?** (Analyze bounce rate and exit pages)

84. **For visitors who don't convert on first visit, what is your follow-up system?** (Email, retargeting, chat)

85. **Do you have clear landing pages** for different traffic sources (ad campaign, email, organic search)?

86. **Are landing pages tightly focused** (single goal) or do they have multiple competing goals?

### Lead Capture and Nurture
87. **What is your lead magnet?** (Free guide, trial, webinar, etc.) How valuable is it?

88. **What is your email signup conversion rate?** (Visitors who provide email / total visitors)

89. **How many touch points exist for email capture?** (Popup, form, lead magnet, content download, etc.)

90. **When does your signup popup appear?** (Immediate? After scroll? Exit intent?) How are people responding?

91. **What happens after someone provides their email?** Is there a nurture sequence, or does the email go nowhere?

92. **How long is your email follow-up sequence?** (5 emails? 20 emails? 1 email?)

93. **Do you segment leads** based on how they came in? (Different sequences for different audiences?)

### Testing and Optimization
94. **Are you running A/B tests?** What elements are you testing? (Headline, CTA, image, form fields?)

95. **What is your testing philosophy?** (Continuous testing? Occasional tests? No testing?)

96. **What are your biggest testing wins?** What improved conversion rate by 5% or more?

97. **Do you have heat maps or session recordings** showing how visitors interact with pages?

98. **What do heat maps show?** (Click hotspots, scroll depth, rage clicks, confusion?)

99. **How do you use data to inform changes?** (Hypothesis-driven? Gut-driven? Data-driven?)

### Narrative Control and Retargeting
100. **What story does your homepage tell?** Can you summarize it in 3 sentences?

101. **How do you address the customer's internal narrative?** (The story they tell themselves about their problem/solution)

102. **Do you have retargeting ads running?** For which audiences? (Pricing page visitors? Cart abandoners? Past visitors?)

103. **What percentage of conversions come from returning visitors?** (Shows effectiveness of retargeting)

104. **Do you have exit-intent popups or strategies?** What's the offer to keep someone on site?

## INTERCONNECTIONS — How the 4 C's Work Together (12 questions)

105. **A visitor lands on a paid ad about "fast shipping." They click and see a page about "reliable delivery." How does this incongruence affect convenience, content, and control?** What happens to their conversion likelihood?

106. **You have great content (C3) but terrible convenience (C2).** Long forms, slow loading, confusing navigation. Will your content be enough?

107. **Your website is incongruent (C1): positioning is "premium" but design looks cheap.** No amount of content or convenience fixes this. Why does congruence come first?

108. **You control the narrative perfectly (C4), but your pricing is unclear (C3).** Interested visitor leaves because they can't find pricing. What's the impact?

109. **Ranking #1 for a keyword (C3 success) but bounce rate is 80% (C2 failure).** What could cause this? How do you fix it?

110. **Your email list grows fast (C4), but your nurture emails are poorly written (C3).** Does list size matter if email content is bad?

111. **You're A/B testing CTAs (C4), but your value proposition is unclear (C1).** Will testing small changes move the needle?

112. **Mobile convenience is poor (C2), but you have great content (C3).** Why might mobile still fail to convert?

113. **Your testimonials are inconsistent with your positioning (C1 failure).** A "premium" brand has testimonials about price. What's the damage?

114. **You control the journey (C4) beautifully, but the website loads in 10 seconds (C2).** Does journey control matter if people leave before pages load?

115. **Your conversion funnel has 7 steps (C4 failure).** You're requiring visitors to jump through hoops. How does this differ from poor convenience (C2)?

116. **You identify that 60% of traffic comes from mobile, but most optimization effort goes to desktop (C2).** What's missing?

## METRICS AND MEASUREMENT (14 questions)

117. **What is your current homepage bounce rate?** What should it be? Is it a problem?

118. **What is your current conversion rate?** How does it compare to industry benchmarks?

119. **Where is your conversion rate lowest?** (Which page or traffic source?)

120. **What percentage of visitors are returning vs. new?** Is this ratio healthy for your business stage?

121. **What is your average session duration?** Are visitors spending enough time to be persuaded?

122. **Which traffic source converts best?** (Organic? Paid? Direct?) Where should you invest?

123. **What is the cost per lead for your paid traffic?** Compare to customer lifetime value—is it profitable?

124. **What is your email list growth rate?** Is it accelerating or stagnating?

125. **Of visitors who sign up for email, what percentage eventually convert to customers?** (Email to customer conversion)

126. **What percentage of customers are repeat customers?** (Shows retention and loyalty)

127. **For visitors who come through ads, how does bounce rate compare to organic?** Why might there be a difference?

128. **What is your site's Google Lighthouse score?** (Target: 80+) How is it broken down (Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices)?

129. **For your funnel (landing → form → conversion), at what stage do most people drop off?** What's the drop-off percentage at each stage?

130. **If you could improve one metric by 10%, which would have the biggest business impact?** Focus there first.

---

## AUDIT SUMMARY (5 questions)

131. **On a scale of 1-10, how "heavy lifting" is your website currently?** What's holding it back?

132. **Of the 4 C's (Congruence, Convenience, Content, Control), which is your biggest weakness?** What's one change that would improve this?

133. **What is the highest-ROI change you could make to your website in the next 30 days?** What's stopping you?

134. **If you were to audit another competitor's website using these 134 questions, what would you notice?** How does yours compare?

135. **What is your website's primary purpose, and is it achieving that purpose?** If not, what's the biggest gap?

---

## Final Reflections

These 135 questions are designed to be comprehensive, not overwhelming. You don't need to answer all of them at once. Instead:

1. **Start with the 4 C's.** Spend 30 minutes on each, rating yourself 1-10.
2. **Identify the lowest-scoring C.** This is your biggest leverage point.
3. **Pick 5-10 questions from that section.** Answer them deeply.
4. **Brainstorm solutions.** What change would improve that C?
5. **Measure and iterate.** Implement one change, measure the result, improve.

Small, consistent improvements across all 4 areas compound into significant conversion gains.
