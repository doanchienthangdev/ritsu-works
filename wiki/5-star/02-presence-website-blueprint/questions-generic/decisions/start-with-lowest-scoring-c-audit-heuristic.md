# Start with the Lowest-Scoring C (Audit Sequencing Heuristic)

**Ruling**: When auditing a website against the 4 C's, the prescribed sequencing is:

1. Spend 30 minutes on each of the 4 C's and rate yourself 1-10.
2. **Identify the lowest-scoring C** — this is the biggest leverage point.
3. Pick 5-10 questions from that section and answer them deeply.
4. Brainstorm solutions: what one change would improve that C?
5. Measure and iterate. Implement one change, measure the result, improve.

The heuristic is grounded in the multiplicative nature of the 4 C's: small consistent improvements across all 4 areas compound, but the largest single-day lift comes from fixing whichever pillar is currently dragging the system down. A 9/10 on Content cannot rescue a 3/10 on Convenience — but lifting that 3 to a 6 doubles overall conversion potential.

Practical caveat: don't try to answer all 135 questions at once. Pick the bottom-scoring C, work 5-10 of its questions, ship one change, then re-audit.
