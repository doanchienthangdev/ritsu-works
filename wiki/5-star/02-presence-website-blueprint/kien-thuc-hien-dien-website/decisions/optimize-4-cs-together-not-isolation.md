# Optimize the 4 C's Together, Not in Isolation

**Ruling**: The four website pillars — Congruence, Convenience, Content, Control — are interconnected; weakness in any one area undermines the entire system. The best websites optimize all four areas **simultaneously**, not sequentially. A premium SaaS with strong positioning but a cheap-looking website (congruence failure) fails regardless of content quality. An e-commerce store with great content but a complex 5-step checkout (convenience failure) leaks conversions despite the persuasion.

Practical implication: a website audit should never report "we're strong on content, weak on control" as a strategy to ignore control. Each C is a multiplier; a 5/10 on any one drags overall performance to 5/10. Founder-allocation of effort should target whichever C scores lowest first.
