# Heavy Lifting Website

A "heavy lifting website" (Vietnamese: *website nặng công suất*) is a website framed as an active participant in the sales process — not a passive digital brochure. It works 24/7 to move visitors toward a decision: available around the clock, never tired, serving thousands simultaneously, continuously improving through data, turning cold traffic into warm leads, and building trust before any conversation begins. The Exposure Ninja claim is that the gap between a passive website and a heavy-lifting one is typically **2-3x in conversion rates** — meaning the website is one of the highest-leverage assets a company has, not a cost center.

The concept reframes the website from "marketing collateral" to "best-performing salesperson." This reframe drives every downstream decision in the 4 C's framework (Congruence, Convenience, Content, Control).
