# Top-of-Funnel Traffic is Virtually Untapped (VN Restatement)

The Vietnamese-language synthesis of Exposure Ninja's full-funnel playbook restates the strategic insight that top-of-funnel traffic — people who have the underlying problem your product solves but don't know your solution exists — is "gần như chưa được khai thác" (virtually untapped) in most markets. The reasons: nearly no competition on Awareness-stage keywords, CPC is 10-100x cheaper than bottom-funnel, and search volume is "lĩnh mị" (massive) because more people search for general problems than for specific solutions.

This observation matters for Ritsu's Vietnam-language SEO work specifically: Vietnamese-language Awareness-stage queries on study methodology (e.g., "cách học hiệu quả," "cách nhớ lâu," "phương pháp active recall") likely have even less English-language-funded competition than their English equivalents, since most international EdTech players run English-only content engines.
