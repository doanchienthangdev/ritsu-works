# Multi-Touch Attribution is Required to Measure Full-Funnel ROI

Single-touch attribution (first-touch or last-touch only) systematically under-credits Awareness-stage content in a full-funnel system, because the touch that "converts" the prospect is usually the Desire-stage demo booking or Action-stage signup — even though 5-15 prior touches across blog, email, and case studies built the trust that made conversion possible. The question framing in the 140-question study set (Q119-Q123) explicitly calls out attribution as a critical operational concern: tracking across content pieces, UTM parameters, and CRM integration is non-optional infrastructure.

Practical implication: any KPI dashboard that ranks content pieces by "conversions" alone will incorrectly conclude that Awareness content underperforms. A working full-funnel analytics setup must capture first-touch source, every intermediate touch, and last-touch — and weight them — to give honest credit to upper-funnel work.
