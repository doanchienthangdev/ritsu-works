# Ritsu Awareness Content Should Not Mention the Product

Applying the symptom-not-solution rule (see `decisions/target-symptom-not-solution-at-awareness`) to Ritsu specifically: Awareness-stage content for university students should target "how to study effectively," "how to remember what I read," "active recall techniques," "spaced repetition," "study hacks" — without naming Ritsu. The Ritsu-specific reference summary at the end of the questions-ritsu file makes this explicit: at Awareness stage, the student mindset is "I struggle with studying, but maybe I'm just not smart enough"; the best content types are "Blog posts, educational videos, free guides, learning science explainers"; the CTAs are "Download free guide," "Email course signup," "Study hacks checklist" — never "Try Ritsu Free."

A title like "7 Study Hacks Top Students Use" is, per the source, more effective than "Why Choose Ritsu" at Awareness stage, because the Awareness-stage student doesn't yet believe they need an AI study app and reads any product mention as a sales pitch. Product mentions belong at Desire stage and later.
