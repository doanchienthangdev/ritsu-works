# Ritsu's Buyer Journey is Medium-Length, Not Micro or Macro

The questions-ritsu source explicitly flags that Ritsu's buyer journey is "likely longer than micro-purchase journeys but shorter than major purchases" (Q5). A struggling student takes longer than an ice cream purchase (30 seconds) but shorter than a house purchase (6-24 months) — the time from realizing study effectiveness is a problem to subscribing to Ritsu is likely weeks-to-a-couple-months, not days and not years.

Implication for content sequencing: Ritsu does not need to compress Awareness→Action into one landing page (micro-style), and does not need a 24-month enterprise nurture (macro-style). A 2-4 week Awareness email sequence plus a 2-week Interest sequence plus a 1-week Desire sequence is the right ballpark. Content volume should match — full-funnel works, but each stage's sequence should be shorter and less elaborate than what Exposure Ninja describes for high-value B2B sales.
