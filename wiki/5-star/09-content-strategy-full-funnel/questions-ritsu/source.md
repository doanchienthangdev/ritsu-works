## Reference: Ritsu's AIDA Summary

### Awareness Stage
- **Student Mindset**: "I struggle with studying, but maybe I'm just not smart enough"
- **Key Searches**: "how to study effectively," "how to remember what I read," "active recall techniques," "spaced repetition," "study hacks"
- **Main Objection**: "I can't change how I study" or "I'm just not smart"
- **Best Content**: Blog posts, educational videos, free guides, learning science explainers
- **CTA**: "Download free guide," "Email course signup," "Study hacks checklist"

### Interest Stage
- **Student Mindset**: "I want to improve my studying, and I'm researching how"
- **Key Searches**: "active recall study method," "how to use flashcards," "spaced repetition system," "study techniques," "best way to create study materials"
- **Main Objection**: "I can do this myself with spreadsheets/ChatGPT" or "This seems complicated"
- **Best Content**: How-to guides, templates, comparisons, assessment tools, case studies without product
- **CTA**: "Free assessment," "Free consultation," "Study plan template," "Join webinar"

### Desire Stage
- **Student Mindset**: "I want an AI study tool, and I'm deciding which one"
- **Key Searches**: "Ritsu vs Anki," "best AI study app," "Ritsu reviews," "Ritsu pricing," "is Ritsu worth it"
- **Main Objection**: "Too expensive," "Is this really better?" or "Will it actually help my grades?"
- **Best Content**: Case studies, comparisons, testimonials, demo videos, pricing page, FAQ
- **CTA**: "Start free 14-day trial," "Book demo," "Get custom quote"

### Action Stage
- **Student Mindset**: "I'm ready to use Ritsu"
- **Key Searches**: "How to use Ritsu," "Ritsu tutorial," "get started with Ritsu"
- **Best Content**: Onboarding guides, help center, getting started video, customer support
- **CTA**: "Start using Ritsu," "Join live onboarding call," "Access help center"
