# Content Strategy Takes 12-24 Months But Compounds For Years

A full buildout of full-funnel content infrastructure takes 12-24 months — Awareness/Interest content alone requires 6-12 months because there are many topics to cover, and Desire/Action content adds another 3-6 months. Exposure Ninja's own benchmark: planned 600+ topics total, produced in batches, with Year 1 yielding 200+ pieces of Awareness/Interest content, 5,000-20,000 email subscribers, and 20,000-100,000+ blog views. Year 2 reaches 400+ pieces of content, 200-1,000+ leads/month, and CAC reduction up to 73%. Year 3 hits 600+ pieces and self-sustaining traffic loops.

The compound effect is what makes the long timeline tolerable: content works for **years** after publication, and each new piece amplifies the discoverability of prior pieces (internal linking, topical authority for SEO, email-list-driven revisits). This is the structural reason full-funnel strategy beats paid-only strategies long-term — the asset base grows while paid ad CAC stays constant.
