# Top-of-Funnel CPC is 10-100x Cheaper Than Bottom-of-Funnel

Cost-per-click for Awareness-stage keywords (symptom-based, like "blocked ears" or "how to study effectively") is 10-100x cheaper than CPC for Action-stage keywords (product-named, like "hearing aids near me" or "Ritsu pricing"). The illustrative economics from the source: a bottom-of-funnel keyword at $50 CPC × 5% conversion = $1,000 CAC; a top-of-funnel keyword at $0.50 CPC × 10-15 touchpoints to convert = $5-7.50 CAC. Same customer, 100-200x cheaper acquisition cost — provided the brand has the nurturing infrastructure (content + email sequences) to escort the cheap top-of-funnel traffic through the funnel.

The reason this gap persists is that virtually nobody competes on top-of-funnel keywords. Most businesses focus paid spend on high-intent bottom-of-funnel terms because they "feel direct and measurable." Search volume on symptom-level queries is also massive — far more people search for a general problem than a specific solution, because most people don't yet know your solution exists.
