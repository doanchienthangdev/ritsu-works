# Indoctrination Email Sequence

An indoctrination sequence is an automated, multi-email nurture flow that escorts a prospect from one AIDA stage to the next after they convert on a lead magnet. The Awareness-stage sequence (5-7 emails over 2 weeks) opens with a welcome, delivers 3 pure-value education emails, then a reframe email ("here's why most people fail at this"), a solution-hint email ("the best practitioners use this approach…"), and finally a transition email pointing to deeper Interest-stage content. The Interest sequence (3-5 emails) deepens engagement via assessment, case study, objection handling, then transitions to a consultation CTA. The Desire sequence (2-3 emails) presents comparisons, social proof, and a trial/demo CTA.

Without an email sequence, content alone is insufficient — most prospects need 5-15 touchpoints before purchase, and an unscheduled visit to one blog post rarely produces a second visit. The sequence is what converts a cheap top-of-funnel email signup into a high-intent, brand-trusting prospect at Action stage.
