# AIDA Funnel Model

AIDA is a four-stage framework — **Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action** — for mapping the buyer journey from first symptom-recognition to purchase. Each stage has a distinct prospect mindset, search intent, and emotional state: Awareness = "I might have a problem but I'm not sure," Interest = "I have a problem and I'm researching solutions," Desire = "I want a solution and I'm deciding between options," Action = "I'm ready to buy." The stages are identical across macro buyer journeys (6-24 months for high-value purchases like a home or enterprise software) and micro buyer journeys (30 seconds for an ice cream at the beach) — only timeline and research depth change.

The marketer's job is to identify the actual timeline + depth of their product's buyer journey, then create stage-specific content that meets each prospect where they are. Treating every visitor as if they are in the Action stage — which most websites do — wastes the opportunity to capture cheap, abundant Awareness-stage traffic.
