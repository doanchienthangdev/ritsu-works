# Target the Symptom, Not the Solution, at Awareness Stage

Step 1 of the full-funnel mapping playbook: identify the **core underlying problem** the product solves — not the product itself — and target that problem's symptoms in Awareness-stage content. A hearing aid company's Awareness content should target "blocked ears," "tinnitus," and "can't hear my spouse," not "hearing aids near me." A performance management software company targets "employee turnover," "how to retain staff," "toxic team culture," not its product category. A study app like Ritsu targets "how to study effectively," "how to remember textbooks," "active recall techniques," not "best AI study app."

This ruling is enforced by the prospect's mental model: someone at Awareness stage doesn't yet know your product category exists, so they cannot type product-category queries. They type the symptom they are experiencing. Creating product-category content for Awareness-stage prospects is a category error — the content cannot reach them by definition.
