# Repurpose Each Content Piece Into 3-5 Formats

Every piece of content should be repurposed into 3-5 formats — minimum — to maximize reach and meet different consumption preferences. A single 3,000-word blog post like "How to Implement Active Recall" should yield: a 10-15 post social media series, a 3-5 email sequence segment, an 8-12 minute video walkthrough, a 15-20 minute podcast episode, a downloadable PDF summary as a lead magnet, 1-3 infographics, and a LinkedIn article version for professional audiences. Each format reaches a different audience segment and adds organic surface area without proportional research/writing cost.

The corollary is that writing original content for each format is wasteful — production capacity should be biased toward repurposing existing high-performance pieces rather than always starting from scratch. This is what enables a small team to produce the 200-400+ pieces of content the strategy assumes for Year 1-2.
