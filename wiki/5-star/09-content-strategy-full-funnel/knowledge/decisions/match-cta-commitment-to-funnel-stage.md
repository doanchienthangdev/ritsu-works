# Match CTA Commitment Level to Funnel Stage

The call-to-action attached to each piece of content must escalate in commitment as the prospect moves through AIDA. Awareness stage = **low commitment, high perceived value** ("Download the free Active Learning Playbook," "Get the free checklist," "Join our free 5-day email course"). Interest stage = **medium commitment** ("Request a free website review," "Take this free assessment," "Book a free strategy session"). Desire stage = **high commitment** ("Start your free 14-day trial," "Book a demo," "Get a custom quote"). Action stage = **transactional** ("Buy now," "Sign up," "Schedule implementation").

The failure mode is offering a high-commitment CTA to a low-commitment prospect. "Book a demo" or "See pricing" on an Awareness-stage blog post is off-putting and converts poorly — the visitor isn't ready to talk to sales, doesn't yet believe they need the product category, and reads the CTA as a sales pitch. Conversely, offering only a free guide on a Desire-stage comparison page underperforms because the prospect is ready to evaluate the actual product.
