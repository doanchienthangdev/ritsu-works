# Generic CLOSER Framework Questions
## Deep Analysis for Any Business

Use these questions to audit your website and funnel against the CLOSER framework. Answer each one honestly. The gaps between "yes" and "no" answers are your conversion optimization opportunities.

---

## CREDIBILITY QUESTIONS (15 questions)

### Professional Design & Branding
1. Does your homepage have a professional, modern design that signals legitimacy on first glance?
2. Is your branding consistent across all pages, marketing materials, and touchpoints?
3. Do you use professional photography, or if stock photos, are they high-quality and relevant?
4. Is your website mobile-responsive and fast-loading (under 3 seconds)?
5. Are there any spelling errors, grammar mistakes, or unprofessional formatting on your key pages?

### Awards, Certifications & Recognition
6. Do you display any relevant industry awards or certifications prominently?
7. Do you have media mentions ("As seen in") that would be credible to your target audience?
8. Are you transparent about your team's professional qualifications and backgrounds?
9. Do you mention partnerships with recognized brands or organizations?
10. Are relevant compliance badges (GDPR, ISO, SOC 2) visible where appropriate?

### Scale & Longevity
11. Do you communicate how long you've been in business or your years of experience?
12. Do you highlight the number of customers served, transactions processed, or other scale metrics?
13. If you're newer, do you have evidence of traction (funding raised, press coverage, early customer success)?

### Team Credentials
14. Do you show photos and detailed bios of key team members or founders?
15. Is there evidence of your team's previous successes, speaking engagements, or publications?

---

## LURE QUESTIONS (15 questions)

### Lead Magnet Strategy
16. Do you have a clear lead magnet or free offering on your homepage?
17. Is your lead magnet highly relevant to your main product/service?
18. Does your lead magnet promise high perceived value (saves time, teaches a skill, prevents a costly mistake)?
19. Is the lead magnet better than what your competitors offer?
20. Can you describe exactly what the prospect gets by accepting your lure (not vague)?

### Lure Design & Presentation
21. Does your lure require minimal information to access (ideally just name + email)?
22. Is your lure accessible immediately (not delayed by email sending or other friction)?
23. Do you have multiple lures for different stages of the buyer journey (awareness, consideration, decision)?
24. Is there a secondary lure that deepens engagement (free trial after the lead magnet, consultation after the guide)?
25. Do you A/B test your lure headlines and offers to find what resonates most?

### Lure Alignment & Positioning
26. Is your lure so compelling that it makes the prospect think, "If this free thing is valuable, the paid thing must be incredible"?
27. Do different audience segments see different lures tailored to their specific pain point?
28. Does your pricing page clearly show what someone gets with paid vs. free (to clarify the upgrade path)?

### Incentivization
29. If you're using a discount or guarantee as a lure, is it compelling enough to overcome hesitation?
30. Do you create urgency around your lure ("Only available for the next week")?

## OBJECTION HANDLING QUESTIONS (20 questions)

### Identifying Objections
31. Have you identified the top 15-20 objections that prevent prospects from buying?
32. Have you interviewed lost customers, read 1-star reviews, and analyzed support tickets to find real objections?
33. Can you categorize your objections into price, trust, need, urgency, complexity, or risk buckets?

### Addressing Common Objections
34. Do you have a dedicated FAQ section addressing the top 5-10 objections?
35. Are your FAQ answers specific and substantive, not vague?
36. If price is a common objection, do you have clear ROI or payback period calculations visible?
37. If trust is an objection, do you have case studies, testimonials, or guarantees that address it directly?
38. If complexity is an objection, do you show proof that your product/service is easy to use (screenshots, videos, customer quotes)?
39. Do you have dedicated case studies that speak directly to your audience's concerns ("Will this work for my industry?" "Can someone non-technical use this?")?

### Guarantee & Risk Reversal
40. Do you offer any guarantee (money-back, satisfaction, performance) that removes the risk of buying?
41. If you offer a guarantee, is it displayed prominently throughout your site?
42. Is your guarantee actually credible and easy to claim (or does it have so many conditions that no one uses it)?
43. Do you have testimonials from people who specifically mention overcoming an objection ("I was worried about X, but...")?

### Objection-Specific Content
44. Do you use the "What if I told you..." reframing to shift limiting beliefs?
45. Do you address timeline objections ("When will I see results?")?
46. Do you address feasibility objections ("Can someone like me actually do this?")?
47. Do you address competitive objections ("Why is this better than [competitor]?")?
48. Do you address self-doubt objections ("Will this work for my specific situation?")?
49. Do you have dedicated landing pages or content addressing the top 3 objections?
50. Do you mention what kinds of customers your product works best for (to filter for fit)?

## SOCIAL PROOF QUESTIONS (25 questions)

### Testimonial Quality & Specificity
51. Do you have at least 10 customer testimonials visible on your site?
52. Are your testimonials specific, not vague ("Increased revenue by 47%" not "Great product")?
53. Do your testimonials include the customer's name, photo, and title/company?
54. Do you have at least 3 video testimonials (more powerful than text)?
55. Are your video testimonials authentic (natural speech, real emotion) not overly scripted?

### Testimonial Strategy
56. Do you have testimonials that specifically address your main objections?
57. Do you have testimonials from different types of customers (different industries, company sizes, roles)?
58. Do you have testimonials that address different benefits (time saved, money made, ease of use, etc.)?
59. Do you collect new testimonials regularly (not recycling the same ones from 2 years ago)?

### Case Studies
60. Do you have at least 3 detailed case studies on your site?
61. Are your case studies in the "before/after transformation" format?
62. Do your case studies include specific metrics (numbers, percentages, timelines)?
63. Are case studies named (company name, person name, photo) or are they anonymous?
64. Does each case study make it easy for the reader to see themselves in the "before" picture?
65. Do your case studies include a real quote from the customer?

### Star Ratings & Reviews
66. Do you display a star rating (ideally 4.5+ stars) prominently on your site?
67. Do you show the number of reviews alongside your star rating?
68. Are your reviews from verified platforms (Google, Trustpilot, Capterra)?
69. Do you have reviews across multiple platforms (not just one)?
70. If you have low reviews, do you respond professionally and show improvement?

### Scale & Adoption Metrics
71. Do you display metrics like "Trusted by 100,000+ users" or "Processes $X in transactions"?
72. Do you have customer logos visible (for B2B) showing well-known clients?
73. Do you show adoption growth over time ("Growing 50% YoY")?

### User-Generated Content & Community
74. Do you feature user-generated content (customer screenshots, posts, reviews)?
75. Do you showcase a thriving community or forum (if applicable)?

### Media Coverage & Authority
76. Do you display "As seen in" logos of recognizable media outlets?
77. Do you have press release links or press coverage visible?
78. Do you have endorsements from industry experts or influencers?

### Social Proof Placement
79. Is social proof distributed throughout your site (not just in one section)?
80. Do you have star ratings/reviews on your pricing page?
81. Do you have social proof on your checkout page?
82. Do you include social proof in your emails and sales materials?
83. Do you have testimonials on feature pages (e.g., "Our clients love this feature")?

## EASE QUESTIONS (20 questions)

### Form Friction
84. Do your lead capture forms ask for only 2-3 fields (name, email, maybe one more)?
85. Do you offer an "optional" label on any fields beyond the essentials?
86. Do you use progressive profiling (asking for more info at later stages, not all at once)?
87. Are your forms mobile-friendly with large, tappable input fields?
88. Do you use smart defaults (pre-filled country, pre-selected common options)?

### Checkout Process
89. Do you offer guest checkout (not forcing account creation)?
90. Is your checkout a maximum of 3 steps (ideally 1-2)?
91. Do you show progress indicators on each step ("Step 1 of 3")?
92. Do you display all costs upfront (shipping, taxes, fees) before the final checkout step?
93. Do you offer multiple payment methods (credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay)?
94. Do you have one-click payment options for returning customers?
95. Can users save their cart and return later?

### Pricing Transparency
96. Is your pricing visible without requiring signup or a demo request?
97. Do you show what's included in each tier clearly?
98. Do you explain the difference between free and paid (for freemium models)?
99. Do you show annual vs. monthly pricing options?
100. If you have different pricing for different segments, is this clear?

### Page Clarity
101. On checkout pages, do you remove navigation and other distractions?
102. Do you hide or simplify the main menu on checkout pages?
103. Do you remove email signup popups and other interruptions from checkout pages?
104. Is your checkout page mobile-optimized?

### Trust & Reassurance
105. Do you display trust badges (SSL, McAfee, Norton) at checkout?
106. Do you display payment logos (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal) at checkout?
107. Do you display a money-back guarantee message at checkout?
108. Do you show customer reviews and ratings at checkout?

### Support & Help
109. Do you have live chat available during the checkout process?
110. If you have a complex process, do you offer a phone number or email for support?
111. Do you have clear error messages that explain what went wrong and how to fix it?

## REPRESENTATIVE RESULT QUESTIONS (12 questions)

### Result Presentation
112. Do you show ranges of results rather than only best-case scenarios?
113. Can you articulate what a "typical" or "median" customer result looks like?
114. Do you specify the conditions required to achieve your advertised results?
115. Do you acknowledge that results vary and explain why?
116. Do you show results for different customer types (different industries, company sizes)?

### Timeframe Transparency
117. Are your results tied to specific timeframes (e.g., "within 6 months")?
118. Do you show both short-term results (month 1) and long-term results (year 2)?
119. Do you acknowledge ramp-up periods (results don't happen instantly)?

### Case Study Representation
120. In your case studies, do you show your best customer, median customer, and another customer (range)?
121. Are your case studies current (from the past 12 months) or are they outdated?
122. Do you disclose the conditions and effort required to achieve the results?
123. Do you ever show a "didn't work as well" case study to build credibility?

## CART & CHECKOUT ABANDONMENT QUESTIONS (18 questions)

### Preventing Abandonment
124. Do you show shipping costs early (on product page or cart, not just checkout)?
125. Do you offer guest checkout without requiring account creation?
126. Is your checkout process a maximum of 3 steps?
127. Do you show progress indicators in checkout?
128. Do you have a visual security/trust indicator at checkout?

### Abandoned Cart Email Recovery
129. Do you send an abandoned cart email within 1 hour?
130. Do you have a second abandoned cart email at 24 hours?
131. Do you have a third abandoned cart email at 72 hours with a stronger incentive?
132. Do your abandoned cart emails include a clear image of what was abandoned?
133. Do your abandoned cart emails have a clear call-to-action button?
134. Do you incentivize cart completion (5-15% discount, free shipping)?
135. Do your abandoned cart emails address common objections (e.g., "Satisfaction guaranteed")?

### Retargeting & Follow-up
136. Do you run retargeting ads to people who abandoned carts?
137. Do you use different ad creatives for different products?
138. Do you test different incentives (discount vs. free shipping vs. guarantee)?

### Exit Intent & Prevention
139. Do you have an exit-intent popup with an offer to reduce abandonment?
140. Do you track why customers abandon carts (Hotjar, session recording)?
141. Do you have live chat available at checkout to answer questions?

## Scoring Your CLOSER Audit

Count your "yes" answers:

- **0-30 yes answers:** Your funnel is weak across all CLOSER elements. This is a major opportunity. Start by fixing credibility and trust signals, then add your lure.
- **31-60 yes answers:** You have some elements in place, but major gaps. Pick the two weakest areas and improve them first.
- **61-90 yes answers:** You have a solid foundation. Now optimize each element for higher conversion.
- **91-120 yes answers:** You're applying CLOSER well. Focus on A/B testing and optimization.
- **121+ yes answers:** You have a mature CLOSER framework. Now focus on personalization and segment-specific versions.

---

## Action Plan

Based on your scoring, prioritize your improvements:

1. **What's your single biggest conversion blocker?** (usually trust/credibility or objection handling)
2. **What element has the lowest "yes" count?** (start here)
3. **What small changes would move the needle fastest?** (A/B test headlines, add testimonials, simplify checkout)
4. **What requires investment?** (video testimonials, case studies, full website redesign)
5. **What's your 30-day priority list?**

Remember: You don't need to fix everything at once. Pick one element, make it exceptional, then move to the next.
