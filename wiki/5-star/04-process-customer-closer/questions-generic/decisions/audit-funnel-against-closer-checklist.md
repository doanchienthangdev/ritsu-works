# Audit your funnel against a CLOSER yes/no checklist; gaps are opportunities

CLOSER becomes operational via a binary audit: walk each of ~150 yes/no questions across the six elements and count the gaps. The gap between "yes" and "no" answers is the conversion optimization opportunity, and the lowest-yes element is where to start.

Score bands give priority:
- **0-30 yes:** funnel is weak across all elements; start with credibility + trust signals, then add the lure
- **31-60 yes:** some elements in place but major gaps; pick the two weakest areas and improve them first
- **61-90 yes:** solid foundation; now optimize each element for higher conversion
- **91-120 yes:** applying CLOSER well; focus on A/B testing and optimization
- **121+ yes:** mature framework; focus on personalization and segment-specific versions

The discipline of binary scoring removes "well, kind of" answers that hide problems. Either the FAQ addresses the top 5 objections specifically, or it doesn't.
