# High perceived value plus low perceived risk equals action

The lure works on a simple equation: **high perceived value + low perceived risk = action**. A lure must feel like a genuine prize (high value) AND be easy and safe to redeem (low risk). Both halves matter independently — a high-value lure behind a long form converts poorly, and a low-friction lure for a worthless freebie converts to garbage leads.

For Ritsu, this means the free entry needs to feel like a real win (high value: a sample interactive activity from a real textbook, not a generic ebook) AND demand minimal commitment (low risk: email + password, no credit card, no phone, instant access).
