# A funnel must qualify and close simultaneously

Most businesses face a critical gap: they attract unqualified traffic and fail to convert it. A website or funnel must perform two jobs at the same time — filter out bad-fit prospects (qualify) and convert qualified prospects into customers (close). The CLOSER framework is designed to do both, regardless of whether the offering is a service, a product, a subscription, or a free trial.

This is the Vietnamese-source restatement of the same point in the English `knowledge.md` module, preserved here because the founder operates in both languages and the Vietnamese framing emphasizes the *lọc / chốt* (filter / close) duality explicitly.
