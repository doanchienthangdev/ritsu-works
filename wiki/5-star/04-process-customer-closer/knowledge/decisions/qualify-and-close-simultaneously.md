# Be irresistibly compelling to ideal customers while filtering out poor fits

The goal of a conversion funnel is **not** to be persuasive to everyone. The goal is to be irresistibly compelling to the ideal customer while naturally filtering out poor fits. This reframes conversion optimization from "maximize sign-ups" to "maximize sign-ups from the right people, and let the wrong people self-select out." Generic persuasion tactics that boost top-line conversion but attract bad-fit customers raise refund rates, support load, and churn — actually destroying value.

For Ritsu specifically, this means landing pages should signal "AI tutor that quizzes you from your own material" loudly enough that students looking for a passive note-app or general chatbot don't sign up. Counter-intuitively, this raises lifetime value of paid users.
