# Show representative results (ranges with conditions), not best-case

If you only show best-case scenarios, prospects think "That's amazing, but it won't happen for me" — creating distrust and increasing refunds and complaints. Show **representative results**: what a typical customer can expect, with ranges and timeframes, qualified by the conditions required to achieve them.

Pattern:
- Replace "Increase revenue by 500%" with "Most customers increase revenue by 15-40% within 6 months. The range depends on starting point and implementation rigor."
- Replace "Save 100+ hours per month" with "A typical user saves 3-8 hours per week on [specific task]."
- Always pair the range with the conditions: "This assumes you implement the system with at least one dedicated person spending 10+ hours per week. Customers who dedicate fewer resources see slower results."

This both sets expectations and pre-filters people who won't succeed (saving you and them trouble). For Ritsu, this means messaging like "Most students see measurable improvement within 2-3 weeks when using 10+ hours/week. Results vary by subject and effort" — not "Go from C to A in 2 weeks."
