# Lures must preview the paid product, not be misaligned freebies

A lure works on the principle **high perceived value + low perceived risk = action**. The lure must feel like a real prize, not a consolation. But beyond raw value, the lure must preview what you sell — it should make the prospect think: "If this free thing is valuable, the paid thing must be incredible." A fitness app with a lure of "Free 7-Day Meal Plan" is misaligned with the workout product; a better lure is "Free 7-Day Personalized Workout Plan" that directly matches the paid offering.

Misaligned lures over-recruit prospects who never convert and train the audience to expect the wrong category of value. For Ritsu, this means the free entry should be a sample interactive activity from a real textbook (matching the paid product) — not a generic "AI study tips ebook" (which would convert ChatGPT users, not future Ritsu subscribers).
