# Six objection categories: price, trust, need, urgency, complexity, risk

Every prospect has objections. The question is not whether they object — it's whether you address objections before or after they leave your site. Objections cluster into six categories that exhaust the reasons someone doesn't buy:

1. **Price** — "Too expensive," "ROI isn't clear," "Cheaper alternatives exist"
2. **Trust** — "Are you a scam?", "How do I know this works?", "What if you go out of business?"
3. **Need** — "I don't need this," "Not a priority," "I can do this myself"
4. **Urgency** — "Not ready to buy now," "Need to think," "Let me check with [spouse/boss]"
5. **Complexity** — "Too complicated," "No time to implement," "Don't have the skills"
6. **Risk** — "What if it doesn't work?", "What if I'm the exception?", "What if this breaks my workflow?"

A productive audit starts by listing 20+ reasons a prospect might NOT buy — sourced from past customer interviews, lost-sale post-mortems, 1-star reviews, and support tickets — then mapping each to one of the six buckets, then placing a counter (FAQ, testimonial, guarantee, case study) at the funnel point where that objection peaks.
