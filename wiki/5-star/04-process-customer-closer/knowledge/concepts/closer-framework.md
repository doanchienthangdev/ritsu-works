# CLOSER framework

CLOSER is a six-element conversion architecture for websites and funnels that must do two jobs simultaneously — qualify (filter out bad-fit prospects) and close (convert qualified prospects). The acronym stands for **C**redibility, **L**ure, **O**bjection handling, **S**ocial proof, **E**ase, and **R**epresentative result. Each element works in concert as the prospect moves from awareness through consideration to decision.

CLOSER is universal across service businesses, e-commerce, subscriptions, and free trials. The framework is not applied all at once on a single page; it unfolds throughout the customer journey, with credibility loading first (above the fold), lures at email capture, objection handling and proof in mid-funnel, and ease at checkout.
