# Each form field cuts conversion 3-5%; use progressive profiling

Every form field added to a lead capture or signup form reduces conversions by 3-5%. The implication is to ask for the absolute minimum at initial capture (name + email only) and use **progressive profiling** — ask for more information gradually as the prospect moves through the funnel:

- Step 1 (lead magnet): email only
- Step 2 (follow-up email): company size
- Step 3 (webinar/demo): budget, timeline
- Step 4 (free trial signup): full details

This sequencing also enforces relevance — you only ask for budget once the prospect has signaled they're evaluating, not when they're still discovering. For Ritsu, this means free signup should be email + password; ask "what subject?" only inside the app, after they've experienced one activity.
