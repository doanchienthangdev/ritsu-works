# Anchor messaging for Ritsu across the six CLOSER elements

Six anchor lines, one per CLOSER element, drafted as starting points for Ritsu's homepage and landing pages. These are NOT canonical copy (the homepage hero "Turn Raw Materials Into True Mastery" still owns the top line per `00-core/product.md`), but they are starting drafts for the elements below the hero and for objection-specific landing pages:

- **Credibility:** "Trusted by 5,000+ students at leading universities. AI-powered interactive learning from real textbooks."
- **Lure:** "Try free for 14 days. No credit card required. Start with a sample activity in 30 seconds."
- **Objection (ChatGPT):** "Unlike ChatGPT, Ritsu gives you structured, interactive activities tailored to your textbook. Designed for learning, not just answers."
- **Social Proof:** "95% of students report improved grades. 'I went from a C to an A in two weeks.' — Sarah, Biology Major."
- **Ease:** "Sign up in 30 seconds. Start learning in under a minute. No setup, no learning curve."
- **Representative Result:** "Most students see measurable improvement within 2-3 weeks when using 10+ hours/week. Results vary by subject and effort."

Risk reversal as final close: "30-day money-back guarantee. If you don't see improvement, we'll refund you fully and show you free study resources."

Two caveats for the founder: (1) the "5,000+ students" and "95% improved grades" figures are placeholders — substitute real data before publishing; (2) the ChatGPT-comparison objection is the highest-leverage one to address based on Ritsu's stated competitive context.
