# CAC payback period is the operational test of whether unit economics are sustainable

The question taxonomy treats CAC payback period — the number of months until a customer recovers their acquisition cost — as the critical operational metric for CLV health. It is repeatedly referenced as the bridge between CLV and CAC: improving CLV directly improves CAC payback, and a healthy payback period is what determines whether further acquisition spend is sustainable.

This framing distinguishes "total CLV" (a vanity number) from "CAC payback" (the cash-flow truth). For Ritsu, the relevant question is not "what's our CLV target?" but "how many months from premium activation until that student's lifetime contribution covers their acquisition cost?" — that's the number that gates further marketing investment.
