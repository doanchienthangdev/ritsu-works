# CLV question taxonomy: 12 dimensions from foundations to long-term strategy

The 295-question CLV inventory organizes the problem space into 12 dimensions: foundational understanding, subscriptions, renewal/restock marketing, upgrade campaigns, add-ons, upsells, premium options & tiering, B2B premium upsells, system-wide integration, psychology & implementation, channel-specific strategies, advanced concepts, real-world challenges, strategy & planning, integration with business model, and checklist/application.

The taxonomy itself is the artifact worth preserving — when designing CLV interventions for a specific business (e.g. Ritsu), it serves as the menu of question categories to walk through. Treat the question list not as a quiz but as a scope map for what "complete CLV thinking" covers.
