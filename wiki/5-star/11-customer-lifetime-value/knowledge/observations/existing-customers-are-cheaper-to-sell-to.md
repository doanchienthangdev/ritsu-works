# Selling to existing customers is generally much cheaper than acquiring new ones

Exposure Ninja claims that selling to an existing customer is "generally much cheaper (often free)" vs cold acquisition — yet most marketers ignore this and stay addicted to the dopamine of new-customer acquisition. The diagnostic example is the unused email list: many businesses sit on 10,000-20,000 existing-customer emails that are never re-marketed.

For Ritsu, this maps directly to the free-user base: every free user is already-acquired audience whose conversion cost is approximately zero compared to a paid ad clicking a cold prospect. The implication is that CLV optimization (re-monetizing the existing base) should be sequenced **before** scaling acquisition spend, not after.
