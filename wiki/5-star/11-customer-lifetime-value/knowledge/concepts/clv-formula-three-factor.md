# CLV formula: purchase size × purchase frequency × customer duration

Customer Lifetime Value decomposes into three multiplicative factors: how much each transaction is worth (size), how often the customer transacts (frequency), and how long they remain a customer (duration). Because the relationship is multiplicative, small percentage gains in each factor compound into outsized total gains — improving each by 25% nearly doubles CLV.

This factorization is the operational reason Exposure Ninja organizes CLV interventions around two "levers": Lever 1 grows frequency (subscriptions, renewal marketing, upgrade campaigns) and Lever 2 grows size (add-ons, upsells, premium tiers). Duration is downstream of both — customers who buy more frequently and at higher value typically also stay longer.
