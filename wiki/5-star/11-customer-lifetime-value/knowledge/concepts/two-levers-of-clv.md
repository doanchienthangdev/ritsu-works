# Two levers of CLV: increase purchase count + increase order value

Exposure Ninja's CLV operating model collapses to two levers. **Lever 1** increases the number of purchases per customer via subscriptions, renewal/restock marketing, and upgrade campaigns. **Lever 2** increases the value of each order via add-ons, upsells, and premium options. Both levers reduce price sensitivity (split-pay, subscriptions, premium tiers) as a side effect.

The strategic insight is that even one lever moves the business meaningfully, but compounding both creates the maximum advantage. Higher CLV from both levers funds higher CAC, which buys market share, which creates the compounding moat against private-equity-funded competitors who can outspend on acquisition but cannot match unit economics.
