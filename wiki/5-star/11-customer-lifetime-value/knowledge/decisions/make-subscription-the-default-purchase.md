# Make subscription the default purchase option, requiring opt-out for one-time

The principle: where the business can offer subscriptions, the subscription should be the **default** option on the purchase page, with one-time purchase requiring an active opt-out. The cited exemplar is Together (one-time purchase requires active opt-out, with customizable volume and frequency). Athletic Greens uses a softer variant — one-time £97 vs subscription £79/month — but explicitly shows cost-per-serving math to anchor the subscription as the rational choice.

Default effects are the active mechanism. People accept defaults; making subscription the default flips the CLV math automatically across an entire customer base without re-marketing cost. For Ritsu's premium tier, this directs the answer to questions-ritsu Q16: premium subscription should be the default checkout option, not opt-in.
