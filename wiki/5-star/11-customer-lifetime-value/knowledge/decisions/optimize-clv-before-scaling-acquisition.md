# Increase CLV before scaling acquisition spend

The sequencing rule: improve CLV first, then scale acquisition. The unit-economics math is the justification — at CAC=$100, CLV=$500 is profitable; CLV=$200 is unsustainable. Many startups fail because they grow customer base without growing CLV: every new customer locks in negative or break-even economics that no amount of scale can fix.

The corollary for SaaS is that breakeven or even loss-leading customer acquisition is viable **only if** CLV is sufficiently high — accepting CAC=$500 is rational if CLV is $5,000+. The discipline this creates: before opening the acquisition firehose, demonstrate the CLV math works on the existing user base. For Ritsu, this argues against burning paid-acquisition budget for free users until premium subscription unit economics are proven.
