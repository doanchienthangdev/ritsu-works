# Upgrade-campaign messaging must focus only on what's new, not the overall product pitch

When running upgrade campaigns to existing customers (e.g. Aura Ring v2 → v3, Telestream/ScreenFlow version upgrades), the marketing copy should focus **exclusively on what's new** in the upgrade — not on what the product does generally. The reasoning: existing customers already know the value proposition; re-pitching it is wasted attention. The conversion lever is the **delta**, not the baseline.

The Aura Ring case adds a second pattern: bundle the upgrade with a discount + a new ancillary benefit (free lifetime membership = a new subscription feature). For Ritsu, when new AI models or features ship, the existing-user campaign should lead with "Now with [model X]" and the discount on continued premium — not re-explain what Ritsu is.
