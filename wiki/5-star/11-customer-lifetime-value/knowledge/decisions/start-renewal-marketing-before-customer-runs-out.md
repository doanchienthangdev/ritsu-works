# Start renewal/restock marketing before the customer runs out or expires

The strict rule: identify the average repurchase frequency, then begin promotion **before** the customer's product runs out or subscription expires. Reason: once a customer re-enters "shopping/research phase," you compete with the open market for their attention. By marketing pre-expiry, you keep them inside the existing relationship.

Execution is multi-channel — automated email sequences timed before renewal date, plus feeding user data into Google/Facebook for targeted display/social ads. The accreditation client example combines emails + Google Display + Facebook/Instagram ads timed before renewal. For Ritsu, the semester boundary is the natural renewal trigger; campaigns should fire weeks before semester start, not after the previous semester ends.
