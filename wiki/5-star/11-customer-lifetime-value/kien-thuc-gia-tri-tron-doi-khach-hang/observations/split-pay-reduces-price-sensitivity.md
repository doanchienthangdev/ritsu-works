# Split-pay reduces price sensitivity and lifts average order value (Klarna: up to 58%)

Klarna's claim: split-pay can increase average order value by up to 58%. The psychological mechanism is the difference between sticker-shock (£1,279 one-off feels expensive) and monthly affordability (£100/month feels manageable). Split-pay is now ubiquitous even on tiny purchases — Pretty Little Thing splits a £2 item into 6 payments — because the AOV gain compounds across the whole basket.

For Ritsu's price-sensitive student segment, this argues for offering split-pay on annual premium plans (e.g. $5/month rather than $50/year) even though the gross revenue is identical. The lift comes from increased conversion at the perceived-affordability threshold, not from changing what the customer ultimately pays.
