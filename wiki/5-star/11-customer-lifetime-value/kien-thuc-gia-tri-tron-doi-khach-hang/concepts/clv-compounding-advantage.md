# CLV compounding advantage: small percentage improvements stack into massive advantages

CLV calculations compound over time — small percentage improvements in each factor (purchase size, frequency, duration) stack multiplicatively into outsized total advantages. This is the operational reason long-term focus on CLV beats short-term transaction-profit optimization: each year's small CLV gain rolls into the next year's baseline.

The strategic loop: higher CLV → more affordable to spend on acquisition → larger market share → compounding advantage. Businesses that systematically optimize both levers (more purchases + higher value) outpace competitors not because of one big move but because the gains compound silently.
