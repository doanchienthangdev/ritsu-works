# Usage-triggered gating: collect data during normal use, then gate the results behind premium

Best Companies' workplace-survey pattern is described as "genius" — the customer runs a basic free survey, the system collects 152 employee comments during normal use, then the customer hits a paywall ("Pro" upgrade) at the moment they try to read those comments. The customer didn't know Pro existed until they saw locked data they desperately wanted.

This inverts traditional promotion-based upselling. The trigger is the customer's own usage, not a marketing message. For Ritsu, this is a speculative but high-leverage idea: collect rich learning-outcome data (concept maps, mastery scores, study streaks, predicted exam grades) during the free tier, then gate the **read** of that data behind premium. The customer earns the value before they're asked to pay for access to it.
