# Four Pain Point Buckets for Blog Topics

Blog topics that drive conversion-relevant traffic fall into four predictable buckets keyed to funnel stage: (1) **Versus/Comparison** ("X vs Y") for buyers actively deciding between two options, (2) **Costs/Pricing/Fees** ("How much does X cost") for buyers in consideration evaluating budget, (3) **How-To/Educational** ("How to X", "Best way to X") for awareness-stage users solving a problem, and (4) **Best X for Y** ("Best tool for Z") for buyers actively shopping with high commercial intent.

Each bucket has a distinct content strategy. Versus content positions the solution favorably and enables retargeting via pixel — HubSpot famously ranks for "Salesforce vs Zoho" to siphon competitor consideration traffic. Cost content pre-frames value before exposing price and addresses budget objections. How-to content builds authority and nurtures awareness-stage prospects. Best-X content converts directly because the searcher is shopping.

The recommended distribution by stage: awareness (Bucket 3) at 40-50% of content output, consideration (Buckets 1 & 2) at 30-40%, decision (Bucket 4) at 10-20%. This calibrates effort to the funnel and prevents the common failure of over-producing top-of-funnel content that drives traffic without revenue.

For Ritsu, this maps cleanly: how-to content like "How to Use Spaced Repetition for Med School" (Bucket 3) drives awareness; "Ritsu vs Anki" / "Ritsu vs Quizlet" (Bucket 1) captures comparison shoppers; "Best AI Study Apps for Medical Students" (Bucket 4) converts.
