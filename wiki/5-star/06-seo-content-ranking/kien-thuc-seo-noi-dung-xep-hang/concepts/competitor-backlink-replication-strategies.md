# Competitor Backlink Replication Strategies

A complete link-building plan is derived by enumerating the *types* of pages linking to competitors and asking, for each pattern, whether it can be replicated. The taxonomy is finite and predictable: PR/press links from news publications, partnership links from complementary businesses, trade publications in the industry vertical, affiliate sites featuring the product, seasonal content (Mother's Day, Valentine's, Christmas), student-discount aggregators (for educational businesses), sponsorships of local events/charities/conferences, interviews and guest features (guest posts, podcasts), supplier testimonials linking back to vendor sites, and product review sites.

The workflow: for each competitor's top-ranking pages, view the backlink list in Semrush/Ahrefs/Moz, sort by domain authority, identify the 5-10 most authoritative linking domains and the 5-10 easiest replicable opportunities, then prioritize for outreach. The pitch differs by link type — newsworthy update for PR, complementary partnership angle for partners, testimonial with metric for vendors, listicle submission for roundups, educational discount for student aggregators.

For Ritsu, this maps to a concrete plan: pitch study-method roundups on student-life sites (replicate Anki/Quizlet's appearances), offer educational discounts to student-discount aggregators (UNiDAYS, Student Beans), provide vendor testimonials to any SaaS Ritsu integrates with (OpenAI, Stripe), and target podcasts/interviews in the EdTech space.
