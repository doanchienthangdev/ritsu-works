# Use Supplier Testimonials as a Legitimate Link-Building Channel

Write specific, results-focused testimonials for every supplier and partner, then ask them to feature the testimonial on their site with a link back. This is a simple, legitimate, mutually beneficial link-building tactic: supplier testimonial pages typically have good domain authority, the resulting backlink is contextual and organic, suppliers want to showcase strong testimonials (so they accept readily), and Google does not penalize this pattern because the placement is editorially earned, not transactional.

The decision rule is procedural: enumerate all vendor/partner websites with testimonial pages, write 3-5 specific testimonials per vendor (each different — never copy-paste), include exact metrics or quantifiable benefits, write in first person at 2-3 sentences, include name/title/company, then contact the vendor and ask them to feature with a link. Generic praise ("Great service!") is useless and won't be featured. Video testimonials are more likely to be featured than text. Follow up after 2-3 weeks if not published.

For Ritsu, the natural vendor list includes: OpenAI/Anthropic (LLM provider), Stripe (payments), Supabase (database), Vercel/Cloudflare (hosting). Each is a candidate for a metric-bearing testimonial that earns a contextual link from a high-authority developer site.
