# Ritsu's student audience has seasonal cycles and viral/organic hooks

The framing context that should be applied to every email sequence Ritsu builds. The audience is university students; the business model is freemium; the entry point is a university pilot program. Three distinguishing properties shape every downstream email decision:

1. **Seasonal behaviour** — exam periods and semester boundaries drive predictable spikes in study urgency and predictable troughs in app engagement. Re-engagement sequences should fire just before exam season, not on calendar dates that ignore the academic cycle. Subject lines that reference seasonal context ("How to cram for finals without burning out") should outperform generic ones.
2. **Viral/organic growth hooks** — students expect peer-driven discovery, not paid acquisition. Referral, UGC, and "real student transformation" formats fit the audience model better than ad-funnel-style sequences.
3. **Transformation as value carrier** — the value proposition is "studying transformation" (time saved, grades improved, confidence) rather than feature lists. Indoctrination sequences should lead with transformation case studies (real students before/after) rather than feature tutorials.

This context block should propagate into every Ritsu-specific email artefact: welcome sequences, onboarding, premium-upsell, lapsed-user reactivation, and NPS check-ins.
