# Email là phương thức giao tiếp cá nhân, không phải kênh broadcast

The framing claim that anchors all Vietnamese-language email marketing guidance for Ritsu: email is a personal communication method and must be treated as such. The Vietnamese phrasing is direct — "Email là phương thức giao tiếp cá nhân — nên được coi như vậy" — and pairs with the statistical context that 4.03 billion people use email (so với 2,2 tỷ trên Facebook), millennials spend 6.4 hours/day in email, and 72% prefer email for business communications.

Operational implication: every Vietnamese email send from Ritsu should pass the "would a friend send this?" test. The failure mode is treating the email channel as a broadcast — the source flags that most email broadcasts fail precisely because they aren't personal and don't matter to the recipient. Vietnamese marketing translations from English templates often carry over corporate-sounding formality that violates this principle; the rendering here keeps the Vietnamese phrasing intact so future Vietnamese-language email drafters can anchor on it.
