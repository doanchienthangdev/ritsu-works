# Máy Tạo Lợi Nhuận Dự Đoán Được (Vietnamese rendering of Predictable Profit Repeater)

The Vietnamese-language framing of Module 5's email automation framework. The English term "Predictable Profit Repeater" is rendered as "Máy Tạo Lợi Nhuận Dự Đoán Được" — literally "machine that produces predictable profit" — emphasising the automation-as-machinery metaphor more explicitly than the English does. The Vietnamese version preserves the same six components and the same headline claim that email is the highest-ROI channel.

Worth preserving as a bilingual concept because (a) Ritsu's domestic market is Vietnamese-speaking, (b) the "máy tạo" (literally "creating machine") framing is more concrete than English "repeater" and lands differently in Vietnamese marketing discourse, and (c) downstream content producing Vietnamese marketing material should anchor on this rendering rather than re-translating the English term.
