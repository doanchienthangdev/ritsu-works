# Discount via email list, not publicly, to preserve brand equity

A direction-of-action rule for offers and launches: the public channel (website, social, ads) should never carry discounts; only the private email list does. Public discounting erodes brand luxury and trains the market to wait for sales; private list-exclusive discounting rewards subscribers, feels exclusive, and preserves price perception on the public surface.

Concrete corollaries from the source:

- **Luxury fashion**: never discount publicly (brand erosion), but a 20%-off-via-email send rewards the list and keeps margin intact on the storefront.
- **Clearance**: a website clearance section makes the brand look desperate and devalues even non-clearance items; the same clearance offered via email feels like an insider perk.
- **SaaS**: never advertise a trial discount publicly, but offer an extended trial to the email list.

The rule generalises into a discipline: privacy IS the offer mechanism. Anything that would degrade public-channel brand equity should be routed through email instead, where the cost of exposure is bounded to people who already opted in. This also explains the offer-frequency cap — even via email, discounting every week trains the same waiting behaviour and erodes perceived value, so 4-6× per year is the recommended cadence with bonus offers filling the gaps.
