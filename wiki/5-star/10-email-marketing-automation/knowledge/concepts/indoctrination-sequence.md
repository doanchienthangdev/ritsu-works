# Indoctrination sequence (priming)

A named, automated email sequence that fires when a new lead or customer enters the list. Its job is to remove objections, build desire, and create a relationship foundation BEFORE any sales pitch lands. The frame is explicitly pedagogical, not coercive: "indoctrination = teaching your worldview, not brainwashing." The sequence operates on the premise that "not everyone is ready to buy when you are ready to sell," so the asynchronous email layer pre-builds readiness across days or weeks.

Typical structure: 5-10 emails spaced 3-7 days apart for a welcome sequence, but Exposure Ninja runs a ~12-month indoctrination of 40+ emails. The content mix is case-studies-as-how-tos (e.g. "How this company reduced IT costs by 50%" — not "Case Study #1"), tutorials and guides that demonstrate expertise without pitching, and interviews/features that borrow third-party credibility. The pattern across the sequence is: build desire → remove fear → build more desire → generate purchase.

Deploys on four triggers: new lead from ads/organic (welcome), new customer post-purchase (onboarding), webinar attendee who didn't buy (nurture), and contact from partnership/referral (introduction).
