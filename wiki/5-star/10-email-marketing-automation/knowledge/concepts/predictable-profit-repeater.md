# Predictable Profit Repeater (6-component email automation framework)

Module 5 (Progression) of the 5-Star marketing system. A six-component automated email architecture that turns new contacts into high-LTV customers, fans, and advocates at scale. The name encodes the three claims: **predictable** (sequences run consistently once set up), **profit** (email is the highest-ROI channel at $36-$40 per $1), **repeater** (automations compound indefinitely with no marginal cost).

The six components — each a self-contained sequence type:

1. **Priming** — indoctrination sequences for new leads/customers (objection removal + desire building before pitch).
2. **Review Collection** — automated review-request emails timed by business type (e-commerce day 3, services same-day, SaaS at aha moment, coaching after results).
3. **Offers & Launches** — list-exclusive discounts plus Nick James-style early-bird-list-to-launch sequences.
4. **Follow-ups & Loyalty** — multi-stage sales follow-ups, "you okay?" check-ins, re-engagement and lapsed-customer reactivation sequences.
5. **Influence & Advocacy** — UGC contests, referral programs, affiliate/partner programs (MyProtein voucher model as exemplar).
6. **Testing & Research** — NPS automation, pre-build surveys, A/B testing as continuous improvement loop.

The components compose into a single end-to-end customer-lifecycle engine, and the whole point is that once configured, the entire system runs without human touch except for testing iteration.
