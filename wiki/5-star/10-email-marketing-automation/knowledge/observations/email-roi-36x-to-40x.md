# Email ROI is $36-$40 per $1 spent — highest of any channel

The headline economic claim that anchors the entire 6-component framework: average email marketing ROI is $36-$40 per $1 spent, making it the highest-ROI channel for most businesses. The claim sits inside a tighter set of supporting facts: 4.03 billion email users globally (vs 2.2 billion on Facebook), millennials spend 6.4 hours/day in email, email subscribers convert at 3-5× the rate of social media followers, and 72% of people prefer email for business communications.

The worked example in the source: an e-commerce store with 5,000 subscribers, $75 average transaction value, and 1.5% email conversion rate produces $5,625 revenue from one $50 send — an 11,150% ROI on that specific email. Benchmark revenue-per-subscriber annual figures: e-commerce $0.50-$2, SaaS $1-$5, high-ticket services $5-$50+.

Implication: list size has compounding economic value, and the cost of NOT building an email list is the foregone $1-$50 per subscriber per year. This is the framing that justifies the upfront 40-80 hours to configure the six automation components and the ongoing 5-10 hours/week to manage them.
