## 1. The belief (brand level)

Ritsu's positioning is deliberately climbing the Kotler brand-level ladder: **attribute → benefit → belief.** (Principles 18e, Ch 8 — beliefs are the strongest, least-copyable level; Nike/Apple/Disney live here.)

- **Attribute** (weakest, copyable): "fast AI."
- **Benefit** (current public hero): "turn raw materials into true mastery."
- **Belief** (where we are climbing — the durable, defensible level):

> **AI can't learn for you — and human cognitive bandwidth is finite. The smartest way to learn is to use AI as leverage on proven learning science. Passive consumption is wasted time; active mastery is the only learning that sticks.**

This is the **counter-narrative** to "AI makes us dumber": Ritsu is how AI makes you *sharper*. Every touchpoint should ladder up to this belief. The belief is what produces *love* (the north star), not just use.

## 7. Points of parity (POP)

The credibility table-stakes Ritsu must hit — *"often the key to positioning is not the point of difference but the points of parity"* (Marketing Management 16e, Ch 7):

- **Category POPs (greens fees):** accurate AI output (frequent wrong quizzes = legitimacy failure), clean fast UX, broad format support. Without these, nothing else matters.
- **Correlational POP (must neutralize):** the implied negative *"AI-native ⇒ unreliable / hallucination."* Neutralize via accuracy messaging + **transparent citations**. The trust-first content engine doubles as this POP defense.

## 12. Forbidden phrases

Words / claims Ritsu never uses (per `brand_voice.md` § "Anti-patterns to refuse"):

**Empty adjectives:** "revolutionary", "amazing", "powerful", "incredible", "groundbreaking", "the best", "the only", "the most" (unless we can cite verifiable rank), "transformative", "next-generation", "cutting-edge".

**Manufactured urgency:** "limited time", "act now", "don't want to miss out", "today only", "exclusive offer".

**Unverified outcome claims:** "Guaranteed to pass your exam", "Learn 10x faster" *(as a measured speed claim)*, "Master anything in [X] days", "Used by top 1% of students" (without verifiable data).

> **Clarifier (added 2026-05-29):** the belief-level line "AI can't learn for you — but the right system makes you learn 10×" is allowed. The "10×" there is a *philosophy of leverage* (active > passive), not a measured speed promise. The forbidden form is "Learn 10x faster!" as a quantified outcome claim. The `pre-tool-publish` hook should distinguish the two.

**Generic AI hype:** "AI-powered" alone (say WHAT the AI does), "Smart" anything, "Magic" / "magical".

**Anti-passive-learning fluff that contradicts our actual stance:** "Effortless learning" (learning takes effort; Ritsu makes the SETUP effortless), "Learn while you sleep" (pseudoscience).

When a draft contains any forbidden phrase, the `pre-tool-publish` hook should flag. Founder reviews + replaces with specific alternative.
