# How Ritsu uses AI

Ritsu is built around AI. The product itself is an AI tutor — that's why you're using us. But AI also helps us run the company: respond to your emails, write our blog posts, track issues, and more. We want you to know exactly when AI is involved.

This page explains:
1. Where AI is in your interactions with us
2. What our AI is allowed (and not allowed) to do
3. How to talk to a human
4. How we handle your data

## Where AI is involved

### In the product

The whole point of Ritsu is AI tutoring. When you upload a document and chat with the tutor about it, that's AI. We use Claude by Anthropic as our primary model. Other AI providers may be used for specific features (image generation, voice).

### In our customer support

When you email `support@ritsu.ai` or chat with us in-app, an AI assistant ("Ritsu Assistant") often replies first. This includes:

- Answering common questions
- Helping with account issues
- Routing more complex questions to a human

The AI always identifies itself at the start of a reply. If you'd prefer a human, just reply with "human" or click the "Talk to human" option, and someone from our team will follow up.

### In our content

Our blog posts, social media replies, and email newsletters are sometimes drafted by AI and reviewed by our team before publishing. We don't separately label individual AI-drafted pieces — but we never publish AI content claiming a human author.

### In our internal operations

We use AI to help with non-customer-facing work: code review, scheduling, data analysis, writing internal documentation. These don't affect you directly, but we mention them for completeness.

## What our AI can decide on its own

Our AI assistant can:

- Answer questions using our knowledge base, FAQ, and public information
- Help you find features within Ritsu
- Reset things like passwords (when you can verify your identity)
- Hand off your conversation to a human when needed

Our AI assistant **cannot** (without explicit human approval):

- Issue refunds
- Change your subscription plan
- Modify or delete your data
- Send you marketing or promotional content you didn't sign up for
- Make commitments about future product features
- Provide medical, legal, or financial advice

When you make a request that needs human judgment, the AI escalates it. You'll usually hear back from a human within one business day.

## How to reach a human

Always available:

- Email `help@ritsu.ai` — goes directly to our team, not to AI
- In any AI conversation, type "human" or "talk to a person" — the AI will route you immediately
- For urgent issues (account compromise, billing emergencies), email `urgent@ritsu.ai`

We're a small team. Response times vary, but we read every message.

## How we handle your data

Your data is yours. Specifically:

- **What you upload** (PDFs, links, documents) — stored only as long as needed for tutoring. You can delete uploads anytime from settings.
- **Your tutoring conversations** — stored to improve your tutoring experience. You can delete conversation history anytime.
- **Your support tickets** — stored for our records (typically 2 years). You can request deletion.

We do **not** train Anthropic's or other providers' models on your data. Your tutoring conversations and uploads stay in your account. AI providers process your queries to generate responses, then delete them per their data retention policies.

For full data practices, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Limits and known issues

We want to be honest about what AI does and doesn't do well in Ritsu:

**Where AI works well:**
- Answering questions clearly stated in your uploaded materials
- Generating practice questions, flashcards, summaries
- Translating between languages
- Helping you study consistently with reminders and structure

**Where AI is imperfect:**
- Some highly specialized topics (advanced medicine, niche legal areas) — AI may give plausible but incorrect information. Always verify important facts.
- Math problems with multiple valid solutions — AI sometimes picks the wrong approach
- Very long uploads (>200 pages) — AI may miss details from the middle of long documents

If you find AI giving incorrect information, please tell us at `feedback@ritsu.ai`. Specific examples help a lot.

## Compliance

We comply with regulations around AI disclosure:

- **EU AI Act (Article 50)** — In effect August 2026. Our disclosure-on-first-contact pattern meets these requirements.
- **California SB 1001** — AI used for commercial purposes in California must disclose. We disclose.
- **Vietnam (current)** — No specific AI disclosure law as of mid-2026. We follow EU standards as the higher bar.
- **GDPR** — For users in the EU, your rights include access, correction, deletion, portability. See [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
