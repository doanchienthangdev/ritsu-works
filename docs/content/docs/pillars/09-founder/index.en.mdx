# 09-founder — Founder Operations Pillar

> Big pillar covering founder operations. Founder is the bottleneck of a 1-person AI-Native company; this pillar codifies how founder thinks, decides, governs, recovers, and learns so the AI workforce doesn't run on top of an exhausted human.

## Scope

This pillar owns:

1. **Cognition** — decision velocity, attention management, top-idea audit (PG signature)
2. **Charter discipline** — keeping the company aligned to its written vision
3. **HITL flow** — Telegram bot, override protocols, approval queue management

6. **Learning** — founder skill compounding (reading, deep-research weeks)

## Rationale for big pillar

AI-Ops is the WORKFORCE. Founder is the OPERATOR of the workforce. Mixing them = AI-Ops becomes god-pillar. Keeping separate = explicit acknowledgment that the bottleneck is human, not AI capacity.

Why a BIG pillar (not just cognition): founder operations are not just "thinking better" — they include health (you can't out-think exhaustion), learning (compounding skill), HITL discipline (governance enforcement), and weekly review (forcing function). All founder-owned, shouldn't scatter.
