## 25.6 Lessons captured

1. **Surface = adapter pattern** (common interface).
2. **Channel registry as Tier 1.**
3. **Format converter library = O(N), not O(N × M)** (channels × locales = exponential).
4. **Locale ≠ language** (formality, honorifics, cultural references differ).
5. **Brand voice per locale** — inherit philosophy, adapt register.
6. **Conversation threading across surfaces.**
7. **Per-surface compliance non-negotiable** (WhatsApp template rejection = permanent ban).
8. **Inbound + outbound symmetric architecture.**
9. **Rate limit enforcement at adapter, not skill.**
10. **Deterministic vs judgment converter routing** (Minions pattern).
11. **Surface health = first-class observability.**
12. **Cross-surface deduplication essential.**
