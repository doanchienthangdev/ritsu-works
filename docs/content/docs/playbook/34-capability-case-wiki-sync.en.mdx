## 34.1 Bài toán bắt đầu

Founder có ~20 GB raw knowledge cần ingest vào ritsu-works:

- 4 PDF marketing textbook (Kotler *Marketing Management*, Kotler *Principles of Marketing*, Ries+Trout *Positioning*, etc.)
- 1 collection markdown growth-playbook (~11 chapter, 5-star "courses" về SEO, copywriting, paid ads)
- Notion exports, YouTube transcripts, Apify-scraped competitor sites

Vấn đề:
1. **Không có entry point để ingest.** Founder phải copy-paste từng phần vào Claude conversation, mất context giữa lần.
2. **Không có citation tracking.** Khi AI sinh "5 ý tưởng growth", founder không biết những ý đó **TỪ ĐÂU** trong textbook → không thể fact-check.
3. **Re-ingest đắt + không idempotent.** Mỗi lần re-process toàn bộ textbook = $30-50 LLM cost.
4. **Tier 4 (vector store) trống.** Bài #14 schema có sẵn nhưng zero data; RAG queries không hoạt động.

## 34.5 Citation spine design

Mỗi derived entity (e.g. `wiki/principles-of-marketing-kotler/concepts/4-Ps-of-marketing.md`) carry frontmatter:

```yaml
---
title: 4 Ps of Marketing
entity_type: concept
source_id: principles-of-marketing-kotler
chunks: [chapter-01-defining-marketing#para-12, chapter-02-marketing-planning#para-3]
confidence: 0.95
distilled_by: claude-haiku-4-5
distilled_at: 2026-05-18T...
founder_decision: accepted
---
```

Mỗi `chunks` reference resolve qua `ops.knowledge_extractions` → tìm raw source chunk → fact-check.

**RAG query path:** founder hỏi qua `/wiki ask "what is 4 Ps of marketing?"` → embed query → HNSW kNN trong `ops.knowledge_embeddings` (page_type=`concept`) → top-5 derived entities → response cites entity slugs → click → trace back qua citation spine.

## 34.6 Lessons learned

1. **Citation spine first; embeddings second.** v2.0 ship trước citation table → không trace được. v3.0 add citation table → tất cả các capability sau (docs-engine) học theo pattern này.

2. **Source-grouped layout > entity-type-grouped.** v3.0 ship `wiki/concept/`, `wiki/observation/` flat — founder không thể scan "this source produced these entities". v4.0 flip thành `wiki/<source-slug>/<entity-type>/`. Migration `00032` add partial UNIQUE per-source.

3. **`confidence` không phải probability.** Coarse 3-bucket reflecting LLM self-report. Không hữu ích cho calibration; chỉ hữu ích để filter "high confidence only" trong RAG retrieval.

4. **`founder_decision` field là circuit breaker.** Without `founder_decision = 'accepted'`, RAG retrieval optionally filters out NULL → founder review gate.

5. **Adapter pattern dễ extend.** Add new source kind = add new `<kind>-adapter.cjs` file + register trong `wiki-sync/ingest`. Pattern này tái sử dụng được trong docs-engine (Chương 35).

6. **Bumpy lineage là OK trong CLA framework.** 5 version trong 5 ngày, mỗi update qua `/cla revise` hoặc `/cla extend` cost ~$0.50-3. Trong pre-CLA world, đây là $50+ rebuild.

## 34.7 KPIs

- `wiki_entities_total` — số derived entities trong `ops.knowledge_pages WHERE page_type IN ('concept', 'observation', 'decision', 'idea')`
- `wiki_citation_coverage` — % entities có ≥ 1 row trong `ops.knowledge_extractions`
- `wiki_founder_decision_accepted_pct` — % entities `founder_decision = 'accepted'` (proxy for AI quality)
- `wiki_rag_query_latency_p95` — `/wiki ask` p95 wall-clock
- `wiki_rag_citation_chain_completeness` — % RAG responses where every cited entity resolves back to source chunk

## 34.8 Open work

1. **`founder_decision` review queue.** Currently 259 entities (after Kotler distill) sit at `founder_decision = NULL`. `/wiki review` skill exists nhưng founder chưa chạy. Backlog growing.

2. **Multi-source attribution.** Khi 2 source independently describe same concept (e.g. "4 Ps" trong cả Marketing Management + Principles of Marketing), how to merge without losing citation chain? `/wiki merge` skill exists nhưng chưa test.

3. **Embedding model upgrade.** Embeddings hiện dùng `text-embedding-3-small`. `wiki-sync/embeddings-backfill` skill cho phép re-embed nhưng cost ~$5-10 cho 259 entities + re-evaluate retrieval quality.

4. **Audio + meeting adapter chưa test end-to-end.** Whisper integration đã wire nhưng chưa có acceptance corpus.

5. **v5 candidate — incremental re-distill.** Khi source thay đổi (founder sửa source.md), re-distill incremental (chỉ chunks đã change) thay vì re-distill toàn bộ source. Analog với docs-engine v1.2 incremental translation (Chương 35).
