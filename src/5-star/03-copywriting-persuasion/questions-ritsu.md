# Copywriting & Persuasion: Ritsu-Specific Questions

**Ritsu Context:**
- AI learning platform transforming textbooks into interactive learning activities
- Target: university/college students with dense course materials
- CLI commands → rich visual/interactive output
- Core value: deep understanding vs memorization
- Competitors: ChatGPT, Anki/Quizlet, traditional studying
- Price-sensitive student audience, freemium model
- Solo founder needs copy that converts without a sales team
