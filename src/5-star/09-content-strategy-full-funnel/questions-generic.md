## Answer Guide Structure

Each question can be answered with:
- **Short answer** (1-2 sentences): Quick definition or response
- **Long answer** (3-5 sentences): Detailed explanation with reasoning
- **Examples**: Real-world instances showing the principle in action
- **Why it matters**: Business impact or strategic significance
