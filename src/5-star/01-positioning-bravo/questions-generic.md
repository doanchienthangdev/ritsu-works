# Positioning (BRAVO Framework) - Critical Questions
## Module 1 of the Exposure Ninja 5-Star Marketing System
### Generic Questions for Deep Understanding

---

## BRAND (B) - Questions 1-15

1. What is the difference between a brand and a logo? Why do many businesses confuse these concepts?

2. Describe three specific ways visual consistency across touchpoints affects customer perception of a brand.

3. If your brand were a person, what would be their personality traits? How would these traits manifest in customer interactions?

4. Why is brand promise more important than brand messaging? What happens when brand promise exceeds or falls short of brand messaging?

5. Analyze a brand you know well. What visual elements (colors, fonts, imagery) trigger immediate brand recognition? Why?

6. How does brand personality relate to target audience selection? Can the same product have different brand personalities for different audiences?

7. What are the consequences of brand personality misalignment with audience values? Give a specific example.

8. How do customer experiences (good and bad) shape brand perception over time? Can one bad customer service interaction damage brand positioning?

9. Describe the difference between intentional brand positioning and accidental brand positioning. Which is more common?

10. When might a brand need to rebrand? What are the risks and rewards of rebranding?

11. How does founder reputation affect brand perception? Can a founder's personal brand overshadow the company brand?

12. What role does social proof play in building or damaging brand perception?

13. How should brand consistency be balanced with brand evolution? When should a brand stay static vs. evolve?

14. Analyze the relationship between brand promise and pricing power. How does strong brand positioning allow premium pricing?

15. Create a brand positioning statement for a hypothetical business, including visual identity, personality, and promise.

---

## REASON FOR EXISTING (R) - Questions 16-30

16. What is the difference between a business mission and a business goal? Why do many businesses conflate these?

17. How does Simon Sinek's "golden circle" (why, how, what) challenge the way most businesses communicate?

18. Why is "we want to make money" not a valid "reason for existing"? What makes an authentic reason for existing?

19. Describe a brand with a strong, authentic "why." How does their reason for existing differentiate them from competitors?

20. What makes an origin story compelling? What elements should a strong origin story include?

21. How do you identify whether a company's stated values are genuine or just marketing? What's the difference between values and value proposition?

22. Can a business succeed without a clear reason for existing? What happens to marketing effectiveness when this is missing?

23. How do values-driven businesses attract and repel customers differently than other businesses?

24. What's the relationship between "reason for existing" and customer lifetime value?

25. How should a business balance profit motive with a deeper mission? Can both exist simultaneously?

26. Describe how a company's reason for existing should influence product development, pricing, and customer service.

27. Can a company's reason for existing become outdated? When might a company need to revisit or redefine its "why"?

28. How does transparency about "why" (or lack thereof) affect customer trust?

29. Create a "reason for existing" statement for a hypothetical business. How would this differ from their mission statement?

30. How might a founder's personal "why" differ from the company's "why"? How should these be aligned?

---

## AUDIENCE (A) - Questions 31-50

31. Why is targeting "everyone" typically a weak positioning strategy?

32. What is a customer avatar, and how is it different from basic demographic targeting?

33. Explain the difference between demographics and psychographics. Which is more predictive of purchasing behavior?

34. How should understanding pain points, desires, and fears inform your positioning and messaging?

35. What does it mean to "narrow your audience"? How does narrower audience targeting sometimes increase your addressable market?

36. Describe how understanding where your audience spends time (both physically and digitally) should inform your positioning strategy.

37. Why is the language your audience uses important for positioning? What happens when you use language outside their experience?

38. How does audience understanding change the product development process? Should product design follow audience understanding?

39. Develop a detailed customer avatar for a hypothetical business. Include demographics, psychographics, pain points, desires, objections, and information sources.

40. How should different customer avatars influence different positioning angles? Can one company have multiple positioning statements?

41. What's the relationship between audience understanding and pricing power?

42. How do you discover your real target audience vs. your assumed target audience?

43. What role do existing customers play in defining your target audience? Should you always double down on who's already buying?

44. Analyze the connection between audience narrowness and brand loyalty. Why do narrow audiences create stronger brand communities?

45. How should audience analysis inform your content strategy?

46. What are the consequences of targeting an audience you don't actually understand?

47. How might your audience evolve over time? How should your positioning evolve with them?

48. Describe how competitive positioning should influence audience selection. Should you target the same audience as competitors or different audiences?

49. How does audience self-perception differ from how others perceive them? Why does this matter for positioning?

50. What's the difference between aspirational audience positioning (targeting who they want to be) vs. identity-based positioning (targeting who they are)?

---

## VOICE (V) - Questions 51-65

51. What is voice, and why is it different from messaging or copy?

52. Describe the spectrum of voice from formal to informal. Where should different businesses position themselves on this spectrum?

53. How should voice align with brand personality? Can voice and brand personality contradict each other?

54. Why is voice consistency across channels important? What happens when voice varies significantly across touchpoints?

55. How does matching voice to audience increase trust and affinity?

56. Analyze a brand with a distinctive voice (e.g., Ryan Reynolds for Aviation American Gin, or a specific SaaS company). How does their voice contribute to brand recognition?

57. What makes a voice authentic vs. fake or forced? How do audiences detect inauthenticity?

58. How should voice evolve as a company scales? When does consistent voice become stale?

59. Describe how voice should differ in different contexts (social media vs. email support vs. website copy). Should there be consistency or should context determine voice?

60. How does a founder's natural voice influence company voice? When should a company's voice differ from the founder's?

61. Can a business have multiple voices for different product lines or customer segments? What are the risks?

62. How does voice affect customer service interactions and support satisfaction?

63. Develop a voice guide for a hypothetical business, including: tone, vocabulary, sentence structure, perspective (first/second/third person), examples of what to say and not say.

64. What's the relationship between voice and authority positioning? Can informal voice build authority?

65. How might industry norms constrain voice choices? When should a business break voice norms to differentiate?

---

## OFFERING (O) - Questions 66-85

66. What does "offering" include beyond just the product? What else is part of the complete offering?

67. Explain the difference between product-focused positioning and outcome-focused positioning. When is each appropriate?

68. What's the difference between a feature and a benefit? Why do most businesses lead with features instead of benefits?

69. Describe how to bridge from a feature to increasingly deeper benefits. Why does this emotional journey matter?

70. What is a Unique Selling Proposition (USP)? What makes a strong USP vs. a weak one?

71. Analyze a competitor's claimed USP. Is it specific, believable, relevant, and difficult to copy?

72. How does price positioning affect the rest of your brand positioning? Can a premium brand have budget pricing?

73. What are the trade-offs between premium positioning, value positioning, and budget positioning?

74. Why do many businesses claim benefits they can't actually deliver? What's the cost of broken positioning promises?

75. How should offering positioning inform product development? Should product design follow positioning or should positioning follow product?

76. Describe how packaging, presentation, and positioning interact. How does the same product packaged differently have different positioning?

77. What role do guarantees, trials, and return policies play in offering positioning?

78. How should different customer segments affect offering positioning? Should you have different offerings for different segments?

79. Create a complete offering positioning statement for a hypothetical business, including: what is it, top 3 benefits, USP, price positioning, and why customers should choose you.

80. How does offering clarity affect marketing effectiveness? What's the cost of vague or confusing offering positioning?

81. What's the relationship between offering positioning and competitive advantage? Can an identical offering have different competitive advantage based on positioning?

82. How should offering positioning adapt as your product evolves? When should positioning change vs. stay stable?

83. How does offering positioning affect customer acquisition cost and customer lifetime value?

84. What's the difference between positioning your offering and positioning your solution? Are these the same thing?

85. How might different messaging channels require different offering positioning emphases (e.g., ads vs. content vs. sales page)?

---

## BRAVO INTEGRATION & ALIGNMENT - Questions 86-100

86. Create a complete BRAVO positioning statement for a hypothetical business. Ensure all five components are clear and aligned.

87. How should B (Brand) and V (Voice) reinforce each other? Can they contradict?

88. How should A (Audience) and R (Reason for Existing) connect? Does your reason for existing need to resonate with your target audience?

89. Analyze a brand where BRAVO components are misaligned. What specific misalignments exist? What's the impact?

90. How does A (Audience) constrain B (Brand) choices? Should audience come before brand or brand before audience?

91. If you had to choose between audience alignment and brand authenticity, which should win? Why?

92. How should O (Offering) align with A (Audience), and how should it differ? What if your offering appeals to a different audience than your brand targets?

93. Describe how a change in one BRAVO component (e.g., narrowing audience) requires adjustments in the other components.

94. Can a business authentically claim a "reason for existing" that doesn't align with market demand? Should it?

95. How does positioning change if you're entering a new audience segment? Which BRAVO components need to shift?

96. Create a scenario where a business needs to reposition. Explain which BRAVO components need to change and why.

---

## FLYWHEEL CONTENT STRATEGY - Questions 97-110

97. How is positioning content different from promotional content?

98. What does it mean that "customers are stupider than you think"? Is this accurate? Why?

99. How should content address the "stupidity principle"? Should business content over-explain?

100. What is content compounding? Give an example of content that compounds vs. content that doesn't.

101. Describe the content flywheel: how does each piece of content feed into discovery, authority, trust, and buying?

102. How should awareness stage inform content strategy? What content moves people from unaware to problem-aware?

103. How should problem-awareness influence content? What content is valuable at this stage?

104. Create a content piece that addresses someone in the "solution-aware" stage. What should it focus on?

105. How should positioning be tested? What role does customer feedback play?

106. Why do ultimate guides and frameworks tend to have high content compounding value?

107. What makes a piece of content "evergreen" vs. "time-sensitive"? Why is evergreen content better for positioning?

108. How should content strategy align with BRAVO positioning? Should content reinforce all components or focus on specific ones?

109. Describe how thought leadership content builds positioning authority differently than educational content.

110. Create a content calendar that uses the flywheel principle to build positioning for a hypothetical business over 12 months. How does each piece compound?

---

## POSITIONING STRATEGY & EVALUATION - Questions 111-130

111. Why should positioning be tested with real customers before full launch?

112. What are the signs that positioning is working? How would you measure positioning effectiveness?

113. How often should a business revisit and potentially adjust positioning?

114. What are the triggers that should cause a business to reconsider their positioning?

115. Describe the cost of bad positioning. What's the financial impact of weak or misaligned positioning?

116. How does positioning affect customer acquisition cost? Does strong positioning reduce CAC?

117. How does positioning affect customer lifetime value? How does strong positioning increase CLV?

118. What role does competitive positioning play? Should you position against competitors or independently?

119. How should you develop positioning if the market doesn't yet exist (first mover)? How is this different from positioning in established markets?

120. Describe how positioning should change at different company stages (early startup, growth stage, mature company).

121. How does team hiring relate to positioning? Should your team embody your brand positioning?

122. What happens when a company's actual product/service doesn't match its positioning? How is this resolved?

123. Can positioning change customer behavior or expectations? Is positioning just reflecting reality or creating it?

124. How should positioning handle negative market perception or bad reputation?

125. Describe the relationship between positioning and pricing power. Does good positioning justify premium pricing?

126. How does positioning affect viral growth and word-of-mouth? Why do some positioned businesses spread faster?

127. When should a business abandon its positioning vs. double down? What's the decision framework?

128. How does market saturation affect positioning strategy?

129. How should b2b positioning differ from b2c positioning? Are the BRAVO components different?

130. Create a positioning evolution roadmap for a hypothetical business over 5 years, showing how positioning should evolve with company growth.
