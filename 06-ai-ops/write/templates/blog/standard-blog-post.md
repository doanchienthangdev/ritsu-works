# Template — Standard blog post

> Fill the beats. Delete the guidance. The reader owes you nothing; every line earns the next.

## Title
A promise or a curiosity gap. Specific beats clever. (Not Title Case.)

## Hook (1–3 sentences)
Open mid-motion or with a concrete claim. No "In today's world." No throat-clearing.
The first line's only job is to make the second line get read.

## Promise (1 sentence)
What the reader will know or be able to do by the end. Implicit is fine.

## Body
Develop one argument or one story. Use sections only if they earn their headers.
- Lead with the point, then the evidence (not the other way around).
- One concrete, hard-to-fabricate detail beats three abstractions.
- Vary sentence length on purpose. Short lands. Longer ones carry the reader somewhere.

## Turn
The moment the reader didn't see coming but immediately believes. The reason to keep this post.

## Takeaway
One line they could repeat to a friend. Not "in conclusion." Not a generic upbeat send-off.
End on the idea, not on you.
