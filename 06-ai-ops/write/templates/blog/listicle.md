# Template — Listicle

> A list only works when each item could not be cut. If two items say the same thing, you have one item.

## Title
"N <things> that <specific payoff>." The number is a promise; keep it.

## Intro (2–4 sentences)
Why this list, why now, why you. State the payoff the reader gets by the end. Skip the dictionary-definition open.

## Items (repeat per item)
### N. <Item as a claim, not a topic>
One-line statement of the point. Then 2–4 sentences of why it's true and what to do with it.
One concrete example or number per item. No filler item to round to 10.

## Close
The one item that matters most, or the through-line connecting them. Not a recap of all N.
