# Template — Decision memo (bottom-line-up-front)

> A memo exists to get a decision made. Lead with the answer; the reader can stop reading once convinced.

## Recommendation (BLUF)
The decision you're asking for, in one or two sentences, at the very top. The ask is unmistakable.

## Context
The minimum a reader needs to evaluate the recommendation. No history lesson.

## Options considered
| Option | Upside | Downside | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (recommended) | ... | ... | ... |
| B | ... | ... | ... |
Why A over B, in a sentence.

## Risks + mitigations
The honest objections and how you'd handle them. Naming the strongest counter-argument builds trust.

## The ask
What you need, from whom, by when. One owner, one date. Make saying yes easy.
