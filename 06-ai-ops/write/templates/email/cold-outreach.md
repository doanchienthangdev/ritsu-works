# Template — Cold outreach email

> The whole email should be readable in the preview pane. If they have to scroll, you lost.

## Subject
Specific and honest. Curiosity or value, never clickbait. 3–6 words.

## Line 1 — relevance
Why them, why now. One sentence proving this isn't a blast. A real detail about them.

## Line 2–3 — the one idea
What you have / noticed / can do. One sentence. Their benefit, not your pitch.

## The ask (one)
A single, low-friction next step. A question they can answer in one line, or a clear yes/no.
No "circle back", no "synergies", no fake P.S. urgency.

## Sign-off
Name. Done.
