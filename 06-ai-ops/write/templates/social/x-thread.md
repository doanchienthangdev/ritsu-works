# Template — X / social thread

> Post 1 is the whole promise. If post 1 doesn't stand alone, the thread is dead on arrival.

## Post 1 — the hook
State the entire payoff of the thread in one post. A bold claim, a result, or a list-promise.
Make the reader need post 2.

## Posts 2…N — one beat each
Each post = one idea that earns the next. Each readable on its own.
- No "(1/12)" if the platform threads natively; let the structure carry it.
- A number, name, or image in most posts.
- No "let that sink in", no "here's the thing".

## Final post — the payoff
Deliver the promise from post 1. Then one clean ask (follow, link, reply) — at most one.
