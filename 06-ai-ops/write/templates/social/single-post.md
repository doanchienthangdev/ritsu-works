# Template — Single social post

> One idea. Sized for the platform. Written like a person, not a brand account.

## First line (the scroll-stopper)
This is the whole game. A claim, a number, a tension, or a confession. Never "Excited to share…".

## Body (1–4 short lines)
Develop the one idea. Concrete > abstract. Line breaks are your punctuation.
Cut every word whose removal changes nothing.

## Close (optional)
A soft prompt or a clean landing. No engagement-bait ("thoughts? 👇"), no hashtag soup (≤2 tags if any).

---
Platform notes (the orchestrator applies the active --medium):
- x: ≤280 chars per post; punchy; no hashtags unless load-bearing.
- linkedin: a little more room; lead with a story or a stat; whitespace.
- reddit: no marketing voice; talk like a member of the subreddit or get downvoted.
- instagram: caption supports the image; first line shows before "more".
