# Template — Research note

> Every claim ties to evidence. If you can't cite it, say it's unknown — don't dress a guess as a fact.

## Question
The precise question this note answers. One sentence.

## Scope / method
What you looked at, how, and what you deliberately excluded. Honest about limits up front.

## Findings
Each finding = a claim + its evidence (citation, data point, or source). Number them.
1. **Finding.** Evidence. (source)
2. ...
Use a chart (--dataviz) where a number series tells the story faster than prose.

## Synthesis
What the findings mean together. The pattern, not a restatement of the list.

## Limits
What this note does NOT establish. Confidence level. What would change the conclusion.

## So-what
The decision or action this enables. Why a reader should care.
