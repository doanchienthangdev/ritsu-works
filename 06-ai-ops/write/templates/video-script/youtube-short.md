# Template — YouTube Short / Reel / TikTok script

> Written for the ear and the thumb. You have 3 seconds before the swipe.

## [0:00–0:03] Hook
A line that creates an open loop or states a surprising result. Visual lands instantly.
Format: spoken line + `[VISUAL: ...]`.

## [0:03–0:10] Promise / setup
What they'll get if they stay. One sentence. Keep the loop open.

## Beats (the payoff)
Each beat = one spoken line + a `[VISUAL]` cue. Keep momentum; no dead air.
- Beat 1: ...
- Beat 2: ...
- Beat 3: ... (close the loop opened in the hook)

## [end] CTA
One action: follow for part 2, comment a word, link in bio. Just one.

---
Write spoken-word, not prose: contractions, short clauses, say it out loud to test.
