# Template — Feature article (reported)

> A feature earns its length with reporting, scenes, and a reason to care — not with padding.

## Headline + dek
Headline: the story in a line. Dek (subhead): the angle / why-it-matters in one sentence.

## Lede (1–2 paragraphs)
Open on a scene, a person, or a sharp specific. Pull the reader in before you explain.

## Nut graf
The paragraph that says what this is about and why it matters now. Earn the rest of the piece here.

## Body (sections / scenes)
Alternate scene and exposition. Quote real sources; attribute specifically (no "experts say").
Each section advances the argument or the narrative. Cut anything that's just "context".

## Kicker
End on an image or line that resonates — circles back to the lede or opens a window. Not a summary.
