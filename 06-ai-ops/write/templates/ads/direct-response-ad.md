# Template — Direct-response ad (Ogilvy discipline)

> "When I write an advertisement, I don't want you to tell me that you find it 'creative.'
> I want you to find it so interesting that you buy the product." Sell, or else.

## Headline (80% of the work)
A specific, checkable promise or fact — not an adjective. Name the reader's want.
Test 10+ headlines; ship the one that says the most concrete thing.
(Ogilvy's Rolls-Royce: "At 60 miles an hour the loudest noise comes from the electric clock.")

## Visual direction (for --image)
The one image that makes the claim undeniable. A specific object, not a vibe.

## Body
- Lead with the strongest benefit, stated as a fact.
- Long copy outsells short when there's something at stake — give the reader the facts to decide.
- Every sentence advances the sale. No cleverness that doesn't sell.
- One concrete proof: a number, a guarantee, a demonstration.

## Offer + CTA (one)
Exactly one action. Make it easy and specific. No manufactured urgency, no dark patterns.
