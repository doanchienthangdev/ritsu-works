# {{Title}} — book bible

> **Purpose:** A single locked document every parallel chapter-drafting agent reads before writing a word. It eliminates contradictions, ensures the argument compounds across chapters rather than repeating, and keeps voice, terminology, and evidence consistent without requiring agents to read each other's output.

> **Status:** DRAFT → **LOCKED** (no part drafting starts until locked; version-stamped).

## thesis
_The book's ONE central claim in two sentences — the falsifiable argument the entire manuscript exists to prove. Every chapter must either advance, complicate, or evidence this claim. Agents must quote it verbatim in their draft's first planning comment._

{{fill}}

## audience
_The specific reader: what they already believe, what they're skeptical of, what pain they arrived with, and what transformation the book promises them. Includes anti-audience (who this book will frustrate). Governs register, assumed knowledge level, and which evidence lands._

{{fill}}

## argument_arc
_Chapter-by-chapter logical spine: what each chapter claims, what it contributes to the overall argument, and how it hands off to the next. Format: Chapter N — [claim one sentence] → [how it advances thesis] → [bridge sentence to Chapter N+1]. This is the contract every parallel agent drafts against._

{{fill}}

## chapter_map
_Full chapter inventory: title, part, word budget, primary evidence sources, core story or case study, key concept introduced, key concept resolved from prior chapter. Prevents two chapters making the same point or two agents assigning the same case study._

{{fill}}

## key_terms
_The book's proprietary vocabulary: every term the author coins or redefines, with its exact definition, the chapter where it is first introduced, and the precise usage distinction from the everyday meaning. Agents must use terms exactly as defined — no synonyms for coined terms._

{{fill}}

## evidence_ledger
_Every study, statistic, case study, or named example allocated to the book — which chapter owns it, its exact citation, and whether it has been used (to prevent double-use). Agents check this before pulling evidence; after drafting they mark their claims as consumed._

{{fill}}

## voice
_The author's tonal register for this book specifically: sentence length pattern, ratio of story to argument, how hedges are handled, what the author NEVER says (banned phrases and stances), two sample paragraphs at the target pitch. The humanize pass calibrates to this section._

{{fill}}

## style_sheet
_Mechanical consistency rules: Oxford comma yes/no, number formatting (spell out under 10 or not), hyphenation of compound modifiers, heading capitalization style, footnote vs endnote, how block quotes are introduced, and any medium-specific formatting rules (e.g., print vs Kindle footnote behavior)._

{{fill}}

## What must stay consistent (the non-negotiables)
- The thesis statement — word-for-word where quoted, paraphrase-consistent everywhere else; no chapter may imply a weaker or stronger claim than the bible thesis
- Coined and redefined terms — always the exact bible definition, never synonyms or casual paraphrases of proprietary vocabulary
- The logical handoff — the last paragraph of Chapter N must create the tension or question that Chapter N+1 opens by addressing; the bible's argument_arc bridge sentence is the contract
- Evidence ownership — a study or named case belongs to the chapter that reserved it; no chapter may introduce evidence marked as owned by another chapter
- Voice register — sentence-length rhythm, hedge style, and the banned-phrase list from the bible voice section apply uniformly across all chapters
- Part-level argument position — at no point in the book should the reader feel the argument has been 'reset'; each part must visibly build on the prior part's conclusions
- Author's stated epistemic stance — if the author writes in first person with acknowledged uncertainty, no chapter may flip to an authoritative third-person broadcast voice or vice versa
