## The wedge, in one line

**The deadline-bearing committed STEM/ML masterer** — a learner gripping a hard, graded, multi-week ML/STEM course who must truly master dense material before a real deadline. ML/AI courses (cs231n, MIT 6.S191, Andrew Ng, fast.ai) are the beachhead; the open-courseware self-learner is the reach layer, not the paying core.

> **v2 positioning note:** the *paying wedge* is unchanged, but the **ad-channel surface (S9-A) is broadened** to the full *"University Mastery with AI"* frame — all hard university subjects (STEM, pre-med/medicine, economics/finance, biology) and **especially learning-method / study-productivity** audiences — because those communities already hold the "active mastery beats passive" belief Ritsu sells. The channel map scores + ranks every source by fit.

## The two load-bearing risks (carry in all copy)

- **R1 — payability unproven:** at true-zero, engagement is observed but payment is not. The N=10 watch's #1 test: *do they pay at the first hard limit?*
- **R2 — free NotebookLM** clones the spine (Apr-2026: +mastery-tracking). Lead on the **multi-week PATH + 17 activities + mastery-tracking + learning-science**, never "we also make quizzes."

## Confidence posture

At true-zero, most attributes are `inferred` / `hypothesis`; `observed` traces to the `supabase-analytics` Door-2 signal. These are a **map to instrument and test**, not proven behavior.
