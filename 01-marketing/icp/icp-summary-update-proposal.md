# Proposed update to `icp-summary.md` — sharpen the wedge to sustained-mastery

> This is a **proposal**, not an applied change. `icp-summary.md` is Tier-1 (00-core). Apply via `/update tier1-file 00-core/icp-summary.md` after review, or merge a hand-edit PR. The segment study (`/think mckinsey ritsu-segment-persona-journey-v1`) is the evidence base; the persona + journey in this folder are the decision-grade detail.

## Why this is a *refinement*, not a contradiction

The current `icp-summary.md` §0 already says: *"The exam-driven US college STEM/pre-professional student who has demanding material to **truly master on a deadline** — believes 'I'm not smart enough', is wrong (their method is the problem), and **will pay to actually learn**."* That psychographic core is **confirmed** by this study — keep it. The study changes **one thing**: the *wedge framing*. Today's wedge (§4) is **"ace your exam in 3 days"** — an *episodic exam-cram* framing. The scorecard showed that framing buys a *churning* customer (KO-1). The sharper wedge is **sustained mastery of a hard, graded, multi-week course**, where the recurring deadline is the durable WTP trigger and cram is a *trigger inside* the segment, not the segment.

## The specific edits

**1. §0 (the one-sentence ICP) — minor sharpen.**
- *From:* "The exam-driven US college STEM/pre-professional student…"
- *To:* "The **deadline-bearing STEM/ML masterer** — a US-led, English-speaking learner gripping a hard, graded, **multi-week** course (ML/AI beachhead) who must truly master dense material before a real deadline; believes 'I'm not smart enough', is wrong (the method is), and will pay to actually learn."

**2. §4 (the transition wedge) — reframe cram as a trigger, not the wedge.**
- *From:* wedge = "ace your exam in 3 days" (sharpest entry workflow).
- *To:* wedge = **"master a hard multi-week course (cs231n / MIT 6.S191 / Andrew Ng / fast.ai) before its graded deadlines."** "Ace your exam in N days" is the sharpest **WTP-trigger event inside** this segment — not the segment itself. Paying core = the *deadline-bearing committed masterer*; the **global open-courseware self-learner is the reach/top-of-funnel layer** (huge + reachable, soft WTP — not the paying core).

**3. §3 / §6 — add the two named risks (NEW).**
- **R1 (payability unproven):** at true-zero, engagement is observed but payment is not (0 real paying). The MASDA "Differentiable/Actionable" open risk is now operationalized: the SOP-PRODUCT-002 N=10 watch must test **"do they pay at the first hard limit?"**
- **R2 (free-substitution):** **Google NotebookLM (free)** clones the doc→quiz→explanation→share spine and narrowed the gap further in **April 2026** (added mastery-tracking + a Socratic Learning Guide). The POD must lead on what it lacks: the **structured multi-week PATH + 17 activity types + concept-level Knowledge Map + learning-science authority**.

**4. §1 / §2 — the observed anchor (NEW, small).** The `supabase-analytics` Door-2 signal now grounds part of the persona: material = PDF 63% + YouTube 16% (dense, multi-session), a sustained-revisit mastery pattern beyond the founder (one user 42 sources / 36 sessions), 0 real paying, 15 founder-only shares (no organic referral). Cite it where the persona currently says "hypothesis."

## What stays unchanged
The psychographic core (§1), the "method not ability" reframe, the substitution stack + WTP anchor (§5), the anti-persona (§7), and the US-led intermarket posture — all confirmed. This is a wedge-sharpening, not a re-segmentation.
